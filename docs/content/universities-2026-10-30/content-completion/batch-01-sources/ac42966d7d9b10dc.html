Tuition Fee Concessions

TUITION FEE CONCESSIONS: 2024-25

A) Award of Scholarship: The following criteria based on percentage of marks obtained in the qualifying examination will be applicable during the first semester only for the newly admitted UG & PG students for the Academic Session 2024-25 subject to payment of full Tuition Fee for a semester or for a year:

95% & above: 75% of the Tuition Fee
90% to <95% : 50% of the Tuition Fee
80% to <90% : 30% of the Tuition Fee
70% to <80% : 10% of the Tuition Fee

B) The tuition fee waiver rules: For the real two sisters, it will be as under:

  • Till the period both the sisters studying in the University : 50% tuition fee will be waived off to the first elder sister.
  • Once the first sister passes out from the University : 100% tuition fee will be paid by the second sister till the completion of the course.

C) 100% tuition fee waiver for the duration of degree course to the students whose parents (Both Mother & Father) have lost their life due to Covid-19 pandemic.

D) 25% tuition fee waiver for the duration of degree course will also be applicable for the following categories:

  • Wards of Defence personnel i.e. (Army/Air Force/Navy/Para Military Forces) both either serving or retired.
  • Wards of the Freedom Fighters.
  • Single girl child.

However, such student as categorised in clause (C) & (D) above, at the time of admission and in consecutive semester/years of the degree course will not be entitled for other scholarship on the merit basis as approved earlier.
