Admission

Admission Procedure

Sixty Five per cent (65%) of the sanctioned admission capacity will be admitted through Tamil Nadu Agricultural University single window system counselling and the remaining 35% will be admitted directly by Palar Agricultural College – Kothamarikuppam by following the Tamil Nadu Agricultural university eligibility norms and merit.

Application

Candidates should register online in TNAU admission website  https://tnau.ucanapply.com/ and should obtain the ranking for admission to both counselling and management.

Fees

Fee structure for degree courses is as prescribed by the Government of Tamil Nadu.
