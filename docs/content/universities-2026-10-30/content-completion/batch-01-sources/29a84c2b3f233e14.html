Why Choose N.S. Agricultural College

✓Hands-on Agriculture EducationEducation goes beyond textbooks — students gain practical experience in agriculture, natural resource management, and land use, preparing them for entry-level positions as well as advanced careers in agriculture.
✓Ideal Location for LearningSituated in Markapur, in the western part of Prakasam District, Andhra Pradesh, the college provides exposure to diversified agricultural practices unique to this region.
✓Exposure to Diverse CropsStudents encounter a wide variety of crops — cereals, pulses, oilseeds, cash crops, and various horticultural crops — reflecting the rich agricultural diversity of Andhra Pradesh.
✓Extensive Farm InfrastructureSpread over 110 acres, the college farm allows students to gain hands-on knowledge in crop cultivation, cropping patterns, water and land management, and sustainable farming practices.
✓Well-furnished Laboratories & LibraryWell-furnished laboratories under different disciplines equip students with practical knowledge and exposure to modern equipment, while a well-stocked library fosters self-study, critical thinking, and research.

Eligibility & Selection Criteria

ParticularsB.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture
Qualifying ExaminationIntermediate / Diploma in Agriculture
Entrance ExaminationEAPCET / AGRICET
Age Limit17 years completed as on 31st December of the year of admission; upper limit 22 years (25 for SC & ST, 27 for physically challenged)

Under the EAPCET quota, students are admitted based on their performance in the state-level Common Entrance Test (EAPCET) conducted during the year of admission by the AP State Council of Higher Education, Government of Andhra Pradesh.

40% of the seats are reserved for candidates from families of agriculturists owning not less than 1 acre of land, who have studied for a minimum of 6 years in schools located in non-municipal areas.

Fee is paid at the time of counselling (admission) through online transfer or in cash; in subsequent semesters, fees are paid online through the ANGRAU Payment Gateway.

2

Step-by-Step Admission Process

1
University NotificationANGRAU invites applications from eligible candidates through notifications in leading newspapers and the University website, generally in the month of June. Admissions are through online and offline modes.
2
Entrance ExaminationIntermediate (10+2) students appear for EAPCET, conducted by the AP State Council of Higher Education. Diploma in Agriculture students appear for AGRICET, conducted by ANGRAU, Lam, Guntur.
3
Counselling & Fee PaymentAdmission is based on entrance performance. Fee is paid by the student at the time of counselling (admission) through online transfer or in cash.
4
First Registration & OrientationAdmitted students receive registration guidelines from the Associate Dean on arrival at the college. A registration and orientation programme is conducted for first-time students; attendance is reckoned from the date of registration.
5
Registration in Subsequent SemestersStudents register in person for the courses offered for their batch, producing their identity card at the registration centre. Payment of fee and other dues (college, department, hostel, library) precedes registration; semester fees are paid online through the ANGRAU Payment Gateway.

As per the ICAR VI Deans Committee curriculum, multiple entry and exit options are also available for students in the UG programme in Agriculture.
