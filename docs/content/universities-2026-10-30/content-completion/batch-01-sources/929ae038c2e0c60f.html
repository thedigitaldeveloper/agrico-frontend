About Us

Eternal University is a NAAC Accredited & ISO 9001:2015 Certified University established under the Himachal Pradesh Private University Act 2006. It operates under Himachal Pradesh Government Act no. 3 of 2009, with full rights to confer degrees as per UGC public notices on private universities. The great visionary of the 20th century, Sant Attar Singh Ji, foresaw that modern scientific education alone cannot fully serve humanity well. He envisioned that science must be deeply amalgamated with Braham Vidya (Spiritual Education) for complete holistic development. Consequently, our graduates excel academically while embodying stellar moral values as global Ambassadors of Peace.
