Facilities

Library
The modern computerized Library has more than Thirty thousand volume of books covering various areas of computer sciences, Life Science and Arts. It has subscriptions of three hundred journals. The Audio-Visual Centre & CD-ROM Workstation has an impressive collection of thousands of video cassettes & CD-ROMs. The Computerized catalogue could be accessed in the library.

Language Lab
The institution has an English Communication Lab with the prime objectives for developing the four major skills among the students. They are: Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking.

Hostel
The B.Sc.(Ag.) degree girls students are accommodated in girls hostel inside TRIARD campus. The boy’s students are accommodated in Roever Engineering Hostel which is 2 Km away from TRIARD campus.
  • Admission Procedures
  • Mess
Application for admission to the hostel along with prescribed form can be had from the office on payment. A student seeking admission should be accompanied by his parent/authorized Guardian and only after an interview by the Principal / Warden he/she will be given admission. Admission will depend on the decision of the Management. A senior student wishing to be re-admitted shall apply again in the prescribed form after meeting the Deputy Warden with the Parent / Guardian. Dismissal or suspension of a member from the College will involve automatic dismissal or suspension from the hostel, such students shall not enter the hostel without the permission of the Principal. Students admitted to the hostel will not be allowed to stay outside the college during the period of their course. The Managing authorities have the right to terminate the membership of the hostel, for breach of discipline or any grave offence even in the middle of the academic year.
The mess is run on a fixed rate per day basis. Inmates of the hostel are not allowed to enter the kitchen premises. Complaints and suggestions about food or service must be made to the Deputy warden in a proper manner. They should conduct themselves decently in the dining hall. The following timings shall be observed strictly by the inmates.

Breakfast : 7.00 a.m. to 7.30 a.m
Lunch : 12.30 p.m. to 1.00p.m
Dinner : 7.30 p.m. to 8.30 p.m

Internet
The Institution is having a fully equipped Computer Centre with 120 Pentium IV. Beside the Curriculum, Auto CAD, Hardware Servicing & Maintenance, C, C++, MS-Office 2000, 10 Mbps speed of Internet Usage and Wi-fi connections facilities for all students.
Apart from the computer laboratories setup for syllabus programs the Institutes have provided computer centres for general purposes and learning during extra hours. This includes Internet Facilities available through leased line and Wi-Fi for providing continuous and uninterrupted internet connectivity to students and faculty members. These facilities are also available to students beyond college hours for preparation of seminars & projects and research-orientated work.

About Us
