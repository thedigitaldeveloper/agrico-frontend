UG Programme

Course Name: B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture

Duration: 4-Years

Admission procedure

          All candidates should register online in TNAU Admission website and should obtain the ranking. 65% strength will be admitted through Tamil Nadu Agricultural University(TNAU) and 35% by ACAR management adopting TNAU eligibility norms.

Course Structure

      The B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture degree is typically a 4-year course under a credit-based semester system,  Genetics and Plant Breeding, Seed Science and Technology, Plant Biotechnology, Biochemistry, Agronomy, Agricultural Engineering, Animal Husbandry, Crop Physiology, Soil Science and Agricultural Chemistry, Environmental Sciences, Agricultural Microbiology, Agricultural Entomology, Plant Pathology, Sericulture, Agricultural Nematology, Horticulture, Food Science and Nutrition, Forestry, Agricultural Extension, Agricultural Economics, Agricultural and Rural Management , Agricultural Statistics, Computer, Mathematics,  Languages (English and Tamil) and Physical Education.
