Admission

Admission Procedure

The admission process is designed to be transparent, student-friendly, and strictly aligned with the guidelines of the affiliating university. Interested candidates may apply by submitting the prescribed application form along with the required documents either online or directly at the college admission office. Applications are carefully reviewed based on eligibility, academic performance, and relevant regulations.

Shortlisted candidates will be invited for counselling or verification, after which seats are allotted on merit. Selected students must confirm their admission by paying the required fees and completing the formal registration process within the specified timeline.

Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility criteria vary depending on the programme applied for.

  • B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture: Candidates must have completed 10+2 or equivalent with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology/Botany/Zoology or Vocational Agriculture.
  • M.Sc. (Agriculture) – Plant Pathology: Applicants must hold a Bachelor’s degree in Agriculture or an equivalent degree from a recognised university.
  • Diploma in Agriculture: Students must have passed SSLC/10th standard from a recognised board.

All admissions are subject to the minimum academic requirements and selection norms prescribed by the institution and the affiliating university.

Course Intake Details

The institution offers well-structured programmes with limited intake to ensure quality training, personalised guidance, and effective practical exposure.

  • B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture: Annual intake as per university/approval norms.
  • M.Sc. (Agriculture) – Plant Pathology: Limited seats to maintain a research-focused learning environment.
  • Diploma in Agriculture: Intake capacity designed to support hands-on training and field-based learning.

The controlled intake ensures optimal access to laboratories, farm units, faculty mentoring, and experiential learning opportunities.

Documents Required

Candidates must submit the following documents during admission and verification:

  • SSLC/10th Mark Sheet (for Diploma applicants)
  • Higher Secondary/12th Mark Sheet (for UG applicants)
  • UG Degree Mark Sheets & Provisional Certificate (for PG applicants)
  • Transfer Certificate (TC)
  • Community Certificate (if applicable)
  • Conduct/Character Certificate
  • Recent Passport-size Photographs
  • Aadhaar Card or Valid ID Proof
  • Any other documents required as per institutional or university norms

All original documents must be produced for verification, and photocopies should be submitted along with the application.
