Two universities, one mission

Eternal University, Baru Sahib and Akal University, Talwandi Sabo are the higher-education institutions built by the Baru Sahib mission — Eternal under The Kalgidhar Trust, Akal under The Kalgidhar Society — carrying value-based education from the classroom into research, professional training and community service.

Eternal University — The Kalgidhar Trust

Punjab

Akal University — The Kalgidhar Society

State-Act

Both established under state university legislation

The Baru Sahib mission built its rural school network first, then extended that same values-based approach into higher education. Eternal University, a women’s university in the Baru Valley of Himachal Pradesh, operates under The Kalgidhar Trust. Akal University, at Talwandi Sabo in Punjab, operates under The Kalgidhar Society. Together, the two universities let students who begin at an Akal Academy continue, under the same values, all the way to postgraduate and doctoral study.

The two universities

Each university has its own campus, its own academic focus and its own official website. This page is the entity record; the pages below go deeper into each institution.

Established 2008–09 · Himachal Pradesh Private University Act · The Kalgidhar Trust

Eternal University

Baru Sahib, Via Rajgarh, Sirmour District, Himachal Pradesh

A women’s university set in the Baru Valley, founded on the principle of Sanskar Nirman — character built alongside academics. Governed by The Kalgidhar Trust, Eternal University offers undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes across agriculture, health and allied sciences, engineering, education, and management.

  • NAAC accredited & ISO 9001:2015 certified
  • Himachal Pradesh’s first ICAR-accredited university
  • Recognised among India’s leading women’s education institutions

Established 2015 · Punjab Act No. 25 of 2015 · The Kalgidhar Society

Akal University

Talwandi Sabo, District Bathinda, Punjab

Set up to fulfil Guru Gobind Singh Ji’s prophecy of “Guru Ki Kashi” at Talwandi Sabo. Governed by The Kalgidhar Society, Akal University is home to the International Center for Comparative Religious Studies, and offers undergraduate and postgraduate degrees alongside free coaching for national competitive exams.

  • Established by an Act of the Punjab Legislature
  • International Center for Comparative Religious Studies on campus
  • Free coaching for UGC-NET, SSC, Bank PO and other exams; faculty trained at IITs, IISc and JNU

Why the Society built two universities

“Modern scientific education alone will not serve humanity well, unless it is amalgamated with Braham Vidya — spiritual education.”

This conviction, first voiced by Sant Attar Singh Ji and carried forward by Sant Teja Singh Ji and Baba Iqbal Singh Ji, is the foundation of both universities. It began with a single school at Baru Sahib in 1986, grew into the Akal Academies network, and in 2008 reached its next stage when The Kalgidhar Trust founded Eternal University.

The Kalgidhar Society carried the mission further in 2015 with Akal University at Talwandi Sabo — the site the tenth Guru, Guru Gobind Singh Ji, foretold would become a great centre of learning. Together, the Trust and the Society let a student’s education continue, under one set of values, from an Akal Academy classroom to a doctoral degree.

How the universities fit into Baru Sahib

Eternal University and Akal University sit alongside the Akal Academies as the two arms of the Society’s education mission.
