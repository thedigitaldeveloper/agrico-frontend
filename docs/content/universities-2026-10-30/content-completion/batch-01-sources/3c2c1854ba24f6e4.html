About PGPCAS

At PGP College of Agricultural Sciences (PGPCAS), we take pride in being a center of excellence in agricultural education and research. Established in 2013, our college is approved by the Government of Tamil Nadu, affiliated with Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU), Coimbatore, and recently accredited by the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) – a testament to our commitment to quality and standards in agricultural education.

Our 120+ acres green campus, set against the scenic Kolli Hills and surrounded by rural farmland, offers an ideal learning environment. We also utilize our 250-acre main campus on NH7 in Paramathi for extensive field-based training.

We offer B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture and Diploma in Agriculture programs that blend strong academics with practical experience. At PGPCAS, we are dedicated to shaping future-ready agricultural professionals who are innovative, ethical, and equipped to lead sustainable rural and agricultural development.
