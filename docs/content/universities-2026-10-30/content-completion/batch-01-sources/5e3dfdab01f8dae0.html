Agriculture

The Department of Agriculture is the pioneer and a fore runner among private teaching institutions imparting agricultural education in the state of Rajasthan.

BSc (Agriculture) Hons. course was started from the academic session 2014-15. It followed the recommendations of 4th Dean's committee report implementing the undergraduate syllabus with 20 percent regional resilience and is common throughout India. The department has a strong faculty, well equipped 13 laboratories, agronomic fields, tractor and farm machines as per ICAR norms. The department has all the required facilities of Poly-house, Vermi Compost unit for organic agriculture, drip and sprinkler irrigation system. It has units of medicinal and aromatic plants. The state government project on Olive plantation is also in progress at the campus.
