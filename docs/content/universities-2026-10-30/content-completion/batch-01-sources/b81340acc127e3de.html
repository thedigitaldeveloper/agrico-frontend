Welcome to Jagan Nath University

NAAC Accredited University in Jaipur, Rajasthan

Jagan Nath University, Jaipur was established by an Act of the Rajasthan State Legislature (Act No. 19 of 2008) on April 16, 2008. It is approved under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, 1956, accredited by NAAC (2nd Cycle) with an “A” grade in 2022, and is a member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU).

The University also holds a distinctive status in agriculture education: its B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture programme is accredited by ICAR for 5 years. Jagan Nath University offers UG and PG programmes across a wide range of disciplines.

  • UGCApproved under 2(f)
