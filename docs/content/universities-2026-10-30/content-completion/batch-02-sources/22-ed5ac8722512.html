Our Founder

Sri Sri University was envisioned by its namesake, Gurudev Sri Sri Ravi Shankar, a renowned spiritual leader and humanitarian, known for his vision of a stress-free, violence-free society, global well-being, interfaith harmony and conflict resolution.

A University Designed Just for You

At Sri Sri University, education is a transformative experience designed to develop the whole individual — intellectually, emotionally, and ethically. Guided by the vision of integrating the best of Eastern wisdom and Western innovation, the University offers a multidisciplinary environment that encourages inquiry, creativity, and character formation. Through rigorous academics, experiential learning, and value-based education, students are empowered to become competent professionals, conscious citizens, and compassionate leaders prepared to contribute meaningfully to society and the world.
