Revised Guidelines for Late Fee and Re-admission
As per the resolution passed in the Fees Structure Review meeting, the competent authority is pleased to approve the following revised late fees and readmissions:
(a) After the 25th day of the due date for the payment of the tuition fee, if the student does not pay the tuition fee, in such case the admission of the student will be cancelled and the student has to take readmission.
The readmission charge is Rs. 15000/-
(b) In the case the student pays the tuition fees within 25 days of the due date, the following are applicable:
(i) Tuition fee Up to Rs. 10000/- per semester there shall be no late fee levied.
(ii) In case of non-payment of tuition fees within the due date, fine @ Rs. 200/- per day shall be levied as late fees for up to 25 days.
(iii) On the 26th day, if the student did not pay the tuition fee, the admission of such student shall be cancelled and the student has to take readmission as per clause (a) above.
(c) Hostel fees and mess fees are all-inclusive and called Hostel Fees. No break-up can be provided.
