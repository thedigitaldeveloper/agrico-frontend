Raffles University provides unprecedented opportunities to be taught by experts in their respective fields with the help of latest technological aids. Students have the option to study Law, Engineering, Basic & Applied Sciences, Commerce, Pharmacy, Social Sciences and Management courses at Graduate, Post Graduate, Ph.D. levels, Diploma Courses and Certificate courses. The University also offers scholarship and free ship to help the deserving students to pursue their education and research.
