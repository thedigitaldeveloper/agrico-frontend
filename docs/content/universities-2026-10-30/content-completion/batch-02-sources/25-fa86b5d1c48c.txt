About Us:
Vivekananda Global University (VGU), Jaipur is a top private university in Rajasthan, known for quality education, industry-oriented courses, and strong placements. VGU offers popular undergraduate and postgraduate programs including B.Tech, BCA, BBA, B.Sc., B.Com., BHMCT, LLB, B.Arch, MBA, M.Tech, M.Sc., MCA, With modern infrastructure, experienced faculty, skill-based learning, and excellent placement opportunities, VGU Jaipur is the preferred choice for career-focused, industry-ready education in Rajasthan.
