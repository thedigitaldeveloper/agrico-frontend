6.5.4.3. Admission Process 
The admission guidelines including number of sanctioned seats, eligibility, fees structure, 
last date of receipt of application forms, declaration of shortlisted students list and last date 
of submission of semester fees for UG, PG and Ph.D programmes are advertised well in 
advance on the MM(DU) website.  
6.5.4.3.1. Fee Payment Mechanism: The admission form can be obtained from the 
Administrative Office of this Deemed to be University on payment of Rs. 1200/- (US $ 35 
in case of NRI/Foreign Nationals). Admission form can also be obtained by post by sending 
DD of Rs. 1250/- in favour of the Registrar of MM(DU). The Admission Form can also be 
downloaded from the website of MM(DU). Admission forms can also be filled online on 
MM(DU) website www.mmumullana.org. However, such candidates will have to enclose a 
DD of Rs.1250/- with the downloaded Admission Form. 
6.5.4.3.2. Eligibility for Admission  
The admission schedule along with last date for the receipt of admission forms and fees 
structure is approved by the Vice-Chancellor for every academic year. The candidates are 
required to submit their applications for admission as per the schedule announced in the 
Newspaper(s)/ MM(DU) Website.

6.5.4.4. Conduct of Practical and Hands on Training  
Each practical class is of two hours. In B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, there are two groups of 
each class; and Masters whole class is taught in one batch.  The state-of-the-art facilities 
are available in the Department for conduct of practical. All the laboratories viz; 
Agronomy, Horticulture, Entomology, Soil Science, Plant Pathology, Biotechnology, 
Plant Breeding and Genetics, Agri-Engineering, Agri-Informatics, Agri-economics, 
Extension Education are well equipped and have sufficient consumable chemicals and 
glassware’s for the conduct of Practical.  Charts and Models are available in laboratories 
as per requirements of the curriculum. For the conduct of research trials, all the requisite 
field facilities are also available.  
Hands –on-Training:  
 For hands on training modules of Bee-keeping, Mushroom Cultivation, Waste 
Management, Commercial Horticulture, Seed Production, Organic Farming are in 
place in the faculty and used by the students.   
 Value added courses of 16 contact hours were offered to emphasize more on hands-
on-training. The courses were: i) Waste Recycling by Vermi-Composting; ii) 
Mushroom Production Technology; iii) Post-Harvest Management of Horticultural 
Crops; and iv) Pesticides Application Techniques for Pest Management.
