About Faculty of Science

The Faculty of Science is an important constituent college of the University, making it an educational institution with a diverse and thriving community of students. The Faculty of Science is continuously enriching its identity. The Faculty of Science was established in the year 2016 at Manikya Lal Verma Shramjeevi College Campus with vision of empowering youth especially deprived classes to carry forward the imprints and benefits of education to their native place and at large in society. The mission of the Faculty of Science is to empower youth by teaching, learning, research and extension activities. At present the Faculty of Science, is running under graduate, post graduate and Ph. D. courses in various streams such as Botany, Biotechnology, Environmental Science, Chemistry, Computer Science, Physics, Mathematics, Bioinformatics, Zoology, Statistics, Renewable Energy and Forensic Science.

Dr. SapnaShrimali

Director

Dear Students, Parents, Faculty Members, and Visitors,
It gives me great pleasure to welcome you to our Science College, a vibrant center of learning, innovation, and academic excellence. In today's rapidly changing scientific and technological landscape, our mission is to provide quality education that equips students with the knowledge, skills, and values required to excel in their professional and personal lives.
In alignment with the vision of the National Education Policy 2020, our curriculum is continuously updated to promote multidisciplinary learning, skill development, research orientation, critical thinking, and experiential education. We strive to create a learner-centric environment that encourages creativity, innovation, and lifelong learning while preparing students to meet global challenges and opportunities.
The department actively promotes a culture of research and academic engagement by organizing national and international conferences, seminars, workshops, faculty development programs, and expert lectures. These initiatives provide valuable platforms for knowledge exchange, collaboration with leading academicians and researchers, and exposure to emerging trends in science. Our dedicated faculty members are committed to mentoring students and fostering scientific inquiry, ethical values, and leadership qualities. We encourage students to participate in research projects, internships, community outreach programs, and co-curricular activities that contribute to their holistic development.
As we move forward, we remain steadfast in our commitment to academic excellence, innovation, and societal progress. I invite all stakeholders to join us in our pursuit of knowledge and our endeavor to shape competent, responsible, and globally conscious future scientists and professionals.
I extend my best wishes to all students for a successful and rewarding academic journey.

Faculty of Science

MVSC Campus, Townhall Road, Udaipur (Raj.)

Our Mission and Vision

JRNRVU University's mission & vision is to empower students with a transformative education that fosters academic excellence, innovation, and social responsibility. We aim to create an inclusive environment where students develop critical thinking and prepare for impactful societal roles.

Our Mission

The mission of the Faculty of Science is to empower youth by teaching, learning, research and extension activities. The mission typically involves providing a high-quality education in various scientific disciplines, fostering critical thinking and problem-solving skills, and preparing students for careers in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM). Such institutions aim to advance scientific knowledge through research and innovation, and often emphasize the importance of ethical practice, interdisciplinary collaboration, and community engagement. The overall mission is to move towards excellence in higher education in order to achieve just, plural and equitable society in consonance with the constitutional values.

Our Vision

The Faculty of Science is an important constituent college of the Janardan Rai Nagar, Rajasthan Vidyapeeth (Deemed to be University), making it an educational institution with a diverse and thriving community of students. The Faculty of Science is continuously enriching it’s identity. The vision is to empowering youth especially deprived classes, to carry forward the imprints and benefits of education to their native place and at large in society. Also to provide knowledge and quality based education to the students by inculcating moral values, scientific temper and employing state of the art technologies. It aims to pursue excellence towards creating manpower with high degree of intellectual, professional and cultural development to meet the national and global challenges.
