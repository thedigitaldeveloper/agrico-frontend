Important Fee Policies

📌
Fee Payment
All fees must be paid through the university ERP system. Get fee receipt printed from ERP immediately after payment.
Access to university facilities is allowed only on clearance of dues.
After 20 days from semester start, unpaid students are de-registered and allowed back only after payment of re-registration charges of ₹11,000.
📄
Refund Policy
Caution Money of ₹10,000 is fully refundable at the end of the program (no dues clearance required).
Registration Fee of ₹1,000 (CDOE) and Enrolment Fee are non-refundable.
Refer to the university's Cancellation / Refund Policy for program-specific details.
🎓
Scholarship Notes
All scholarships are applied in the even semester only.
Scholarships apply to academic (tuition) fees only — hostel, mess and transport are not covered.
Maximum scholarship offer falls under the non-refundable category.
📊
Fee Revisions
Fees listed are for Academic Session 2026–27. The university reserves the right to revise fees annually.
Examination fee is notified separately at the beginning of each academic year.
Fees for specialised industry-collaborated programs may vary — confirm at time of admission.
