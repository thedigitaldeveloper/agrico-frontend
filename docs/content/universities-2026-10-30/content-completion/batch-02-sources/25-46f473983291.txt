How to Apply
Apply to VGU in
5 Simple Steps
—
Completely Online
The entire VGUCET registration and admission process is online and takes less than 30 minutes. Apply from the comfort of your home. Results are declared within 7 days of the exam.
VGUCET Registration Process
VGUCET (VGU Common Entrance Test)
— Free online exam. Taken from home. Determines eligibility AND scholarship amount. Register once, apply to any program at VGU.
1
Register on VGUCET Portal
Visit
vgucet.vgu.ac.in
and register with your Name, Email, Mobile, State and Gender. Registration is completely
free of cost
. You'll receive a confirmation SMS and email with your VGUCET ID.
2
Complete Your Profile
Login and complete your academic profile — 10th and 12th details, subjects, board name, percentage/CGPA. Select your program of interest and preferred specialization.
3
Choose Your Exam Date
Select an exam date and time slot from the available calendar. VGUCET is conducted in
online mode from home
using a webcam-proctored platform. Slots available April–June 2026.
Early phases have higher scholarship potential.
4
Appear in VGUCET
The exam is
60 minutes long
— covering English Proficiency, Logical Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude and General Awareness.
No negative marking.
Admit card emailed 3 days before the exam.
5
Receive Results & Confirm Seat
Results declared within
7 working days
via email and SMS. Your score determines scholarship percentage. Pay admission fee online to confirm your seat. Session starts August 2026.
