About Faculty
Faculty of Social Sciences and Humanities
The School of Social Sciences and Humanities at
JRN RVU
is committed to developing well-rounded individuals with strong academic knowledge, critical thinking, creativity, and ethical values. The school focuses on understanding society, culture, language, history, human behavior, and social development, helping students become responsible citizens and future leaders.
The programs are designed to provide a balanced learning experience through classroom teaching, interactive discussions, research-based learning, presentations, seminars, workshops, and field-based activities. Students are encouraged to explore real-world issues, analyze social challenges, and build communication and analytical skills that are essential for personal and professional growth.

Our Objectives
Faculty of Social Sciences and Humanities
The Department of Social Sciences and Humanities is committed to delivering high-quality education through a blend of strong foundational knowledge, critical thinking, and practical learning. The department aims to nurture socially responsible individuals who can understand human behavior, society, culture, and contemporary issues, while contributing to positive social development and meaningful change.
Faculty Objectives
Provide a strong foundation in core concepts of social sciences and humanities, including society, culture, history, and human values.
Develop practical understanding through research-based learning, fieldwork, case studies, and community engagement activities.
Foster analytical thinking, communication skills, and problem-solving abilities to address real-world social and human challenges.
Promote interdisciplinary learning and encourage innovation, creativity, and academic growth through seminars, projects, and collaborative learning.
