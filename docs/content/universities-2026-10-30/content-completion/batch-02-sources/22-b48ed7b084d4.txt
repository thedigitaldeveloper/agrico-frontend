Become an Agriculturist
Study at one of the best agriculture universities in India, renowned for excellence in agricultural sciences and allied fields. We combine world-class research and innovative education to lead the modern natural-farming revolution and develop future-ready professionals.
How Long Does It Take?
4 years Bachelors Degree with Specialisation + 2 years Masters Program
Why choose SSU?
SSU stands out as a premier institution for agricultural studies in India, recognised for its academic excellence and top ranking in Odisha. The university offers industry-driven, hands-on learning and advanced training to prepare students for real-world agricultural challenges. SSU’s focus on innovation, sustainable practices, and Agri-entrepreneurship ensures graduates are well-equipped for successful careers in modern agriculture.
What are the future career prospects?
Graduates in agriculture enjoy exceptional career prospects across a broad spectrum of high-growth industries. Opportunities span government and private sectors, research and technical roles, and dynamic new fields such as agri-consultancy, agri-entrepreneurship, agri-marketing, and agri-logistics, among many others. This diverse landscape ensures that professionals are well-positioned to make a significant impact and build rewarding careers in the rapidly evolving agricultural sector.
What are the Placement Opportunities provided?
The Placement Cell maintains strong partnerships with leading national and international organizations, ensuring excellent recruitment opportunities. Through expert guidance, targeted training, and ongoing support from our experienced faculty, students are empowered to make informed career choices and achieve professional success.

About the Course
Agriculture@SSU
The
Faculty of Agriculture
at SSU, recognized as one of the
best agriculture universities in India and a top agriculture university in Odisha
, is distinguished by its world-class expertise and innovative approach. We offer a comprehensive portfolio of specialized programs, each supported by advanced laboratories and practical modules:
Horticulture
: Advanced training in fruit, vegetable, and ornamental crop production.
Food Nutrition and Dietetics
: In-depth study of human nutrition, food safety, and diet planning.
Agronomy
: Focus on crop management, sustainable practices, and soil health.
Entomology:
Specialization in insect biology and integrated pest management.
Agricultural Statistics
: Data analysis for research, forecasting, and agribusiness decisions.
Agricultural Economics
: Economic principles applied to agriculture, market analysis, and policy.
Agricultural Extension
: Training in technology transfer, rural development, and community engagement.
Genetics and Plant Breeding
: Crop improvement through cutting-edge genetic innovation.
Plant Pathology
: Study and management of plant diseases to enhance crop resilience.
Soil Science
: Soil fertility, conservation, and management for sustainable agriculture.
Agribusiness
: Business management skills for agri-enterprises, supply chain management, marketing, and entrepreneurship.
Our teaching-learning philosophy is rooted in rigorous exposure to theoretical concepts and experiential learning at every level, supported by innovative, focused teaching methods and strong faculty-student bonding. Students benefit from:
Agri-industry internships and
hands-on training programs
(
HOT
) with leading national and international institutions, industries, and NGOs.
Agro-Industrial Attachment (AIA)
and study tours to agricultural research institutes, universities, and private organizations, providing exposure to cutting-edge innovations and practices.
Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE)
, offering placements with farmers for real-world, grassroots learning.
Experiential Learning Programs (ELP)
that foster Agri-based start-up creation, Agri-entrepreneurship, and leadership in Agri-tech.
Our curriculum is career-driven, including industry-aligned Master’s programs in high-demand fields, preparing students to become experts highly sought after in the job market. SSU’s spiritually enriched campus environment nurtures holistic personality development, empowering graduates to become thinkers, researchers, innovators, and scientists who will redefine the future of agriculture from farm to fork. Nationally and internationally, the Faculty of Agriculture stands apart for its unique commitment to excellence and leadership in agricultural education.

Vision & Mission
Vision:
Our vision is to push the boundaries of curiosity-driven and research-backed science to excel in agriculture and farming practices that can balance the need for food for humanity and the health of Earth and other species living in it.
Mission :
To build a faculty and team of interdisciplinary expertise to address comprehensive education in agriculture.
To develop tools and cutting-edge technologies for crop management to enhance the productivity of crops.
To develop pro-farmer outreach programs– scientist-farmer forums, farmer cooperatives, forward market linkages, certificate courses, ICT-based user-friendly mobile-based apps, interactive knowledge base on agricultural practices, etc.
To build a ‘Centre of Regenerative Agriculture and Sustainable Technologies’ on the principles of natural farming.
Develop an ‘Institute of Agricultural Sciences and Technology’ as a Centre of Excellence.
Uniqueness of pedagogy:
We have experiential learning, exposure visits to fields/institutions in each semester of all courses, apart from student seminars, internships, entrepreneurship ideation and professional competency building training for our students.
Counselling and Emotional Intelligence:
We engage with students to understand their limitations and help them enhance their emotional intelligence through spiritual interventions and counselling sessions. We have residential counsellors with decades of experience and expertise in working with youth internationally. They are available to help students resolve personal, professional or existential crises that they face currently.
