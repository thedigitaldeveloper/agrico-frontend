About AEEL
Admission 2026
Amrita Entrance Examination – Life Sciences, Agriculture & Medical Sciences (AEEL) 2026 is the admission test for various programs offered by Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham. These programs include Life Sciences, Agriculture, Pharmacy and Nursing available across multiple campuses.
This exam is for students who have completed their +2 (Class 12) with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology as core subjects. If you want to join any of these programs, you need to appear for AEEL 2026.
The exam will be conducted in 60+ cities across India, making it accessible to students nationwide.
AEEL Exam Date: May 16 and 17
Last Date for AEEL Registration: May 10

AEEL Handbook
Attention Please !
Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham has not appointed any Agent or Third-Party Client for securing admission in any programme. Students are hereby requested to contact only the toll-free number on our website for any admission related queries.

Admission Process
Seat Allotment Process
for AEEL 2025
After the entrance exam, candidates must register for CSAP (Centralized Seat Allotment Process) to participate in seat allocation. Admission will be confirmed only if the candidate meets the university’s eligibility criteria for the chosen program.
The CSAP registration link will be available along with the AEEL rank list.
Steps for CSAP Registration
Enter Marks: Submit your Class 12 subject marks on the portal.
Upload Marksheet: Upload a copy of your marksheet.
Set Academic Preferences: Choose the programs and campuses you want to join, in the order of your priority.
Example of Academic Preferences
1st Preference: B. Pharm at Kochi campus.
2nd Preference: B.Sc. Agriculture at Coimbatore campus.
3rd Preference: B.Sc. Cardiac Perfusion Technology at Faridabad campus.
4th Preference: B.Sc. Nursing at Kochi campus.
Important Note
Admission to Allied Health Science programmes will be through NEET 2026.
