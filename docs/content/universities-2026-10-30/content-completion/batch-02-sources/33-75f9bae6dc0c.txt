2.1.%u0009 Institutional ProfileSRM Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST), is one of India%u2019s premier and top-ranking universities, renowned for its excellence in engineering and multidisciplinary Education. Established in 1985 by the Founder Chancellor, Dr. T.R. Paarivendhar, SRMIST operates across six campuses. SRMIST offers a comprehensive range of Academic programs at the undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral levels across diverse disciplines including Engineering, Technology, Architecture, Design, Management, Medicine and Health Sciences, Law, Science and Humanities, and Agricultural Sciences. The university is committed to nurturing intellectual growth and fostering academic excellence within a vibrant and green campus environment that serves as a second home to Lakhs of students.With a strong emphasis on global engagement, SRMIST has established international alliances and collaborative initiatives to elevate its research and teaching methodologies. The SRMIST International Advisory Board (IAB) plays a pivotal role in strengthening the institution%u2019s international dimension, creating enhanced opportunities for research and career development. The university collaborates with prestigious foreign institutions such as MIT, Carnegie Mellon SRM Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST), established under the University Grants Commission Act and holding deemed university status since 2002, is one of India%u2019s most comprehensive multi-disciplinary private universities, accredited NAAC A++ with a robust research ecosystem. SRMIST presents this Institutional Development Plan (IDP) for the period 2026 to 2035 as a synthesis of three complementary inputs: the strategic framework and detailed thematic elaboration prepared by the IDP working group in alignment with UGC pillars; the SWOC (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Challenges) analysis conducted by the Faculty of Management Studies with inputs from faculty, students, staff, administrative heads, industry experts, alumni, and social media feedback; and the guidance of Mr. V.R.K. Prasad, Vice President at Morgan Stanley, who served as the external advisor.SRMIST operates across six campuses %u2014 Kattankulathur (KTR), Ramapuram, Vadapalani, Trichy, Baburayanpettai and NCR Ghaziabad, with a combined builtup area exceeding 12 million square feet. The university hosts approximately 1,00,000 + students which include 5,000 doctoral students and engages over 1,000 corporate recruiters annually.
