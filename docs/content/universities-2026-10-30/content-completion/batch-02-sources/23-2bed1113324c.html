Our Objectives

Faculty of Computer Science and IT

The Department of Computer & IT is committed to delivering high-quality technical education through a blend of strong foundational knowledge, industry-oriented skills, and hands-on learning. The department aims to nurture future IT professionals who are capable of innovation, problem-solving, and contributing to technological advancement across various computing and digital domains.

Faculty Objectives



  • Provide a strong foundation in core concepts of computer science, information technology, and computing fundamentals.




  • Develop practical skills through programming labs, software development projects, workshops, and modern IT tools.




  • Foster analytical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving abilities to address real-world technical challenges.




  • Promote interdisciplinary learning and encourage innovation through research activities, industry exposure, and collaborative projects.
