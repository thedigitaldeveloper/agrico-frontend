Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to commonly asked questions about Suresh Gyan Vihar University.

Yes, SGVU is a UGC-recognized university and has received NAAC A+ accreditation. Various programs are approved by statutory bodies such as AICTE, PCI, NCTE, BCI, and other relevant authorities as applicable.
SGVU offers diverse programs across engineering, computer science, management, pharmacy, law, agriculture, health sciences, applied sciences, education, design, hospitality, and many other disciplines at UG, PG, diploma, and PhD levels.
Students choose SGVU for its industry-oriented curriculum, experienced faculty, modern infrastructure, research opportunities, innovation labs, strong industry collaborations, and focus on career-ready education.
Yes, SGVU has a dedicated placement cell that supports students with internships, industry interactions, skill development, and placement assistance. The university has connections with numerous recruiting companies across different sectors.
Students can apply for admission through the official SGVU admission portal by selecting their preferred program, completing the application process, and submitting the required academic documents.
Yes, SGVU offers scholarship opportunities and financial assistance to eligible students based on various criteria. Students can check the scholarship details during the admission process.
SGVU provides modern campus facilities, including well-equipped laboratories, libraries, sports facilities, cafeterias, innovation spaces, research centers, and a student-friendly learning environment.
Yes, research and innovation are among the key priorities of SGVU. The university encourages students and faculty members to participate in research projects, publications, innovation activities, and interdisciplinary learning.
SGVU stands out through its blend of academic excellence, research-driven learning, industry partnerships, practical exposure, modern facilities, and commitment to developing future-ready professionals.
Yes, SGVU emphasizes industry exposure through internships, live projects, workshops, expert sessions, industrial visits, and collaborations with industry partners to prepare students for real-world careers.
