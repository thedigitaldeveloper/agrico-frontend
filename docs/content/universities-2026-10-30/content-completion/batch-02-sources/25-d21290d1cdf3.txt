"The Faculty of Agriculture is committed to addressing the pressing challenges facing our global food systems and environment. Through our innovative research, interdisciplinary learning, and community engagement, we are empowering the next generation of agricultural leaders and scientists to develop sustainable solutions that will nourish our world and protect our natural resources. Together, we are cultivating the knowledge, skills, and passion needed to tackle the complex issues of food security, climate change, and environmental stewardship."
Welcome at the Faculty of Agriculture at Vivekananda Global University, education goes beyond classroom learning. Our programs combine strong academic foundations with hands-on field experience to prepare students for real-world agricultural challenges.
Our students actively participate in modern farming practices, laboratory research, field training, and agribusiness development, gaining practical knowledge that aligns with today’s agricultural industry needs. Through activities such as crop trials, agricultural research projects, internships with agri-industries, and exposure to sustainable farming techniques, students build the skills required to excel in the evolving agriculture sector.
With we invite aspiring agricultural professionals to join our dynamic academic community and become part of a university that is committed to developing skilled agri-scientists, entrepreneurs, and leaders who will shape the future of sustainable agriculture in India and beyond.

Horticulture
The Horticulture Lab at Vivekananda Global University provides hands-on training in fruit, vegetable, and ornamental crop cultivation using modern horticultural techniques.
Soil Science
The Soil Science Lab helps students analyze soil properties, fertility, and nutrient management to improve sustainable crop production and soil health.
Genetics & Plant Breeding
The Plant Breeding Lab focuses on crop improvement techniques, helping students learn about genetic selection, hybridization, and modern plant breeding methods.
Plant Pathology
This lab trains students to identify plant diseases and study microorganisms that affect crops, supporting better plant protection and disease management.
Agronomy
The Agronomy Lab provides practical learning on crop production, field management, and modern agricultural practices for improving crop yield and farm productivity.
Agriculture Engineering
The Agriculture Engineering Lab introduces students to farm machinery, irrigation systems, and modern agricultural equipment used in advanced farming practices.

Why Choose VGU for

6 reasons why VGU is Rajasthan's most sought-after engineering destination
Industry-Oriented Agriculture Education
The Faculty of Agriculture at Vivekananda Global University offers industry-focused agricultural education that combines theoretical knowledge with practical field training to prepare students for real-world agricultural careers.
Advanced Agriculture Laboratories
Students gain hands-on experience through well-equipped laboratories in soil science, plant breeding, agronomy, horticulture, and agricultural engineering, helping them understand modern agricultural technologies.
Practical Learning & Field Training
The program emphasizes experiential learning through field demonstrations, crop experiments, nursery management, and farm training to develop practical agricultural skills.
Research & Innovation Opportunities
Students are encouraged to participate in research projects, conferences, and innovative studies in areas such as precision farming, organic farming, and sustainable agriculture.
Industry Exposure & Educational Visits
The faculty organizes industrial visits, agricultural fairs, and educational tours to research institutes and agricultural industries to provide valuable industry exposure.
Diverse Career Opportunities
Graduates can pursue careers as agriculture officers, agronomists, soil scientists, agri-entrepreneurs, and research professionals in government, private sector, and agribusiness industries.
