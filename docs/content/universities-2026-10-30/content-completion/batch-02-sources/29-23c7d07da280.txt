FAQs
Everything you need to know about life at Suresh Gyan Vihar University.
Is hostel facility available for students?
Yes, we have separate hostels available for boys and girls, both equipped with high-speed Wi-Fi, laundry, and 24/7 security.
How is SGVU different from traditional institutes?
SGVU stands apart through its EDU-REVOLUTION model — 60% learning outside classrooms, industry co-designed curriculum, AI-powered ecosystems, and a focus on employability over attendance.
Is there an entrance exam for admission?
Yes, admissions are conducted through SGVUEE. Eligible candidates can also gain direct admission based on their qualifying exam scores. Merit-based scholarships are linked to entrance performance.
Are scholarships available for students?
Absolutely. SGVU offers a wide range of merit-based scholarships and freeships for Session 2026-27. Financial assistance is available across multiple programs.
Does the curriculum ensure that I am industry-ready?
Yes. SGVU's curriculum is co-designed with industry leaders. Students get hands-on exposure through live projects, industry immersion, Centres of Excellence, and a robust placement cell with 125+ recruiting companies.
