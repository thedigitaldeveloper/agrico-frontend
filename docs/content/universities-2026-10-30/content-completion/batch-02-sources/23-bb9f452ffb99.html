About Us

Janardan Rai Nagar Rajasthan Vidyapeeth, Udaipur, is a beacon of education, culture, and social transformation in India. Established in 1937 by the visionary, reformer, and educationist Manishi Pandit Janardan Rai Nagar, the institution began as a literacy and education movement for the underprivileged and marginalized communities of Southern Rajasthan. From these humble beginnings, it steadily grew into a vibrant center of learning dedicated to the ideals of self-reliance, inclusivity, democratic values, and cultural preservation. In recognition of its remarkable contributions, the Government of India conferred the status of ‘Deemed-to-be University’ in 1987.

The institution was founded with the idea of creating a center of learning that would remain deeply rooted in Indian values yet remain open to global perspectives. Pandit Janardan Rai Nagar envisioned an educational movement that would bridge the gap between traditional wisdom and modern knowledge systems. He believed that true education must awaken both the intellect and the conscience, preparing individuals to face challenges with courage, creativity, and compassion.

Over the decades, the University has expanded its horizons, offering diverse academic programs in humanities, sciences, social sciences, education, law, management, health sciences, and emerging fields of research and technology. Its teaching-learning process emphasizes not only academic rigor but also moral responsibility, critical inquiry, and practical application. The faculty, drawn from various disciplines, is dedicated to fostering an environment where students are encouraged to think independently, innovate fearlessly, and contribute meaningfully to society.

The University carries a strong legacy of community engagement and extension activities, consistently working to connect higher education with grassroots realities. Outreach programs, literacy campaigns, rural development initiatives, and women empowerment projects have been integral to its mission. This commitment to social responsibility makes the University not just a seat of learning but also a catalyst of change. In resonance with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, it emphasizes holistic, multidisciplinary, and experiential education, shaping socially responsible and skilled citizens for nation-building.

Another significant strength of the University lies in its emphasis on research and innovation. Scholars are encouraged to explore subjects of contemporary relevance while keeping in mind the ethical and cultural dimensions of knowledge creation. The institution actively promotes interdisciplinary approaches and provides platforms for dialogue between traditional knowledge systems and modern scientific thought. This spirit of integration ensures that education here remains holistic, dynamic, and transformative.

The University is constantly upgrading its academic structure, pedagogy, and infrastructure to remain future-ready. Digital learning, skill development programs, incubation centers, and international collaborations have added new dimensions to its growth. Yet, at its heart, the University remains deeply committed to its founding mission of creating responsible citizens who are intellectually competent and socially conscious.

The lush green campus, situated in the historic city of Udaipur, offers a serene and inspiring environment for learning. The cultural richness of Rajasthan blends seamlessly with the academic vibrancy of the institution, providing students with a unique experience that combines tradition with modernity.

Today, Janardan Rai Nagar Rajasthan Vidyapeeth stands tall as a beacon of higher learning and human values. It continues to carry forward the legacy of its founder by shaping generations of students into enlightened individuals who can contribute to building a just, progressive, and harmonious society.

The University has received many awards few of them are National Excellence Award 2015 for Community Services Excellence, Public Folk and Cultural Award by Hon’ble President of India; FICCI Award for working in the field of Rural Development; and National Child Development Award by Hon’ble President of India. The University also got good ranks at national level by IIRF and Unirank. The University has also achieved University of the year 2021 from the academic insight and created a record of maximum participations in an online quiz while lockdown period of India due to COVID-19.

Our Mission and Vision

JRNRVU University's mission & vision is to empower students with a transformative education that fosters academic excellence, innovation, and social responsibility. We aim to create an inclusive environment where students develop critical thinking and prepare for impactful societal roles.

Our Mission

The Mission of the University has been knowledge empowerment of youth, especially tribal youth, for enhancement of the quality of their life. The specific missions of the University are as under:

  • To provide professional and technological knowledge in the present scenario.
  • To ensure the active participation of the students in learning, discussions, field-workshops, training programmes, group discussions, and field-work etc.
  • To promote interaction within the community through projects in the urban, rural and remote areas and also to reach them through mobile library.
  • To conserve and preserve the values of the past and to interpret them for the purpose of reconstructing history and understanding of the present.
  • To develop a democratic set-up to run the administration known as “Jan Tantra Shilanyas”.
  • Excellence in education through quality teaching linked with research and extension activities.

Our Vision

The Vision of the University is “equitable knowledge empowerment of masses by blending the modern means with conventional values, execution of problem oriented research leading to policy interventions and extension services linked with quality improvement in life of the deprived classes and marginalized group, especially tribal population.”

Academic Excellence & Innovation

We offer a wide range of Undergraduate, Postgraduate, and Doctoral programs designed for the modern world. Our curriculum is fully aligned with NEP 2020, focusing on skill development and independent thinking across various faculties:

Technology & IT

Future-ready engineering and a designated AICTE Information Facility Center.

Health Sciences

A comprehensive wing featuring Homeopathy, Physiotherapy, Nursing, and Pharmacy, supported by our own Allopathic Hospital.

Agriculture

Specialized programs providing location-specific technology to empower the farming community.

Traditional Knowledge

Unique departments dedicated to Jyotish, Vastu, and Archaeology, preserving our rich cultural roots.

Intellectual Property

Committed to Excellence in Education

Our Heart: Social Commitment

We believe the true test of education is how it serves the community. JRNRV is the largest provider of adult and continuing education in the region. We have adopted 200 villages that act as "educational laboratories" where students and faculty work directly on rural development, women's empowerment, and folk art preservation. This dedication has earned us prestigious honors, including the FICCI Award for Rural Development.

Looking Ahead to 2026-27

As we move forward, our vision is clear. We are launching market-aligned programs in AI, Data Science, and Renewable Energy while expanding our digital footprint. We aim to foster a new generation of entrepreneurs through our incubation centers and stay committed to the UN Sustainable Development Goals. At JRNRV, we don't just grant degrees; we shape enlightened individuals ready to build a progressive and harmonious society. Join us in our mission to serve the "last man in the line" while embracing the digital frontier.
