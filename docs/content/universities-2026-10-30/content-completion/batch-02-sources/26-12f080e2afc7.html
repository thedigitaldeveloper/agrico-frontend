Overview

The B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture is a four-year undergraduate degree programme in Agricultural Sciences designed to equip students with comprehensive knowledge and practical skills required to address contemporary challenges in agriculture. The programme is offered by the Faculty of Agricultural Sciences (FASC), SGT University, which is accredited by the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR), ensuring adherence to national academic standards and quality benchmarks. The curriculum is multidisciplinary in nature, integrating core domains such as Agronomy, Horticulture, Soil Science, Plant Breeding and Genetics, Entomology, Plant Pathology, Agricultural Economics, Extension Education, and Seed Science. In addition, the programme incorporates emerging and advanced areas including Precision Agriculture, Biotechnology, Internet of Things (IoT), and Geographic Information Systems (GIS), thereby preparing students for future-ready agricultural practices. A distinguishing feature of the programme is its strong emphasis on experiential learning. Students gain hands-on exposure through laboratory experiments, field training, research farm activities, and protected cultivation structures such as polyhouses. Structured components like Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE), Agro-Industrial Attachment (AIA), and Experiential Learning Units (ELUs) provide real-world exposure to farming systems, agribusiness operations and value addition practices. The programme fosters innovation, entrepreneurship, and practical problem-solving abilities, encouraging students to engage in agri-startups and large-scale production systems. Graduates of the programme are well-prepared to pursue diverse career pathways in government services, research and development, agribusiness industries, startups and higher education.

Eligibility

Qualification: 10+2 Science Group (PCM / PCB / PCMB) or Agriculture related subjects with a minimum score of 50% marks

Learning Outcomes

  • Conceptual Understanding: Demonstrate a comprehensive understanding of fundamental concepts, principles, and scientific foundations in core agricultural disciplines.
  • Application of Knowledge: Apply theoretical knowledge effectively to real-world agricultural situations, including crop production, resource management, and sustainable farming practices.
  • Experimental and Analytical Skills: Develop proficiency in designing and conducting experiments, making systematic observations, and performing data collection, analysis, and interpretation.
  • Problem-Solving Ability: Identify and solve subject-specific agricultural problems using appropriate tools, technologies, and scientific methodologies.
  • Practical Competence: Exhibit hands-on skills and technical competencies through fieldwork, laboratory practices, and experiential learning components such as RAWE, AIA, and ELUs.

Fees & Scholarships

  • Program Fees: ₹ 87,500 per semester
  • Scholarship: Up to 100% merit-based scholarships available
