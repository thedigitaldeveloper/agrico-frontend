B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture
Course Overview
The B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture programme at the ICAR-accredited School of Agricultural Sciences, Shri Guru Ram Rai University (SGRRU), Dehradun, is a four-years (Eight Semesters) undergraduate degree designed in accordance with the ICAR curriculum. The programme combines strong theoretical foundations with extensive practical training, preparing students for careers in modern agriculture, agribusiness, research, and entrepreneurship.
The curriculum covers major disciplines including Agronomy, Horticulture, Genetics & Plant Breeding, Soil Science, Agricultural Microbiology, Entomology, Plant Pathology, Seed Science & Technology, Agricultural Economics, Agricultural Extension, and Agricultural Engineering. Students gain practical exposure through laboratories, a 30+ hectare Instructional and Research Farm, Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE), field visits, internships, and research projects.
Why Choose B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture?
Excellent Career Opportunities in government departments, ICAR institutes, agricultural universities, agribusiness companies, banks, seed and fertilizer industries, food processing industries, and multinational companies.
Industry-Relevant Curriculum based on the latest ICAR recommendations and emerging agricultural technologies.
Hands-on Learning through laboratory practicals, field demonstrations, crop production, internships, and experiential learning programmes.
Research and Innovation with opportunities to participate in research projects, scientific events, and agricultural innovations.
Growing Demand for agricultural professionals in India and across the world due to increasing focus on food security, climate-smart agriculture, and sustainable farming.
Entrepreneurship Opportunities in organic farming, protected cultivation, precision agriculture, agribusiness, agri-startups, seed production, and horticultural enterprises.
Higher Education Pathways leading to M.Sc. (Ag.), MBA (Agribusiness), Ph.D., and various competitive examinations.
Why Choose Shri Guru Ram Rai University, Dehradun?
Experienced Faculty
Learn from highly qualified and experienced faculty members dedicated to quality teaching, research, innovation, and student mentoring.
Modern Infrastructure
The School offers state-of-the-art laboratories, ICT-enabled smart classrooms, digital learning facilities, and a 30+ hectare Instructional and Research Farm equipped with a Hi-Tech Polyhouse, Automated Weather Station, Surface Observatory, Apiary, Mushroom Unit, Nursery, Orchard, Vermicompost Unit, Biogas Plant, and specialized crop production units.
Experiential Learning
Students gain practical knowledge through laboratory exercises, field demonstrations, internships, Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE), educational tours, industrial visits, and extension programmes.
Research and Innovation
The University promotes research, innovation, sustainable agriculture, precision farming, digital agriculture, and climate-smart agricultural practices through student research projects and collaborations.
Industry Exposure and Placements
Strong industry linkages, expert lectures, internship opportunities, entrepreneurship development programmes, and placement assistance prepare students for successful careers in agriculture and allied sectors.

Welcome to the Student FAQ! Here you'll find answers to common questions to help you navigate your academic journey. To register for classes, log in to the student portal, navigate to the course registration section, select your courses, and confirm your schedule.
Q1. What can I do after completing B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture from SGRR University?
Graduates can pursue higher education (M.Sc., MBA Agri-Business, Ph.D.), prepare for government competitive examinations, join private agri-industries, work in research organizations, or start their own agricultural enterprise.
Q2. What are the latest trends in agriculture that students should learn?
Modern agriculture is driven by:
Precision Farming
Artificial Intelligence (AI)
Drone-based Crop Monitoring
GIS & Remote Sensing
Smart Irrigation
Climate-Smart Agriculture
Protected Cultivation
Digital Agriculture
Sustainable Farming Technologies
SGRR University emphasizes practical exposure to these emerging technologies.
Q3. What career options are available beyond farming?
Graduates can build careers in:
Agribusiness Management
Agricultural Marketing
Seed Industry
Food Processing
Precision Agriculture
Agri-Finance
Agricultural Consultancy
Research & Development
Biotechnology
Agri-Tech Startups
Rural Development
International Agricultural Organizations
Q4. Is there scope for higher studies and research?
Absolutely. Students can pursue M.Sc., Ph.D., and research programmes in specialized agricultural disciplines, leading to careers in universities, ICAR institutes, research organizations, and international agencies.
Q5. Does the programme provide practical training?
Yes. The curriculum includes:
Experimental Farms
Practical Laboratory Sessions
Industrial Visits
Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE)
Experiential Learning Programmes (ELP)
Internships
Field Demonstrations
Research Projects
These activities ensure graduates are industry-ready.
Q6. Are international career opportunities available after B.Sc. Agriculture?
Yes. Graduates with strong technical knowledge and practical skills can explore opportunities in:
International research organizations
Agri-business companies
Sustainable agriculture projects
Food security programmes
Higher education abroad
Q7. How can I enquire or apply for admission?
Prospective students can submit an enquiry through the Online Admission Enquiry Form available on the SGRR University website or contact the admissions office for personalized counselling.
Program Outcome (POs)
PO1
In depth understanding of operational aspects, principles and objectives of Agriculture and Allied Sciences.
PO2
Demonstrate the professional agricultural solutions in societal and environmental contexts, with the knowledge and need for sustainable development.
PO3
Critical thinking and problem solving skills to understand the importance of crop production and animal husbandry.
PO4
Use of research based knowledge in collection, presentation of data, analysis of results of experiments in laboratories and fields for their validation
PO5
Creation and selection of different technologies for agricultural purposes
PO6
Application of contextual knowledge of agricultural farming for societal purposes
PO7
Understand and evaluate ethical environment issues related to agricultural farming.
PO8
Follow professional ethics and responsibilities and norms of agricultural subjects while practicing.
PO9
Demonstrate and ability to engage in analyzing situations and constructing viable solutions to work effectively with each other.
PO10
Develop an understanding of communication methods, resources utilization, cultivation, management of crops, and value addition of agricultural produces
PO11
Motivate for entrepreneurship, start-up through project planning and execution, research and training during field visits etc.
PO12
Generate a culture of life-long learning in an inclined environment to achieve personal and enhance their employability for jobs in different sectors
