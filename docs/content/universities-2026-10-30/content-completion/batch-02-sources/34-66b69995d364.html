ADMISSION

Rabindranath Tagore University (RNTU) is India’s first skill-based private university that strives to impart skill-based quality education. The University promotes research-driven advancement of knowledge to create successful professionals. Established by the AISECT Group in 2010, it has carved a niche in Madhya Pradesh. With strong industry linkages, the university’s focus lies on the holistic learning & development of students.

Key Highlights

NAAC accredited and NIRF Ranked for Four Consecutive Years among top 200 Universities
Startup Focused - Set up MP’s First Atal Incubation Centre by Niti Ayog, Govt. of India
Centres of Excellence in the Field of Adv. Technology, Science, Agriculture, Arts , Indian Culture
Strong Industry Academia Collaborations
The First Private National School of Drama in Central India
Highly Qualified Faculties With Doctorate Degree
