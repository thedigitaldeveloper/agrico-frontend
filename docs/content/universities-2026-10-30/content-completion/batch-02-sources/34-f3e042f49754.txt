Faculty of Agriculture
Modern facilities, including labs, spacious classrooms, a research farm, and a greenhouse, provide exceptional learning and research opportunities.
Adequate irrigation provision at the research farm.
Abundant farm equipment such as tractors, rotavators, cultivators, ploughs, seed cum fertiliser drills, zero tillage seed drills, a food processing cum training centre, and a hi-tech custom hiring centre to ensure timely completion of agricultural tasks.
Conduct research experiments and demonstrations to provide practical outdoor training to students.
Direct supervision of research farms by the Dean of the Faculty of Agriculture, working in coordination with the farm manager, technical staff, and field personnel.

OVERVIEW
To provide education in agriculture, a college called the "Faculty of Agriculture" was established and started as a constituent college in the academic session of 2015–16 to achieve excellence in academics and research in underserved areas of the Raisen district by distributing quality agriculture education. With the approval of the Academic Council and Board of Management of Rabindranath Tagore University, a four-year degree programme was launched in the fall of the 2015–16 academic year. According to the ICAR's guidance, the Faculty of Agriculture has approved the course guidelines and content. The 4th Deans' Committee (ICAR) curriculum and guidelines were approved for the 2015–16 academic year, while the 5th Deans' Committee (ICAR) recommendations began with the 2016–17 academic year.

Students Research
Rabindranath Tagore University has a well-established research culture that provides the necessary infrastructure, financial assistance, and a proper academic environment to all its students as well as to its Scholars. It has enabled the maintenance of the growth trajectory of research and development at the university. Research activities at the University are governed by the research policy and guidelines approved by the University authorities.
Faculties Research
The Agriculture Research Centre, established as a Centre of Excellence within the Faculty of Agriculture, oversees research and extension activities. All endeavours at ARC follow a 100% organic management approach. It conserves a diverse collection of oilseeds, fodder, pulses, cereals, and vegetable crops, incorporating advanced and rare germplasm from across the nation.The Core Research and Innovative Group (CRIG), an initiative of the University, aims to foster a research-oriented environment. Recognising the substantial resources—financial, temporal, and otherwise—required for research endeavours, Rabindranath Tagore University established the CRIG.
