Scholarship
Renewal Conditions
All VGU scholarships are automatically renewed each year — provided the following conditions are met every semester.
✅
Even Semester Only
All scholarships will be applied in the even semester only.
✅
NON Refundable Category
Maximum offer in scholarship will come under the NON Refundable category.
✅
Minimum 6 SGPA
All scholarship will be continued only if the student gets minimum 6 SGPA in each semester.
✅
No Attendance Shortage
Scholarship will be applicable only in one condition — if any student gets detained because of shortage of attendance in any subject, their scholarship will be revoked.
✅
Indian Students Only
All scholarships are only for Indian Students and applicable only on Academic fee.
✅
One Category Only
Student can avail any one category of the above listed applicable scholarship only.
How to
Apply for Scholarship
Scholarships at VGU are automatically assessed at the time of admission — no separate application needed for most categories.
01
🎓
Register for VGUCET
Register free at forms.vgu.ac.in/cet. Take the VGUCET exam — your score automatically determines your scholarship eligibility band.
→
02
📋
Submit Documents
At admission, submit documents relevant to your category: mark sheets, student enrolment, employee ID card, achievement certificate, etc.
→
03
🔍
Scholarship Assessment
The admission committee assesses all eligible categories and applies the highest applicable scholarship on your tuition fee automatically.
→
04
📄
Admission Letter
Your admission letter will clearly state the scholarship category and percentage awarded — valid for the full program duration subject to renewal conditions.
→
05
✅
Scholarship Applied
Scholarship is credited in the even semester every year. Maintain 6 SGPA and attend all classes regularly for automatic renewal throughout your program.
