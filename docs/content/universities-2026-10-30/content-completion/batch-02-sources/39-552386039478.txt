Admission Procedure
Following are admission pathways of Raffles University, Neemrana
Inquire and know more about the programme
Raffles University offers a unique opportunity to explore your studies in the University with a great academic reputation. Kindly explore the Raffles University website to know more about a program of your choice.
You can also download our e-brochure from www.rafflesuniversity.edu.in
For any additional information or clarification, you may post/email or visit the University campus and our counselor will get in touch with you. You may also call us on our contact number for any assistance.

Registration through online or offline by visit the University Campus
Raffles University has an online application process. You are required to provide information such as your personal contact details, educational qualifications, achievements/ extracurricular activities and statement of purpose, etc.
Please register on the Raffles University website (http://pay.rafflesuniversity.edu.in/) to register yourself or visit the University campus for registration.
Situation Test and Personal Interview
The candidates will be finally selected for admission based on the candidates' performance in the Situation Test and Personal Interview.
The following weightage will be given for each component of the selection criteria and composite scores will be computed based on the following selection criteria.
1. Situation Test: 40%
2. Personal Interview: 60%
Confirmation of Selection and declaration of result
Raffles University shall evaluate your candidature based on ST and PI and send offer of Registration/ Admission.
Offer of Registration/ Admission
Letter of Registration /Admission will be sent to selected candidates through email / by post.
Acceptance of the offer of Registration/ Admission
Selected candidates need to accept their admissions offer within the validity period. Required information will be detailed in your admission offer letter.
The required documents to be submitted at the time of registration/Admission are:
Copy of Letter of Registration/ Admission
Migration Certificate (No objection certificate from the Board/ University last attended)
Attested copy of 10th , 12th, Graduation and Post-Graduation Degree/certificate (As per Couse Eligibility Criteria)
Character certificate (from the head of the Institute from which the student has obtained his qualifying degree or any Gazetted officer.
Documentary proof for date of birth
Gap certificate (in Original) (if applicable)
Equivalence Certificate from Association of Indian Universities, New Delhi (if an applicant has passed qualifying examination from outside India)
Attested Copy of Aadhar Card
4 Passport size Colour photographs.
Any other relevant certificate
Payment of Registration / Admission Fee
Admission deposit fees can be paid through the following modes:
Cash Deposit to Account office in University Campus
Swapping of your Debit Card or Credit Card on swap machine.
Demand Draft (DD)
Cheque in favour of Raffles University payable at Neemrana
Cash deposit at any branch of  YES Bank

Confirmation of Provisional Admission
Successful submission of fee along with academic document as per rules, provisional admission letter will be issued to students and They will communicate all the necessary information about your joining such as reporting date and time, academic calendar, housing details, documents to be submitted during enrolment, items to bring to campus, and induction schedule before the commencement of academic program.
Withdrawal of Admission and fee
If a candidate wishes to withdraw his/her admission after accepting the offer and having paid the admission deposit fee, he/ she can make a request by sending an e-mail to admission@rafflesuniversity.edu.in from his/her registered e-mail id seeking refund.
The university shall refund the deposit fee after deducting INR 11000, if such a request is received before the specified date.
Privacy Policy
All information submitted by the applicants will be kept confidential, except as desired by the law.
