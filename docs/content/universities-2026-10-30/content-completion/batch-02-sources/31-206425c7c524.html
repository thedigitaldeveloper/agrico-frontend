Discover How To Reach SGRR University Campuses 

Wondering how to reach SGRR University? We've got you covered! Whether you're arriving by air, train, or bus, our campuses are easily accessible. Check out the hassle-free routes below:

SGRR University Patel Nagar Campus: 

1) By Bus ( ISBT ): 

For those opting for bus travel, the ( ISBT ) in Dehradun serves as a major transportation hub. From ISBT, you can find various modes of transport to reach SGRR University. 

Upon reaching ISBT, one can conveniently board a bus or Vikram ( Tampo ) destined for Patel Nagar (Laal Pull). The distance between ISBT and Patel Nagar is a mere 3.7 kilometers, translating to a quick and efficient commute of approximately 10 to 15 minutes. The SGRR University Patel Nagar Campus is strategically located in close proximity to Patel Nagar, near the Dainik Jagran Office. 

For those choosing the Kargi Chauk route, a seamless journey to SGRR University is facilitated through a two-step transportation approach. Commencing from ISBT, travelers can initially board a Vikram (Tampo) destined for Kargi Chauk. Subsequently, from Kargi Chauk, the next mode of transport is the convenient "E-Riksha."

This route not only leads directly to SGRR University Patel Nagar Campus but also passes by Shri Mahant Indiresh Hospital. 

2) By Train ( Railway Station ): 

For those who opt for train travel, the nearest railway station is the Dehradun Railway Station, conveniently located just 2.4 kilometers away from SGRR University. Upon arriving at the station, accessibility to SGRR University is made simple with various transportation options such as taxis, buses, or Vikram (Tempo) readily available.

3) By Plane ( Airport ):

For travelers choosing air transport, the nearest airport to SGRR University is the Jolly Grant Airport in Dehradun. The distance between Jolly Grant and Patel Nagar Dehradun is 30 kilometers only. Upon arrival, you have the option to hire a taxi or utilize local transportation services, including buses, to seamlessly reach the university campus. The campus is strategically situated for a smooth transition from the airport.

In addition to the travel distance, you can refer to the directions from Jolly Grant Airport to Patel Nagar for road driving instructions, ensuring a straightforward route to your destination.

 

SGRR University Pathri Bagh Campus:

1) By Bus ( ISBT ) :

When you reach ISBT, getting to SGRR University Pathri Bagh Campus is easy. Just hop on a bus or Vikram (Tampo) going to Patel Nagar (Laal Pull). It's a short 3.7-kilometer ride, taking only 10 to 15 minutes. SGRR Pathri Bagh Campus is within a quick 7 to 8-minute E-Riksha ride from Patel Nagar and the Shri Mahant Indiresh Hospital is along the way.

For those selecting the Kargi Chauk route, a smooth journey to SGRR University Pathri Bagh Campus involves a two-step transportation process. Starting from ISBT, travelers can initially take a Vikram (Tampo) headed for Kargi Chauk. Following this, the next mode of transport is the convenient "E-Riksha." Along this route, there's no need to pass by Shri Mahant Indiresh Hospital to reach the Pathri Bagh Campus.

2) By Train ( Railway Station ) :

Dehradun Railway Station is the nearest railway station to SGRR University, conveniently located just 2.4 kilometers away. There are a variety of transportation options available from the station to SGRR University, including taxis, buses, and Vikram (Tempo). SGRR Pathri Bagh Campus is accessible from Patel Nagar within walking distance of only 7 to 8 Minutes.

3) By Plane ( Airport ) :

If you're flying in, the closest airport to SGRR University is Jolly Grant Airport in Dehradun. Once you land, you can easily get a taxi or use local transportation like buses to smoothly get to the university campus. The campus is strategically placed for a hassle-free journey from the airport. For an easy road trip, you can check directions from Jolly Grant Airport to Patel Nagar. This will guide you to your destination without any complications. From Patel Nagar, the SGRR Pathri Bagh Campus is just a quick 7 to 8-minute E-Riksha ride, and you'll pass by Shri Mahant Indiresh Hospital on the way.

"SGRR University is committed to providing convenient accessibility for students, visitors, and prospective candidates. If you have any queries or require further assistance in planning your journey, feel free to contact our admissions office (1800-120-102-102). We look forward to welcoming you to our vibrant academic community at SGRR University."
