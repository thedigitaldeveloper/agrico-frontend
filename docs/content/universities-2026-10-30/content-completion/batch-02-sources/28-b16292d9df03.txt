Admission for All B.Tech. Programs
Selection for admission to all Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.) programs will follow a two-step process:
Karunya Entrance Examination (KEE) 2027 is mandatory for admission to all B.Tech. programs.
Single Window Counselling (SWC) 2027: Candidates who qualify in KEE 2027 will be eligible for participation in SWC’27. This centralized counselling process will determine the final program allocation based on merit and seat availability.
Important Dates
Applications Open
KARUNYA ENTRANCE EXAM(KEE 2027)
Apply Now
Application Process:
Candidates must submit an online application and participate in the Karunya Entrance Examination 2027 (KEE’27). Program allocation will be based on the KEE’27 rank through the Single Window Counselling 2027 (SWC’27) process.
Step 1: Initial Registration and Email Verification
Enter your basic details to register. You will receive a verification link in your registered email.
Click
the link to confirm your email and proceed to the next step.
Step 2: Login Access and Application Submission
After verifying your email, log in with the provided credentials to submit your application form along with the additional required details.
Step 3: Attend KEE’27 in Your Chosen Slot
KEE’27 will be conducted online. You’ll receive the examination link and login credentials via email before the test. Additional details are available on the KEE portal.
Step 4: Rank Notification
Candidate ranks based on KEE’27 scores will be posted on the admissions portal following the completion of Phase II of KEE, tentatively in April 2027.
Step 5: Counselling Registration
After KEE ranks are released, register for Single Window Counselling (SWC) on the admissions portal and submit your program preferences. Program allocation will be determined based on your rank, seat availability, and program category. Allocated program and payment instructions will be communicated to you directly.
Step 6: Fee Payment & Course Confirmation
To secure your allotted course, complete the fee payment as instructed.
Step 7: Enrolment and Class Commencement
Candidates with a minimum of 60% in PCM/PCB (as applicable) who have successfully completed the necessary payment will be enrolled online. Notifications regarding the commencement of classes will be informed.
