How to Apply

Apply to VGU in 5 Simple Steps — Completely Online

The entire VGUCET registration and admission process is online and takes less than 30 minutes. Apply from the comfort of your home. Results are declared within 7 days of the exam.

VGUCET Registration Process

VGUCET (VGU Common Entrance Test) — Free online exam. Taken from home. Determines eligibility AND scholarship amount. Register once, apply to any program at VGU.
1

Register on VGUCET Portal

Visit vgucet.vgu.ac.in and register with your Name, Email, Mobile, State and Gender. Registration is completely free of cost. You'll receive a confirmation SMS and email with your VGUCET ID.

2

Complete Your Profile

Login and complete your academic profile — 10th and 12th details, subjects, board name, percentage/CGPA. Select your program of interest and preferred specialization.

3

Choose Your Exam Date

Select an exam date and time slot from the available calendar. VGUCET is conducted in online mode from home using a webcam-proctored platform. Slots available April–June 2026. Early phases have higher scholarship potential.

4

Appear in VGUCET

The exam is 60 minutes long — covering English Proficiency, Logical Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude and General Awareness. No negative marking. Admit card emailed 3 days before the exam.

5

Receive Results & Confirm Seat

Results declared within 7 working days via email and SMS. Your score determines scholarship percentage. Pay admission fee online to confirm your seat. Session starts August 2026.

How to Apply for an Education Loan

1

Secure VGU Admission First

Complete your VGUCET and receive the official Admission Offer Letter from VGU. This letter is mandatory for bank loan applications.

2

Visit VGU Loan Facilitation Cell

Contact VGU's dedicated Education Loan Facilitation Cell on campus. Our team will help you choose the right bank and fill the application.

3

Submit Loan Application

Apply online at vidyalakshmi.co.in for centralized government-scheme loans, or directly at our partner bank branches near VGU campus.

4

Loan Disbursement to VGU

Once approved, the bank disburses the fee directly to VGU's account semester-by-semester. No manual handling required from the student.

📄 Migration Policy

Migration Policy — Transferring to VGU from Another University

Students currently enrolled at other universities can transfer to VGU under Lateral Entry or University Transfer — subject to eligibility and seat availability.

📋
Eligibility for Migration / Transfer

✓
Student must have cleared all examinations of semesters studied without any backlogs
✓
Minimum CGPA of 6.0 (or equivalent percentage) in all semesters cleared at the previous university
✓
The program at the previous university must be equivalent and recognized by UGC / AICTE / relevant council
✓
No disciplinary action or pending dues at the previous institution
✓
Migration possible only up to 4th semester for a 4-year program and 2nd semester for a 2-year program

📝
Documents Required for Migration

📄
Migration Certificate from previous university — issued by the registrar office
📄
All semester marksheets and internal assessment records from the previous institution
📄
Character / Conduct Certificate from previous institution
📄
Enrolment Proof — admission letter and university ID card from previous institution
📄
Syllabus of all completed semesters (for course equivalency mapping by VGU academic team)
ℹ️
Migration applications are reviewed by the VGU Academic Council on a case-by-case basis. Credit transfer will be granted based on curriculum equivalency review. Contact the Registrar's Office at registrar@vgu.ac.in for migration queries.
💰 Refund Policy

Fee Refund Policy — Transparent & UGC Compliant

VGU follows the UGC Guidelines on Fee Refund (2018) strictly. Students who withdraw their admission within the specified time periods are entitled to refunds as per the schedule below.

💰
UGC-Compliant Refund Schedule

Withdrawal TimingRefund %Deduction
15+ days before start of session100% RefundOnly processing fee of ₹1,000 deducted
Less than 15 days before start of session80% Refund20% of fees deducted as administrative charges
Within 30 days after session starts50% Refund50% of fees deducted
31–60 days after session starts25% Refund75% of fees deducted
More than 60 days after session startsNo Refund100% fees forfeited

📋
Refund Process

1

Submit Written Application

Submit a written withdrawal / cancellation request to the VGU Admission Office — in person or via email to admissions@vgu.ac.in with your enrollment number and bank details.

2

Document Verification

Submit original documents received at the time of admission — ID card, fee receipts, allotment letter and any other university-issued documents for verification.

3

Refund Processing

Refund is processed within 15 working days of approval directly to the source account via NEFT/RTGS.

⚠️
Important: Hostel fee is refunded separately as per hostel withdrawal policy. Mess advance (if paid) is refundable on a pro-rata basis. Registration / admission processing fee of ₹5,000 is non-refundable in all cases.
