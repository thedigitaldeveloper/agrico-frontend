School of Agricultural Sciences


About SAS

The ICAR-Accredited School of Agricultural Sciences at Shri Guru Ram Rai University (SGRRU), Dehradun, is a premier institution committed to excellence in Agricultural Education, Research, and innovation. Equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories, advanced scientific instruments, and a well-established instructional and research farm, the School provides outstanding hands-on learning opportunities for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, M.Sc. Agriculture and doctoral students, preparing them to excel in modern Agriculture and allied sectors.

Read More

 

ICAR Accrediated

"Discover a campus where innovation meets tradition, empowering students to excel and lead in the Agricultural Sector."

At the School of Agricultural Sciences, we take pride in our extensive alumni network that spans industries and continents. Our graduates are equipped with the skills, values, and knowledge to make a positive impact in their chosen fields and society at large.

State-of-the-Art Agricultural Laboratories

Our more than 30-hectare agricultural farm is complemented by well-equipped laboratories, a mushroom cultivation center, and orchards featuring litchi, guava, mango, and citrus. These facilities provide hands-on experience, fostering practical knowledge and research skills.

Smart Classrooms for Agri-Education

Our smart classrooms integrate ICT tools to create an engaging and interactive learning environment. These digital learning spaces are tailored to meet the evolving needs of agricultural and horticultural education, bridging theory with real-world applications.

Digital Hubs for Agri-Tech Innovation

Equipped with cutting-edge systems, our computer labs act as digital hubs where students explore agri-informatics, data-driven farming techniques, and horticultural research tools—nurturing innovation and technical expertise in the green sciences.

Dean's Message

Welcome to the ICAR-Accredited School of Agricultural Sciences, Shri Guru Ram Rai University (SGRRU), Dehradun. Our School provides a vibrant learning environment supported by experienced faculty, state-of-the-art laboratories, smart classrooms, modern research facilities, and well-developed 30+ Hactare instructional and research farms with in the campus that enable students to gain valuable hands-on experience.

View Detail

Our specialized departments offer industry-relevant undergraduate and postgraduate programmes designed to equip students with the knowledge, practical skills, and professional competence required for the rapidly evolving agricultural sector. The School upholds the highest standards of academic quality through its ICAR accreditation, reflecting our commitment to excellence in curriculum, infrastructure, faculty, research, and student development.

At the School of Agricultural Sciences, we nurture future-ready professionals, researchers, entrepreneurs, and leaders capable of addressing global challenges related to food and nutritional security, climate-resilient agriculture, natural resource conservation, and sustainable rural development. Our graduates are well prepared for successful careers in higher education, research organizations, government departments, agribusiness industries, extension services, and agricultural startups.

I warmly invite aspiring students to join our academic community and become part of a new generation of agricultural professionals dedicated to shaping the future of agriculture through knowledge, innovation, and sustainable development.

Prof.(Dr.) Priyanka Bankoti

Dean, SGRR School of Agricultural Sciences

Our Vision

To be a premier institution in agricultural education, research, and extension, dedicated to fostering knowledge and innovation that contribute to national food security, rural development, and environmental sustainability. We envision producing globally competent professionals who lead advancements in agricultural sciences with responsibility and integrity.

Our Mission

  • To provide quality education in agriculture, horticulture, and allied sciences through modern pedagogy and field-based learning.

  • To conduct impactful research and promote innovation in sustainable farming practices.

  • To engage in extension activities that benefit farming communities and support national agricultural development goals.

  • To instill ethical values, scientific temperament, and leadership among students for meaningful contributions to the agri-sector.
