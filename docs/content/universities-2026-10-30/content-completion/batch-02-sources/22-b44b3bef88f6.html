ADMISSION
PROCESS & GUIDELINES

Admission Procedure for the batch of 2026-27 AY

Application for admission 2026-27 AY is open w.e.f 1st November 2026
SSU Admission Process 2026
 

01. Application 

  • The application form is available only through online mode. 
  • The cost of the application form for all programs is Rs.500/- for Indian nationals and 50 USD for International Applicants. 
  • The application form fee is non-refundable. 
  • If applying for more than one course, separate forms should be filled for each course.

02. Selection of the Program

Sri Sri University offers a wide range of academic programs to suit your aptitude and interests

03. Completion & Submission of the Application Form

3.1 The Application Form is to be filled and submitted online as per the instructions on the university website.

3.2 Fill out the Application Form only if you meet the eligibility criteria** for admission to the program.

3.3 The instructions in the application form are self-explanatory. Please follow carefully.

3.4 Fill all columns with complete and correct information. Incomplete/false/wrong information will result in the rejection of the application.

3.5 It is mandatory to provide your correct email address and mobile number in the space provided in the application form as it will be used by the admission office for communication related to your admission.

3.6 If you have appeared in a qualifying examination and the result is awaited, you can apply provisionally and go through the admission process at your own risk.

04. Procedure for Admission

4.1 On submission of the duly filled application form, you will receive the acknowledgment email. 

4.2 Eligible candidates will additionally be informed via email about the date, time, and venue of the selection process (interview and/ or written test and/or group discussion and/or direct admission) within 10 working days.

4.3 All selected candidates will be intimated via email regarding the fee details and documents to be uploaded through the provisional offer letter along with the due date. Based on this, provisional admission will be granted.

4.4 Final admission will be granted after the verification of required documents and payment of complete 1st-semester fees. 

** The Minimum eligibility criteria for different programs (for Indian Students) are as prescribed in the appropriate sections as well as in attached annexures.

(2) Age Limit

  • For Undergraduate Programs, age as of July 1st 2026, should not be over 21 years of age. 
  1. For Post Graduate Programs, there is no age limit prescribed. 

However, we believe, that since age should not be a criterion for getting an education, the candidates above the age of 21 for UG and above the age of 35 years for PG, will be considered for admissions based on an interview conducted by a panel and the decision of the respective department as well as the admissions committee.  

(3) Provisional Admission:

  • All the students who qualify for the selection criteria are sent the provisional offer letter.
  • The applicant on receiving the offer letter needs to pay 50% of the 1st-semester tuition fee (or as mentioned in the provisional offer letter) and upload the mandatory documents mentioned, within 7 working days. In case of non-availability of any mandatory document, an undertaking should be submitted (in the prescribed format of the University). With this, provisional admission is granted.
  • On confirmation of all the documents and eligibility, payment of fees, and any other criteria of admission as stated by the university, final admission is granted. 
  • The Provisional Admission is valid only till 30th September 2026* Beyond this date, the seat would be canceled and considered as vacant and no refund of the amount of the provisional admission will be done.

*The date is subject to change and would be notified on the University website. 

(4) Late Admissions:

Application for Admission will not be entertained after the last date of admission. However, under certain exceptional circumstances, the university may allow late admission. 

Because of the exceptionality of circumstances, 

  1. Application for a refund for such admission will not be entertained 
  2. Such admission will not have any bearing on the last date of refund for admissions announced in respect of admissions done before the last date of admission. 

All students are required to report/join the university as per the schedule for the start of the session.

However, if a student joins late, then the following guidelines may be used unless otherwise specified:

  1. If the admitted student reports within one week of the reporting date for the concerned program, then he/she may be allowed to join after submission of application on the prescribed form stating the valid reason for his/her late joining.
  2. If the admitted student reports between one to two weeks after the reporting date of the concerned program then he/she may be allowed to join after submission of application on the prescribed form stating the valid reason for his/her late joining with a fee of Rs.500 and the application must be approved by Head of Admissions Dept.
  3. If the admitted student reports between two-three weeks after the reporting date of the concerned program then he/she may be allowed to join after submission of application on the prescribed form stating the valid reason for his/her late joining with a fee of Rs.1000 and the application must be approved by Head of Admissions Dept.
  4. If the admitted student reports after three weeks from the date of reporting of the concerned program but before the last date by which late admission can be considered, then he/she may be allowed to join after submission of application on the prescribed form stating the valid reason of his/her late joining with a fee of Rs.2000 and the application must be approved by Head of Admissions Dept and the Dean of the concerned Dept.

Please Note :

  1. The last date for considering late admissions is the date prescribed in such a way that, after such date, students may not be able to fulfill attendance criteria after due consideration of all the classes to be conducted for that term. This date would be mentioned on the university website. 
  2. Students may be allowed to report/join the university late as per the details and timelines mentioned above, however, the minimum attendance required to be maintained will remain the same, and no relaxation instead of late joining will be provided. 
  3. Further, no compensation for the loss of continuous assessment will be allowed to late joining students.
  4. If the admitted student reports after one week from the date of reporting for the concerned program, the residential facility/transport facility allotted to him/her may be de-allocated.

However, on late joining/reporting, if permitted as per the guidelines mentioned above, the residential facility/transport facility may be allocated again subject to the availability of seats. 

(5) Change of Program:

  1. Any applicant who wishes to change the program or specialization from one program to another will have to fill the Program transfer form (Obtained from the Admissions Office or download from the website) and deposit the form along with the program transfer fee of Rs.1000 (or 50 USD). This applies only to the change of program after the start of the session. 
  2. Please Note: The requested change/transfer will be purely based at the discretion of the University subject to: 
  1. Seat availability in the program to which transfer is applied
  2. Fulfillment of eligibility and other admission requirements
  3. Any other condition prescribed by the university.
  1. The change of program application must be submitted within 15 days from the last date of admissions 
  2. Those applicants whose program transfer is accepted by the university will be liable to pay the tuition fee and other charges as prescribed by the university.
  3. In case the student is allowed to transfer to a program with a lesser fee, the balance will be adjusted in the fee of next term
  4. If the program transfer is done after the start of the session, no compensation for loss of attendance or any component will be given.

(6) Lateral Admissions:

  1. This is applicable for those students who wish to take admission in the second year of any of the programs offered in the university. Admission for any subsequent years will not be allowed.
  2. The applicants must fulfill the following criteria:
  1. The applicant must be from a recognized university
  2. The syllabus/curricula of the program, the applicant is studying should match with our university syllabus/curricula.
  3. The applicant should have passed the 1st year of the program he/she is studying and wishes to seek admission to Sri Sri university for the second year
  4. The applicant should be eligible as per the criteria stated by the University.
  5. The applicant must submit all the mandatory documents as mentioned by the university for seeking admission.
  6. Admission will be granted only if seats are available in the program the applicant wishes to get enrolled in and if he/she fulfills all the necessary criteria stated above.
  7. If admission is granted, the applicant is liable to pay the fees as per the fees of the second year of that program.

       Process of Admission 

  1. The applicant needs to fill the lateral admission form (available on the website)
  2. Submit the form and upload/submit all the mandatory documents to the Asst Registrar Desk in the Registrar’s Office.
  3. On verifying the eligibility and all the documents, the admission confirmation letter will be sent to the student from the Asst.Registrar

(7) General Guidelines regarding Admission:

  1. Candidates placed in the compartment are not eligible for admission to any program of study in the University.
  2. Admissions will be purely based on merit and candidates are strongly advised against giving any pressure on the selection process. Any candidate found guilty in the above activities is liable for disqualification. 
  3. The University reserves the right to cancel any advertised program due to insufficient enrollments or for any other reason beyond its control. In such an eventuality, the full fee shall be refunded to the student. 
  4. The admission will be considered final only when the student has met the norms of eligibility criteria. The onus of meeting the criteria is the sole responsibility of the student. The admission of a student will stand canceled at any point of time during the time of registration with the University if the student is found not meeting the criteria and where the student has not submitted the relevant documents.
  5. University reserves the right to itself to offer any or all of the program electives/specialization electives. Electives thus offered may be based on the number of students who opt for such elective and will be offered at the sole discretion of the university. 
  6. Reservation:

The university gives relaxation of 5% in the eligibility criteria of all courses for students belonging to SC/ST and OBC (Non-creamy layer) category as well as for physically handicapped and Kashmiri migrants. The applicants who belong to any of this category who want to avail themselves the relaxation needs to submit the relevant documents as proof. 

  1. In case of any matter not covered herein and/or for interpretation of any content herein, the decision of the competent authority of the university shall be final and binding on all concerned. 

(8) Commencement of Admissions:

The commencement of Final Admissions begins from 1st July 2026 onwards. 

Steps for Final Admissions:

  1. This process begins once you have all the mandatory documents (mentioned in the annexure) ready with you.
  2. Upload all the mentioned documents. 
  3. Pay the complete first semester fees and keep the receipts ready with you in case asked to submit them.

This can happen as the counseling round in the physical model in the university campus or the online mode. Once your documents are received and found valid, the Admissions Confirmation letter will be sent to you with details of the reporting on campus, the Induction program timetable, and the commencement schedule. 

Last Date of Admission: The last date of Admission is August 30th, 2026 (This is subject to change and will be updated on the university website)

(9) Reporting on Campus

Those who are offered the final admissions will have to report on the campus on the mentioned dates with all the mentioned documents and the original certificates for verification. 

On verification, the student is allotted the Identity card, enrollment number along with hostel allocation (if opted for a hostel). 

An Orientation session for all students and parents followed by Campus Tour will be done and the detailed timetable/students’ handbook will be given to the students. 

(10) Induction Program:

When new students enter a University or an institution, they come with diverse thoughts, backgrounds, and preparations. It is important to help them adjust to the new environment and inculcate in them the ethos of the university with a sense of larger purpose. Familiarization of the University culture, setting a healthy daily routine, creating a bond in the batch as well as between faculty and students, developing awareness, sensitivity, and understanding of the self, people around them, society at large, and nature and along with this revising the basics or foundations of their respective subjects is the purpose of the induction.

The 7-day long induction program contains the Art of Living’s Happiness Program for the Youth which is specifically designed to accelerate personal all-around excellence intends to draw forth clarity in observation, perception, and expression. The program has a world record in increasing the ‘Happiness Quotient’ irrespective of any caste, creed, or religion.

The Sudarshan Kriya®, a powerful, rhythmic breathing technique taught in the Happiness Program aids in mental clarity, eases the build-up of emotions, and uplifts one spiritually. Every new entrant to the university undergoes the Happiness Program as an integral part of the curriculum. It is a smooth blend of ancient wisdom, yoga, pranayama (breath control), meditation, and the fused with contemporary intellectual exchanges. After completing this program, students are encouraged as well as provided with opportunities to take to a higher level of spiritually oriented workshops such as the Advance Meditation Programs, etc. This gives them a profound understanding of their selves as well as tools to handle their minds and emotions most constructively.

Along with this, the induction program also includes workshops in communication, excel, dance, creative arts, mind management along with training specific to the respective industry. 

The induction also familiarizes the students with the vision of the University, the various departments, local places of interest, facilities offered in the university, the parent organization “Art of Living” and to their seniors/alumni and faculties!

All students must attend the induction program.

Admissions at SSU are done purely on merit basis. All payments are received only through our official website srisriuniversity.edu.in.

Sri Sri University has only one campus which is located at Cuttack, Odisha, India and has no other branches

For reporting any fraudulent activity or for any query, feel free to reach us on phone at 7894424562 or on email at info@srisriuniversity.edu.in
