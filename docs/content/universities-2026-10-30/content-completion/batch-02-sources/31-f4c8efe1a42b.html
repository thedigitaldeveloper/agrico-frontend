About Us

SGRR University: Shaping Futures, Inspiring Excellence

Shri Guru Ram Rai University was established by a religious and philanthropic leader, Shri Mahant Devendra Dass Ji Maharaj in the year 2017. It is situated in the heart of the city, Uttarakhand. We are extremely privileged to extend the values and ethos of the Shri Guru Ram Rai Education Mission through SGRR University to impart quality education and successfully place more than 80% of students in various companies across the globe. SGRR University has a humongous campus spread over 80 acres of land. Its state-of-the-art facilities provide opportunities to develop leadership skills and achieve professional excellence. The university has 10,000+ students from different countries, 29 states and Union Territories, creating a rich cultural melange and global exposure for students. One of the biggest strengths of the university is its unmatched experience of 72 years in delivering quality education that helps students develop confidence, gain industry exposure, build strong networks, and enhance self-esteem. This overall experience transforms students into complete professionals ready to face every challenge.

Shri Mahant Devendra Dass Ji Maharaj

Learning is the only thing the mind never exhausts, never fears and never regrets — it's the treasure that follows its owner everywhere.
— Leonardo da Vinci

Shri Guru Ram Rai Darbar Sahibis a 346-year-old social, religious and philanthropic organization established by Shri Guru Ram Rai Ji Maharaj. Since its inception it has been deeply rooted in selfless social service. To keep pace with a rapidly changing world, Shri Guru Ram Rai Education Mission and Shri Guru Ram Rai University were established in 1952 and 2017 respectively.

Over a journey of more than 72 years, SGRR Education has carved a distinguished niche in the fields of Education, Medical Education and Health Care — bringing revolutionary change by providing quality learning to the underprivileged and to girls, making an enormous difference in society.

The University offers an all-inclusive ambience in consonance with its motto, "Quest for Excellence"— our unending pursuit to establish a centre for global learning that embodies the best practices of the world's leading universities.
