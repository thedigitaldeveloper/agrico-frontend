Introduction
SGT University, a UGC-recognized institution established in
                            2013, is committed to advancing research, innovation, and multi-disciplinary
                            education.
Spread across a sprawling 100-acre campus on the outskirts
                            of Gurgaon, the university offers world-class infrastructure and resources
                            designed to maximize academic excellence and research outcomes.
SGT University offers over 200 programs in 19 medical and non-medical
                            streams. The medical programs are supported by state-of-the-art infrastructure,
                            including an 850-bedded NABH & NABL accredited tertiary care hospital,
                            alongside renowned Medical and Dental Colleges and a dedicated Ayurveda
                            Hospital and College. These facilities provide students with unmatched
                            opportunities for hands-on learning and clinical exposure.
Our relentless pursuit of excellence and focus on research-based
                            pedagogy has earned us the distinction of being one of the youngest
                            universities to achieve NAAC A+ accreditation.
                            With a thriving community of over 11,000 students enjoying a
                            vibrant campus life, SGT University offers an enriching environment that is
                            seamlessly connected to national and international destinations—just a 40‑minute
                            drive from Indira Gandhi International Airport.
The Humble Beginnings of SGT University
SGT University is humble to be part of the Dashmesh Educational
                            Charitable Trust. Following Shree
                            Guru Gobind Singh Ji's ideals, the Dashmesh Educational Charitable Trust established the SGT
                            Dental
                            College, Hospital and Research Institute in 2002. It steadily added Faculties of
                            Physiotherapy,
                            Nursing, Medical Sciences, and other disciplines to reach the status of university,
                            conferred on
                            24
                            January 2013 under the Haryana Private Universities (Amendment) Act No. 8 of 2013.
SGT University scaled horizontally by establishing Centers of Excellence like NRSC (National
                            Reference Simulation Centre), CCSP (Centre for Cosmology Science & Popularization), CEAPS
                            (Centre of
                            Excellence for Andragogical and Pedagogical Sciences), and institutionalized CCA (Center for
                            Career Advancement), EDC (Entrepreneurship Development Centre), ACIC (Atal Community
                            Innovation Centre),
                            IIC
                            (Institution's Innovation Council) - SGTU for focused attention and holistic development of
                            students, faculty members, and Nation.

1. To impart higher education at par with global standards that meets the changing needs

About Our Trust
DASHMESH EDUCATIONAL CHARITABLE TRUST (DECT) was constituted in 1999 as a public charitable
                            trust and is registered under Section 12A of the Income Tax Act 1961, aiming to achieve
                            sustainable development and higher growth rates at the national level with a higher
                            education system. The trust’s constant endeavor is to encourage higher and technical
                            education systems and research and development activities in a concerted effort aimed at
                            nation-building.
DECT and its management believe in providing a proper impetus to HEIs in producing competent
                            professionals like doctors, pharmacists, engineers, and managers, encouraging them to think
                            beyond the curriculum while imparting training for the advancement of knowledge.
With an emphasis on “Access to Quality” and “Education to All,” the management and the board
                            of trustees have created a thrust for quality in professional education. It has established
                            the university to cater to the needs of the country in the field of higher and technical
                            education in the NCR area, particularly Delhi and Haryana.
Shree Guru Gobind Singh Tricentenary (SGT) University is a unit of DECT, with the avowed
                            objective of spreading the message of Shree Guru Gobind Singh that the highest service is
                            rendered by spreading knowledge.
