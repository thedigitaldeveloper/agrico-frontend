      advantage of being close to major urban centers. SoAS is proud to be accredited by the Indian

Our Mission and Vision
JRNRVU University's mission & vision is to empower students with a transformative education that fosters academic excellence, innovation, and social responsibility. We aim to create an inclusive environment where students develop critical thinking and prepare for impactful societal roles.
Our Mission
To teach courses on agriculture science in a comprehensive manner for undergraduate students.
To update the students’ knowledge through quality education and provide guidance for academic excellence to cater the career needs.
To develop technologies to enhance the production by integrated crop and pest management, and educate the students for value addition and prevent postharvest losses.
To transfer the developed technologies to farming communities.
Our Vision
“To develop human resources to promote sustainable agriculture in general and for the tribal dominated Southern Rajasthan in particular by blending traditional and modern knowledge of agriculture and allied areas supported with location specific and problem oriented research as well as need based extension services focused on improvement and quality life of all sections of population, specially the marginalized classes and tribal communities.”

College Campus
The college is located at the Dabok Campus of the Janardan Rai Nagar Rajasthan Vidyapeeth (Deemed To Be University), locally known as पंचायतन यूनिट, 15 kilometer from Ranapratpnagar Railway Station of Udaipur and is about 3 kilometers from the Maharanapratap Airport, Dabok, Udaipur.
