ADMISSION
Rabindranath Tagore University (RNTU) is India’s first skill-based private university that strives to impart skill-based quality education. The University promotes research-driven advancement of knowledge to create successful professionals. Established by the AISECT Group in 2010, it has carved a niche in Madhya Pradesh. With strong industry linkages, the university’s focus lies on the holistic learning & development of students.
Key Highlights
NAAC accredited and NIRF Ranked for Four Consecutive Years among top 200 Universities
Startup Focused - Set up MP’s First Atal Incubation Centre by Niti Ayog, Govt. of India
Centres of Excellence in the Field of Adv. Technology, Science, Agriculture, Arts , Indian Culture
Strong Industry Academia Collaborations
The First Private National School of Drama in Central India

FAQs
When was RNTU established ?
Rabindranath Tagore University was Established in 2010 to foster research- oriented culture in the university and to provide education through extensive and innovative use of technology, to be recognised as a premium national university providing dedicated services for the social and economic development of the nation, and to forge collaborations with academic, social, and corporate bodies across the world.
Is this a well-recognised and approved university?
Yes, Rabindranath Tagore University is a highly reputable and well-recognised university that has received approvals from various prestigious institutions such as NCTE, INC, BCI, AICTE, UGC, and AIU. This means that the university meets the necessary standards and regulations set by these institutions, ensuring that the education provided by the university is of high quality and recognised by other institutions and organisations. Pursuing a degree from such a reputed university can provide students with excellent career opportunities and a bright future in their respective fields.
Is Rabindranath Tagore University a regular, on-campus university?
Yes, Rabindranath Tagore University is a regular, on-campus university that offers a wide range of courses to students. The university is committed to providing quality education to its students through regular, on-campus courses. The university has state-of-the-art infrastructure and facilities that create an ideal learning environment for students. With a focus on practical, hands-on learning, the university ensures that students receive a well-rounded education that prepares them for the real world. Pursuing a degree from a regular, on-campus university like Rabindranath Tagore University can provide students with a competitive edge in the job market and equip them with the skills and knowledge necessary for success in their chosen careers.
Is there an Entrance Exam?
Yes, the AISECT Group of University (AGU) conducts an entrance exam for admission to its various Undergraduate, Postgraduate, and Doctoral Programmes. The entrance exam is known as the AISECT Joint Entrance Examination (AJEE).
How do hostel rooms get allotted?
Hostel rooms are assigned on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to the payment of the hostel fee.
What are the documents required for admission?
On the date of appointment, applicants will be required to bring the following documents-
- 10th Marksheet
- 12th Marksheet
- Graduation Marksheet
- Aadhar Card
- Passport Size Photos
Does this university offer scholarships?
Yes, RNTU, offers various scholarships such as Scholarship for underprivileged,Scholarship for Meritorious,You can know more about the scholarships, application process, and eligibility criteria on the official website of the university.
Does the University provide any transportation facilities?
In order to ensure the safety of students, a fleet of buses is run, facilitating pick-up and drop-off service for students staying within a radius of 35 km from the campus.
What is the eligibility for a Scholarship?
To help the government with activities shared on the M.P. Scholarship  Portal, RNTU has launched its own scholarship programme with the name Shiksha Mitra Scholarships. It is the largest University aided Scholarship Programme at the state level. The scholarships worth Rs. 1 Crore are to be handed out under this programme.
