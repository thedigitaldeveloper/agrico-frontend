Rajasthan Vidyapeeth Homeopathic Medical College Announces BHMS Admissions 2026–27: Key Dates, Cut-Offs, and Process
admin
October 12, 2026
October 12, 2026
0
1 mins
Aspiring homeopathic doctors have an important update regarding medical admissions
.
Rajasthan Vidyapeeth Homeopathic Medical College & Hospital
, a constituent unit of J.R.N. Rajasthan Vidyapeeth (Deemed-to-be University) located in Dabok, Udaipur, has officially opened admissions for its Bachelor of Homeopathic Medicine and Surgery (BHMS) undergraduate program for the academic session 2026–27
.
Admissions are conducted under the
Deemed University All India Quota (100% seats)
, offering candidates nationwide an opportunity to secure a seat at this established institution
.
Eligibility Criteria and Qualifying Cut-Offs
To qualify for admission, candidates must fulfill mandatory academic qualifications alongside NEET eligibility
:
General Category Candidates:
Must possess a valid NEET qualifying score of
213+ marks
and at least
50% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB)
.
Reserved Category Candidates (SC / ST / OBC):
Must score
177+ marks
in NEET, along with a minimum of
40% aggregate in PCB
Crucial Counselling Schedule
Eligible candidates must register through the central Ayush Admissions Central Counseling Committee (AACCC) portal
. Note: The mop-up round has been eliminated from this year’s schedule
3rd Round of Counselling:
Scheduled from
October 14, 2026, to October 20, 2026 (up to 2:00 PM)
.
Stray Round:
Scheduled from
November 3, 2026, to November 9, 2026 (up to 2:00 PM)
.
How to Apply and Contact Details
Interested applicants can register online via the official national counseling website:
Registration Portal:
www.aaccc.gov.in
College Email:
[email protected]
Campus Address:
Airport Road, Dabok, Udaipur, Rajasthan – 313022
For admission guidance, campus verification, or document queries, students and parents can contact the counseling cell at:
Helpline Numbers:
+91 8769746883 | +91 9414374326 | +91 9928253150
