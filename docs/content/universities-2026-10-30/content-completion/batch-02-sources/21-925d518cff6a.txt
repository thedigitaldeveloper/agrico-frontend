Scholarship
AKS University, Satna strongly believes in the fact that monetary constraints should not be an obstacle for a student to have access to quality education. So, AKS University offers scholarships scheme for the students taking admission at AKS. The Scheme for admission in UG & Diploma courses are as under (Name of courses are mentioned in Fee Structure)
Scholarship from MP Govt. to MP Students:
Post Metric Scholarship for SC (Department Post Metric Scholarship for SC)
SC (Schedule Casts) Eligible Income Limit 2.5 Lakhs per Annum
SC (Schedule Tribes) Eligible Income Limit 2.5 Lakhs per Annum
OBC (Other Backward Classes) Eligible Income Limit 2Lakhs per Annum
Minority Eligible Income Limit 75 Thousands per Annum
Documents required for the above scholarship :
Samagra ID, Photocopy of MP Permanent Domicile Certificate, Photocopy of Income Certificate, Photocopy of Cast Certificate, photocopy of 5th and 10th Mark sheet, Photocopy of recently passed exam, photocopy of Adhaar Card, Photocopy of Bank Passbook, Photocopy of TC, Photocopy of Fees receipt, GAP Certificate (If Applicable), 2 Passport size photo.
AWAS Sahayta Scheme SC/ST
SC/ST students who are residing on rent for study and studying in the institution
Documents required for the above scholarship :
No objection certificate from Landlord on 10Rs stamp Paper, Declarations on Rs 10 stamp paper for not having own house in Satna city. Samagra ID, Photocopy of MP Permanent Domicile Certificate, Photocopy of Income Certificate, Photocopy of Cast Certificate, photocopy of 5th and 10th Mark sheet, Photocopy of recently passed exam, photocopy of Adhaar Card, Photocopy of Bank Passbook, Photocopy of TC, Photocopy of Fees receipt, GAP Certificate (If Applicable), 2 Passport size photo.
Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherji Scholarship Scheme OBC/General/Minority
For meritorious students of all sections of society scoring 80 percent or more marks in the 12th examinations, Students whose parents/guardians’ annual income does not exceed Rs three lakh would be eligible for this scholarship
Documents required for the above scholarship :
Samagra ID, Photocopy of MP Permanent Domicile Certificate, Photocopy of Income Certificate, Photocopy of 12th Mark sheet, 2 Passport size photo, BPL Card (For Full scholarship)
Ganv ki Beti Scheme
The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to encourage talented girls of rural areas to acquire higher education. Scholarships are given every year for the girls who have received 60% or more marks in the 12th examination.
Documents required for the above scholarship:
12th Mark sheet photocopy, Certificate from Sarpanch for domicile of village, photocopy of Bank Passbook, Passport size photo
Pratibha Kiran Scheme
The objective of this scheme is improved educational standard of girls belonging to urban BPL families, who pass their 12th exam in the first division. But she has to take admission in higher classes the same year.
Documents required for the above scholarship:
12th Mark sheet photocopy, BPL Certificate, photocopy of Bank Passbook, Passport size photo, Domicile Certificate
DIVYANG Scholarship
Scholarship for Students with Disabilities. Students who are disabled more than 41% and their Guardian Parents income is more than 96,000 per Annum.
Documents required for the above scholarship:
Disable Certificate, Income Certificate, Recently passed Mark sheet photocopy, photocopy of Bank Passbook, Passport size photo.
