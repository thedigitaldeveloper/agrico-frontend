6.4. Self Study Report for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture Programme 
The B.Sc. (Hons) Agriculture programme being offered at Faculty of Agriculture (FoA), 
Maharishi Markandeshwar (Deemed to be University) {MM(DU)} is implemented as per 
the guidelines of ICAR including minimum requirements of 5th Deans’ Committee 
recommendations and Model Act for Higher Agricultural Educational Institutions in India 
(Revised 2023). The MM(DU) is a NAAC grade A++ accredited University. It is committed 
to deliver best of the resources to its stake holders for this program. 
6.4.1. Brief History of Degree Programme 
Maharishi Markandeshwar (Deemed to be University) {MM(DU)} is situated at Mullana on 
the outskirts of Ambala, towards Yamunanagar in Haryana under the parasol of Maharishi 
Markandeshwar Trust, which was founded in 1993. The vision and mission of the 
Management of the Trust is to establish a citadel of education for aspirants from rural and 
urban settings to become responsible citizens of civil society after completing their 
education. Accordingly, state-of-the-art lab facilities, Hospital, fields, teaching and research, 
farm facilities, etc., have been developed to impart holistic education at undergraduate and 
post-graduate levels. 
The Faculty of Agriculture (FoA) is one of the constituent institutes of the MM(DU), which 
came into existence w.e.f. 12th June 2007. Currently, following programs have been 
approved by UGC under FoA of the MM(DU): 
1. B. Sc (Hons.) Agriculture (4-year) 
2. B. Sc (Hons.) Agriculture Integrated (6-year) 
3. M.Sc. (Agronomy)  
4. M.Sc. (Entomology)  
5. Ph.D.  (Agronomy)  
The Faculty has strived to create infrastructure and other requirements as per ICAR norms. 
These include Agri-Incubation Centre and facilities for Vermicompost, Bee-keeping, 
Mushroom Cultivation, Hydroponics, Protected Cultivation, Fisheries, Weather Station, 
Research Farms, Orchards, Animal Sheds, etc.  
The MM(DU) supports various faculty and student development schemes for participation 
in National/International Workshops/Conferences/Seminars/Webinars, etc. The FoA has 
 6
also organised various National/International Workshops/Conferences/Seminars/Webinars, 
etc. where all Faculty members and Students are exposed to recent developments in various 
disciplines of Science and Technology. The Faculty and Students have recently been 
working on research areas covering crop improvement, crop production, crop protection, 
horticulture, plant biotechnology, etc. 
The Faculty has submitted projects to various National Funding agencies covering aspects 
of Crop Residue Management, Plant Breeding, Agro-Forestry, Nutri-Cereals Based Value-
Added Products, Fisheries, Honey bee rearing and Honey Processing, Bio-fertilizers and 
Bio-pesticides Production Lab, and Soil Testing Lab worth Rs 14 Crores. 
Faculty along with students regularly interact with stakeholders at different forums. 
Interaction with farmers and Agri-Entrepreneurs is a regular practice realised through visits 
of experts to their farms or the visits of farmers to the MM(DU) research farm and various 
experiential learning units. The FoA on a regular basis organizes Exposure Visits, Front line 
Demonstrations, Field Days, Gyan Diwas, Kisan Gosthi, Kisan Mela, Exhibitions and 
Campaigns, etc.  
6.4.1.1 Objectives 
i. To create and develop skilled, talented and entrepreneurial human resources and 
knowledge pools of agriculture technologies.  
ii. To prepare the graduates with learning-centered education to achieve high-quality 
products, farmers’ profitability and zero environmental footprints ensuring 
sustainable agriculture in changing climatic scenario. 
iii. To develop leadership in agricultural sciences to ensure economic attractiveness, 
global competitiveness, social equity, environmental sustainability and food and 
nutritional security. 
iv. To carry out any tasks, projects or activities appropriate for achieving the goals to 
establish start-ups and agri-entrepreneurship for self-employment.
