AIU's 39th Inter-University National Youth Festival (UNIFEST)

AKS University achieved remarkable success at the 39th Inter-University National Youth Festival (UNIFEST), organized by the Association of Indian Universities and hosted by Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology, Chennai. 

Representing the university with great enthusiasm and talent, Team AKS University, Satna secured First Position in both Vad-Vivad (Hindi) and Folk/Tribal Dance. The team also earned Fourth Position in Folk Orchestra and Classical Instrumental Solo (Non-Percussion). These achievements stand as a testament to the students’ dedication, cultural excellence, and outstanding performance at the national level.
