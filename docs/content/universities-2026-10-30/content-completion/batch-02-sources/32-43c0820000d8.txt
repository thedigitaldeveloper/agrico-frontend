About VIT
Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT), evolved from the
								former Vellore Engineering College during 1984 and conferred the
								status of a Deemed-to-be-University in the year 2001 by the
								Government of India under Sec. 3 of the UGC Act. During the four
								decades, VIT has emerged as an innovator in higher education,
								growing from 180 students to 67,000+ students. Today, VIT is
								amongst the most respected Universities in India, featuring
								top-ranked academic programmes. The principal campus of VIT is
								at Vellore, Tamil Nadu and off-campus at Chennai. VIT has its
								campuses at Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh; Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh.
								The latter two campuses are State Private Universities.
								Eco-friendly in design and equipped with cutting-edge
								infrastructure, VIT provides a favorable atmosphere for learning
								and living. Quality in teaching-learning, futuristic research,
								and innovation are VIT's raison d'etre. VIT adopts
								student-centric culture and places importance on quality and
								consistency in learning, teaching, and research experiences.
About B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture
The study of agriculture is called agricultural science or
								agricultural technology. The field combines the natural and
								social sciences, along with economics, engineering and
								management (particularly natural resources management and
								business management) of biological systems to practice,
								development and understand the fields of agriculture and
								forestry. Being related to the field of Life & Biological
								Sciences, agricultural science is multidisciplinary and
								inter-related to Food Science & Technology. Agricultural
								Science uses the fundamentals of biology, chemistry, physics,
								mathematics and statistics to solve the problems of
								agriculture-food industry.

Photo uploading
											procedure:
The photograph must be
											in colour and should be taken professionally in a studio with
											the following specifications :
The photograph must be taken against a light background.
												Only White/off-white background is preferred.
The photograph must show the full frontal view without
												tilting of face, both ears visible, open eyes with neutral
												expression.
Make sure there is no glare from spectacles and no blur in
												the focus.
The file should be only in (.jpeg or .jpg) format.
The file size of the photograph should be within
20 kB
												to 300 kB.
The photograph dimension should be of Width (300 to 400
												pixels) X Height (400 to 550 pixels).
The photograph should not be too dark or too bright but
												should have optimum exposure.
The photograph should have only one image.
Signature uploading
											procedure:
Please draw a rectangular box of size 6 cm (width) X 2 cm
												(height) on a A4 white paper. Sign with dark blue or black
												pen within the box.
Scan the signature in (.jpeg or .jpg) format using scanner
												and crop the image.
You can open the scanned signature image with MS Paint and
												crop the image at the particular rectangular box which was
												already marked and signed on the A4 white paper.
While cropping the image make sure that the dimension should
												be 3.5 cm (width) X 1.5 cm (height).
The scanned signature image size should be within
5 kB
												to 150 kB.

Admission Process
Eligibility
There is NO ENTRANCE EXAMINATION.
Applicants who have studied through regular,
											full-time, and formal education with English as one of the
											subjects are eligible to apply.
Students who have appeared for their 10+2 / HSC
											examination and are awaiting results are also eligible to
											apply, provided they submit their passing certificate by the
											notified date.
Nationality
The applicant should be a Resident Indian National.
Age Limit
Applicants born on or after
01.07.2006
are eligible to apply for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture programme
											for the academic year 2026-27.
Applicants must submit the Age Proof (Birth certificate
											/ Passport Copy) as proof of their age during
											counselling / admission. Failure to do so will result in
											disqualification from the admission process.
Qualifying Examination
Candidates applying for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture
											programme should have either completed or shall be appearing
											in any one of the following qualifying examinations in 2026 :
The final examination of the 10+2 / HSC system,
											conducted by any recognized Central/ State Board, such as the
											Central Board of Secondary Education, New Delhi; Council for
											the Indian School Certificate Examinations or any other Board
											accepted by VIT as equivalent to 10+2 / HSC examination.
Intermediate or two-year Pre-University Examination
											conducted by a recognized Board/ University.
General Certificate Education (GCE) examination
											(London/Cambridge) at the Advanced (A) level.
For PCB : Physics & Biology - A Level/
											Chemistry - A/AS Level and English - A/AS Level
High School Certificate Examination at the Advanced (A)
											level of the Cambridge University or International
											Baccalaureate Diploma of the International Baccalaureate
											Office, Geneva.
For PCB :
											Physics & Biology - HL, Chemistry - SL/HL.
Senior Secondary School Examination conducted by the
											National Institute of Open Schooling with minimum of 5
											subjects as recognized by central board.
Candidates who have appeared for multiple board
											examinations in the same academic year are not eligible.
Subject Eligibility
A pass with minimum overall aggregate of 60% in
												addition to an aggregate of 60% in Physics, Chemistry and
												Biology (Botany & Zoology) (PCB) as subjects in the
												qualifying examination mentioned above.
In case of applicants belonging to SC/ST community
											or applicants hailing from Jammu and Kashmir/ Ladakh and the
											North Eastern States of Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Manipur,
											Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim and Tripura, 10%
											relaxation is provided. Certificate to prove community /
											nativity should be produced at the time of counselling,
											failing which they will not be considered for admission.
Selection Criteria
There is NO ENTRANCE EXAMINATION
Admission is through merit and based on qualifying
											examination
A Rank list will be prepared based on the eligibility criteria (PCB).
The shortlisted applicants will be called for
											counselling (online).
The fact that a candidate has been qualified and
											called for counselling does not guarantee admission to him /
											her. This depends upon the candidate rank and the
											availability of seats.
Candidates can download the provisional admission
											letter after full fee payment.
Provisional admission will be cancelled if the
											candidate does not meet the eligibility norms.
