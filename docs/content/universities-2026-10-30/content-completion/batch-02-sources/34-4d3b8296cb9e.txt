About RNTU - Best Private University in Bhopal
Rabindranath Tagore University (RNTU), Bhopal is a nationally recognized institution committed to redefining higher education through a skill-integrated, future-ready approach. Established in 2010 as India’s first skill-based university, RNTU is a premier initiative of the AISECT Group of Universities, one of the country’s most respected education networks.

Inspired by the legacy of Nobel Laureate Gurudev Rabindranath Tagore, the university focuses on holistic student development—blending academic excellence with creativity, character-building, and practical competencies. Its ecosystem nurtures innovation, entrepreneurship, and engagement in liberal arts, sports, and cultural activities.

RNTU is recognized under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act and approved by key regulatory bodies including AICTE, NCTE, BCI, INC, ICAR, PCI, MPPMB, and MPPURC. It is also a proud member of the AIU and ACU (UK).


With world-class infrastructure, experiential learning, and industry-aligned curricula, RNTU stands among Central India’s top private universities—empowering students to thrive in a rapidly evolving global landscape.

Placements
Rabindranath Tagore University (RNTU) is recognised as one of the top universities in Bhopal and is known for having one of the most successful placement cells in Central India. The Training and Placement Cell at RNTU has established strong collaborations with more than 800 companies, providing students with a robust platform for internship and placement opportunities.
The university is committed to preparing industry-ready professionals by offering specialised training programmes, career guidance, and skill-development initiatives. Through these continuous efforts, over 8,000 students have been successfully placed, with the highest package reaching an impressive 18 lacs, reflecting the university’s strong industry connections and focus on career-oriented education.

Accreditation and Affiliation
Rabindranath Tagore University proudly features in the NIRF rankings for seven consecutive years, reflecting its consistent commitment to quality education, research, and academic excellence.
Rabindranath Tagore University (RNTU), Bhopal is recognized under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act and is approved by several statutory and regulatory bodies, including AICTE, NCTE, BCI, INC, PCI, ICAR, the Madhya Pradesh Paramedical Council, and the Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration Council for programmes in Engineering & Management, Education (B.Ed.), Law, Pharmacy, Agriculture, Nursing, and Paramedical Sciences.
The University is governed by the Madhya Pradesh Private Universities Regulatory Commission (MPPURC) and holds membership with prestigious national and international bodies such as the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) and the Association of Commonwealth Universities (ACU).
