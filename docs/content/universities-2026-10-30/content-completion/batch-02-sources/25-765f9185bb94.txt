📋 Ph.D. Admission Process
1
Submit Application
Apply online at
forms.vgu.ac.in
. Select Ph.D. and your preferred discipline. Upload PG marksheets and NET/JRF/GATE certificate (if applicable). Application fee: ₹2,500 (non-refundable).
2
Ph.D. Entrance Exam
Candidates without NET/JRF/GATE qualification must appear in the VGU Ph.D. Entrance Exam. NET/JRF/GATE qualified candidates are
exempt
and proceed directly to Interview.
3
Research Interview
Shortlisted candidates appear before the VGU Research Selection Committee. The interview evaluates your research area, subject depth, and potential for original contribution.
4
Supervisor Allotment
Selected candidates are matched with a Ph.D. supervisor based on research area and supervisor availability. VGU has 200+ Ph.D.-qualified research guides across all disciplines.
5
Course Work + Research
All Ph.D. scholars complete mandatory Course Work in the first year — Research Methodology, domain-specific advanced courses and Publication Workshop. Followed by independent research. Minimum duration: 3 years.
6
Thesis Submission & Defence
After completing research and publishing minimum 2 papers in Scopus/Web of Science indexed journals, thesis is submitted. Open viva-voce is conducted before a panel of internal and external experts.
