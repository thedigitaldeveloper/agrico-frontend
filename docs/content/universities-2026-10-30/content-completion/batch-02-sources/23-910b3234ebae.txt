About Faculty
Faculty of Management Studies
The Faculty of Management Studies at
JRN RVU
is committed to developing future-ready business leaders and professionals with strong managerial knowledge, practical skills, and ethical values. The faculty focuses on providing industry-oriented education that helps students understand modern business challenges and make effective decisions in competitive environments.
The academic programs are designed to offer a strong foundation in key management areas such as marketing, finance, human resource management, operations, entrepreneurship, business strategy, and organizational behavior. Learning is enhanced through case studies, projects, presentations, group discussions, workshops, internships, and industry interactions that connect classroom knowledge with real-world business practices.

Our Objectives
Faculty of Management Studies
The Faculty of Management Studies is committed to delivering high-quality management education through a blend of strong foundational knowledge, industry-oriented skills, and practical learning. The faculty aims to nurture future leaders and managers who are capable of strategic thinking, innovation, decision-making, and contributing to organizational growth in a dynamic global business environment.
Faculty Objectives
Provide a strong foundation in core management principles, business strategies, and organizational behavior.
Develop practical skills through case studies, projects, presentations, internships, and real-world industry exposure.
Foster leadership qualities, analytical thinking, and problem-solving abilities to manage complex business challenges effectively.
Promote entrepreneurship, professional ethics, and continuous learning through research activities, industry collaborations, and skill development initiatives.
