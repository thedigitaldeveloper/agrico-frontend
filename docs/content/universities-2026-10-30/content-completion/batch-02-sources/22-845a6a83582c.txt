Introduction
Architects are the real force behind amazing buildings and structures. Skyscrapers would not have been possible without them. Architecture has changed tremendously in the last couple of decades where the demographics of students has undergone tremendous change and students from varied social backgrounds, disciplines and streams have come to choose this stream.
Our Vision
Blending tradition with modernity, to lay a strong foundation for a meaningful curriculum that balances technically-sound and aesthetically-pleasing design principles, in-tune with environmental thought leaders of the modern and ancient world
Our Mission
To blend theory and practice, vernacular and modern, research and design into an innovative, practice-oriented curriculum aimed at a futuristic world that is sustainable and in harmony with nature.
Further, to lay the mold for students to be ethical and ecologically-sensitive, professional, building-practitioners who combine intuitive and rational thinking to design for the “Real World.”
Architecture @ SSU
Bachelor of Architecture degree here is about nurturing you to become conscious citizens who ‘revere nature and honour mother earth’. We make you believe the entire campus as being your canvas. We create you as future architects to think at the grass-roots level to be able to create things that impact every aspect of the environment. Equips you to create conscious and purposeful structures that are breathable, modern and sustainable.
You will graduate as an ethical, ecologically-sensitive and professional building-practitioner. You will be taught in our studio – where you will have your own dedicated design space on which you set projects that require you to produce models and drawings to communicate your ideas.

Become an Architect
Join one of
the best colleges for architecture and interior design in India
, where you learn to shape the future of built environments and discover your potential to transform how people live, work, and connect.
How Long Does It Take?
Bachelor of Architecture – 5 years full-time
Bachelor of Interior Design – 4 years full-time
Why choose SSU?
Choosing SSU for architecture and interior design means investing in an education that blends academic excellence with real-world experience. At SSU, you benefit from world-class faculty, strong industry partnerships, and access to cutting-edge studios and labs that nurture innovation and hands-on learning. Our vibrant campus culture encourages creativity and leadership, while international collaborations and study tours provide a global perspective. With dedicated career support and a network of successful alumni, SSU is one of the top destinations for shaping your future in the dynamic fields of architecture and interior design.
What are the future career prospects?
As a professional architect or designer, you can establish your own practice, join private architectural firms working on commercial, residential, or institutional projects, or contribute to conservation and renovation efforts both in India and abroad. The diverse career paths available reflect the global demand and the profession’s dynamic nature, ensuring that SSU graduates are prepared to thrive in any setting.
What are the Placement Opportunities provided?
The Placement cell maintains prolific placement ties with prominent business houses in India and abroad. It provides guidance, support and necessary training to students for their job search and interviews.

About the Course
Architecture @ SSU
At SSU’s
Faculty of Architecture
—recognized among the best architecture colleges in India and the top architecture colleges in Odisha—we offer comprehensive undergraduate programs in both Architecture and Interior Design. Our Bachelor of Architecture degree is designed to nurture you into a conscious citizen who reveres nature and honours Mother Earth. The entire campus becomes your canvas, inspiring you to think creatively and holistically about the built environment. Future architects are empowered to address challenges at the grassroots level, ensuring their designs make a positive impact on every aspect of the natural environment. You will be equipped to create structures that are not only modern and visually striking but also breathable, sustainable, and purposeful.
The Bachelor of Interior Design at SSU is equally committed to developing creative, socially responsible, and environmentally conscious professionals. The program integrates a strong foundation in design theory with hands-on learning in state-of-the-art studios, preparing you to shape inspiring interiors for residential, commercial, and public spaces. With guidance from accomplished faculty and exposure to industry best practices, students become adept at creating spaces that are both functional and aesthetically impactful. SSU provides an unmatched learning environment, making it the best choice for those seeking the finest education in interior design and architecture in the region.
Vision & Mission
Vision
Blending tradition with modernity, to lay a strong foundation for a meaningful curriculum that balances technically-sound and aesthetically-pleasing design principles, in-tune with environmentally conscious leaders of the modern and ancient world
Mission
To blend theory and practice, vernacular and modern, research and design into an innovative, practice-oriented curriculum aimed at a futuristic world that is sustainable and in harmony with nature.
Further, to lay the mould for students to be ethical and ecologically sensitive, professional building practitioners who combine intuitive and rational thinking to design for the “Real World.”
Uniqueness of pedagogy:
We offer experiential learning, field and institutional exposure visits in each semester of all courses, apart from student seminars, internships, entrepreneurship ideation, and professional competency-building training for our students.
Counselling and Emotional Intelligence:
We engage with students to understand their limitations and help them enhance their emotional intelligence through spiritual interventions and counselling sessions. We have residential counsellors with decades of experience and expertise in working with youth internationally. They are available to help students resolve personal, professional, or existential crises they are currently facing.
