About Faculty
Faculty of Law
Established in 2015 at Manikya Lal Verma Shramjeevi College, the Department of Law stands as a vibrant hub of innovative, research-oriented, and interdisciplinary legal education. Founded under the visionary leadership of Prof. Col. S.S. Sarangdevot, the Department is dedicated to cultivating a new generation of legal professionals who are intellectually rigorous, socially responsive, and ethically grounded. The Department strives to nurture students who are progressive yet rooted in cultural values, independent yet collaborative, and competitive yet compassionate, equipping them to meet the evolving demands of the legal profession and society.
Offering a comprehensive spectrum of programmes- including the five-year integrated B.A. LL.B., three-year LL.B., two-year LL.M., Ph.D., and Postgraduate Diplomas in specialized domains such as Cyber Law, Labour Law, Intellectual Property Law, Taxation and Accountancy, and Forensic Science - the Department ensures academic depth alongside professional relevance. With a strong emphasis on interdisciplinary learning and research engagement, the Department encourages critical inquiry, policy-oriented thinking, and academic scholarship across emerging areas of law and allied disciplines. Students are actively exposed to contemporary legal challenges, technological advancements, and global perspectives.

STUDENT SUPPORT
The Department is committed to providing a comprehensive and inclusive student support system that promotes academic success, well-being, and professional growth. Students are guided through mentoring, academic counselling, and career support services, including internships and placement assistance. The University facilitates access to a range of scholarships, fee concessions, and financial assistance schemes offered by government and institutional sources, ensuring that deserving and meritorious students are supported irrespective of their socio-economic background. Through this holistic approach, the Department fosters an equitable learning environment and empowers students to achieve their full potential.
INFRASTRUCTURE
Moot Court Hall (Fully Equipped)
A modern, fully equipped Moot Court Hall designed as per Bar Council of India norms, providing a real courtroom experience. It supports moot courts, mock trials, and ADR simulations, strengthening advocacy and practical legal skills.
Library & Legal Resource Centre
A well-stocked, research-oriented library with extensive legal resources, including books, journals, law reports, and e-databases. It fosters interdisciplinary learning and supports academic excellence and legal research.
Legal Aid Clinic & ADR Facilities
An active Legal Aid Clinic in line with Bar Council of India guidelines, promoting access to justice and community engagement. Students gain hands-on exposure to mediation, negotiation, and legal outreach.
Computer Lab & Research Support
Well-equipped computer facilities with internet access, enabling students to engage in digital legal research, access online databases, and develop academic and drafting skills.
Common Rooms (GCR & BCR)
Separate, well-maintained Common Rooms for Girls and Boys as per Bar Council of India norms, providing a safe, inclusive, and comfortable student space.
Seminar & Conference Facilities
Dedicated spaces for seminars, workshops, and guest lectures, fostering academic dialogue, professional exposure, and research dissemination.

PLACEMENT
The Department actively facilitates internships for students to enhance employability and develop real-life advocacy skills. Students are encouraged and guided to intern across diverse legal domains, including corporate firms, NGOs, and advocate offices, enabling them to gain practical exposure and professional insight. The Departments have various students placed in different public as well as private institutions. The Department also provides continuous support and mentorship for participation in moot court competitions, helping students strengthen their research, drafting, and advocacy abilities while building confidence and courtroom competence.

Our Objectives
Department of Law
The Department of Law is committed to delivering high-quality legal education through a blend of strong foundational knowledge, practical training, and professional ethics. The department aims to nurture future legal professionals who are capable of critical thinking, effective advocacy, and contributing to justice, social responsibility, and legal development across diverse areas of law.
Faculty Objectives
Provide a strong foundation in core legal principles, constitutional values, and judicial systems.
Develop practical skills through moot courts, legal aid activities, internships, case studies, and drafting exercises.
Foster analytical thinking, legal reasoning, and problem-solving abilities to address complex legal and social issues.
Promote interdisciplinary learning and encourage research, innovation, and professional growth through seminars, workshops, and legal awareness programs.
