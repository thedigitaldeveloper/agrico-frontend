Programme Overview

B.Sc. Agriculture (Honours) is a four-year undergraduate programme offered at RNTU, Raisen, designed to provide comprehensive knowledge of agricultural sciences. The curriculum covers key disciplines including Agronomy, Horticulture in Fruits and Vegetables, Soil Science, Entomology, Plant Pathology, Genetics & Plant Breeding, Agricultural Extension, and Agricultural Economics. The programme integrates biology, technology, and modern scientific methods with traditional agricultural practices to enhance productivity sustainably. It equips students with practical skills, field exposure, and scientific expertise required to address real-world agricultural challenges.

  • 4 Years

    Duration of programme

  • UG

    Level of Study

Key Highlights

Interdisciplinary agriculture science curriculum
Hands-on field and lab training
Focus on sustainable farming practices
Exposure to modern agri technologies
Strong foundation in agri economics and extension

How will you benefit

B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture offers a comprehensive understanding of diverse agricultural disciplines, enabling students to explore multiple career pathways upon graduation.
The programme opens up rewarding opportunities in both government and private sectors. Reputed organizations such as NABARD, IFFCO, Central Warehousing Corporation, and FCI offer promising career prospects.
Graduates can pursue research-oriented careers by qualifying for JRF and opting for higher studies such as M.Sc. in Agriculture or an MBA in Agribusiness, leading to advanced professional opportunities.
The programme also helps students develop essential skill sets, including agri-entrepreneurship, research aptitude, and effective farm management practices.

What will you study

Fundamental concepts and principles of agriculture

Diverse disciplines within agricultural sciences

Comprehensive understanding of agriculture and allied fields

In-depth knowledge for holistic agricultural expertise

  • PO-3. To provide extensive knowledge on agri-allied sectors like livestock and poultry.
  • PO-4. To disseminate different technologies through various extension activities.
  • PO-5. To identify and overcome the problems encountered in day-to-day agriculture.
  • PO-6. To provide knowledge on commercial agricultural production practices.
  • PO-7. To make students competitive in pursuing higher studies.

  • PSO-1. To provide knowledge from ancient to modern agricultural practices.
  • PSO-2. To impart in-depth practical knowledge in crop cultivation practices.
  • PSO-3. To give detailed knowledge about agri-allied sectors.
  • PSO-4. To provide knowledge on the working of different farm implements.
  • PSO-5. To disseminate recent agricultural technologies through extension technologies.
  • PSO-6. To serve the rural agricultural population.
  • PSO-7. Detailed knowledge of various agri-business activities.

  • PSO-8. Detailed knowledge of horticulture and sericulture practices.

  • PEO-1. Imparting subject-related knowledge along with developing a connection between practical solutions and theory.
