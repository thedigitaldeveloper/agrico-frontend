Overview
The B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture is a four-year undergraduate degree programme in
                                Agricultural Sciences designed to equip students with comprehensive knowledge and
                                practical skills required to address contemporary challenges in agriculture. The programme is
                                offered by the Faculty of Agricultural Sciences (FASC), SGT University, which is accredited
                                by the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR), ensuring adherence to national
                                academic standards and quality benchmarks.
                                The curriculum is multidisciplinary in nature, integrating core domains such as Agronomy,
                                Horticulture, Soil Science, Plant Breeding and Genetics, Entomology, Plant Pathology,
                                Agricultural Economics, Extension Education, and Seed Science. In addition, the programme
                                incorporates emerging and advanced areas including Precision Agriculture, Biotechnology,
                                Internet of Things (IoT), and Geographic Information Systems (GIS), thereby preparing
                                students for future-ready agricultural practices.
                                A distinguishing feature of the programme is its strong emphasis on experiential learning.
                                Students gain hands-on exposure through laboratory experiments, field training, research
                                farm activities, and protected cultivation structures such as polyhouses. Structured
                                components like Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE), Agro-Industrial
                                Attachment (AIA), and Experiential Learning Units (ELUs) provide real-world exposure
                                to farming systems, agribusiness operations and value addition practices.
                                The programme fosters innovation, entrepreneurship, and practical problem-solving abilities,
                                encouraging students to engage in agri-startups and large-scale production systems.
                                Graduates of the programme are well-prepared to pursue diverse career pathways in
                                government services, research and development, agribusiness industries, startups and higher
                                education.
Eligibility
Qualification:
10+2 Science Group (PCM / PCB / PCMB) or Agriculture related subjects
                                with a minimum score of 50% marks
Learning Outcomes
Conceptual Understanding:
Demonstrate a comprehensive understanding of fundamental concepts, principles, and scientific foundations in core agricultural disciplines.
Application of Knowledge:
Apply theoretical knowledge effectively to real-world agricultural situations, including crop production, resource management, and sustainable farming practices.
Experimental and Analytical Skills:
Develop proficiency in designing and conducting experiments, making systematic observations, and performing data collection, analysis, and interpretation.
Problem-Solving Ability:
Identify and solve subject-specific agricultural problems using appropriate tools, technologies, and scientific methodologies.
Practical Competence:
Exhibit hands-on skills and technical competencies through fieldwork, laboratory practices, and experiential learning components such as RAWE, AIA, and ELUs.

FAQs
What is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture?
B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture is a 4-year undergraduate degree programme that integrates agricultural science, technology, and management. As per ICAR norms, it includes theory, practicals, fieldwork, and experiential learning to develop skilled agricultural professionals.
What is the duration and structure of the programme?
Duration: 4 years (8 semesters)
Structure (as per ICAR 6th Deans’ Committee): Core courses (Agronomy, Soil Science, Horticulture, etc.), Experiential Learning Programme (ELP), Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE), Student READY Programme, Hands-on training & field visits.
What are the eligibility criteria for admission?
10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology/Mathematics/Agriculture related subjects with minimum 50–60% marks. Admission through ICAR AIEEA or State/University entrance exams.
What are the key learning outcomes of this programme?
Gain fundamental and applied knowledge in agriculture, develop problem-solving and decision-making skills, acquire entrepreneurial and managerial competencies, demonstrate sustainable practices, and enhance communication and leadership skills.
What practical exposure is provided?
Field visits, laboratory experiments, RAWE programme (village attachment), Experiential Learning Units (ELUs), and internships in agri-industries.
What is RAWE and why is it important?
RAWE (Rural Agricultural Work Experience) allows students to work with farmers, understand real-world challenges, and apply scientific knowledge in field conditions.
What is the Student READY Programme?
It focuses on skill development, entrepreneurship training, agribusiness exposure, and hands-on experience to ensure industry readiness and employability.
What career opportunities are available after B.Sc. Agriculture?
Opportunities include Government jobs (Agriculture Officer, NABARD, Civil Services), Private sector roles (Agribusiness, FMCG, Agrochemical industries), and self-employment through agripreneurship.
What higher education options are available?
M.Sc. Agriculture, MBA (Agribusiness/Management), ICAR PG exams, and international education opportunities.
What are the major subjects covered?
Agronomy, Soil Science, Genetics & Plant Breeding, Horticulture, Agricultural Economics, Plant Pathology, Entomology, and Agricultural Engineering.
Is the programme aligned with industry requirements?
Yes, with updated curriculum, skill development focus, and integrated internships and industry linkages.
What skills will I gain from this programme?
Technical, analytical, communication, entrepreneurial, and digital agriculture skills.
What facilities should a good agriculture college provide?
Well-equipped labs, instructional farms, smart classrooms, digital library, and placement support.
What is the scope of agriculture in the future?
High growth in sustainable farming, agri-tech, food security, and climate-resilient agriculture.
Why should I choose B.Sc. Agriculture?
Diverse careers, national importance, entrepreneurship opportunities, and global demand.
How does NAAC accreditation benefit students?
Ensures quality education, transparency, better infrastructure, and improved placements.
Is there scope for entrepreneurship after this course?
Yes, including organic farming, agri-input businesses, food processing units, and consultancy services.
Are scholarships available?
Yes, including ICAR, state government, and university merit-based scholarships.
What is the evaluation system in this programme?
Continuous assessment, mid-term and final exams, field evaluation, and project/internship assessment.
How is placement support provided?
Through Training & Placement Cell, industry collaborations, campus drives, and skill development workshops.
