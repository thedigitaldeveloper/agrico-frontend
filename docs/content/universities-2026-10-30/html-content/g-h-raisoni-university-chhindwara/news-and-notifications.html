News & Notices: questions answered

Which dated admission notices are recorded for GHRU?

No dated admission event is verified in this profile. The official channel is https://admissions.ghru.edu.in/; check the notice for the programme and entry year you intend to join.

How does admission to GHRU work?

The course page links to the university application portal. Its eligibility thresholds are corroborated by an NTA table explicitly for 2025, so they are not treated as a verified 2026 policy. Ask admissions to confirm the applicable selection route and subject eligibility for the new cycle. Confirm the current prospectus: Ask the admissions office for the applicable intake, subject requirements and selection rules before submitting an application. Request a written cost statement: Obtain tuition, compulsory charges, accommodation options and refund terms for the intended entry year.

How can I contact GHRU about an application?

Published phone: +91 91110 42900. Published email: info@ghru.edu.in. Official admissions: https://admissions.ghru.edu.in/
