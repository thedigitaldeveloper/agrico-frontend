Agriculture study at the Saikheda campus

G H Raisoni University’s agriculture course is located at Saikheda in Madhya Pradesh. The official programme page describes a four-year full-time honours degree divided into eight semesters. It brings together soil science, plant breeding, microbiology and crop-health subjects with practical exposure.

The university describes field practice, trips, study tours and projects within agricultural learning. Its course page also discusses work with farmers and agricultural companies as a way to connect classroom study with the sector. Applicants should ask for the current fieldwork calendar and internship arrangements, alongside a written fee statement and the admission rules for their intended entry year.
