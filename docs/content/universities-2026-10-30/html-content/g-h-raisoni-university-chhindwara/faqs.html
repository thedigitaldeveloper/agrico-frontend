How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at GHRU?

4 years (8 semesters); study format: Full time. The four-year full-time degree spans agricultural microbiology, soils, genetics and plant breeding, plant pathology and related subjects. The university describes field practice, projects and study tours.

Sources: [1][2]

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at GHRU?

The undated university course page lists 12th Science in PCM/PCB/PCMB/Crop Science with 45% for open and 40% for reserved categories. NTA’s 2025 table repeats those thresholds; 2026 conditions need confirmation.

Sources: [1][2]
