How does admission to SKYCAS work?

For Category A admission, use ANGRAU’s 2026–27 brochure and counselling portal. Confirm the correct college choice and fee category before accepting an allotment. ANGRAU’s revised 30 October 2026 notice schedules November manual counselling for registered candidates. Follow the exact rank/session and check vacancies; the notice does not promise a seat at SKYCAS. Read the applicable ANGRAU brochure: Review eligibility, local-area and category conditions for the 2026–27 counselling route.
