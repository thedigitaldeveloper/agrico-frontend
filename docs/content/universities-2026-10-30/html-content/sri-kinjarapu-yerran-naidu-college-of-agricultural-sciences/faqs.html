FAQs: questions answered

Is SKYCAS a university?

No. It is an affiliated college; ANGRAU is its affiliating university.

Where is the college located?

ANGRAU lists Etcherla in Srikakulam district, Andhra Pradesh.

What year does the directory give for establishment?

The official ANGRAU directory gives 2016.

Which 2026 entrance route is documented?

The ANGRAU Category A brochure uses AP EAPCET 2026 ranks for the counselling route it describes.

What is the ICAR accreditation term?

The audited college record runs from 27 January 2025 to 26 January 2030.

What does SKYCAS’s 2026–27 convenor-quota fee cover?

ANGRAU’s prospectus lists a total annual academic fee of ₹128,700. Hostel, transport and mess are excluded. Its management-quota maximum is up to ₹257,400 per year.

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at SKYCAS?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture. The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at SKYCAS?

ANGRAU 2026–27 BiPC brochure specifies Intermediate BiPC/MBiPC or equivalent with physical and biological/natural sciences; other conditions and categories apply.

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at SKYCAS?

₹1,28,700 / year · 2026-2027. Official 2026–27 ANGRAU prospectus page 7. Management-quota maximum is up to ₹257,400 per year. Convenor fee excludes hostel, transport and mess charges. Included charges: Total annual academic fee for convenor-quota admission at SKYCAS, Etcherla.

How does admission to SKYCAS work?

For Category A admission, use ANGRAU’s 2026–27 brochure and counselling portal. Confirm the correct college choice and fee category before accepting an allotment. ANGRAU’s revised 30 October 2026 notice schedules November manual counselling for registered candidates. Follow the exact rank/session and check vacancies; the notice does not promise a seat at SKYCAS. Read the applicable ANGRAU brochure: Review eligibility, local-area and category conditions for the 2026–27 counselling route. Check the college allotment: Use the official portal for the allotted college and reporting instructions.
