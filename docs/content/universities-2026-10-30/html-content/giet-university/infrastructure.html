The documented academic setting

The school describes two instructional farms and an orchard at Bandhuguda. Its facility account names irrigation, a crop cafeteria, research blocks, mushroom, azolla and vermicompost units, and farm machinery.

Facilities identified in the sources

Instructional crop farms

The farm account describes undergraduate crop-production plots and research blocks.

Bandhuguda orchard

The university identifies an orchard about one kilometre from the main campus.
