Courses & Fees: questions answered

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at GIET University?

₹1,60,000 / year · 2024-25. Historical B.Sc. Agriculture annual fee, also shown as INR80,000 per semester. Other charges separate; current applicability not established. Included charges: Published programme fee; other charges are separate unless stated.

What fee is documented for M.Sc. Agriculture — Agronomy at GIET University?

₹1,20,000 / year · 2024-25. Historical combined M.Sc. Agriculture row, also INR60,000 per semester. Confirm Agronomy-specific current offer. It is not assigned to the separately named Horticulture course. Included charges: Published programme fee; other charges are separate unless stated.

What fee is documented for M.Sc. Horticulture — Fruit Science at GIET University?

A verified numerical fee is not available. A dated fee schedule for the intended admission year has not been verified.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at GIET University?

4 years (8 semesters). A full-time agricultural science degree with crop-production plots and Student READY activities.

What scholarship information is available for GIET University?

GIET’s general scholarship page contains schemes with different programme scopes, including GIETEE/Board-of-Governance categories explicitly marked B.Tech-only. Agriculture applicants should ask which government reimbursement or institutional assistance actually applies to their course and entry year. Engineering award limits should not be transferred to agriculture.

How can I contact GIET University about an application?

Published phone: +91-7735745535. Published email: enquiry@giet.edu. Official admissions: https://www.giet.edu/admissions/gietee/

Is a current fee confirmed?

A historical official 2024-25 schedule gives B.Sc. Agriculture at INR1,60,000 per year and a combined M.Sc. Agriculture row at INR1,20,000 per year. These do not establish current 2026-27 charges or a Horticulture-specific amount.
