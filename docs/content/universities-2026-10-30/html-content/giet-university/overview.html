Agricultural teaching at the Eastern Ghats foothills

GIET’s agriculture school dates its establishment to 2017 and locates its work in the farming context of southern Odisha. Its four-year degree covers agronomy, horticulture, soils, crop improvement, plant health, economics and extension. The school describes Student READY as a route to practical and industry exposure.

The farm account provides specific learning locations: two instructional farms and an orchard at Bandhuguda outside the main campus. It states that undergraduate students receive separate plots for crop-production activities. This field infrastructure can be assessed separately from university-wide claims about placements or facilities in other disciplines.
