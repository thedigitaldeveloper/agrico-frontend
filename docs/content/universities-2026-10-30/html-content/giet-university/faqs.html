How does admission to GIET University work?

The official entrance page identifies GIETEE for B.Sc. Agriculture and displays 2026-27 material, alongside a newer 2027 banner and an old 2020 fee note. Applicants need the specific intake prospectus rather than relying on mixed-year page labels. Confirm the course: Use the official programme contact to confirm that the listed degree is taking applications for your intended intake. Request the intake notice: Ask for the applicable selection rules, fee breakdown and document checklist before submitting an application.

Sources: [5][6]

What practical learning is described at GIET University?

A dated August 2026 university report records a farmer exposure visit to Instructional Farm-I and research demonstration plots.

Sources: [3][7]

Which teaching facilities are documented at GIET University?

Instructional crop farms: The farm account describes undergraduate crop-production plots and research blocks. Bandhuguda orchard: The university identifies an orchard about one kilometre from the main campus.

Sources: [3][7]

What student facilities are documented for GIET University?

Agriculture hostel allocation: The school states that five hostels serve agriculture students: three for boys and two for girls. Its description gives three to four students per room; individual allocations require confirmation. Dining and drinking water: The school describes a central mess, monitored food provision and hostel water coolers. Hostel recreation and emergency support: The facility account lists indoor games, gymnasiums and emergency ambulance provision, alongside internet access.

Sources: [3][7][13]

What scholarship information is available for GIET University?

GIET’s general scholarship page contains schemes with different programme scopes, including GIETEE/Board-of-Governance categories explicitly marked B.Tech-only. Agriculture applicants should ask which government reimbursement or institutional assistance actually applies to their course and entry year. Engineering award limits should not be transferred to agriculture.

Sources: [11]

What training and outcome evidence is available for GIET University?

GIET University publishes an Agriculture and Allied Sectors submission for NIRF 2026. Its programme-duration cohorts report the graduating group, students placed, students entering higher studies and the median salary of placed graduates. These historical institutional submissions are not a placement promise or a salary estimate for every student or individual specialisation.

Sources: [10]
