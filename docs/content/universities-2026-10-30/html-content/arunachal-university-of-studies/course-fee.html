Courses & Fees: questions answered

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at AUS?

₹90,000 / year · 2026-2027. Published annual amount for the agriculture bachelor’s degree. Obtain a complete payable statement; adjacent Life Sciences examination/OJT charges do not establish agriculture charges. Included charges: Annual programme fee in the 2026–27 agriculture table; itemised compulsory, residential and other charges are not specified in this row.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at AUS?

4 years (8 semesters). The 2026–27 prospectus lists an eight-semester agriculture honours degree, with separate first-year and second-year entry qualifications.

What scholarship information is available for AUS?

The 2026–27 agriculture table describes government fee-reimbursement assistance for eligible APST students, subject to scheme terms and approval. The university also limits concurrent scholarship applications to one award. Its separate scholarship form deadline of 30 October 2026 is already past. Conditional APST fee-reimbursement assistance: The 2026–27 agriculture table states reimbursement up to ₹50,000 for APST candidates with annual family income below ₹2,50,000, under Central/State government schemes and prescribed terms. This is a maximum conditional benefit, not an automatic deduction or a guaranteed award; the brochure does not give renewal rules. Government stipend / scholarship application route: The university directs eligible students to Arunachal Pradesh or Government of India granting agencies and limits concurrent applications to one award. The listed university submission deadline was30October2026; no current agriculture-specific entitlement is established.
