Career support and reported outcomes

The college lists a Career Guidance and Placements Cell and publishes civil-services awareness material. Individual postgraduate admission achievements and awareness activities should not be counted as a programme-wide employment rate. No dated agriculture placement statistics were verified.

Learning experiences connected with preparation

PAC’s official site lists agricultural teaching units, while its extension department describes rural engagement through RAWE and village appraisal. Confirm the current cohort’s fieldwork schedule and teaching-unit access.

  • The official site publishes activity material labelled mushroom cultivation and hand pollination in watermelon.
  • The extension department describes RAWE and village work, including participatory rural-appraisal exercises in Pallalakuppam.

How to assess a placement claim

For B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, ask for a report that matches the course, campus and graduating batch. Compare outcomes on the same basis rather than using an isolated maximum package.

Evidence to requestWhat to read
Cohort and reporting yearWhich programme, campus and graduating batch the report covers
Eligible students and offersHow the institution defines participation and reported placement
Roles and employersWhether the work and qualification requirements match the degree
Salary descriptionWhether a quoted figure is median, average, highest or another measure
Internship versus employmentWhether an experience is curricular training, a paid internship or a final job offer

Which teaching facilities are documented at PAC?

Agricultural teaching units: The official site lists an apiary, mushroom cultivation and vermicompost units. Central library: A dedicated central-library section is listed on the college website. Student hostels and welfare spaces: PAC publishes separate accommodation for male and female students. Its listed common facilities include a computer-equipped reading room, sick bay, guest room and television hall; hostel records cover entry/exit, complaints and medical matters.
