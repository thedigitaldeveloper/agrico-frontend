A Vellore district college focused on agriculture

Palar Agricultural College was established in 2017 under RS Educational and Charitable Trust. It is located at Kothamarikuppam Village, Mailpatti Post, in Vellore district and offers a four-year B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture under TNAU affiliation.

Its academic divisions cover crop science and associated subjects, with agricultural teaching units and student-support cells listed across the campus website. The extension department describes rural learning and farmer engagement through RAWE. Applicants can ask how the teaching units and village activities fit together across the degree, while confirming admission ranking requirements and the complete cost of attendance.
