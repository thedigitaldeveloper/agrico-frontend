How does admission to PAC work?

PAC instructs candidates to register with TNAU and obtain ranking for counselling as well as management admission. Its fee section refers to the Government of Tamil Nadu’s prescribed structure without publishing a verified numeric schedule. Obtain TNAU ranking: The official page asks applicants for both admission channels to register through the TNAU admission website. Confirm allocation and costs: Ask the college for the current sanctioned seat allocation, applicable merit rules and a written fee breakdown.
