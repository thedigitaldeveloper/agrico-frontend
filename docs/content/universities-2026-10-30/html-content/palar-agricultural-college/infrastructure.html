The documented academic setting

PAC’s official site lists agricultural teaching units, while its extension department describes rural engagement through RAWE and village appraisal. Confirm the current cohort’s fieldwork schedule and teaching-unit access.

Facilities identified in the sources

Agricultural teaching units

The official site lists an apiary, mushroom cultivation and vermicompost units.

Central library

A dedicated central-library section is listed on the college website.

Student hostels and welfare spaces

PAC publishes separate accommodation for male and female students. Its listed common facilities include a computer-equipped reading room, sick bay, guest room and television hall; hostel records cover entry/exit, complaints and medical matters.

Practical learning and student access

For B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, the usefulness of a facility depends on actual teaching access, supervision and the programme timetable. The documented learning activities are listed below; ask the department about your own cohort’s arrangements.

  • The official site publishes activity material labelled mushroom cultivation and hand pollination in watermelon.
  • The extension department describes RAWE and village work, including participatory rural-appraisal exercises in Pallalakuppam.
