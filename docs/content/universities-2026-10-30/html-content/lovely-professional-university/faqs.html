FAQs: questions answered

Which campus location appears in the ICAR record?

The accredited unit is the School of Agriculture, Phagwara.

Does this record include agriculture master’s courses?

Yes. It includes eight named master’s specialisations as well as the bachelor’s degree.

What is the ICAR term for LPU’s listed programmes?

The audited term is 1 April 2023 to 31 March 2028.

What is the verified 2026 B.Sc. Agriculture eligibility at LPU?

The NTA CUET UG 2026 mapping requires 60% in 10+2 with English and three subjects from Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology and Agriculture. Confirm the age cutoff, because its age sentence still refers to December 2025.

Does the school show protected cultivation facilities?

Yes. The official school page lists a Hi-Tech Polyhouse.

Can a scholarship amount be assumed from LPUNEST participation?

No. LPU’s scholarship page describes several categories; the award depends on the applicable programme rules and performance.

Are verified agriculture placement figures available?

The NIRF 2026 agriculture/allied submission reports UG four-year programmes (2024-2025): 359 placed from 449 graduates in minimum time, with ₹6,60,000 median annual salary among those placed; PG two-year programmes (2024-2025): 160 placed from 193 graduates in minimum time, with ₹7,20,000 median annual salary among those placed. These are historical group-level outcomes, not specialisation-level placement promises.

How does admission to LPU work?

Use the 2026 CUET mapping for bachelor’s subject requirements and the dated School of Agriculture article for master’s entry combinations. Confirm the governing prospectus, counselling route and fee statement with LPU. The live B.Sc. Agriculture page now advertises Admission 2027. The 2026 CUET mapping and dated 2026 school guidance below retain their historical session; obtain the 2027 rules before a next-cycle application. Select the agriculture programme: Use the central admissions page to distinguish the bachelor’s degree from the relevant master’s specialisation. Check intake-year conditions: Confirm the accepted qualifications, LPUNEST rules and fee category directly for that programme.

What practical learning is described at LPU?

Agricultural farms provide a setting for crop cultivation work. The school lists apiculture alongside its other agriculture facilities.

Which teaching facilities are documented at LPU?

Hi-Tech Polyhouse: A controlled-environment crop teaching facility. Post-harvest management laboratory: A laboratory concerned with produce preservation and processing. Soil science laboratory: A space for soil analysis and fertility practicals.

What student facilities are documented for LPU?

The school identifies teaching spaces spanning crop production, livestock and the handling of harvested produce. Accommodation availability, meal charges and transport arrangements for your entry year still need direct confirmation.

What scholarship information is available for LPU?

LPU publishes university-wide scholarship categories based on academic performance, LPUNEST and other achievements. Agriculture applicants must establish which category and renewal conditions apply to their exact programme. LPUNEST-linked scholarship: The official scholarship page identifies performance in LPUNEST as one basis for scholarship consideration; no individual award is promised.
