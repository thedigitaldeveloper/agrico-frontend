How does admission to LPU work?

Use the 2026 CUET mapping for bachelor’s subject requirements and the dated School of Agriculture article for master’s entry combinations. Confirm the governing prospectus, counselling route and fee statement with LPU. The live B.Sc. Agriculture page now advertises Admission 2027. The 2026 CUET mapping and dated 2026 school guidance below retain their historical session; obtain the 2027 rules before a next-cycle application. Select the agriculture programme: Use the central admissions page to distinguish the bachelor’s degree from the relevant master’s specialisation. Check intake-year conditions: Confirm the accepted qualifications, LPUNEST rules and fee category directly for that programme.

Sources: [3][4][7][8][11][13]

How can I contact LPU about an application?

Published phone: +91 1824 517000. Published email: admissions@lpu.co.in. Official admissions: https://www.lpu.in/admission/admissions.php
