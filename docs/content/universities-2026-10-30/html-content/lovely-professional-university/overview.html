Agriculture foundations and specialist postgraduate study

LPU’s agriculture school brings crop, livestock and post-harvest learning into one academic setting. Its facilities page identifies an animal husbandry and dairy unit, agricultural farms and subject laboratories. The breadth matters when comparing a general agriculture degree with a focused postgraduate specialisation.

The bachelor’s degree offers a broad entry to agriculture, while the school’s master’s subjects allow a closer focus on agronomy, genetics, plant protection, soils or horticulture. These choices share an agricultural setting but serve different stages of study. The official CUET 2026 mapping supplies a dated bachelor’s eligibility reference, and the admissions office can clarify how that route fits the intended intake.
