The documented academic setting

The school describes experiential learning, rural attachment, industry training, skills work and projects. A detailed current laboratory inventory could not be verified from the linked pages. A readable official school inventory identifies genetics/plant-breeding, plant-pathology, horticulture and agronomy equipment. Confirm present access and practical schedules with the department.

Facilities identified in the sources

Genetics and plant breeding laboratory

The official inventory lists seed display boxes, a seed germinator, BOD incubator, hot-air oven, deep freezer and binocular microscope.

Plant pathology laboratory

The official school inventory identifies microscopes, hot-air oven, autoclave and incubator.

Horticulture and agronomy practical equipment

The school page lists garden tools, sprayers and protected structures including shade nets/polyhouses, alongside tillage implements and agricultural hand tools.

Practical learning and student access

For B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, the usefulness of a facility depends on actual teaching access, supervision and the programme timetable. The documented learning activities are listed below; ask the department about your own cohort’s arrangements.
