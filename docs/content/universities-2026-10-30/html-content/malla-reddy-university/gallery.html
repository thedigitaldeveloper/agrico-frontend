Understand the academic context

The school describes experiential learning, rural attachment, industry training, skills work and projects. A detailed current laboratory inventory could not be verified from the linked pages. A readable official school inventory identifies genetics/plant-breeding, plant-pathology, horticulture and agronomy equipment. Confirm present access and practical schedules with the department.
