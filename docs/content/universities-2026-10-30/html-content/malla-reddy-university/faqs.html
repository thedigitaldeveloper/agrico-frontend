FAQs: questions answered

Where is MRU’s agriculture school located?

The official school page gives Maisammaguda, Dulapally, Hyderabad, Telangana 500100.

What is the published 10+2 marks threshold?

The eligibility page states at least 50% for the undergraduate agriculture category, with relevant subjects.

What practical components does the school name?

Its academic-practices page names experiential learning, RAWE, industry training, hands-on work and projects.

Are horticulture and food science also listed?

Yes, the school lists them alongside agriculture. Their accreditation must be checked separately.

Which degree is covered by this ICAR record?

B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, with the term 16 March 2026 to 15 March 2031.

Is a current agriculture tuition amount verified?

No. The official fee page is labelled 2025–26, but its programme amounts were not readable in the source used.

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at MRU?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture. The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at MRU?

Official eligibility page states 10+2 or equivalent in relevant subjects with at least 50%; confirm exact subject combinations for the intake.

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at MRU?

A verified numerical fee is not available. An academic-year-specific official fee schedule for this programme was not verified.

How does admission to MRU work?

MRU publishes separate eligibility and fee pages. Use the agriculture entry conditions and request an intake-specific selection and cost notice before completing the application. Check the agriculture category: Confirm that your subject combination meets the school’s current requirements. Obtain the current programme notice: Request the applicable selection route and the complete B.Sc. Agriculture fee schedule.

What is the recorded ICAR scope for MRU?

Malla Reddy University: B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture (Intake- 240), recorded term 2026-03-16 to 2031-03-15. This entry applies to the named unit and programme.

What practical learning is described at MRU?

The school lists experiential learning and Rural Agricultural Work Experience. Its published practices include in-plant/industry attachment, skills training and student projects.

Which teaching facilities are documented at MRU?

Genetics and plant breeding laboratory: The official inventory lists seed display boxes, a seed germinator, BOD incubator, hot-air oven, deep freezer and binocular microscope. Plant pathology laboratory: The official school inventory identifies microscopes, hot-air oven, autoclave and incubator. Horticulture and agronomy practical equipment: The school page lists garden tools, sprayers and protected structures including shade nets/polyhouses, alongside tillage implements and agricultural hand tools.

What student facilities are documented for MRU?

The school describes experiential learning, rural attachment, industry training, skills work and projects. A detailed current laboratory inventory could not be verified from the linked pages. A readable official school inventory identifies genetics/plant-breeding, plant-pathology, horticulture and agronomy equipment. Confirm present access and practical schedules with the department. Accommodation availability, meal charges and transport arrangements for your entry year still need direct confirmation.

What scholarship information is available for MRU?

The official eligibility page states that merit scholarships exist across academic programmes. Agriculture-specific award amounts, score bands and renewal conditions were not verified.

What training and outcome evidence is available for MRU?

MRU provides a university placement route, but the agriculture sources used do not establish a dated programme-level employment result. No general-university salary is transferred to the agriculture degree.
