Career support and reported outcomes

Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences publishes an Agriculture and Allied Sectors submission for NIRF 2026. Its programme-duration cohorts report the graduating group, students placed, students entering higher studies and the median salary of placed graduates. These historical institutional submissions are not a placement promise or a salary estimate for every student or individual specialisation.

How to assess a placement claim

For B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. (Agriculture) Agronomy; M.Sc. (Agriculture) Genetics and Plant Breeding; M.Sc. (Horticulture) Fruit Sciences, ask for a report that matches the course, campus and graduating batch. Compare outcomes on the same basis rather than using an isolated maximum package.
