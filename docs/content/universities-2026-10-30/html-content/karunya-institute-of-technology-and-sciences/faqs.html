FAQs: questions answered

When did Karunya’s agriculture school begin?

The school’s official page states 2017; that is the school date, not the university’s founding year.

Which agricultural programmes are listed?

The current school page names B.Sc. Agriculture and master’s courses in agronomy, genetics and plant breeding, and fruit sciences.

Are protected-cultivation facilities documented?

Yes. The school names polyhouses and mist chambers alongside research plots.

Does the checked ICAR entry accredit Karunya’s master’s programmes?

The audited row prints only B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture. A broader website claim is unresolved and is not adopted here.

Is PCM alone definitely accepted?

The academics page includes PCM, while the located brochure uses different biology/agriculture wording. Confirm the current intake rule directly.

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at KITS?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. (Agriculture) Agronomy; M.Sc. (Agriculture) Genetics and Plant Breeding; M.Sc. (Horticulture) Fruit Sciences. The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at KITS?

4 years. The school describes crop, soil and plant-health study linked with field practice.

How long is M.Sc. (Agriculture) Agronomy at KITS?

2 years. Postgraduate study includes field projects, laboratory analysis and research documentation.

How long is M.Sc. (Agriculture) Genetics and Plant Breeding at KITS?

2 years. A specialist crop-improvement degree in the current school programme list.

How long is M.Sc. (Horticulture) Fruit Sciences at KITS?

2 years. The school lists a separate fruit-crop postgraduate programme.

What scholarship information is available for KITS?

Karunya’s 2026–27 scholarship page explicitly includes B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture in its Indian-student merit grid. The separate Founder’s Scholarship offers a limited 100% tuition waiver for eligible Indian Christian applicants; its 2026 application and selection dates have passed. B.Sc. Agriculture Indian-student merit grid, 2026–27: The agriculture column lists INR 1,20,000 at 95% PCM/PCB, INR 60,000 at 90%, and INR 40,000 at 85%. These are scholarship amounts, not net tuition payable. Obtain the approved award and applicable continuation terms. Founder’s Scholarship 2026–27: Limited 100% tuition waiver for selected Indian Christian applicants with at least 90% PCM/PCB and annual family income below INR 8 lakh. Screening includes documentary verification and interview. The 29 May 2026 deadline is past.
