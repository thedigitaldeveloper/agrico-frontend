How does admission to KITS work?

The live official UG admissions page now describes the 2027 cycle. It states that KEE 2027 is mandatory for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture and that confirmation depends on the stated qualifying-examination requirements. Treat these as next-cycle instructions; the 2026–30 cohort fee sheet below remains explicitly labelled by its own source session. Older academic and brochure descriptions differ, so request the exact intake-year subject rules. Confirm the course: Use the official programme contact to confirm that the listed degree is taking applications for your intended intake. Request the intake notice: Ask for the applicable selection rules, fee breakdown and document checklist before submitting an application.

Sources: [3][4][9][14]
