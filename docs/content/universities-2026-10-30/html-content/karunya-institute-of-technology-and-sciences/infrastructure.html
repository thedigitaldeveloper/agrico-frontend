Infrastructure: questions answered

Which teaching facilities are documented at KITS?

Protected cultivation structures: Polyhouses and mist chambers are named in the school’s facilities account. Agrometeorological facilities: The brochure identifies an observatory and automatic weather station.

What practical learning is described at KITS?

The school describes instructional and research farmland, polyhouses, mist chambers and research plots. Its brochure additionally identifies experiential-learning modules, an agrometeorological observatory and an automatic weather station.
