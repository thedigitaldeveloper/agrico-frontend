Field-based agricultural study in Coimbatore

Karunya dates its School of Agricultural Sciences to 2017. The current school page describes eleven academic divisions, covering crop, soil, plant-health, engineering and allied subjects. Its farm and laboratory setting supports a programme account that extends beyond lecture teaching to crop experiments, protected cultivation and agricultural enterprise learning.

Official sources list a four-year B.Sc. Agriculture together with postgraduate agronomy, genetics and plant breeding, and fruit science. These subject options allow students to distinguish crop production, crop improvement and horticultural study. Entry wording differs between the academics page and the brochure located during research, so applicants need the definitive course notice before selecting a school-subject or postgraduate route.
