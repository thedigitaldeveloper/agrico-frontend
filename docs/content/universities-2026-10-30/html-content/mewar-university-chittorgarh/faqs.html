FAQs: questions answered

What is the B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture fee at Mewar University, Chittorgarh for 2026-27?

The published annual tuition fee for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Mewar University, Chittorgarh is INR 72,000 per year. Confirm hostel, examination and registration charges separately.

How can I get admission to B.Sc. Agriculture at Mewar University, Chittorgarh?

Admission is offered through Rajasthan JET / CUET (UG) / MUEE for candidates who have passed 10+2 with Agriculture, PCB, PCM or PCA.

Where is the agriculture campus of Mewar University, Chittorgarh located?

The Faculty of Agriculture and Veterinary Sciences is located at NH-48, Gangrar, Chittorgarh, Rajasthan 312901, India.

What practical farm facilities are available at Mewar University, Chittorgarh?

Gangrar campus crop cafeteria, orchard block, meteorological observatory and RAWE village attachment across Mewar agro-climatic zone.

Does Mewar University, Chittorgarh offer M.Sc. Agriculture or postgraduate courses?

Covered programmes include: B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture (4 years); M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) (2 years).

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at MU Chittorgarh?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy). The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at MU Chittorgarh?

4 years; study format: Full-time on campus. B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Mewar University, Chittorgarh is delivered on campus through the Faculty of Agriculture and Veterinary Sciences, combining classroom agronomy, laboratory practicals and field crop rotations.

How long is M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at MU Chittorgarh?

2 years; study format: Full-time on campus. M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at Mewar University, Chittorgarh is delivered on campus through the Faculty of Agriculture and Veterinary Sciences, combining classroom agronomy, laboratory practicals and field crop rotations.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at MU Chittorgarh?

10+2 with Agriculture or Science (PCB / PCM) with minimum 50% marks (45% for SC/ST/OBC).

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at MU Chittorgarh?

4-year B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture degree from a recognized university.

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at MU Chittorgarh?

₹72,000 / year · 2026-27. Published annual tuition fee for the 2026-27 session; confirm category or JET quota fee schedule before fee payment. Included charges: Published annual academic tuition fee; hostel, examination and caution money are billed separately.

What fee is documented for M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at MU Chittorgarh?

₹68,000 / year · 2026-27. Published annual tuition fee for the 2026-27 session; confirm category or JET quota fee schedule before fee payment. Included charges: Published annual academic tuition fee; hostel, examination and caution money are billed separately.

How does admission to MU Chittorgarh work?

Admission to agriculture programmes at Mewar University, Chittorgarh is conducted through Rajasthan JET / CUET (UG) / MUEE, alongside direct university merit counselling for eligible 10+2 Agriculture/Science candidates. Both Rajasthan JET state counselling seats and university-level seats require verification of Class XII subject combinations (PCB, PCM, PCA or Agriculture) and original academic certificates at reporting. Check Rajasthan JET or University Application Window: Register through the official Mewar University, Chittorgarh admissions portal (https://www.mewaruniversity.org/pages/Agriculture_and_Veterinary_Science.aspx) or select the institution during Rajasthan JET / Pre-PG choice filling. Submit Academic & Entrance Score Details: Provide Class X and XII mark sheets (or B.Sc. Agriculture transcript for PG), JET/CUET/University entrance scorecard and category certificate. Document Verification & Tuition Fee Confirmation: Verify original documents on campus at Chittorgarh and obtain the itemised semester/annual fee receipt including hostel and examination heads.

What practical learning is described at MU Chittorgarh?

Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE) in adopted villages during the final year. Experiential Learning Programme (ELP) modules in organic inputs, nursery management, Mushroom cultivation and seed production. Field visits to Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs), state agricultural research stations and agri-input processing plants in Rajasthan.

Which teaching facilities are documented at MU Chittorgarh?

Agronomy & Instructional Crop Farm: Field demonstration plots at Chittorgarh for kharif and rabi crop trials, seed bed preparation, weed management and micro-irrigation exercises. Soil Science & Agricultural Chemistry Lab: Equipped for soil pH, electrical conductivity, organic carbon, macronutrient (N-P-K) and micronutrient testing. Horticulture, Polyhouse & Orchard Unit: Protected cultivation polyhouse/shade-net house, fruit orchard nursery and post-harvest handling practical space. Plant Pathology, Entomology & Genetics Labs: Compound and stereo microscopy, insect rearing cages, disease specimen collection and seed purity testing equipment. Student Hostels, Library & Transport: Separate boys and girls residential hostels at Chittorgarh, agricultural library journals, mess and city bus connectivity.

What student facilities are documented for MU Chittorgarh?

Student Hostels, Library & Transport: Separate boys and girls residential hostels at Chittorgarh, agricultural library journals, mess and city bus connectivity.

What scholarship information is available for MU Chittorgarh?

Mewar University, Chittorgarh offers merit-based tuition scholarships based on Class XII / qualifying examination marks or JET rank, alongside Rajasthan government post-matric scholarships (SJMS / Samaj Kalyan Vibhag) for eligible SC, ST, OBC, MBC and EBC students. University Entrance & Qualifying Marks Merit Scholarship (2026-27): Tuition fee waiver slabs (10% to 50% in first year) for applicants scoring 75%+ to 90%+ in 10+2 or high rank in Rajasthan JET / CUET; continuation requires maintaining minimum SGPA and 75% attendance. Rajasthan State Post-Matric & Girl Child Agriculture Incentive: Eligible Rajasthan domicile students can apply on the Rajasthan SSO Scholarship Portal; girl students in agriculture may also check state Rajshree / Agriculture Department incentive schemes.

What training and outcome evidence is available for MU Chittorgarh?

The placement and training cell at Mewar University, Chittorgarh coordinates final-year agribusiness agro-industrial attachments, KVK internships and preparation for Rajasthan Agriculture Supervisor, AAO, Pre-PG, ICAR JRF and IBPS AFO competitive examinations. Graduates pursue roles in seed, fertilizer, crop-protection, micro-irrigation, agri-fintech, warehousing and rural banking sectors, as well as M.Sc. Agriculture through Rajasthan Pre-PG and CUET-PG.
