FAQs: questions answered

When did PGPCAS start?

The official college home page states 2013.

How long is its agriculture degree?

The college specifies four years and eight semesters, with English as the medium of instruction.

Is TNAU registration needed for management admission?

The college’s 2026–27 page says it is compulsory for management applicants as well as counselling applicants.

Are the listed June 2026 dates current deadlines?

They are published dates for the 2026–27 cycle. Because the check occurred in October, they should be read as historical rather than an invitation to apply.

Is the diploma also covered by the audited ICAR row?

The college offers a diploma, but the audited row explicitly lists B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture. Diploma accreditation is not inferred.

What entry qualification is published for PGPCAS?

The official college admission page requires the applicable TNAU eligibility norms. Current subject combinations, category thresholds and equivalence rules were not independently verified.

What residential or welfare facilities does PGPCAS describe?

PGPCAS describes separate accommodation for male and female students, with ventilated rooms and regular maintenance. A current room-allocation and residential fee statement is still needed.

Which named academic staff are listed for PGPCAS?

PGPCAS lists faculty by department and identifies heads for several subject groups. Selected teaching entries below cover management, improvement, protection, horticulture and social science. Dr. M. Which university is named as the affiliating institution for PGPCAS?

Tamil Nadu Agricultural University, Coimbatore is the affiliating institution named in this profile.

Where is the official application information for PGPCAS?

https://pgpagricollege.ac.in/admission/. Read the programme, campus and applicable intake instructions on this official channel.

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at PGPCAS?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture. The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at PGPCAS?

4 years (8 semesters). The four-year, eight-semester curriculum covers agronomy, plant protection, soil science and agricultural economics. Published practical examples include sowing, seed treatment, nursery work, crop diagnosis and farm machinery.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at PGPCAS?

The official college admission page requires the applicable TNAU eligibility norms. Current subject combinations, category thresholds and equivalence rules were not independently verified.

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at PGPCAS?

A verified numerical fee is not available. A dated official fee schedule with tuition, compulsory charges and residential costs was not verified.

How does admission to PGPCAS work?

PGPCAS publishes a 65% TNAU single-window and 35% management split. Its 2026–27 page says applicants seeking management seats must first register with TNAU, then contact the college. These instructions need confirmation for a later cycle. Complete TNAU registration: The college states that online submission to TNAU is mandatory for counselling and management channels. Follow the relevant channel: For counselling, allocation follows rank and preferences. For management admission, the page asks registered applicants to contact the college directly.

What is the recorded ICAR scope for PGPCAS?

PGP College of Agricultural Sciences, Namakkal, Tamil Nadu: • B.Sc. (Hons) Agriculture (Intake- 120), recorded term 2024-06-20 to 2029-06-19. This entry applies to the named unit and programme.

What practical learning is described at PGPCAS?

The curriculum lists seed treatment, tillage and sowing exercises. Later-semester descriptions include specimen identification, seed-processing practice and machinery demonstrations.

Which teaching facilities are documented at PGPCAS?

Agricultural field setting: The college describes farmland around its Namakkal campus and additional training land at Paramathi. Separate student hostels: PGPCAS describes separate accommodation for male and female students, with ventilated rooms and regular maintenance. A current room-allocation and residential fee statement is still needed.

What student facilities are documented for PGPCAS?

Separate student hostels: PGPCAS describes separate accommodation for male and female students, with ventilated rooms and regular maintenance. A current room-allocation and residential fee statement is still needed.
