How does admission to SU Dausa work?

Admission to agriculture programmes at Shyam University, Dausa is conducted through Rajasthan JET / University Merit Admission, alongside direct university merit counselling for eligible 10+2 Agriculture/Science candidates. Both Rajasthan JET state counselling seats and university-level seats require verification of Class XII subject combinations (PCB, PCM, PCA or Agriculture) and original academic certificates at reporting. Check Rajasthan JET or University Application Window: Register through the official Shyam University, Dausa admissions portal (https://shyamuniversity.in/agriculture/) or select the institution during Rajasthan JET / Pre-PG choice filling. Submit Academic & Entrance Score Details: Provide Class X and XII mark sheets (or B.Sc. Agriculture transcript for PG), JET/CUET/University entrance scorecard and category certificate. Document Verification & Tuition Fee Confirmation: Verify original documents on campus at Dausa and obtain the itemised semester/annual fee receipt including hostel and examination heads.
