FAQs: questions answered

What is the B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture fee at Shridhar University, Pilani for 2026-27?

The published annual tuition fee for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Shridhar University, Pilani is INR 60,000 per year. Confirm hostel, examination and registration charges separately.

How can I get admission to B.Sc. Agriculture at Shridhar University, Pilani?

Admission is offered through Rajasthan JET / Shridhar University Merit for candidates who have passed 10+2 with Agriculture, PCB, PCM or PCA.

Where is the agriculture campus of Shridhar University, Pilani located?

The Department of Agriculture is located at Pilani-Chirawa Road, Pilani, Jhunjhunu, Rajasthan 333031, India.

What practical farm facilities are available at Shridhar University, Pilani?

Shekhawati arid-zone instructional farm on Pilani-Chirawa Road focusing on dryland farming, pearl millet, gram, mustard and drip-irrigated horticulture.

Does Shridhar University, Pilani offer M.Sc. Agriculture or postgraduate courses?

Covered programmes include: B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture (4 years); M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) (2 years).

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at SU Pilani?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy). The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at SU Pilani?

4 years; study format: Full-time on campus. B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Shridhar University, Pilani is delivered on campus through the Department of Agriculture, combining classroom agronomy, laboratory practicals and field crop rotations.

How long is M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at SU Pilani?

2 years; study format: Full-time on campus. M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at Shridhar University, Pilani is delivered on campus through the Department of Agriculture, combining classroom agronomy, laboratory practicals and field crop rotations.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at SU Pilani?

10+2 with Agriculture or Science (PCB / PCM) with minimum 45% marks.

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at SU Pilani?

4-year B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture from a recognized university.

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at SU Pilani?

₹60,000 / year · 2026-27. Published annual tuition fee for the 2026-27 session; confirm category or JET quota fee schedule before fee payment. Included charges: Published annual academic tuition fee; hostel, examination and caution money are billed separately.

What fee is documented for M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at SU Pilani?

₹58,000 / year · 2026-27. Published annual tuition fee for the 2026-27 session; confirm category or JET quota fee schedule before fee payment. Included charges: Published annual academic tuition fee; hostel, examination and caution money are billed separately.

How does admission to SU Pilani work?

Admission to agriculture programmes at Shridhar University, Pilani is conducted through Rajasthan JET / Shridhar University Merit, alongside direct university merit counselling for eligible 10+2 Agriculture/Science candidates. Both Rajasthan JET state counselling seats and university-level seats require verification of Class XII subject combinations (PCB, PCM, PCA or Agriculture) and original academic certificates at reporting. Check Rajasthan JET or University Application Window: Register through the official Shridhar University, Pilani admissions portal (https://www.shridharuniversity.ac.in/department-of-agriculture/) or select the institution during Rajasthan JET / Pre-PG choice filling. Submit Academic & Entrance Score Details: Provide Class X and XII mark sheets (or B.Sc. Agriculture transcript for PG), JET/CUET/University entrance scorecard and category certificate. Document Verification & Tuition Fee Confirmation: Verify original documents on campus at Pilani and obtain the itemised semester/annual fee receipt including hostel and examination heads.

What is the recorded ICAR scope for SU Pilani?

Department of Agriculture, Shridhar University, Pilani: B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy), recorded term 2022-23 to 2026-27 (UGC / State University Act & Rajasthan JET participating faculty; check ICAR NAEAB cohort notice). This entry applies to the named unit and programme.

What practical learning is described at SU Pilani?

Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE) in adopted villages during the final year. Experiential Learning Programme (ELP) modules in organic inputs, nursery management, Mushroom cultivation and seed production. Field visits to Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs), state agricultural research stations and agri-input processing plants in Rajasthan.

Which teaching facilities are documented at SU Pilani?

Agronomy & Instructional Crop Farm: Field demonstration plots at Pilani for kharif and rabi crop trials, seed bed preparation, weed management and micro-irrigation exercises. Soil Science & Agricultural Chemistry Lab: Equipped for soil pH, electrical conductivity, organic carbon, macronutrient (N-P-K) and micronutrient testing. Horticulture, Polyhouse & Orchard Unit: Protected cultivation polyhouse/shade-net house, fruit orchard nursery and post-harvest handling practical space. Plant Pathology, Entomology & Genetics Labs: Compound and stereo microscopy, insect rearing cages, disease specimen collection and seed purity testing equipment. Student Hostels, Library & Transport: Separate boys and girls residential hostels at Pilani, agricultural library journals, mess and city bus connectivity.

What student facilities are documented for SU Pilani?

Student Hostels, Library & Transport: Separate boys and girls residential hostels at Pilani, agricultural library journals, mess and city bus connectivity.

What scholarship information is available for SU Pilani?

Shridhar University, Pilani offers merit-based tuition scholarships based on Class XII / qualifying examination marks or JET rank, alongside Rajasthan government post-matric scholarships (SJMS / Samaj Kalyan Vibhag) for eligible SC, ST, OBC, MBC and EBC students. University Entrance & Qualifying Marks Merit Scholarship (2026-27): Tuition fee waiver slabs (10% to 50% in first year) for applicants scoring 75%+ to 90%+ in 10+2 or high rank in Rajasthan JET / CUET; continuation requires maintaining minimum SGPA and 75% attendance. Rajasthan State Post-Matric & Girl Child Agriculture Incentive: Eligible Rajasthan domicile students can apply on the Rajasthan SSO Scholarship Portal; girl students in agriculture may also check state Rajshree / Agriculture Department incentive schemes.

What training and outcome evidence is available for SU Pilani?

The placement and training cell at Shridhar University, Pilani coordinates final-year agribusiness agro-industrial attachments, KVK internships and preparation for Rajasthan Agriculture Supervisor, AAO, Pre-PG, ICAR JRF and IBPS AFO competitive examinations. Graduates pursue roles in seed, fertilizer, crop-protection, micro-irrigation, agri-fintech, warehousing and rural banking sectors, as well as M.Sc. Agriculture through Rajasthan Pre-PG and CUET-PG.
