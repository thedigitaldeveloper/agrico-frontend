FAQs: questions answered

Is B.Sc. Agriculture the ICAR-accredited course in this checked SRMIST row?

The row explicitly prints B.Sc. (Hons.) Horticulture, not Agriculture. Broader accreditation requires separate regulator evidence.

Sources: [1]

Does SRMIST also offer B.Sc. Agriculture?

Yes. Official college materials identify a four-year agriculture degree introduced in 2019-20.

Sources: [2][3]

Where is the agricultural college campus?

Recent official materials locate it at Baburayanpettai near Acharapakkam in Chengalpattu district.

Sources: [2]

When did horticulture teaching begin?

The official 2026 workshop brochure states 2018-19.

Sources: [2]

Is the old application fee a current quotation?

No. The form specifying INR 1,200 is explicitly labelled 2024. Current fee and selection details must be confirmed.

Sources: [5]

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at SRMIST?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Horticulture; B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture. The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

Sources: [1][2][4]

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Horticulture at SRMIST?

4 years. A separate horticultural science degree, introduced in 2018-19 and explicitly printed in the ICAR row.

Sources: [1][2][4]

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at SRMIST?

4 years. A broader agricultural science degree introduced in 2019-20. Its availability does not extend the checked horticulture accreditation row.

Sources: [2][4]

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Horticulture at SRMIST?

2026 agriculture application page: recognised 10+2 with at least 50% aggregate. Accepted academic groups are Physics-Chemistry with Biology and/or Mathematics, Botany-Zoology, Forestry, Biology-Agriculture or Agriculture; the vocational route is Biology with Agricultural Practices, including theory and practicals.

Sources: [1][2][4]

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at SRMIST?

2026 page: 10+2 with one of seven published science/agriculture combinations, or the specified vocational stream, and 50% aggregate.

Sources: [2][4]

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at SRMIST?

A verified numerical fee is not available. A dated fee schedule for the intended admission year has not been verified.

How does admission to SRMIST work?

SRM has a dedicated Agricultural Sciences Application Form 2026, which gives programme-specific qualifying subjects and the 50% threshold. Its separately accessible older form is labelled 2024; the INR1,200 charge, selection wording and upload limits on that older page should not be treated as current rules. Confirm the course: Use the official programme contact to confirm that the listed degree is taking applications for your intended intake. Request the intake notice: Ask for the applicable selection rules, fee breakdown and document checklist before submitting an application.

Sources: [4][5]

What is the recorded ICAR scope for SRMIST?

SRM College of Agricultural Sciences, SRM Nagar: B.Sc. (Hons.) Horticulture (Intake- 90), recorded term 2024-02-08 to 2029-02-07. This entry applies to the named unit and programme.

Sources: [1]

What practical learning is described at SRMIST?

The college’s self-study report describes Rural Agricultural/Horticultural Work Experience. The 2024-25 brochure identifies training with agricultural GIS software.
