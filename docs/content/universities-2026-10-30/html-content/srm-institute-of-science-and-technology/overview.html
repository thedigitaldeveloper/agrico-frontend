Agriculture and horticulture at Baburayanpettai

SRMIST’s agricultural college provides separate four-year degrees in horticulture and agriculture. Its official 2026 workshop brochure places the campus at Vendhar Nagar, Baburayanpettai, near Acharapakkam. This location needs to be distinguished from the Kattankulathur wording used on an older application form and in the regulator’s recorded address.

The college’s self-study report describes a curriculum combining theory, laboratory work, field visits, experiential learning and rural agricultural or horticultural work experience. Applicants interested in fruit, vegetable and ornamental crops can compare the horticulture route with the broader agriculture degree. Both are advertised offerings; the specific ICAR row checked here names horticulture alone.
