Courses & Fees: questions answered

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at RNBGU?

₹55,000 / semester · 2026. The official 2026 schedule lists outside-Rajasthan tuition ₹45,000 plus examination ₹10,000 per semester; Rajasthan domicile tuition ₹35,000 plus examination ₹10,000, total ₹45,000. Non-refundable enrolment ₹10,000 is one time. Accommodation is separate. Included charges: Outside-Rajasthan programme page: ₹45,000 tuition and ₹10,000 examination fee.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at RNBGU?

4 years. An honours agriculture degree with rural work, industry exposure and production projects.

What scholarship information is available for RNBGU?

The 2026 policy includes agriculture within its undergraduate scholarship grouping. Awards are limited to tuition; attendance, grades, backlogs and conduct affect continuation. Undergraduate merit scholarships, 2026: The official 2026 policy sets achievement-based categories for its agriculture grouping. Confirm the award and exam-validity conditions; examination and enrolment charges are excluded from scholarship coverage.

How can I contact RNBGU about an application?

Published phone: 0151 3507600. Published email: admissions@rnbglobal.edu.in. Official admissions: https://www.rnbglobal.edu.in/admission-procedure

Why does domicile matter when checking fees?

The university publishes separate agriculture course pages for Rajasthan-domicile and outside-Rajasthan applicants.

What does the 2026 outside-Rajasthan agriculture fee include?

The official schedule totals ₹55,000 per semester: ₹45,000 tuition and ₹10,000 examination fee. Non-refundable enrolment is ₹10,000 once; accommodation is separate.
