Agriculture study with rural and enterprise exposure

RNBGU’s agriculture school describes a curriculum that connects classroom subjects with farms and commercial activity. Its published pedagogy includes rural agricultural work experience, skill and entrepreneurship modules, and demonstrations involving crop production, nurseries and horticultural crops. The programme page lists a four-year bachelor’s degree.

The university was established under a Rajasthan act in 2015. Its agriculture course pages distinguish Rajasthan-domicile applicants from those outside the state. Applicants should therefore compare the correct category, tuition, examination fee and one-time enrolment charges. The university also publishes a dated 2026 scholarship policy, which specifies that awards apply to tuition and have academic continuation conditions.
