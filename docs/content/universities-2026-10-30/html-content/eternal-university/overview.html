Women’s agriculture education at Baru Sahib

Eternal University is based in the Baru Valley of Himachal Pradesh and is governed by The Kalgidhar Trust. Its registration portal explicitly restricts programmes to female applicants. This is a material admission condition for families considering agriculture at the university.

Dr. Khem Singh Gill Akal College of Agriculture was established within the university in 2014. The college’s published curriculum covers agronomy, horticulture, soils, crop improvement and protection, livestock production and food-related subjects. It also publishes laboratory manuals and a named academic roster. Applicants can assess how these subjects connect to their goals while confirming the current practical timetable, residential costs and entry-year eligibility directly.
