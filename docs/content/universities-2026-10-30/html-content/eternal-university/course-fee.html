What scholarship information is available for EU?

Eternal’s published tuition-concession page contains a 2024–25 policy for newly admitted undergraduate and postgraduate students. It uses qualifying marks to set a first-semester tuition concession and separately describes certain family/category waivers. The session is historical; applicants need the policy for their intended intake. Historical 2024–25 first-semester merit tuition concession: The published 2024–25 matrix gives 75% tuition concession for 95%and above,50% for 90–below 95,30% for 80–below 90, and 10% for 70–below 80, in the first semester for new UG/PG admissions, subject to the stated fee-payment condition. This is not a current-session scholarship promise.
