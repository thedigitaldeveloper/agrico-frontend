What is documented about the setting?

How could the programme shape your week?

Read the actual practical components for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture alongside the academic timetable. Field work, laboratory sessions or clinical teaching may affect daily travel and preparation; confirm the arrangements rather than inferring them from a campus photograph.

  • Rural Agricultural Work Experience is listed as a curriculum component involving village and farmer interaction.
  • Student READY is described through internships, skill modules and experiential units.
