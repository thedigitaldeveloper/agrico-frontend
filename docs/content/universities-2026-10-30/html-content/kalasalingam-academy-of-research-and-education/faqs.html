Are verified agriculture placement figures available?

The NIRF 2026 agriculture/allied submission reports UG four-year programmes (2024-2025): 66 placed from 119 graduates in minimum time, with ₹3,50,000 median annual salary among those placed. These are historical group-level outcomes, not specialisation-level placement promises.

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture. The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at KARE?

8 semesters (2022 curriculum). The 2022 curriculum covers crop science, agricultural resources and extension.

Sources: [1][3][4]

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at KARE?

Official admissions page: 10+2 science with relevant subjects; course page additionally states 50% and specified combinations. Obtain the intake-year rule.

Sources: [1][3][4]

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at KARE?

₹1,20,000 / year · 2025-2026. Historical official table also lists one-time admission fee ₹10,000, other fee ₹12,000 and application cost ₹600. Not verified as 2026-2027 fees. Included charges: Annual programme fee for resident Indian B.Sc. Agriculture applicants.

Sources: [4]

How does admission to KARE work?

The university publishes agriculture-specific eligibility and selection information within its admissions page. The course and central pages differ in detail, so request the applying-year subject and marks requirements. Check the agriculture eligibility row: Match the 10+2 subjects with the published agriculture requirements. Confirm the selection notice: Ask for the current KCEE, counselling and fee instructions before committing funds.

Sources: [2][4]

What practical learning is described at KARE?

The published curriculum includes agricultural field and laboratory subjects.

Which teaching facilities are documented at KARE?

Kalasalingam Anandammal Farm: The course page identifies a fertigation unit at the teaching farm. Farm implements: Listed equipment includes a tractor, cultivator, disc plough and rotary tiller. Agriculture laboratories: The page identifies soil, seed, crop physiology, plant protection and genetics laboratories.

What student facilities are documented for KARE?

The course page documents field equipment and laboratories used for agricultural teaching. Accommodation availability, meal charges and transport arrangements for your entry year still need direct confirmation.

What scholarship information is available for KARE?

KARE’s admissions page includes scholarship material, but its scheme tables cover multiple programme groups. A current award and renewal rule specifically applicable to B.Sc. Agriculture was not verified.

What training and outcome evidence is available for KARE?

The university's NIRF 2026 Agriculture and Allied Sectors submission reports graduates, placements, higher-study progression and the median annual salary of placed graduates for the cohorts below. The figures apply to the reported programme-duration group, rather than an individual specialisation, and do not predict an applicant's future outcome.
