Agriculture teaching in the Krishnankoil setting

KARE’s published agriculture curriculum links crop production and improvement with natural resources, agricultural economics and extension. The 2022 syllabus provides a detailed academic reference for prospective students who want to examine course content beyond a programme title.

The Kalasalingam Anandammal Farm gives the school a practical setting for crop work. The course page identifies fertigation equipment and implements including a tractor, cultivator, disc plough and rotary tiller, alongside nine subject laboratories. Together, these facilities connect the curriculum’s biological and resource-management subjects with cultivation and machinery. KARE’s institutional history begins with an engineering college in 1984 and records university status from 2006.
