An affiliated agriculture college at Kapasan

RNT is a college profile, with a specific undergraduate agricultural focus. Its official homepage identifies affiliation to Maharana Pratap University of Agriculture and Technology, Udaipur. The course page lists B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture for applicants with 10+2 Agriculture or Science and gives the duration as eight semesters.

The published syllabus carries MPUAT’s agriculture-faculty framework and the Fifth Deans’ Committee curriculum. Early study includes soils, horticulture, agronomy, genetics and agricultural economics. Later semesters identify rural agricultural work experience, agro-industrial attachment and skill-development modules. Applicants should ask the college for the current cohort’s detailed timetable and module choices rather than treating every listed option as guaranteed to run.
