What is documented about the setting?

The syllabus places Rural Agricultural Work Experience and Agro-Industrial Attachment in the seventh semester. Its final-semester list includes practical enterprise modules; the checked farm webpage itself contains no detailed facility inventory.

How could the programme shape your week?

Read the actual practical components for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture alongside the academic timetable. Field work, laboratory sessions or clinical teaching may affect daily travel and preparation; confirm the arrangements rather than inferring them from a campus photograph.

  • Seventh-semester RAWE and agro-industrial attachment are in the published syllabus.
  • The syllabus lists enterprise options including seed production, beekeeping, mushroom cultivation and nursery management.

Campus Life: questions answered

Where is RNT located?

RNT College of Agriculture, Kapasan, Chittorgarh district, Rajasthan, India

What student facilities are documented for RNT?

The syllabus places Rural Agricultural Work Experience and Agro-Industrial Attachment in the seventh semester. Its final-semester list includes practical enterprise modules; the checked farm webpage itself contains no detailed facility inventory. Accommodation availability, meal charges and transport arrangements for your entry year still need direct confirmation.

What practical learning is described at RNT?

Seventh-semester RAWE and agro-industrial attachment are in the published syllabus. The syllabus lists enterprise options including seed production, beekeeping, mushroom cultivation and nursery management.
