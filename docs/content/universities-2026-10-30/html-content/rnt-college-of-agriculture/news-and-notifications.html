News & Notices: questions answered

Which dated admission notices are recorded for RNT?

No dated admission event is verified in this profile. The official channel is https://rntagcollege.edu.in/Onlineadm.html; check the notice for the programme and entry year you intend to join.

How does admission to RNT work?

RNT publishes a course table and an official contact page. Its linked fee page timed out during research, and a dated 2026-27 admission notice was not verified. The college should confirm applicable MPUAT or counselling rules. Confirm the course: Use the official programme contact to confirm that the listed degree is taking applications for your intended intake. Request the intake notice: Ask for the applicable selection rules, fee breakdown and document checklist before submitting an application.

How can I contact RNT about an application?

Published phone: +91 74148 70774. Published email: ag.dept@rntagcollege.edu.in. Official admissions: https://rntagcollege.edu.in/Onlineadm.html
