Career support and reported outcomes

The placement and training cell at Career Point University, Kota coordinates final-year agribusiness agro-industrial attachments, KVK internships and preparation for Rajasthan Agriculture Supervisor, AAO, Pre-PG, ICAR JRF and IBPS AFO competitive examinations.

  • Graduates pursue roles in seed, fertilizer, crop-protection, micro-irrigation, agri-fintech, warehousing and rural banking sectors, as well as M.Sc. Agriculture through Rajasthan Pre-PG and CUET-PG.

Learning experiences connected with preparation

Hadoti region instructional agricultural farm at Alaniya (Kota) supporting soybean, mustard, coriander and citrus cultivation trials alongside soil health and plant pathology laboratories.

  • Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE) in adopted villages during the final year.
  • Experiential Learning Programme (ELP) modules in organic inputs, nursery management, Mushroom cultivation and seed production.
  • Field visits to Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs), state agricultural research stations and agri-input processing plants in Rajasthan.

How to assess a placement claim

For B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy), ask for a report that matches the course, campus and graduating batch. Compare outcomes on the same basis rather than using an isolated maximum package.

Evidence to requestWhat to read
Cohort and reporting yearWhich programme, campus and graduating batch the report covers
Eligible students and offersHow the institution defines participation and reported placement
Roles and employersWhether the work and qualification requirements match the degree
Salary descriptionWhether a quoted figure is median, average, highest or another measure
Internship versus employmentWhether an experience is curricular training, a paid internship or a final job offer

Training & Placements: questions answered

What training and outcome evidence is available for CPU Kota?

The placement and training cell at Career Point University, Kota coordinates final-year agribusiness agro-industrial attachments, KVK internships and preparation for Rajasthan Agriculture Supervisor, AAO, Pre-PG, ICAR JRF and IBPS AFO competitive examinations. Graduates pursue roles in seed, fertilizer, crop-protection, micro-irrigation, agri-fintech, warehousing and rural banking sectors, as well as M.Sc. Agriculture through Rajasthan Pre-PG and CUET-PG.

What practical learning is described at CPU Kota?

Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE) in adopted villages during the final year. Experiential Learning Programme (ELP) modules in organic inputs, nursery management, Mushroom cultivation and seed production. Field visits to Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs), state agricultural research stations and agri-input processing plants in Rajasthan.

Which employers or training organisations are named for CPU Kota?

Agri-input, seed & crop-care companies across Rajasthan and North India; Micro-irrigation, solar pump & farm-mechanisation enterprises; Agri-warehousing, organic certification & rural banking / microfinance institutions. Read the stated programme and reporting scope in training and placements.

Which teaching facilities are documented at CPU Kota?

Agronomy & Instructional Crop Farm: Field demonstration plots at Kota for kharif and rabi crop trials, seed bed preparation, weed management and micro-irrigation exercises. Soil Science & Agricultural Chemistry Lab: Equipped for soil pH, electrical conductivity, organic carbon, macronutrient (N-P-K) and micronutrient testing. Horticulture, Polyhouse & Orchard Unit: Protected cultivation polyhouse/shade-net house, fruit orchard nursery and post-harvest handling practical space. Plant Pathology, Entomology & Genetics Labs: Compound and stereo microscopy, insect rearing cages, disease specimen collection and seed purity testing equipment. Student Hostels, Library & Transport: Separate boys and girls residential hostels at Kota, agricultural library journals, mess and city bus connectivity.
