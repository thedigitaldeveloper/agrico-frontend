FAQs: questions answered

What is the B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture fee at Career Point University, Kota for 2026-27?

The published annual tuition fee for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Career Point University, Kota is INR 76,000 per year. Confirm hostel, examination and registration charges separately.

How can I get admission to B.Sc. Agriculture at Career Point University, Kota?

Admission is offered through Rajasthan JET / CUET / CPU Merit Counselling for candidates who have passed 10+2 with Agriculture, PCB, PCM or PCA.

Where is the agriculture campus of Career Point University, Kota located?

The School of Agricultural Sciences is located at National Highway 52, Opp. Alaniya Mata Ji Mandir, Alaniya, Kota, Rajasthan 325003, India.

What practical farm facilities are available at Career Point University, Kota?

Hadoti region instructional agricultural farm at Alaniya (Kota) supporting soybean, mustard, coriander and citrus cultivation trials alongside soil health and plant pathology laboratories.

Does Career Point University, Kota offer M.Sc. Agriculture or postgraduate courses?

Covered programmes include: B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture (4 years); M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) (2 years).

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at CPU Kota?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy). The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at CPU Kota?

4 years; study format: Full-time on campus. B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Career Point University, Kota is delivered on campus through the School of Agricultural Sciences, combining classroom agronomy, laboratory practicals and field crop rotations.

How long is M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at CPU Kota?

2 years; study format: Full-time on campus. M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at Career Point University, Kota is delivered on campus through the School of Agricultural Sciences, combining classroom agronomy, laboratory practicals and field crop rotations.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at CPU Kota?

10+2 in Science (PCB / PCM / Agriculture) from a recognized board with minimum 50% marks.

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at CPU Kota?

B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture (4-year programme) with at least 55% aggregate marks.

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at CPU Kota?

₹76,000 / year · 2026-27. Published annual tuition fee for the 2026-27 session; confirm category or JET quota fee schedule before fee payment. Included charges: Published annual academic tuition fee; hostel, examination and caution money are billed separately.

What fee is documented for M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at CPU Kota?

₹72,000 / year · 2026-27. Published annual tuition fee for the 2026-27 session; confirm category or JET quota fee schedule before fee payment. Included charges: Published annual academic tuition fee; hostel, examination and caution money are billed separately.

How does admission to CPU Kota work?

Admission to agriculture programmes at Career Point University, Kota is conducted through Rajasthan JET / CUET / CPU Merit Counselling, alongside direct university merit counselling for eligible 10+2 Agriculture/Science candidates. Both Rajasthan JET state counselling seats and university-level seats require verification of Class XII subject combinations (PCB, PCM, PCA or Agriculture) and original academic certificates at reporting. Check Rajasthan JET or University Application Window: Register through the official Career Point University, Kota admissions portal (https://cpur.in/schools/school-of-agricultural-sciences/) or select the institution during Rajasthan JET / Pre-PG choice filling. Submit Academic & Entrance Score Details: Provide Class X and XII mark sheets (or B.Sc. Agriculture transcript for PG), JET/CUET/University entrance scorecard and category certificate. Document Verification & Tuition Fee Confirmation: Verify original documents on campus at Kota and obtain the itemised semester/annual fee receipt including hostel and examination heads.

What practical learning is described at CPU Kota?

Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE) in adopted villages during the final year. Experiential Learning Programme (ELP) modules in organic inputs, nursery management, Mushroom cultivation and seed production. Field visits to Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs), state agricultural research stations and agri-input processing plants in Rajasthan.

Which teaching facilities are documented at CPU Kota?

Agronomy & Instructional Crop Farm: Field demonstration plots at Kota for kharif and rabi crop trials, seed bed preparation, weed management and micro-irrigation exercises. Soil Science & Agricultural Chemistry Lab: Equipped for soil pH, electrical conductivity, organic carbon, macronutrient (N-P-K) and micronutrient testing. Horticulture, Polyhouse & Orchard Unit: Protected cultivation polyhouse/shade-net house, fruit orchard nursery and post-harvest handling practical space. Plant Pathology, Entomology & Genetics Labs: Compound and stereo microscopy, insect rearing cages, disease specimen collection and seed purity testing equipment. Student Hostels, Library & Transport: Separate boys and girls residential hostels at Kota, agricultural library journals, mess and city bus connectivity.

What student facilities are documented for CPU Kota?

Student Hostels, Library & Transport: Separate boys and girls residential hostels at Kota, agricultural library journals, mess and city bus connectivity.

What scholarship information is available for CPU Kota?

Career Point University, Kota offers merit-based tuition scholarships based on Class XII / qualifying examination marks or JET rank, alongside Rajasthan government post-matric scholarships (SJMS / Samaj Kalyan Vibhag) for eligible SC, ST, OBC, MBC and EBC students. University Entrance & Qualifying Marks Merit Scholarship (2026-27): Tuition fee waiver slabs (10% to 50% in first year) for applicants scoring 75%+ to 90%+ in 10+2 or high rank in Rajasthan JET / CUET; continuation requires maintaining minimum SGPA and 75% attendance. Rajasthan State Post-Matric & Girl Child Agriculture Incentive: Eligible Rajasthan domicile students can apply on the Rajasthan SSO Scholarship Portal; girl students in agriculture may also check state Rajshree / Agriculture Department incentive schemes.

What training and outcome evidence is available for CPU Kota?

The placement and training cell at Career Point University, Kota coordinates final-year agribusiness agro-industrial attachments, KVK internships and preparation for Rajasthan Agriculture Supervisor, AAO, Pre-PG, ICAR JRF and IBPS AFO competitive examinations. Graduates pursue roles in seed, fertilizer, crop-protection, micro-irrigation, agri-fintech, warehousing and rural banking sectors, as well as M.Sc. Agriculture through Rajasthan Pre-PG and CUET-PG.
