The documented academic setting

The agronomy department reports practical training alongside classroom teaching. Its extension activities include workshops, field visits and demonstrations intended to connect university knowledge with farming practice.

Sources: [2][5][10]

Facilities identified in the sources

Faculty teaching laboratories

The faculty lists soil and water conservation, farm machinery, irrigation and remote-sensing laboratories; several support agricultural-engineering teaching.

Sources: [10]

Field and protected-cultivation training

An experimental learning field, micro-irrigation training setup and polyhouse appear in the faculty list.

Sources: [10]

Food-processing incubation

The faculty identifies a food-processing and preservation incubation centre.

Practical learning and student access

For B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. (Agriculture) Agronomy, the usefulness of a facility depends on actual teaching access, supervision and the programme timetable. The documented learning activities are listed below; ask the department about your own cohort’s arrangements.

  • Department-led farmer training and demonstrations are described on the agronomy page.
  • The B.Sc. curriculum assigns practical credit to subjects including soil science, crop physiology and entomology.
