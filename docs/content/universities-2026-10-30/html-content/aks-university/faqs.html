FAQs: questions answered

Which AKS agriculture degree appears in the ICAR register?

The audited entry names B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture for 11 October 2022 to 10 October 2027.

How long is the undergraduate agriculture course?

The university admissions listing gives four years.

Does AKS offer postgraduate agronomy?

Yes. Its agronomy department lists a two-year M.Sc. requiring a B.Sc. Agriculture qualification.

What does the agriculture curriculum cover?

The published course structure includes crops, soils, horticulture, plant health and agricultural economics, with theory and practical credits.

What is the B.Sc. Agriculture fee for 2026-27?

The official table gives INR 55,000 per semester before its General/OBC scholarship; that column shows INR 48,000 payable. Registration is separate.

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at AKSU?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. (Agriculture) Agronomy. The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at AKSU?

4 years. The published curriculum introduces soil science, agronomy, horticulture, genetics, plant pathology and agricultural economics.

How long is M.Sc. (Agriculture) Agronomy at AKSU?

2 years (4 semesters). Specialist study builds on an agriculture degree and focuses on crop production and management.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at AKSU?

2026-27: 12th PCM/PCB/Agriculture stream with at least 60%.

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. (Agriculture) Agronomy at AKSU?

2026-27: B.Sc. Agriculture with at least 60%, including RAWE and experiential work.

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at AKSU?

₹55,000 / semester · 2026-27. Base fee. Published General/OBC university scholarship reduces this to INR 48,000; category conditions and additional charges need confirmation. Non-refundable registration INR 4,900. Included charges: Published tuition/course fee; additional charges must be checked separately.

What fee is documented for M.Sc. (Agriculture) Agronomy at AKSU?

₹42,000 / semester · 2026-27. Base fee. Published General/OBC scholarship reduces this to INR 40,000; registration INR 4,900 separately. Included charges: Published tuition/course fee; additional charges must be checked separately.

How does admission to AKSU work?

AKS links an online admission portal and publishes a 2026-27 fee-and-eligibility table. Applicants can compare the base semester fee with the university’s category-specific scholarship columns before requesting their final offer. Check the course requirements: Read the published agriculture qualification and any selection requirement under the university ordinance. Complete the application: AKS accepts its online form or an offline form obtained through the admissions office. Submit the required records: Provide qualifying records and identity/transfer/character documents from the official common checklist. Confirm admission and enrolment: After the admissions section issues confirmation, complete the specified payment, registration and enrolment formalities.

What is the recorded ICAR scope for AKSU?

AKS University, Satna, M.P.: B. Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture (Intake- 240), recorded term 2022-10-11 to 2027-10-10. This entry applies to the named unit and programme.

What practical learning is described at AKSU?

Department-led farmer training and demonstrations are described on the agronomy page. The B.Sc. curriculum assigns practical credit to subjects including soil science, crop physiology and entomology.

Which teaching facilities are documented at AKSU?

Faculty teaching laboratories: The faculty lists soil and water conservation, farm machinery, irrigation and remote-sensing laboratories; several support agricultural-engineering teaching. Field and protected-cultivation training: An experimental learning field, micro-irrigation training setup and polyhouse appear in the faculty list. Food-processing incubation: The faculty identifies a food-processing and preservation incubation centre.

What student facilities are documented for AKSU?

The agronomy department reports practical training alongside classroom teaching. Its extension activities include workshops, field visits and demonstrations intended to connect university knowledge with farming practice. Accommodation availability, meal charges and transport arrangements for your entry year still need direct confirmation.

What scholarship information is available for AKSU?

The 2026-27 table gives a General/OBC university scholarship of INR 7,000 per semester for B.Sc. Agriculture and INR 2,000 for M.Sc. Agronomy. Confirm award and continuation conditions before relying on the reduced payable fee. University scholarship: General/OBC fee table: Published semester reductions for the listed agriculture courses; individual eligibility is subject to university confirmation.

What training and outcome evidence is available for AKSU?

The agronomy department describes campus recruitment, career counselling and preparation for interviews and CV writing. These are support activities; the checked page does not supply a dated agriculture placement rate or salary distribution.
