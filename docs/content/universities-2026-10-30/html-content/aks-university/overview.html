Crop science within a wider agricultural faculty

The undergraduate agriculture course at AKS sits within a faculty that also publishes curricula for agricultural engineering, food technology and several postgraduate agricultural subjects. This allows applicants to distinguish a broad first degree from later specialist study rather than treating every agriculture-related course as the same qualification.

The agronomy department dates its establishment to 2014. Its published teaching and research interests include crop production, soil management and weed control. The department also describes farmer training and field demonstrations, giving a concrete extension context to academic work. Applicants can progress from the broad undergraduate degree to specialist agronomy study, subject to the published qualification and practical-work requirements.
