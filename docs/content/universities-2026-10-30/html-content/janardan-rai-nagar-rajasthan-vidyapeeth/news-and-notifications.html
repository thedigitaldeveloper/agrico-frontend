How does admission to JRNRVU work?

The school page directs applicants to a dedicated agriculture enquiry form. The landing page publishes course durations and annual fee figures but does not state the academic year of those prices. Confirm the applicable course notice with the school. Confirm the course: Use the official programme contact to confirm that the listed degree is taking applications for your intended intake. Request the intake notice: Ask for the applicable selection rules, fee breakdown and document checklist before submitting an application.
