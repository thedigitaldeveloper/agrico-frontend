FAQs: questions answered

Where does the School of Agricultural Sciences teach?

The official school page locates it at Dabok, near Udaipur. The university’s administrative contact address is a separate reference.

Is the B.Sc. Agriculture entry date-valid in the audited ICAR list?

Yes. The listed term runs from 11 October 2022 to 10 October 2027.

Can students study fruit science after a bachelor’s degree?

The school lists a two-year M.Sc. Horticulture in Fruit Science. Its current entrance conditions should be obtained separately.

Does the school list a business qualification?

Yes. A two-year MBA Agribusiness Management appears in the official school course list.

Can the annual fee on the enquiry page be treated as a 2026-27 quotation?

No. The page shows INR 84,000 annually for undergraduate agriculture without an academic year; a dated schedule is needed.

How does admission to JRNRVU work?

The school page directs applicants to a dedicated agriculture enquiry form. The landing page publishes course durations and annual fee figures but does not state the academic year of those prices.
