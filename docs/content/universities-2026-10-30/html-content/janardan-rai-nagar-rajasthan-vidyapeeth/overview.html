Agricultural education with a southern Rajasthan focus

The School of Agricultural Sciences is based at the university’s Dabok campus, rather than simply sharing the central administrative address in Udaipur. Its published teaching objectives address crop and pest management, preventing postharvest losses and taking agricultural knowledge to farming communities. These priorities provide useful context for applicants interested in extension as well as production science.

The official school page lists B.Sc. Agriculture, several M.Sc. Agriculture and Horticulture specialisations, an agribusiness MBA and doctoral study. These are distinct awards with different academic entry points. The published postgraduate list includes crop production, plant health, agricultural economics, extension and livestock management, together with fruit, vegetable and ornamental horticulture.
