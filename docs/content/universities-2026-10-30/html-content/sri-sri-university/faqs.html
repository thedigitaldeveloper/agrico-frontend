FAQs: questions answered

Does SSU’s ICAR entry cover all its allied degrees?

The audited row explicitly lists B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture. Horticulture and agribusiness are verified offerings but are not added to that accreditation scope.

What science subjects did SSU require for agriculture in 2025?

The NTA listing states PCB or PCM at 10+2 level with at least 50%. Later intake rules need confirmation.

Is there an agribusiness option for non-science students?

The NTA 2025 listing accepted any 10+2 stream with at least 50%, but SSU’s undated Agribusiness course fact sheet specifies science with PCB/PCM. Because those sources differ, a non-science applicant should obtain the intended intake rule directly.

What practical agricultural units are documented?

SSU reports farmland, an apiculture unit and mushroom production in its sustainability evidence.

Are the tuition figures on this page current intake fees?

The programme fact sheets quote INR85,000 for Agriculture, INR68,000 for Horticulture and INR63,000 for Agribusiness per semester, but do not identify an intake year. These are undated official amounts rather than confirmed 2026/2027 prices.

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at SSU?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; B.Sc. (Hons.) Horticulture; B.Sc. (Hons.) Agribusiness. The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at SSU?

4 years (8 semesters); study format: Full time. A broad agricultural science degree listed by the university and NTA.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Horticulture at SSU?

4 years (8 semesters); study format: Full time. An allied undergraduate option named in university sustainability reporting.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agribusiness at SSU?

4 years (8 semesters); study format: Full time. An agriculture-related business option distinct from the crop-science degree.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at SSU?

Undated official course fact sheet: 10+2 science with PCB or PCM and at least 50%; confirm the intended intake rule.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Horticulture at SSU?

Undated official course fact sheet: 10+2 science with PCB or PCM and at least 50%; confirm the intended intake rule.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agribusiness at SSU?

The undated official course fact sheet requires 10+2 science with PCB or PCM and at least 50%. This differs from the NTA 2025 listing, which accepted any 10+2 stream with 50%; obtain the intended intake rule.

How does admission to SSU work?

SSU’s admission guideline page describes online applications and a non-refundable INR 500 application charge for Indian nationals. Its displayed 2026-27 dates are internally inconsistent, so obtain the applicable selection notice and deadline directly. Apply through the university: The guidelines specify an online application and a separate form for each programme. Read the selection and offer communication: The university describes email notification of selection arrangements and a provisional offer identifying fees and required documents.

What is the recorded ICAR scope for SSU?

Sri Sri University, Cuttack, Odisha: B. Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture (Intake- 60), recorded term 2022-10-11 to 2027-10-10. This entry applies to the named unit and programme.

What practical learning is described at SSU?

University sustainability evidence describes 40 acres of agricultural land, apiculture and mushroom production. Its report associates farm output with campus kitchens and local food procurement.

Which teaching facilities are documented at SSU?

University farmland: The 2026 sustainability evidence describes fruits, vegetables and cereals grown on campus. Apiculture and mushroom units: Both units are identified in the university’s campus food-production account.

What student facilities are documented for SSU?

Hostel and mess support: The finance help page directs hostel and mess questions to officeofdirectoratestudentaffairs@srisriuniversity.edu.in. It describes optional booking subject to capacity; applicants need confirmation of an available room. Student grievance channel: The published student support channel is studentgrievance@srisriuniversity.edu.in. Individual dues and receipts are accessed through the university payment system.

What scholarship information is available for SSU?

SSU’s Scholarship Support Division helps its enrolled students find scholarship information and complete applications. Its published scope covers bona fide students across faculties. The checked page does not establish a dated agriculture-specific award amount or renewal entitlement. Scholarship application support: The division disseminates available scheme information and helps students complete scholarship processes. A support service does not itself guarantee an award.
