Agricultural study connected with campus food production

The Faculty of Agriculture offers more than one route into agricultural study. University sustainability reporting describes undergraduate agriculture, horticulture, agribusiness and food, nutrition and dietetics, together with postgraduate agricultural disciplines. Applicants should compare the science and business orientation of these qualifications before selecting a course.

The university reports that its farmland contributes produce to campus food services. A 2026 sustainability submission describes fruit, vegetable and cereal cultivation, together with apiculture and mushroom units. These activities place agricultural production within a working campus food system, while the different science and business degrees allow students to choose an emphasis suited to their interests.
