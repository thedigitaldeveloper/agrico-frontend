What scholarship information is available for SSU?

SSU’s Scholarship Support Division helps its enrolled students find scholarship information and complete applications. Its published scope covers bona fide students across faculties. The checked page does not establish a dated agriculture-specific award amount or renewal entitlement. Scholarship application support: The division disseminates available scheme information and helps students complete scholarship processes. A support service does not itself guarantee an award.

Sources: [13]

How can I contact SSU about an application?

Published phone: +91 78944 24562. Published email: admissions@srisriuniversity.edu.in. Official admissions: https://srisriuniversity.edu.in/admission-process/

Are the tuition figures on this page current intake fees?

The programme fact sheets quote INR85,000 for Agriculture, INR68,000 for Horticulture and INR63,000 for Agribusiness per semester, but do not identify an intake year. These are undated official amounts rather than confirmed 2026/2027 prices.
