What is documented about the setting?

University sustainability evidence describes 40 acres of agricultural land, apiculture and mushroom production. Its report associates farm output with campus kitchens and local food procurement.

Hostel and mess supportThe finance help page directs hostel and mess questions to officeofdirectoratestudentaffairs@srisriuniversity.edu.in. It describes optional booking subject to capacity; applicants need confirmation of an available room.
Student grievance channelThe published student support channel is studentgrievance@srisriuniversity.edu.in. Individual dues and receipts are accessed through the university payment system.
