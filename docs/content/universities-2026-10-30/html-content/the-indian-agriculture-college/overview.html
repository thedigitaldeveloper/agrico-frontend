Agricultural education in southern Tamil Nadu

The Indian Agriculture College is located on the Kavalkinaru–Radhapuram Road in Tirunelveli district. Established in 2016 under Joshua Educational and Charitable Trust, it focuses on agricultural education and the farming needs of southern Tamil Nadu.

Its four-year degree is organised over eight semesters. The course page covers agricultural science alongside surveying, soils, water management and allied subjects. The college also publishes campus material grouped around laboratories, agricultural fields, farms and industrial visits. Applicants can use those resources to identify which practical areas interest them and request the current timetable for field and laboratory learning.
