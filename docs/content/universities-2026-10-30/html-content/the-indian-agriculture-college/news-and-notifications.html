News & Notices: questions answered

Which dated admission notices are recorded for TIAC?

No dated admission event is verified in this profile. The official channel is https://tiac.rajas.edu/admission-open/; check the notice for the programme and entry year you intend to join.

How does admission to TIAC work?

TIAC publishes a four-year, eight-semester agriculture course page and category-based entry thresholds. Its online form is an application starting point; the current TNAU selection channel, deadline and itemised fees should be confirmed before submission. Read the college application form: The official page collects personal, parent and contact information and identifies the agriculture degree. Confirm formal admission requirements: Before submitting sensitive records, obtain the current TNAU-linked eligibility, selection channel, deadline and fee schedule from TIAC.

How can I contact TIAC about an application?

Published phone: +91 70944 34295. Published email: info@rajas.edu. Official admissions: https://tiac.rajas.edu/admission-open/
