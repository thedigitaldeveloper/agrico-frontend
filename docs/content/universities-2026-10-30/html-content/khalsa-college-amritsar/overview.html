Agriculture within a historic Amritsar college

Khalsa College was established in 1892 and is affiliated to Guru Nanak Dev University. Its agriculture department has a long academic history: the official department account records degree affiliation to Panjab University in 1931 and then GNDU in 1971, with postgraduate teaching developed in 1967.

The department describes dedicated agricultural teaching blocks and laboratories covering agronomy, crop protection, soil science, horticulture and agricultural economics. Its activity archive includes rural work experience, commercial beekeeping, mushroom cultivation and paddy-residue-management programmes. These dated examples help applicants understand the department’s practical orientation while leaving the current cohort’s exact activity calendar for direct confirmation.
