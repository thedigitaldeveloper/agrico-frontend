How does admission to Khalsa College work?

Khalsa College publishes a dedicated agriculture entrance-registration link alongside its 2026–27 admissions material. Its linked procedure and fee PDFs were image-only in the reading tool. Applicants should request accessible current copies before relying on a cutoff or payable amount. Register in the stated Punjab admission portal: The 2026–27 college document directs applicants to admission.punjab.gov.in and a separate new registration for UG orPG study. Complete academic and personal registration: Enter the requested details, board/university roll number and passing year; follow the document’s instructions if academic data is not retrieved automatically. Log in and submit the registration fee/form: Use the credentials sent to the registered phone/email, then complete the portal form and payment. Follow the agriculture-specific selection notice: The college document labels B.Sc. Agriculture admission as entrance-test/merit based and leaves actual dates to be notified later. Portal registration alone does not guarantee a seat.
