Agricultural study across production and plant-health disciplines

SGRRU places agriculture in a dedicated constituent school. Its departmental list includes agronomy, horticulture, seed science, soil science, entomology, plant pathology, genetics and plant breeding, and food technology. For an undergraduate applicant, this shows the range of scientific subjects surrounding the agriculture degree rather than a single crop specialism.

The B.Sc. course page describes an eight-semester programme with laboratory and farm learning. Its eligibility text accepts specified science, agriculture or horticulture backgrounds. A 2025 prospectus names an entrance examination, whereas the current webpage only says admission follows university norms. Applicants should request the rule for their intended intake rather than assuming that an older route is unchanged.
