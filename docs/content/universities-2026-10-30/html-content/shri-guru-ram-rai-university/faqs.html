FAQs: questions answered

How long is SGRRU’s agriculture degree?

The official programme page gives four years and eight semesters.

What minimum marks are shown?

The undated course page gives 45% at intermediate level with listed science/agriculture/horticulture subjects, with SC/ST conditions under university rules.

What practical enterprise units are named?

The programme account names an apiary, mushroom, vermicompost and biogas units, among other resources.

Does the checked ICAR row include the M.Sc. courses?

The row explicitly names B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; the verified postgraduate offerings are not added to that accreditation scope.

Who is named as the agriculture dean?

The official school page lists Prof. Priyanka Bankoti. The roster has no explicit appointment date.

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at SGRRU?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture — Agronomy; M.Sc. Agriculture — Horticulture. The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at SGRRU?

4 years (8 semesters). The undergraduate degree is delivered by the School of Agricultural Sciences with farm and laboratory components.

How long is M.Sc. Agriculture — Agronomy at SGRRU?

2 years. A postgraduate crop-management specialisation in the 2025 prospectus.

How long is M.Sc. Agriculture — Horticulture at SGRRU?

2 years. An allied postgraduate pathway in the published prospectus.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at SGRRU?

Undated official course page: intermediate with 45%, using PCB/PCM or Agriculture/Horticulture subjects; SC/ST as university rules.

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. Agriculture — Agronomy at SGRRU?

2025 prospectus: B.Sc. Agriculture with 45% aggregate; reconfirm for intended intake.

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. Agriculture — Horticulture at SGRRU?

2025 prospectus: B.Sc. Agriculture/Horticulture with 45% aggregate; reconfirm for intended intake.

Use the university’s official admission enquiry route for a current course notice. The 2025 prospectus and undated B.Sc. page give useful academic information, but a confirmed 2026-27 entrance and counselling schedule was not verified.
