News & Notices: questions answered

Which dated admission notices are recorded for SGRRU?

No dated admission event is verified in this profile. The official channel is https://admissions.sgrru.ac.in/; check the notice for the programme and entry year you intend to join.

Sources: [2][4]

How does admission to SGRRU work?

Use the university’s official admission enquiry route for a current course notice. The 2025 prospectus and undated B.Sc. page give useful academic information, but a confirmed 2026-27 entrance and counselling schedule was not verified. Confirm the course: Use the official programme contact to confirm that the listed degree is taking applications for your intended intake. Request the intake notice: Ask for the applicable selection rules, fee breakdown and document checklist before submitting an application.

Sources: [2][4]

How can I contact SGRRU about an application?

Published phone: 1800 120 102 102. Published email: info@sgrru.ac.in. Official admissions: https://admissions.sgrru.ac.in/
