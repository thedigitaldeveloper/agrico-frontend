Agriculture teaching with a rural development context

RNTU’s agriculture faculty describes its original purpose as expanding agricultural education in underserved parts of Raisen district. The degree began in 2015-16. The current course account covers agronomy, horticulture, soil science, plant health, breeding, economics and extension, bringing production science and the social setting of farming into the same undergraduate route.

The published programme structure includes village surveys, soil sampling, plant-protection interventions and agro-industrial attachment. It also lists enterprise learning options such as beekeeping, mushroom cultivation and seed production. These are curriculum descriptions; applicants should ask which modules will actually run for their cohort and how the practical timetable is organised.
