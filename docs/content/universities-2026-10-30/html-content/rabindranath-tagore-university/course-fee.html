How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at RNTU?

4 years. The course structure combines agricultural science with village-oriented work and enterprise modules.

What scholarship information is available for RNTU?

The admissions page names Shiksha Mitra Scholarships but its displayed 2022 scheme explicitly excludes B.Sc. Agriculture. Applicants should not assume that the advertised percentage waivers apply to this degree.
