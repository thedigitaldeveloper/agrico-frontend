News & Notices: questions answered

Which dated admission notices are recorded for RNTU?

No dated admission event is verified in this profile. The official channel is https://rntu.ac.in/admission; check the notice for the programme and entry year you intend to join.

How does admission to RNTU work?

The 2026-27 admissions page describes account registration, programme selection, application completion, payment and submission. A course-specific tuition schedule and final agriculture selection notice were not located. Create an account and choose the course: The university admission page lists registration and programme selection as its initial steps. Complete and review the application: The published sequence asks applicants to complete the form, pay the application fee and review before submission; the amount was not verified.

How can I contact RNTU about an application?

Published phone: 0755-2700401. Published email: info@rntu.ac.in. Official admissions: https://rntu.ac.in/admission
