Minimum eligibility and closing rank answer different questions

A qualifying subject or minimum academic threshold determines whether an applicant can be considered under the applicable rules. A closing rank or score describes a particular selection list, counselling round or category. One should not be substituted for the other.

If a number is described as…Check these details before using it
Minimum academic marksQualifying examination, subjects, category and intake-year rule
Entrance qualifying scoreNamed examination and its relevant year/category instructions
Closing rank or scoreExact institution, programme, round, quota, category and year
An older admission figureIts historical context; do not label it as the current cutoff
