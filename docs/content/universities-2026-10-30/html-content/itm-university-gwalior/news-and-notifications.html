News & Notices: questions answered

Which dated admission notices are recorded for ITM University?

No dated admission event is verified in this profile. The official channel is https://www.itmuniversity.ac.in/admission/eligibility; check the notice for the programme and entry year you intend to join.

How does admission to ITM University work?

The central eligibility page and individual course pages provide useful entry information. The agronomy pages use differing marks/qualification wording, so applicants should obtain the applying-year rule in writing. Match the degree-level entry rule: Check the bachelor’s science/agriculture requirement or the master’s qualifying-degree rule. Resolve the master’s eligibility wording: Ask admissions to confirm which current minimum and accepted degree apply to M.Sc. Agronomy.

How can I contact ITM University about an application?

Published phone: +91-9329934215. Published email: admission@itmuniversity.ac.in. Official admissions: https://www.itmuniversity.ac.in/admission/eligibility

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at ITM University?
