From agricultural foundations to agronomy specialisation

ITM’s agriculture bachelor’s curriculum is organised around fundamental sciences, production systems and later skill development. The university’s brochure places the school at its Sithouli setting and identifies an instructional farm, laboratory and library resources. A separate agronomy master’s course provides a focused postgraduate route.

The programme pages identify agriculture partnerships with research institutions and describe Student READY learning. For career comparisons, ITM also publishes an agriculture-and-allied-sector NIRF submission with dated undergraduate and postgraduate outcomes. These grouped returns are more specific than the university’s overall salary counters, though they still should not be read as a guarantee for one degree or a future student.
