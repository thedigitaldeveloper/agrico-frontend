Admission Process: questions answered

How does admission to ITM University work?

The central eligibility page and individual course pages provide useful entry information. The agronomy pages use differing marks/qualification wording, so applicants should obtain the applying-year rule in writing. Match the degree-level entry rule: Check the bachelor’s science/agriculture requirement or the master’s qualifying-degree rule. Resolve the master’s eligibility wording: Ask admissions to confirm which current minimum and accepted degree apply to M.Sc. Agronomy.

Sources: [2][3][6]

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at ITM University?

Central table: 10+2 Science/Agriculture with at least 50%; the course page names PCMB, PCB, PCM or Agriculture subjects.

Sources: [1][2][6]

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. (Ag.) Agronomy at ITM University?

Course page states B.Sc. Agriculture/Honours with minimum 5.5 CGPA; its FAQ also includes horticulture, while the central table states 50% in agriculture stream. Confirm the current governing rule.
