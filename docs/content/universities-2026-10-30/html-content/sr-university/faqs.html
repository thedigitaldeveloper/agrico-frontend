FAQs: questions answered

How long is SRU’s agriculture bachelor’s degree?

The official course page states four years and eight semesters.

Does the programme include rural learning?

Yes. The course page includes rural work experience and other Student READY elements.

What school subjects are stated for direct entry?

The programme directory specifies PCB with at least 55% at 10+2.

Is there a diploma route?

The directory lists a first-class two-year Agriculture or Seed Technology diploma after Class 10.

What is the tuition table’s limitation?

It displays ₹97,500 per semester but does not explicitly identify the academic year of that table. Obtain a dated confirmation.

Which ICAR term applies to the listed bachelor’s programme?

The audited record runs from 16 March 2026 to 15 March 2031.

What tuition does SR University publish for agriculture?

The undated official table lists ₹97,500 per semester for B.Sc. Agriculture, plus separate ₹5,000 enrolment and ₹25,000 one-time admission charges. The governing intake year is unverified; scholarship net fees must not be inferred from inconsistent rounded figures.
