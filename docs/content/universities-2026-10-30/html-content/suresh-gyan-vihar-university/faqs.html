What practical learning is described at SGVU?

The school’s farmer-interaction account describes work with farm families and extension tools. The official school brochure describes summer/winter internships, field training and educational visits to ICAR research institutes, agricultural universities and KVKs. These are described learning activities, not guaranteed placement offers.

Which teaching facilities are documented at SGVU?

Hydroponics and protected cultivation: The school identifies working hydroponics and protected-cultivation facilities.
