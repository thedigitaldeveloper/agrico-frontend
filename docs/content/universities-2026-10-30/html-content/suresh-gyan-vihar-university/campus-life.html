How could the programme shape your week?

Read the actual practical components for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture — Agronomy; M.Sc. Horticulture — Vegetable Science alongside the academic timetable. Field work, laboratory sessions or clinical teaching may affect daily travel and preparation; confirm the arrangements rather than inferring them from a campus photograph.

  • The school’s farmer-interaction account describes work with farm families and extension tools.
  • The official school brochure describes summer/winter internships, field training and educational visits to ICAR research institutes, agricultural universities and KVKs. These are described learning activities, not guaranteed placement offers.
