Which dated admission notices are recorded for SGVU?

How does admission to SGVU work?

The current school FAQ mentions PCM, PCB or Agriculture for undergraduate applicants and JET or ICAR channels. Because that page is undated, obtain the intake-specific subject, marks and counselling rules before choosing an examination route. Confirm the course: Use the official programme contact to confirm that the listed degree is taking applications for your intended intake. Request the intake notice: Ask for the applicable selection rules, fee breakdown and document checklist before submitting an application.

How can I contact SGVU about an application?

Published phone: 1800 309 4545. Published email: admissions@mygyanvihar.com. Official admissions: https://admissions.gyanvihar.org/
