Learning experiences connected with preparation

SGVU’s school page identifies instructional farms and protected-cultivation units. A separate school account describes farmer-student and farmer-faculty interactions on campus and in an adopted village.

  • The school’s farmer-interaction account describes work with farm families and extension tools.
  • The official school brochure describes summer/winter internships, field training and educational visits to ICAR research institutes, agricultural universities and KVKs. These are described learning activities, not guaranteed placement offers.

How to assess a placement claim

For B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture — Agronomy; M.Sc. Horticulture — Vegetable Science, ask for a report that matches the course, campus and graduating batch. Compare outcomes on the same basis rather than using an isolated maximum package.

Evidence to requestWhat to read
Cohort and reporting yearWhich programme, campus and graduating batch the report covers
Eligible students and offersHow the institution defines participation and reported placement
Roles and employersWhether the work and qualification requirements match the degree
Salary descriptionWhether a quoted figure is median, average, highest or another measure
Internship versus employmentWhether an experience is curricular training, a paid internship or a final job offer
