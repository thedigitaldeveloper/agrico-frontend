The documented academic setting

SGVU’s school page identifies instructional farms and protected-cultivation units. A separate school account describes farmer-student and farmer-faculty interactions on campus and in an adopted village.

Facilities identified in the sources

Hydroponics and protected cultivation

The school identifies working hydroponics and protected-cultivation facilities.

Soil science laboratory

The school describes soil-property and fertility-assessment practicals.

Enterprise and production units in the school brochure

The undated brochure lists mushroom production, seed processing, vermicompost, azolla, a greenhouse/nursery and a poly-pond/fisheries unit. Ask the school which units are available for the intended cohort.

Practical learning and student access

For B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture — Agronomy; M.Sc. Horticulture — Vegetable Science, the usefulness of a facility depends on actual teaching access, supervision and the programme timetable. The documented learning activities are listed below; ask the department about your own cohort’s arrangements.

  • The school’s farmer-interaction account describes work with farm families and extension tools.
  • The official school brochure describes summer/winter internships, field training and educational visits to ICAR research institutes, agricultural universities and KVKs. These are described learning activities, not guaranteed placement offers.
