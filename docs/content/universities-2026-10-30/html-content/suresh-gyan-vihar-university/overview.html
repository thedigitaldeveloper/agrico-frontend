Agriculture courses spanning crops, horticulture and engineering

SGVU was established through Rajasthan legislation in 2008. Its agriculture landing page lists B.Sc. Agriculture, agricultural engineering and master’s and doctoral subjects in agriculture and horticulture. The undergraduate agriculture degree is the programme explicitly printed in the checked ICAR row.

The school’s current facilities description includes soil science, plant pathology, entomology and biotechnology laboratories. Hydroponics and protected cultivation add a horticultural component to field-based crop study. The programme catalogue includes both agricultural science and engineering, while the master’s subject list extends from crop production and protection to horticultural specialisations.
