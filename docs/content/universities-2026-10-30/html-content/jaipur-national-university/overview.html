Agriculture teaching within JNU Jaipur

Jaipur National University is a self-financed private institution sponsored by Mahima Shiksha Samiti. Its School of Agricultural Sciences presents agricultural study within the university’s wider range of programmes. The agriculture undergraduate listing gives a four-year honours degree and a selection description of JET plus personal interview.

The degree outline spans crop production and allied agricultural sciences, with a substantial list of practical modules. Applicants can use that curriculum to compare their interests in seed work, cultivation, soils or enterprise. Ask the school which experiential modules are running for the intended batch and request the current programme handbook, since the website contains both updated academic material and older FAQ text.

4 years. The four-year degree covers agronomy, soils, plant breeding, crop protection and related disciplines. The published curriculum lists practical choices in seed production, mushroom cultivation, testing and beekeeping.
