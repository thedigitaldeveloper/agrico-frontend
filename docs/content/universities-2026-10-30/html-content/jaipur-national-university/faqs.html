How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at JNU Jaipur?

4 years. The four-year degree covers agronomy, soils, plant breeding, crop protection and related disciplines. The published curriculum lists practical choices in seed production, mushroom cultivation, testing and beekeeping.

Sources: [1][3]

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at JNU Jaipur?

The official UG listing states 10+2 with minimum 50% in Science/Agriculture. The subject-combination text is incomplete on the page; obtain the current prospectus before assessing individual eligibility.

Sources: [1][3]

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at JNU Jaipur?

A verified numerical fee is not available. The official page displays ₹75,100 tuition but does not establish a clear academic year and charging period. The amount is not converted into an annual or total-degree fee; lab/library costs are stated separately.

How does admission to JNU Jaipur work?

JNU Jaipur’s agriculture listing gives a 50% Science/Agriculture threshold and JET plus personal interview. The university also runs a wider scholarship-test process; do not assume that this replaces the agriculture-specific selection route. Confirm the current prospectus: Ask the admissions office for the applicable intake, subject requirements and selection rules before submitting an application. Request a written cost statement: Obtain tuition, compulsory charges, accommodation options and refund terms for the intended entry year.

Sources: [3][4]
