FAQs: questions answered

What are the undergraduate subjects required in 2026-27?

The booklet requires five 10+2 subjects including English and the specified Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Mathematics or Agriculture route.

What is the published B.Sc. first-year academic total?

The 2026-27 fee sheet gives INR 1,40,000; its INR 1,21,500 tuition component is only part of that total.

Does tuition stay fixed across four years?

The fee sheet states a 7% annual tuition increase and separately prints later-year charges.

Can horticulture graduates apply for entomology?

The 2026-27 booklet includes Agriculture, Horticulture and Forestry bachelor’s qualifications, with its stated marks threshold.

Are both master’s courses covered by the checked ICAR row?

The audited Faculty of Agriculture row explicitly prints B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture. The master’s offerings do not expand that scope.

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at MM(DU)?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture — Agronomy; M.Sc. Agriculture — Entomology. The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at MM(DU)?

4 years. The Faculty of Agriculture’s undergraduate course, explicitly named in the ICAR register.

How long is M.Sc. Agriculture — Agronomy at MM(DU)?

2 years. Postgraduate crop-management study supported by a departmental laboratory and research farm.

How long is M.Sc. Agriculture — Entomology at MM(DU)?

2 years. Specialist insect-science study listed in the 2026-27 programme booklet.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at MM(DU)?

2026-27 booklet: 10+2 with five subjects including English and Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Mathematics or Agriculture stream; 50% aggregate, 45% SC/ST.

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. Agriculture — Agronomy at MM(DU)?

2026-27 booklet: B.Sc. Agriculture/equivalent with 60%; 50% SC/ST.

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. Agriculture — Entomology at MM(DU)?

2026-27 booklet: Agriculture/Horticulture/Forestry bachelor’s degree with 60% or equivalent; 50% SC/ST.

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at MM(DU)?

₹1,21,500 / year · 2026-27. First-year tuition component. Published total academic fee is INR 1,40,000 in year 1 and INR 6,01,453 across four years; tuition rises 7% annually; hostel/transport extra. Included charges: Published tuition/course fee; additional charges must be checked separately.

What fee is documented for M.Sc. Agriculture — Agronomy at MM(DU)?

₹70,000 / year · 2026-27. Published annual tuition component; programme academic total INR 1,67,900, additional living costs excluded. Included charges: Published tuition/course fee; additional charges must be checked separately.

What fee is documented for M.Sc. Agriculture — Entomology at MM(DU)?

₹70,000 / year · 2026-27. University fee table has a combined M.Sc. Agriculture row; confirm specialisation-specific offer charges. Included charges: Published tuition/course fee; additional charges must be checked separately.
