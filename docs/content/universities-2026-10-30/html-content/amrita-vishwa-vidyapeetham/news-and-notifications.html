How does admission to Amrita work?

The dedicated AEEL 2026 page includes undergraduate agriculture among its entrance programmes. The programme page states that admission depends on a valid AEEL rank. Reconfirm the final counselling notice and subject eligibility for the intended intake. The dated AEEL 2026 handbook specifies the agriculture PCB requirement and online application through applyug.amrita.edu. Its tentative 9–10 May 2026 examination dates are historical, not a current application opening. Confirm the course: Use the official programme contact to confirm that the listed degree is taking applications for your intended intake. Request the intake notice: Ask for the applicable selection rules, fee breakdown and document checklist before submitting an application.
