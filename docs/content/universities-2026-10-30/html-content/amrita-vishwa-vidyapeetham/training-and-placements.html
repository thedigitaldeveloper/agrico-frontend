How to assess a placement claim

For B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture — Agronomy; Ph.D. Agronomy, ask for a report that matches the course, campus and graduating batch. Compare outcomes on the same basis rather than using an isolated maximum package.

Evidence to requestWhat to read
Cohort and reporting yearWhich programme, campus and graduating batch the report covers
Eligible students and offersHow the institution defines participation and reported placement
Roles and employersWhether the work and qualification requirements match the degree
Salary descriptionWhether a quoted figure is median, average, highest or another measure
Internship versus employmentWhether an experience is curricular training, a paid internship or a final job offer
