Agricultural science at Arasampalayam

Amrita offers the four-year agriculture degree through its School of Agricultural Sciences in Coimbatore. The school emphasises field-oriented learning and connects agricultural training with technology. Its course list also names postgraduate agronomy and doctoral agronomy, creating a subject pathway beyond the undergraduate degree without implying that every course shares the same accreditation scope.

The programme page provides eligibility, entrance and fee information, but several sections carry different years. The clearly labelled annual tuition table is for 2025-26, while hostel and mess information is labelled 2026-27. Applicants should use the dedicated AEEL 2026 page for the entrance route and request a single consolidated fee schedule for their own cohort.
