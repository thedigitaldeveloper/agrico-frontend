How does admission to SOA work?

SOA publishes a dedicated 2026 agriculture eligibility page. Bachelor’s and master’s applicants face different subject, marks/OGPA and minimum-age requirements. The SAAT 2026 merit framework explicitly includes B.Sc. and M.Sc. Agriculture, with rank-based counselling subject to seats. Use the agriculture eligibility page: Check the correct degree level and category-specific criteria. Confirm the selection and fee notice: Obtain the applicable admission instructions and the complete academic and residential fee documents.

Sources: [3][10][11][12]

How can I contact SOA about an application?

Published phone: +91 82605 01310. Published email: saat2026@soa.ac.in. Official admissions: https://www.admission.soa.ac.in/agriculture-2026
