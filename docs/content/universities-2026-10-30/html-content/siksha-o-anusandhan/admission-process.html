Which admission route applies?

SOA publishes a dedicated 2026 agriculture eligibility page. Bachelor’s and master’s applicants face different subject, marks/OGPA and minimum-age requirements. The SAAT 2026 merit framework explicitly includes B.Sc. and M.Sc. Agriculture, with rank-based counselling subject to seats.
