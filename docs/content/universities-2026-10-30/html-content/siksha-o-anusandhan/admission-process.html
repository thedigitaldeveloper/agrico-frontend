Which admission route applies?

SOA publishes a dedicated 2026 agriculture eligibility page. Bachelor’s and master’s applicants face different subject, marks/OGPA and minimum-age requirements. The SAAT 2026 merit framework explicitly includes B.Sc. and M.Sc. Agriculture, with rank-based counselling subject to seats.

Application steps for this institution

  • 2026 bachelor’s entry: English with PCM/PCB/PCMB; 60% General/OBC or 50% SC/ST/PH; minimum age 16 on 31 August 2026.
  • 2026 master’s entry: B.Sc. Agriculture/Horticulture; 60% General/OBC or 50% SC/ST, with published OGPA alternatives; minimum age 19 on 31 August 2026. Eligibility marks cannot be rounded.
