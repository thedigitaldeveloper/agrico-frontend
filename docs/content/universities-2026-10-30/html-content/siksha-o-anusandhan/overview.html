Agriculture learning through departments, villages and research

SOA’s Institute of Agricultural Sciences lists departments spanning agricultural engineering, economics, extension, crop production, plant protection and horticulture. The 2026 programme list describes a four-year agriculture bachelor’s degree and two-year master’s routes, providing both a broad foundation and routes into specialised study.

A 2026 university report describes village attachment in Khordha district, where students assessed cropping systems and worked on village action plans. Another 2026 report records an ICAR-CRRI partnership concerning rice research and postgraduate training. These are documented examples of academic activity; access to a particular research placement still depends on the programme and arrangements in force.
