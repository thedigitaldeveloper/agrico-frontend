News & Notices: questions answered

Which dated admission notices are recorded for VIT?

No dated admission event is verified in this profile. The official channel is https://admissions.vit.ac.in/bsc-hons-agri-2026-applications/login; check the notice for the programme and entry year you intend to join.

How does admission to VIT work?

VIT’s 2026 agriculture portal describes registration, application completion, payment and online counselling. Counselling eligibility does not guarantee a seat; allocation depends on rank and availability. Register and complete the application: Use an active email address and Indian mobile number; the portal verifies the mobile number by OTP. Pay and upload the photograph: The portal specifies a non-refundable INR 700 application payment and a JPG/JPEG photograph. Participate in counselling: Shortlisted applicants attend online counselling based on the qualifying-examination rank list. Enter fee-category preferences during online counselling: The 2026–27 portal asks shortlisted applicants to enter preferred fee categories and review their choices. It states that allotment is rank-based; individual results require the applicant’s own login.
