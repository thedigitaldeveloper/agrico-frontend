FAQs: questions answered

Is an entrance examination required for VIT agriculture in 2026?

The dedicated 2026 portal explicitly says no entrance examination; selection uses qualifying-examination merit and counselling.

Are mathematics-only applicants eligible under the 2026 rules?

The 2026 portal requires Physics, Chemistry and Biology; older prospectuses show different combinations. Use the current portal.

What is the application cost?

The 2026 portal specifies a non-refundable INR 700.

Why are three tuition figures shown?

VIT publishes annual tuition categories of INR 1,10,000, INR 1,95,000 and INR 2,25,000 for the 2026-27 intake.

Which campus offers the researched course?

The dedicated application portal identifies the Vellore campus.

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at VIT?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture. The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at VIT?

4 years. Agricultural science at the Vellore campus, taught through VAIAL with field, laboratory and outreach resources.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at VIT?

2026-27: Resident Indian; born on/after 1 July 2006; 60% overall and 60% in PCB, with published 10% relaxation for specified categories/regions; English required.

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at VIT?

₹1,10,000 / year · 2026-27. Category 1 tuition. Category 2: INR 1,95,000; Category 3: INR 2,25,000 annually. One-time refundable caution deposit INR 5,000. Included charges: Published tuition/course fee; additional charges must be checked separately.

How does admission to VIT work?

VIT’s 2026 agriculture portal describes registration, application completion, payment and online counselling. Counselling eligibility does not guarantee a seat; allocation depends on rank and availability. Register and complete the application: Use an active email address and Indian mobile number; the portal verifies the mobile number by OTP. Pay and upload the photograph: The portal specifies a non-refundable INR 700 application payment and a JPG/JPEG photograph. Participate in counselling: Shortlisted applicants attend online counselling based on the qualifying-examination rank list. Enter fee-category preferences during online counselling: The 2026–27 portal asks shortlisted applicants to enter preferred fee categories and review their choices. It states that allotment is rank-based; individual results require the applicant’s own login.

What practical learning is described at VIT?

VAIAL describes agricultural and horticultural land, teaching and research laboratories, gardens, composting units, irrigation and machinery. Its events list records farmer-facing training as well as student and research activities.

Which teaching facilities are documented at VIT?

Teaching and research laboratories: VAIAL identifies analytical instrumentation and multidisciplinary research laboratories. Botanical and medicinal gardens: Both gardens are named alongside farm and composting resources.
