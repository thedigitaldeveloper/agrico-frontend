A Vellore agriculture school with clear 2026 entry rules

VAIAL is VIT’s School of Agricultural Innovations and Advanced Learning. Its published activities combine degree teaching, agricultural research and farmer outreach. The school describes facilities for crop and horticultural work, analytical teaching laboratories, botanical and medicinal gardens, composting and farm machinery. Its events record includes training on organic farming, coconut cultivation and vegetable value addition.

The 2026-27 agriculture portal provides unusually specific applicant information: selection uses qualifying-examination merit, followed by online counselling. It states academic, residency and age conditions, and publishes three tuition categories. These are Vellore agriculture rules; applicants should not assume the same process applies to other VIT campuses or engineering programmes.

Planning an agriculture application

VIT’s 2026 agriculture portal describes registration, application completion, payment and online counselling. Counselling eligibility does not guarantee a seat; allocation depends on rank and availability.

Read the complete admission process for programme-wise requirements, documents and source-dated notices.

Register and complete the application

Use an active email address and Indian mobile number; the portal verifies the mobile number by OTP.

Pay and upload the photograph

The portal specifies a non-refundable INR 700 application payment and a JPG/JPEG photograph.

Participate in counselling

Shortlisted applicants attend online counselling based on the qualifying-examination rank list.

Enter fee-category preferences during online counselling

The 2026–27 portal asks shortlisted applicants to enter preferred fee categories and review their choices. It states that allotment is rank-based; individual results require the applicant’s own login.
