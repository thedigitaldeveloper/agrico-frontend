What fee is documented for M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at NU Jaipur?

₹62,000 / year · 2026-27. Published annual tuition fee for the 2026-27 session; confirm category or JET quota fee schedule before fee payment. Included charges: Published annual academic tuition fee; hostel, examination and caution money are billed separately.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at NU Jaipur?

4 years; study format: Full-time on campus. B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Nirwan University, Jaipur is delivered on campus through the School of Agricultural Sciences, combining classroom agronomy, laboratory practicals and field crop rotations.

How long is M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at NU Jaipur?

2 years; study format: Full-time on campus. M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at Nirwan University, Jaipur is delivered on campus through the School of Agricultural Sciences, combining classroom agronomy, laboratory practicals and field crop rotations.

What scholarship information is available for NU Jaipur?

Nirwan University, Jaipur offers merit-based tuition scholarships based on Class XII / qualifying examination marks or JET rank, alongside Rajasthan government post-matric scholarships (SJMS / Samaj Kalyan Vibhag) for eligible SC, ST, OBC, MBC and EBC students. University Entrance & Qualifying Marks Merit Scholarship (2026-27): Tuition fee waiver slabs (10% to 50% in first year) for applicants scoring 75%+ to 90%+ in 10+2 or high rank in Rajasthan JET / CUET; continuation requires maintaining minimum SGPA and 75% attendance. Rajasthan State Post-Matric & Girl Child Agriculture Incentive: Eligible Rajasthan domicile students can apply on the Rajasthan SSO Scholarship Portal; girl students in agriculture may also check state Rajshree / Agriculture Department incentive schemes.
