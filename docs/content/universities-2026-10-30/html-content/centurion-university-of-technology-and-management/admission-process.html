What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at CUTM?

10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics/Biology, at least 50%; the programme page also requires a CUEE score. Confirm applicability to the intended intake year.

Agriculture, Horticulture or Forestry bachelor’s degree from a recognised Indian university with at least 50%. Confirm applicability to the intended intake year.

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. (Hort.) Vegetable Science at CUTM?

Four-year Agriculture bachelor’s degree or equivalent with at least 50%, from a recognised university listed under UGC/AIU. Confirm applicability to the intended intake year.

What are the entry requirements for MBA Agribusiness Management at CUTM?

Graduation in any discipline plus an entrance examination, as stated on the undated official programme page. Confirm the accepted test and intake-specific minimum marks.
