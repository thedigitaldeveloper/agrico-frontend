Two allied schools within one Odisha university

The M.S. Swaminathan School of Agriculture describes learning through fields, laboratories and enterprise activity. Its school page identifies agronomy, crop improvement, horticulture, soil science and extension as teaching and research areas. Student READY activities connect experiential work, rural exposure and agro-industrial attachment.

The School of Fisheries provides another applied pathway at Paralakhemundi. Its regulations describe aquaculture, fish processing, aquatic health and resource management, supported by training ponds, a hatchery and specialist production activity. Agriculture and fisheries therefore offer different practical settings within the university, and applicants can compare crop-based, engineering, agribusiness and aquatic-science routes according to their interests.
