How to assess a placement claim

For B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; B.Tech. Agricultural Engineering; M.Sc. (Agri.) Agronomy; M.Sc. (Agri.) Agricultural Extension Education; M.Sc. (Agri.) Genetics and Plant Breeding; M.Sc. (Hort.) Vegetable Science; MBA Agribusiness Management; Bachelor of Fisheries Science (B.F.Sc.), ask for a report that matches the course, campus and graduating batch. Compare outcomes on the same basis rather than using an isolated maximum package.

Evidence to requestWhat to read
Cohort and reporting yearWhich programme, campus and graduating batch the report covers
Eligible students and offersHow the institution defines participation and reported placement
Roles and employersWhether the work and qualification requirements match the degree
Salary descriptionWhether a quoted figure is median, average, highest or another measure
Internship versus employmentWhether an experience is curricular training, a paid internship or a final job offer

Training & Placements: questions answered

What training and outcome evidence is available for CUTM?

The university's NIRF 2026 Agriculture and Allied Sectors submission reports graduates, placements, higher-study progression and the median annual salary of placed graduates for the cohorts below. The figures apply to the reported programme-duration group, rather than an individual specialisation, and do not predict an applicant's future outcome.

What practical learning is described at CUTM?

Agriculture READY activities include experiential learning, rural work and agro-industrial attachment. Fisheries regulations describe hands-on aquaponics and ornamental fish culture.

Which employers or training organisations are named for CUTM?

Coromandel; Kaveri Seeds; Syngenta. Read the stated programme and reporting scope in training and placements.

Which teaching facilities are documented at CUTM?

Agricultural research farms: The agriculture school identifies farm-based teaching and enterprise learning. Aquaculture Research Farm: The 2022 fisheries regulations describe fish ponds and a carp hatchery.
