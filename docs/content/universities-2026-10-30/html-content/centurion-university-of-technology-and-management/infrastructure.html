Practical learning and student access

For B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; B.Tech. Agricultural Engineering; M.Sc. (Agri.) Agronomy; M.Sc. (Agri.) Agricultural Extension Education; M.Sc. (Agri.) Genetics and Plant Breeding; M.Sc. (Hort.) Vegetable Science; MBA Agribusiness Management; Bachelor of Fisheries Science (B.F.Sc.), the usefulness of a facility depends on actual teaching access, supervision and the programme timetable. The documented learning activities are listed below; ask the department about your own cohort’s arrangements.

  • Agriculture READY activities include experiential learning, rural work and agro-industrial attachment.
  • Fisheries regulations describe hands-on aquaponics and ornamental fish culture.
