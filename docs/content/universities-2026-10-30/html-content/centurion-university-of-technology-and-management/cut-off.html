Minimum eligibility and closing rank answer different questions

A qualifying subject or minimum academic threshold determines whether an applicant can be considered under the applicable rules. A closing rank or score describes a particular selection list, counselling round or category. One should not be substituted for the other.

If a number is described as…Check these details before using it
Minimum academic marksQualifying examination, subjects, category and intake-year rule
Entrance qualifying scoreNamed examination and its relevant year/category instructions
Closing rank or scoreExact institution, programme, round, quota, category and year
An older admission figureIts historical context; do not label it as the current cutoff

Eligibility & Cut-offs: questions answered

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at CUTM?

The official programme page specifies 50% in 10+2 Science and prints the subject combination as Physics/Math, Chemistry, Biology. Confirm that wording against the intake-year bulletin.

Sources: [1][2][8]

What are the entry requirements for B.Tech. Agricultural Engineering at CUTM?

10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics/Biology, at least 50%; the programme page also requires a CUEE score. Confirm applicability to the intended intake year.

Sources: [1][11]

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. (Agri.) Agronomy at CUTM?

A four-year Agriculture bachelor’s degree or equivalent with at least 50%, from a recognised university listed under UGC/AIU. Confirm applicability to the intended intake year.

Sources: [1][2][12]

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. (Agri.) Agricultural Extension Education at CUTM?

Agriculture, Horticulture or Forestry bachelor’s degree from a recognised Indian university with at least 50%. Confirm applicability to the intended intake year.

Sources: [1][2][15]

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. (Agri.) Genetics and Plant Breeding at CUTM?

Agriculture bachelor’s degree with second division and at least 50%, from a recognised college or university. Confirm applicability to the intended intake year.

Sources: [1][2][13]

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. (Hort.) Vegetable Science at CUTM?

Four-year Agriculture bachelor’s degree or equivalent with at least 50%, from a recognised university listed under UGC/AIU. Confirm applicability to the intended intake year.

Sources: [1][2][14]

What are the entry requirements for MBA Agribusiness Management at CUTM?

Graduation in any discipline plus an entrance examination, as stated on the undated official programme page. Confirm the accepted test and intake-specific minimum marks.

Sources: [1][16]

What are the entry requirements for Bachelor of Fisheries Science (B.F.Sc.) at CUTM?

The programme page lists PCB, biology-containing PCMB/PCBA combinations, with at least 50% for General/OBC/UPS and 40% for SC/ST/PwD. The page has no intake-year label.

Sources: [1][3][9]
