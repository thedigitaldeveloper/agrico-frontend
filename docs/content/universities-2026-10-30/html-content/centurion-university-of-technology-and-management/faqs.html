FAQs: questions answered

Why are two ICAR units shown for Centurion?

Agriculture and fisheries have separate source rows; they belong to one Odisha university profile.

Does the fisheries degree use the agriculture accreditation dates?

No. The fisheries record runs from 8 February 2024 to 7 February 2029.

Where is the fisheries school described in the regulations?

The July 2022 regulations place it at the Paralakhemundi campus in Gajapati district.

Is agricultural engineering in the audit?

Yes. B.Tech. Agricultural Engineering is included under the agriculture school record.

What distinguishes agriculture practical work?

The school describes READY activities involving enterprise, rural and industrial exposure.

How are Centurion’s agriculture and fisheries fees quoted?

The central table labels its Paralakhemundi rows 2026, while an older heading says 2025–26. Programme pages confirm annual B.Sc./B.F.Sc. charges and an MBA package; obtain a cohort-specific, itemised payable statement.

Are verified agriculture placement figures available?

The NIRF 2026 agriculture/allied submission reports UG four-year programmes (2024-2025): 170 placed from 253 graduates in minimum time, with ₹4,80,000 median annual salary among those placed; PG two-year programmes (2024-2025): 37 placed from 54 graduates in minimum time, with ₹4,30,000 median annual salary among those placed. These are historical group-level outcomes, not specialisation-level placement promises.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at CUTM?

The official programme page specifies 50% in 10+2 Science and prints the subject combination as Physics/Math, Chemistry, Biology. Confirm that wording against the intake-year bulletin.

What are the entry requirements for B.Tech. Agricultural Engineering at CUTM?

10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics/Biology, at least 50%; the programme page also requires a CUEE score. Confirm applicability to the intended intake year.

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. (Agri.) Agronomy at CUTM?

A four-year Agriculture bachelor’s degree or equivalent with at least 50%, from a recognised university listed under UGC/AIU. Confirm applicability to the intended intake year.

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. (Agri.) Agricultural Extension Education at CUTM?

Agriculture, Horticulture or Forestry bachelor’s degree from a recognised Indian university with at least 50%. Confirm applicability to the intended intake year.

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. (Agri.) Genetics and Plant Breeding at CUTM?

Agriculture bachelor’s degree with second division and at least 50%, from a recognised college or university. Confirm applicability to the intended intake year.

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. (Hort.) Vegetable Science at CUTM?

Four-year Agriculture bachelor’s degree or equivalent with at least 50%, from a recognised university listed under UGC/AIU. Confirm applicability to the intended intake year.

What are the entry requirements for MBA Agribusiness Management at CUTM?

Graduation in any discipline plus an entrance examination, as stated on the undated official programme page. Confirm the accepted test and intake-specific minimum marks.

What are the entry requirements for Bachelor of Fisheries Science (B.F.Sc.) at CUTM?

What practical learning is described at CUTM?

Agriculture READY activities include experiential learning, rural work and agro-industrial attachment. Fisheries regulations describe hands-on aquaponics and ornamental fish culture.

Which teaching facilities are documented at CUTM?

Agricultural research farms: The agriculture school identifies farm-based teaching and enterprise learning. Aquaculture Research Farm: The 2022 fisheries regulations describe fish ponds and a carp hatchery.

What student facilities are documented for CUTM?

Agriculture uses research farms and laboratories, while fisheries has its own aquaculture teaching resources documented in the 2022 regulations. Accommodation availability, meal charges and transport arrangements for your entry year still need direct confirmation.

What scholarship information is available for CUTM?

The published Amrit Kaal table includes Agricultural Engineering within its non-CSE B.Tech group at Paralakhemundi/Bhubaneswar. It sets first-year fee concessions by qualifying results. This is not evidence of the same award for B.Sc. Agriculture, M.Sc. Agriculture or Fisheries, and the page does not give a governing academic year. Amrit Kaal scholarship: non-CSE B.Tech including Agri: The official table lists 10% for 60–70% qualifying marks and 15% for 70–80%; a separate top-score/JEE/OJEE band lists 20%. These are first-year fee concessions. Boundary wording, programme/campus eligibility and the current policy must be confirmed in the award letter.
