Agricultural science with a documented application route

The four-year B.Sc. Agriculture page describes study across crop production and protection, including plant disease, insects, weeds and nutrient problems. The school also lists master’s study and a separate agribusiness MBA offered by the School of Management. Applicants can therefore distinguish agricultural science specialisation from a management pathway.

The official undergraduate page sets out an application, entrance test and interview sequence. Its fee figures are not clearly labelled by academic year, however. The school’s events list provides dated evidence of activity: 2026 entries include crop water-use discussions, soil-quality sessions and a Pusa Krishi Vigyan Mela visit. These records show specific educational activity without establishing a standard experience for every student.
