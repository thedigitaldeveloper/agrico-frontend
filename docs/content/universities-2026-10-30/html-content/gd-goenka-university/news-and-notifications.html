How does admission to GD Goenka University work?

The B.Sc. course page describes an application followed by an entrance test and interview, shortlisting and an offer. Reconfirm the intake-specific test arrangements and any revised academic criteria. Complete the university application: The official course page begins its admission sequence with the application. Take the test and interview: The programme requirement identifies GATA and a personal interview. Review the offer: The page describes shortlisting and an offer letter after assessment.
