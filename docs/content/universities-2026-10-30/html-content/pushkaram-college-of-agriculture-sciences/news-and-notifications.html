How does admission to PCAS work?

Pushkaram College applicants should use the TNAU admission channel and confirm their exact programme, quota and current institutional contact. The former college domain is not used for admission enquiries because it did not present college content during the 31 October 2026 check. These steps describe the TNAU counselling channel. Any separate college-management route must be checked independently. Register with TNAU: Submit a single online application. Set course preferences: Order eligible college and course combinations. Follow counselling: General-quota counselling runs online; special-reservation sessions may be offline.
