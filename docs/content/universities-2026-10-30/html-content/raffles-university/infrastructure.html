The documented academic setting

The school names subject laboratories across crop, soil, animal and allied fields. Its RAWE and industry-attachment account describes work outside the classroom with farm and industry communities.

Facilities identified in the sources

Agricultural subject laboratories

The school identifies laboratories including soils, plant health and crop improvement.

Practical learning and student access

For B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, the usefulness of a facility depends on actual teaching access, supervision and the programme timetable. The documented learning activities are listed below; ask the department about your own cohort’s arrangements.

  • Rural Agricultural Work Experience and Agro-Industrial Attachment are described by the school.
