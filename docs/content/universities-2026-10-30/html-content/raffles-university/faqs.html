FAQs: questions answered

How long is Raffles’ agriculture course?

The programme table lists four years and eight semesters.

What practical attachment model does the school describe?

It names Rural Agricultural Work Experience and Agro-Industrial Attachment.

What is Raffles’ agriculture academic fee for 2026-27?

The signed 2026-27 sheet lists INR 65,000 per year. Registration, admission and refundable security are separate one-time charges; hostel costs are additional.

Are scholarships automatic?

The university FAQ describes case-by-case consideration by the Gomber Education Foundation, subject to its conditions and available funds.

Should agriculture applicants follow the general Situation Test route?

The general procedure describes it, but the agriculture compliance report mentions JET scheduling. Confirm the current programme-specific route.

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at Raffles University?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture. The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Raffles University?

4 years (8 semesters). The programme combines agricultural science, farm practicals and a final-year hands-on component.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Raffles University?

Undated official course page: 10+2 Science/Agriculture with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics/Biology/Agriculture, at least 50% aggregate.

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Raffles University?

₹65,000 / year · 2026-27. Annual academic fee. One-time registration INR 1,000, admission INR 9,000 and refundable academic security INR 5,000 separately; hostel charges excluded. Included charges: Published tuition/course fee; additional charges must be checked separately.

How does admission to Raffles University work?

The university has a general application and assessment procedure, while its 2023 agriculture self-study compliance report refers to the Rajasthan JET schedule. Obtain the current agriculture-specific route before following the general Situation Test and interview process. Confirm the course: Use the official programme contact to confirm that the listed degree is taking applications for your intended intake. Request the intake notice: Ask for the applicable selection rules, fee breakdown and document checklist before submitting an application.

What is the recorded ICAR scope for Raffles University?

What practical learning is described at Raffles University?

Rural Agricultural Work Experience and Agro-Industrial Attachment are described by the school.

Which teaching facilities are documented at Raffles University?

Agricultural subject laboratories: The school identifies laboratories including soils, plant health and crop improvement.

What student facilities are documented for Raffles University?

The school names subject laboratories across crop, soil, animal and allied fields. Its RAWE and industry-attachment account describes work outside the classroom with farm and industry communities. Accommodation availability, meal charges and transport arrangements for your entry year still need direct confirmation.

What scholarship information is available for Raffles University?

The university FAQ says the Gomber Education Foundation considers scholarship and fee concessions case by case, subject to merit, need and available funds. An agriculture-specific current award amount was not verified.
