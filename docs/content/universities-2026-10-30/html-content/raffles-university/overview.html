Agricultural study linking field science and farm decisions

The Raffles agriculture course includes crop production and protection, plant breeding, soils, water management and agricultural economics. Teaching is described through lectures, seminars and practical work in laboratories and farms. The school also identifies departments spanning animal science, dairy, poultry, agricultural engineering and agrometeorology.

Rural Agricultural Work Experience and Agro-Industrial Attachment are part of the school’s stated educational model. These activities connect students with farmers’ fields and industry settings. The dated 2026-27 charge sheet separately identifies annual academic fees, one-time joining charges and optional accommodation costs, allowing applicants to distinguish study costs from their living budget.
