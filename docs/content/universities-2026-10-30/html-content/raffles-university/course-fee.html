Courses & Fees: questions answered

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Raffles University?

₹65,000 / year · 2026-27. Annual academic fee. One-time registration INR 1,000, admission INR 9,000 and refundable academic security INR 5,000 separately; hostel charges excluded. Included charges: Published tuition/course fee; additional charges must be checked separately.

Sources: [10]

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Raffles University?

4 years (8 semesters). The programme combines agricultural science, farm practicals and a final-year hands-on component.

Sources: [2][3]

What scholarship information is available for Raffles University?

The university FAQ says the Gomber Education Foundation considers scholarship and fee concessions case by case, subject to merit, need and available funds. An agriculture-specific current award amount was not verified.

Sources: [7]

How can I contact Raffles University about an application?

Published phone: +91-9928777777. Published email: admissions@rafflesuniversity.edu.in. Official admissions: https://rafflesuniversity.edu.in/admission-procedure

What is Raffles’ agriculture academic fee for 2026-27?

The signed 2026-27 sheet lists INR 65,000 per year. Registration, admission and refundable security are separate one-time charges; hostel costs are additional.

Sources: [10]
