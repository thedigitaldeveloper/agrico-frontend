An Udaipur university with a named agricultural college

Bhupal Nobles' University identifies its establishing legislation as Rajasthan Act No. 23 of 2015 and names Vidya Pracharini Sabha, Bhupal Nobles' Sansthan as its promoter. Its agriculture course listing provides a faculty contact and a defined undergraduate route rather than a broad catalogue of unrelated professional courses.

The course table lists a four-year agriculture degree for students from science or agriculture at 10+2 level. It identifies Hindi and English as media of instruction. The university’s general admission guidelines emphasise completion of the application and final university approval. Those general rules should be read alongside a current agriculture admission notice, which was not verified in this research.
