Minimum eligibility and closing rank answer different questions

A qualifying subject or minimum academic threshold determines whether an applicant can be considered under the applicable rules. A closing rank or score describes a particular selection list, counselling round or category. One should not be substituted for the other.

If a number is described as…Check these details before using it
Minimum academic marksQualifying examination, subjects, category and intake-year rule
Entrance qualifying scoreNamed examination and its relevant year/category instructions
Closing rank or scoreExact institution, programme, round, quota, category and year
An older admission figureIts historical context; do not label it as the current cutoff

Which rules should you read for your programme?

Admission to agriculture programmes at Pacific University, Udaipur is conducted through Rajasthan JET / Pacific University Entrance Test, alongside direct university merit counselling for eligible 10+2 Agriculture/Science candidates.

Both Rajasthan JET state counselling seats and university-level seats require verification of Class XII subject combinations (PCB, PCM, PCA or Agriculture) and original academic certificates at reporting.

Follow the admission process for Pacific University, Udaipur and the official notice. Seat availability and document verification remain separate from meeting a qualifying threshold.

How does admission to PAHER Udaipur work?

Admission to agriculture programmes at Pacific University, Udaipur is conducted through Rajasthan JET / Pacific University Entrance Test, alongside direct university merit counselling for eligible 10+2 Agriculture/Science candidates. Both Rajasthan JET state counselling seats and university-level seats require verification of Class XII subject combinations (PCB, PCM, PCA or Agriculture) and original academic certificates at reporting. Check Rajasthan JET or University Application Window: Register through the official Pacific University, Udaipur admissions portal (https://www.pacific-university.ac.in/College-of-Agriculture) or select the institution during Rajasthan JET / Pre-PG choice filling. Submit Academic & Entrance Score Details: Provide Class X and XII mark sheets (or B.Sc. Agriculture transcript for PG), JET/CUET/University entrance scorecard and category certificate. Document Verification & Tuition Fee Confirmation: Verify original documents on campus at Udaipur and obtain the itemised semester/annual fee receipt including hostel and examination heads.
