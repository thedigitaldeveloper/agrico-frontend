FAQs: questions answered

What is the B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture fee at Pacific University, Udaipur for 2026-27?

The published annual tuition fee for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Pacific University, Udaipur is INR 70,000 per year. Confirm hostel, examination and registration charges separately.

How can I get admission to B.Sc. Agriculture at Pacific University, Udaipur?

Admission is offered through Rajasthan JET / Pacific University Entrance Test for candidates who have passed 10+2 with Agriculture, PCB, PCM or PCA.

Where is the agriculture campus of Pacific University, Udaipur located?

The College of Agriculture (Faculty of Agricultural Sciences) is located at Pacific Hills, Pratapnagar Extn., Airport Road, Debari, Udaipur, Rajasthan 313003, India.

What practical farm facilities are available at Pacific University, Udaipur?

Southern Rajasthan instructional farm at Debari (Udaipur) with maize, pulses, protected cultivation polyhouse and soil-plant analysis laboratories.

Does Pacific University, Udaipur offer M.Sc. Agriculture or postgraduate courses?

Covered programmes include: B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture (4 years); M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) (2 years).

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at PAHER Udaipur?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy). The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at PAHER Udaipur?

4 years; study format: Full-time on campus. B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Pacific University, Udaipur is delivered on campus through the College of Agriculture (Faculty of Agricultural Sciences), combining classroom agronomy, laboratory practicals and field crop rotations.

How long is M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at PAHER Udaipur?

2 years; study format: Full-time on campus. M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at Pacific University, Udaipur is delivered on campus through the College of Agriculture (Faculty of Agricultural Sciences), combining classroom agronomy, laboratory practicals and field crop rotations.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at PAHER Udaipur?

10+2 in Agriculture or Science (PCB / PCM / PCA) with at least 45%-50% marks.

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at PAHER Udaipur?

4-year B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture degree from a recognized institution.

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at PAHER Udaipur?

What fee is documented for M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at PAHER Udaipur?

How does admission to PAHER Udaipur work?

Admission to agriculture programmes at Pacific University, Udaipur is conducted through Rajasthan JET / Pacific University Entrance Test, alongside direct university merit counselling for eligible 10+2 Agriculture/Science candidates. Both Rajasthan JET state counselling seats and university-level seats require verification of Class XII subject combinations (PCB, PCM, PCA or Agriculture) and original academic certificates at reporting. Check Rajasthan JET or University Application Window: Register through the official Pacific University, Udaipur admissions portal (https://www.pacific-university.ac.in/College-of-Agriculture) or select the institution during Rajasthan JET / Pre-PG choice filling. Submit Academic & Entrance Score Details: Provide Class X and XII mark sheets (or B.Sc. Agriculture transcript for PG), JET/CUET/University entrance scorecard and category certificate. Document Verification & Tuition Fee Confirmation: Verify original documents on campus at Udaipur and obtain the itemised semester/annual fee receipt including hostel and examination heads.

What practical learning is described at PAHER Udaipur?

Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE) in adopted villages during the final year. Experiential Learning Programme (ELP) modules in organic inputs, nursery management, Mushroom cultivation and seed production. Field visits to Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs), state agricultural research stations and agri-input processing plants in Rajasthan.

Which teaching facilities are documented at PAHER Udaipur?

Agronomy & Instructional Crop Farm: Field demonstration plots at Udaipur for kharif and rabi crop trials, seed bed preparation, weed management and micro-irrigation exercises. Soil Science & Agricultural Chemistry Lab: Equipped for soil pH, electrical conductivity, organic carbon, macronutrient (N-P-K) and micronutrient testing. Horticulture, Polyhouse & Orchard Unit: Protected cultivation polyhouse/shade-net house, fruit orchard nursery and post-harvest handling practical space. Plant Pathology, Entomology & Genetics Labs: Compound and stereo microscopy, insect rearing cages, disease specimen collection and seed purity testing equipment. Student Hostels, Library & Transport: Separate boys and girls residential hostels at Udaipur, agricultural library journals, mess and city bus connectivity.

What student facilities are documented for PAHER Udaipur?

Student Hostels, Library & Transport: Separate boys and girls residential hostels at Udaipur, agricultural library journals, mess and city bus connectivity.

What scholarship information is available for PAHER Udaipur?

Pacific University, Udaipur offers merit-based tuition scholarships based on Class XII / qualifying examination marks or JET rank, alongside Rajasthan government post-matric scholarships (SJMS / Samaj Kalyan Vibhag) for eligible SC, ST, OBC, MBC and EBC students. University Entrance & Qualifying Marks Merit Scholarship (2026-27): Tuition fee waiver slabs (10% to 50% in first year) for applicants scoring 75%+ to 90%+ in 10+2 or high rank in Rajasthan JET / CUET; continuation requires maintaining minimum SGPA and 75% attendance. Rajasthan State Post-Matric & Girl Child Agriculture Incentive: Eligible Rajasthan domicile students can apply on the Rajasthan SSO Scholarship Portal; girl students in agriculture may also check state Rajshree / Agriculture Department incentive schemes.

What training and outcome evidence is available for PAHER Udaipur?

The placement and training cell at Pacific University, Udaipur coordinates final-year agribusiness agro-industrial attachments, KVK internships and preparation for Rajasthan Agriculture Supervisor, AAO, Pre-PG, ICAR JRF and IBPS AFO competitive examinations. Graduates pursue roles in seed, fertilizer, crop-protection, micro-irrigation, agri-fintech, warehousing and rural banking sectors, as well as M.Sc. Agriculture through Rajasthan Pre-PG and CUET-PG.
