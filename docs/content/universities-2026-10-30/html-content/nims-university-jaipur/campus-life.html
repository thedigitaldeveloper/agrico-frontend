What is documented about the setting?

Dedicated experimental farm on the Chandwaji campus with drip irrigation blocks, vermicomposting units, seed technology laboratory and KVK field exposure.

How could the programme shape your week?

Read the actual practical components for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy); MBA Agribusiness Management alongside the academic timetable. Field work, laboratory sessions or clinical teaching may affect daily travel and preparation; confirm the arrangements rather than inferring them from a campus photograph.

  • Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE) in adopted villages during the final year.
  • Experiential Learning Programme (ELP) modules in organic inputs, nursery management, Mushroom cultivation and seed production.
  • Field visits to Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs), state agricultural research stations and agri-input processing plants in Rajasthan.
