FAQs: questions answered

What is the B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture fee at NIMS University Rajasthan, Jaipur for 2026-27?

The published annual tuition fee for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at NIMS University Rajasthan, Jaipur is INR 75,000 per year. Confirm hostel, examination and registration charges separately.

How can I get admission to B.Sc. Agriculture at NIMS University Rajasthan, Jaipur?

Admission is offered through Rajasthan JET / NIMSEE / CUET (UG) for candidates who have passed 10+2 with Agriculture, PCB, PCM or PCA.

Where is the agriculture campus of NIMS University Rajasthan, Jaipur located?

The NIMS Institute of Agricultural Sciences is located at Jaipur-Delhi Highway (NH-11C), Chandwaji, Jaipur, Rajasthan 303121, India.

What practical farm facilities are available at NIMS University Rajasthan, Jaipur?

Dedicated experimental farm on the Chandwaji campus with drip irrigation blocks, vermicomposting units, seed technology laboratory and KVK field exposure.

Does NIMS University Rajasthan, Jaipur offer M.Sc. Agriculture or postgraduate courses?

Covered programmes include: B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture (4 years); M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) (2 years); MBA Agribusiness Management (2 years).

How does admission to NIMS Jaipur work?

Admission to agriculture programmes at NIMS University Rajasthan, Jaipur is conducted through Rajasthan JET / NIMSEE / CUET (UG), alongside direct university merit counselling for eligible 10+2 Agriculture/Science candidates. Both Rajasthan JET state counselling seats and university-level seats require verification of Class XII subject combinations (PCB, PCM, PCA or Agriculture) and original academic certificates at reporting. Check Rajasthan JET or University Application Window: Register through the official NIMS University Rajasthan, Jaipur admissions portal (https://www.nimsuniversity.org/nims-institute-of-agricultural-sciences/) or select the institution during Rajasthan JET / Pre-PG choice filling. Submit Academic & Entrance Score Details: Provide Class X and XII mark sheets (or B.Sc. Agriculture transcript for PG), JET/CUET/University entrance scorecard and category certificate. Document Verification & Tuition Fee Confirmation: Verify original documents on campus at Jaipur and obtain the itemised semester/annual fee receipt including hostel and examination heads.

What practical learning is described at NIMS Jaipur?

Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE) in adopted villages during the final year. Experiential Learning Programme (ELP) modules in organic inputs, nursery management, Mushroom cultivation and seed production. Field visits to Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs), state agricultural research stations and agri-input processing plants in Rajasthan.

Which teaching facilities are documented at NIMS Jaipur?

Agronomy & Instructional Crop Farm: Field demonstration plots at Jaipur for kharif and rabi crop trials, seed bed preparation, weed management and micro-irrigation exercises. Soil Science & Agricultural Chemistry Lab: Equipped for soil pH, electrical conductivity, organic carbon, macronutrient (N-P-K) and micronutrient testing. Horticulture, Polyhouse & Orchard Unit: Protected cultivation polyhouse/shade-net house, fruit orchard nursery and post-harvest handling practical space. Plant Pathology, Entomology & Genetics Labs: Compound and stereo microscopy, insect rearing cages, disease specimen collection and seed purity testing equipment. Student Hostels, Library & Transport: Separate boys and girls residential hostels at Jaipur, agricultural library journals, mess and city bus connectivity.

What student facilities are documented for NIMS Jaipur?

Student Hostels, Library & Transport: Separate boys and girls residential hostels at Jaipur, agricultural library journals, mess and city bus connectivity.

What scholarship information is available for NIMS Jaipur?

NIMS University Rajasthan, Jaipur offers merit-based tuition scholarships based on Class XII / qualifying examination marks or JET rank, alongside Rajasthan government post-matric scholarships (SJMS / Samaj Kalyan Vibhag) for eligible SC, ST, OBC, MBC and EBC students. University Entrance & Qualifying Marks Merit Scholarship (2026-27): Tuition fee waiver slabs (10% to 50% in first year) for applicants scoring 75%+ to 90%+ in 10+2 or high rank in Rajasthan JET / CUET; continuation requires maintaining minimum SGPA and 75% attendance. Rajasthan State Post-Matric & Girl Child Agriculture Incentive: Eligible Rajasthan domicile students can apply on the Rajasthan SSO Scholarship Portal; girl students in agriculture may also check state Rajshree / Agriculture Department incentive schemes.

What training and outcome evidence is available for NIMS Jaipur?

The placement and training cell at NIMS University Rajasthan, Jaipur coordinates final-year agribusiness agro-industrial attachments, KVK internships and preparation for Rajasthan Agriculture Supervisor, AAO, Pre-PG, ICAR JRF and IBPS AFO competitive examinations. Graduates pursue roles in seed, fertilizer, crop-protection, micro-irrigation, agri-fintech, warehousing and rural banking sectors, as well as M.Sc. Agriculture through Rajasthan Pre-PG and CUET-PG.
