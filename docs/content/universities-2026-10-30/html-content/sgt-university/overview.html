Agriculture with rural and industry attachments

SGT’s undergraduate programme description combines crop, soil and plant-health subjects with practical components. It identifies Rural Agricultural Work Experience, Agro-Industrial Attachment and experiential learning units. These named components make the practical structure clearer than a general promise of hands-on study.

The university also advertises several agricultural master’s courses. Agronomy provides one route into specialist crop-management study after an agriculture bachelor’s degree. Published eligibility figures vary between an older 2024-25 schedule and the undated current undergraduate page, so applicants should use the final intake notice to settle the marks threshold before applying.
