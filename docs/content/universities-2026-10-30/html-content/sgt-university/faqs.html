FAQs: questions answered

Does SGT’s ICAR record include postgraduate agronomy?

Yes. The audited row prints M.Sc. (Ag) Agronomy as well as B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture.

What practical attachments are described?

The B.Sc. page names rural agricultural work, agro-industrial attachment and experiential learning units.

What does the 2026-27 application procedure cost?

The university procedure specifies INR 1,500 for registration.

Is an interview described?

Yes. The 2026-27 procedure describes an online personal interview before provisional admission.

Are the course-page fees confirmed for 2026-27?

The undergraduate page gives INR 87,500 per semester without a year. This has not been treated as a dated 2026-27 quotation.

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at SGT?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. (Agriculture) Agronomy. The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at SGT?

4 years (8 semesters). A full-time, in-person programme whose published structure includes field, rural and industry learning.

How long is M.Sc. (Agriculture) Agronomy at SGT?

4 semesters. The agronomy postgraduate course appears in both the university catalogue and audited ICAR row.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at SGT?

Undated course-page eligibility box: 10+2 PCM/PCB/PCMB or agriculture-related subjects with 50%; confirm intake rules.

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. (Agriculture) Agronomy at SGT?

Official catalogue: B.Sc. Agriculture or B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture with 55%; catalogue year not stated.

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at SGT?

₹1,75,000 / year · source undated; current intake not confirmed. Undated official annual amount; intake-year applicability is not established. Not a confirmed 2026/2027 quotation. The separate B.Sc. course page shows INR87,500 per semester. Included charges: Published programme fee; other charges are separate unless stated.

What fee is documented for M.Sc. (Agriculture) Agronomy at SGT?

₹2,50,000 / year · source undated; current intake not confirmed. Undated official annual amount; not a confirmed 2026/2027 quotation. The dedicated masters landing shows INR125,000 per semester for Agronomy. Included charges: Published programme fee; other charges are separate unless stated.

How does admission to SGT work?

SGT’s 2026-27 procedure describes online registration, an application form, a registration payment and an online personal interview. Admission is subject to eligibility and applicable regulatory provisions. Register and select the programme: The official procedure asks applicants to choose a programme or specialisation and generate a brochure number. Complete the application: The procedure specifies a registration fee of INR 1,500 and confirmation by SMS or email. Attend the interview: The 2026-27 page describes an online personal interview before eligible candidates proceed to provisional admission.

What is the recorded ICAR scope for SGT?

SGT University: B. Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture (Intake- 120); M. Sc. (Ag) Agronomy (Intake- 10), recorded term 2022-10-11 to 2027-10-10. This entry applies to the named unit and programme.

What practical learning is described at SGT?

Rural Agricultural Work Experience, Agro-Industrial Attachment and experiential learning units are named in the programme description.

Which teaching facilities are documented at SGT?

Research farm and polyhouses: Named in the undergraduate programme’s practical-learning account.

What student facilities are documented for SGT?

Optional campus accommodation: SGT describes separate hostels for boys and girls and says residence is optional. Its undated tariff lacks an explicit period, so no annual room price is inferred. Day-scholar transport: Students apply for pickup point and route through ERP; the transport team approves the request and issues a bus pass. Health and student support: The FAQ identifies access to SGT Hospital, counselling and mental-health support for hostel residents.

What scholarship information is available for SGT?

The agriculture masters landing explicitly excludes M.Sc. Ag. Agronomy from its displayed postgraduate merit-scholarship scheme. Other listed agriculture/horticulture programmes have UG-mark-based tuition concessions on that page. B.Sc. applicants and Agronomy applicants should obtain the policy for their own programme rather than assume those awards apply. Agriculture masters merit scheme: Agronomy exclusion: M.Sc. Ag. Agronomy is expressly excluded from the landing page’s postgraduate merit scheme. The page is undated and does not establish an award for a B.Sc. Agriculture applicant.

What training and outcome evidence is available for SGT?

SGT University publishes an Agriculture and Allied Sectors submission for NIRF 2026. Its programme-duration cohorts report the graduating group, students placed, students entering higher studies and the median salary of placed graduates. These historical institutional submissions are not a placement promise or a salary estimate for every student or individual specialisation.

How can I contact SGT about an application?

Published phone: 1800 102 5661. Official admissions: https://sgtuniversity.ac.in/admissions/admission-procedure

Which dated admission notices are recorded for SGT?

No dated admission event is verified in this profile. The official channel is https://sgtuniversity.ac.in/admissions/admission-procedure; check the notice for the programme and entry year you intend to join.
