Career support and reported outcomes

SGT University publishes an Agriculture and Allied Sectors submission for NIRF 2026. Its programme-duration cohorts report the graduating group, students placed, students entering higher studies and the median salary of placed graduates. These historical institutional submissions are not a placement promise or a salary estimate for every student or individual specialisation.

How to assess a placement claim

For B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. (Agriculture) Agronomy, ask for a report that matches the course, campus and graduating batch. Compare outcomes on the same basis rather than using an isolated maximum package.

Evidence to requestWhat to read
Cohort and reporting yearWhich programme, campus and graduating batch the report covers
Eligible students and offersHow the institution defines participation and reported placement
Roles and employersWhether the work and qualification requirements match the degree
Salary descriptionWhether a quoted figure is median, average, highest or another measure
Internship versus employmentWhether an experience is curricular training, a paid internship or a final job offer
