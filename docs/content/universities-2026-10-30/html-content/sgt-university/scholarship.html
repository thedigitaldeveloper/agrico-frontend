What is documented for this institution?

The agriculture masters landing explicitly excludes M.Sc. Ag. Agronomy from its displayed postgraduate merit-scholarship scheme. Other listed agriculture/horticulture programmes have UG-mark-based tuition concessions on that page. B.Sc. applicants and Agronomy applicants should obtain the policy for their own programme rather than assume those awards apply.

Which part of the cost could an award cover?

Start with the actual programme fee and charge notes. Ask whether an offered concession covers tuition alone or any other charge; do not subtract it from the whole cost without written confirmation.

Prepare a programme-specific request

Identify your proposed qualification at SGT University, intended intake and fee category. Ask for the scheme document, application procedure, assessment date, documentary requirements and award letter. Only submit the documents the applicable policy actually asks for.
