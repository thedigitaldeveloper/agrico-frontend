Agriculture learning in Sri Ganganagar

Tantia’s agriculture faculty introduced its four-year B.Sc. Agriculture programme in the 2015-16 academic session. The official programme catalogue also lists two-year agricultural master’s study across crop, soil, horticultural, economic and extension subjects. These subject choices provide a useful distinction between a broad undergraduate course and later agricultural specialisation.

The faculty’s infrastructure page describes an irrigated agriculture farm for technical training, together with laboratories and library facilities. The university’s revised 2025-26 brochure gives separate B.Sc. fee rows for JET and CUET. The fee distinction is historical evidence for that session and should not be carried into another admission year without a fresh schedule.
