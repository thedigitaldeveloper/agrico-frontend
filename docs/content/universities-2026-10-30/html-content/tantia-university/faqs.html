The faculty account dates the B.Sc. programme to 2015-16.

The undergraduate catalogue accepts 10+2 PCM, PCB or Agriculture; an intake-specific minimum-marks notice remains necessary.

Is there a training farm?

Are master’s subjects included in the checked ICAR entry?

How does admission to Tantia University work?

The university provides an admission-procedure page and programme enquiry routes. For agriculture, request a current notice confirming whether the intended application is through JET, CUET or another permitted category, together with the corresponding payable charges. Confirm the course: Use the official programme contact to confirm that the listed degree is taking applications for your intended intake. Request the intake notice: Ask for the applicable selection rules, fee breakdown and document checklist before submitting an application.
