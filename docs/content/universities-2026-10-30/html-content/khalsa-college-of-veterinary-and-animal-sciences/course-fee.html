Fee categories, inclusions and exclusions

B.V.Sc. & A.H.

Charge scope: University-quota tuition only; other six-monthly charges, one-time fees, securities and accommodation are separate.

KCVAS table 2.2.5(C), printedp 13: six-monthly tuition is ₹1,80,000 for University Quota, ₹3,80,000 forManagement Quota and ₹4,37,500 forSpecial Management Quota. Other six-monthly charges are ₹31,500. The published entry-time totals include one-time charges/security; hostel/mess and category terms must be read separately. Tuition applies to 4½ years of course work; KCVAS other charges continue during internship. No flat five-year total is inferred.

What each programme involves

B.V.Sc. & A.H.

B.V.Sc. & A.H. is a 5½-year veterinary programme in the GADVASU admission rules. KCVAS combines veterinary study with clinical and animal-management teaching; professional-registration conditions should be checked with the relevant authorities.

What scholarship information is available for KCVAS?

The 2026–27 GADVASU prospectus states that KCVAS students eligible for the Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme and admitted in the SC/ST university-quota category are charged the same fee as eligible GADVASU students. This is a restricted category rule, not a scholarship promise for management, NRI or every veterinary student. SC/ST Post-Matric Scholarship-linked fee treatment, University Quota: Prospectus section 2.2.5.2(iii) limits the rule to eligible SC/ST students under University Quota. Confirm current government eligibility, documents, reimbursement processing and the payable statement for your individual offer.
