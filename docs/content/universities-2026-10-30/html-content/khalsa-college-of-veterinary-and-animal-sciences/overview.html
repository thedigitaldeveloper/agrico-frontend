Veterinary learning with clinical exposure at Mahal

Khalsa College of Veterinary and Animal Sciences was established by Khalsa College Charitable Society. The college dates its formation to 2009 and GADVASU affiliation to 2010. It focuses on veterinary health, animal production and management, making it a distinct option from an undergraduate crop-science college.

Its Veterinary Clinical Complex is at village Mahal on Ram Tirath Road, separate from the main campus. The department describes services for small and large animals, diagnostic imaging, surgery and clinical laboratory work. For applicants, that clinical setting is central to evaluating the programme: ask how clinical rotations, case exposure and internship supervision are organised for the intended cohort.

Scholarships and financial support

The 2026–27 GADVASU prospectus states that KCVAS students eligible for the Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme and admitted in the SC/ST university-quota category are charged the same fee as eligible GADVASU students. This is a restricted category rule, not a scholarship promise for management, NRI or every veterinary student.

What are the entry requirements for B.V.Sc. & A.H. at KCVAS?

GADVASU’s NEET-UG-2026 rules require 10+2 or equivalent with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English and 50% aggregate excluding optional subjects (47.5% for SC/ST, BC and DP categories), plus the relevant NEET cutoff. Category, domicile and quota conditions also apply.

How long is B.V.Sc. & A.H. at KCVAS?

5½ years. B.V.Sc. & A.H. is a 5½-year veterinary programme in the GADVASU admission rules. KCVAS combines veterinary study with clinical and animal-management teaching; professional-registration conditions should be checked with the relevant authorities.
