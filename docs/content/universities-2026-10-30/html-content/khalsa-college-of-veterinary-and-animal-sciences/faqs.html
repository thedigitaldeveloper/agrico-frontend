FAQs: questions answered

Does KCVAS offer the agriculture degree covered on other profiles?

The audited ICAR row explicitly lists B.V.Sc. & A.H. only. No B.Sc. Agriculture accreditation is inferred for KCVAS.

Which university is it affiliated to?

The college and GADVASU identify affiliation to Guru Angad Dev Veterinary and Animal Sciences University, Ludhiana.

How long is the veterinary programme?

GADVASU’s undergraduate rules specify 5½ years. This current programme statement is preferred to the college’s older five-year historical description.

Where is the clinical complex?

The college places it at village Mahal on Ram Tirath Road, about two kilometres from the main campus.

When does the verified ICAR term end?

The audited term ends on 8 November 2026. A renewal decision after that date must be checked before describing later accreditation.

What tuition does the 2026–27 KCVAS table quote?

The KCVAS section of the GADVASU 2026–27 prospectus quotes six-monthly tuition of₹1,80,000 for University Quota,₹3,80,000 forManagement Quota and₹4,37,500 forSpecial Management Quota. Other charges, securities and accommodation are separate; this is not an annual or full-degree price.

What entry qualification is published for KCVAS?

GADVASU’s NEET-UG-2026 rules require 10+2 or equivalent with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English and 50% aggregate excluding optional subjects (47.5% for SC/ST, BC and DP categories), plus the relevant NEET cutoff. Category, domicile and quota conditions also apply.

Which named academic staff are listed for KCVAS?

The Veterinary Clinical Complex page provides named staff and roles; these are the public listings checked on 30 October 2026. Dr. P. S. Mavi — Professor, Veterinary Clinical Complex; Dr. Rajvinder Grover — Assistant Professor, Veterinary Clinical Complex.

Which scholarship or support information is published for KCVAS?

The 2026–27 GADVASU prospectus states that KCVAS students eligible for the Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme and admitted in the SC/ST university-quota category are charged the same fee as eligible GADVASU students. This is a restricted category rule, not a scholarship promise for management, NRI or every veterinary student. Prospectus section 2.2.5.2(iii) limits the rule to eligible SC/ST students under University Quota. Confirm current government eligibility, documents, reimbursement processing and the payable statement for your individual offer.

Which application documents are listed for KCVAS?

NEET-UG 2026 result/score evidence for the applicable Indian-national admission category; Original academic certificates and relevant quota/category certificates as specified in the 2026–27 prospectus. Match these to the official requirements for your chosen programme and quota.

Which learning facilities are documented at KCVAS?

What establishment year is documented for KCVAS?

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at KCVAS?

This profile covers B.V.Sc. & A.H.. The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

How long is B.V.Sc. & A.H. at KCVAS?

5½ years. B.V.Sc. & A.H. is a 5½-year veterinary programme in the GADVASU admission rules. KCVAS combines veterinary study with clinical and animal-management teaching; professional-registration conditions should be checked with the relevant authorities.

What are the entry requirements for B.V.Sc. & A.H. at KCVAS?

GADVASU’s NEET-UG-2026 rules require 10+2 or equivalent with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English and 50% aggregate excluding optional subjects (47.5% for SC/ST, BC and DP categories), plus the relevant NEET cutoff. Category, domicile and quota conditions also apply.

What fee is documented for B.V.Sc. & A.H. at KCVAS?

₹1,80,000 / six months · 2026–27. KCVAS table 2.2.5(C), printedp 13: six-monthly tuition is ₹1,80,000 for University Quota, ₹3,80,000 forManagement Quota and ₹4,37,500 forSpecial Management Quota. Other six-monthly charges are ₹31,500. The published entry-time totals include one-time charges/security; hostel/mess and category terms must be read separately.
