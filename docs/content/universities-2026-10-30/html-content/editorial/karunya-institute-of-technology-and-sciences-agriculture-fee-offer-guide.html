Start with the qualification you would actually join

Karunya dates its School of Agricultural Sciences to 2017. The current school page describes eleven academic divisions, covering crop, soil, plant-health, engineering and allied subjects. Its farm and laboratory setting supports a programme account that extends beyond lecture teaching to crop experiments, protected cultivation and agricultural enterprise learning.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. (Agriculture) Agronomy; M.Sc. (Agriculture) Genetics and Plant Breeding; M.Sc. (Horticulture) Fruit Sciences at Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.

Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

Karunya’s 2026–27 scholarship page explicitly includes B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture in its Indian-student merit grid. The separate Founder’s Scholarship offers a limited 100% tuition waiver for eligible Indian Christian applicants; its 2026 application and selection dates have passed.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.

B.Sc. Agriculture Indian-student merit grid, 2026–27

The agriculture column lists INR 1,20,000 at 95% PCM/PCB, INR 60,000 at 90%, and INR 40,000 at 85%. These are scholarship amounts, not net tuition payable. Obtain the approved award and applicable continuation terms.

Founder’s Scholarship 2026–27

Limited 100% tuition waiver for selected Indian Christian applicants with at least 90% PCM/PCB and annual family income below INR 8 lakh. Screening includes documentary verification and interview. The 29 May 2026 deadline is past.
