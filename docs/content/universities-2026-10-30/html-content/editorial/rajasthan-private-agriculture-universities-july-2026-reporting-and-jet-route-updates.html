Sourced July 2026 milestones at Rajasthan universities

Two Rajasthan universities in the research collection preserved specific July 2026 administrative dates on their public admission pages: Vivekananda Global University in Jaipur published 24 July 2026 as its undergraduate reporting date, while RNB Global University in Bikaner listed 30 July 2026 as the closing date on its 2026–27 admission procedure page.

Because university web headers often continue displaying "Admissions 2026" banners after a stated deadline passes, applicants must verify whether late or spot seats remain open before paying an application fee.

Comparing Rajasthan JET and university-level admission channels

Across Rajasthan, applicants can compare ten ICAR-listed institutions: Jagan Nath University, Jaipur, Jaipur National University, Janardan Rai Nagar Rajasthan Vidyapeeth, Vivekananda Global University, Bhupal Nobles' University, Suresh Gyan Vihar University, RNT College of Agriculture (affiliated to MPUAT Udaipur), Tantia University, Raffles University and RNB Global University.

Read Rajasthan JET and agriculture college admission routes to see where state JET counselling applies and where private universities use direct merit or CUET/university entrance tests.

Questions answered

Does every private university in Rajasthan admit students only through Rajasthan JET?

No. While MPUAT-affiliated colleges such as RNT College of Agriculture and designated state seats follow Rajasthan JET, several private universities also publish university entrance or CUET/merit routes.

Why do some Rajasthan universities show different fees for Rajasthan and outside-Rajasthan students?

Institutions like RNB Global University publish domicile-differentiated fee pages. Always check the fee schedule matching your domicile category.
