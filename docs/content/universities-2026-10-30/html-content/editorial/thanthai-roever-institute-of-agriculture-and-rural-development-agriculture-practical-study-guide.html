Start with the qualification you would actually join

Thanthai Roever Institute of Agriculture and Rural Development belongs to the Roever educational group and is run by St. John Sangam Trust. Its history records agriculture diploma teaching from 1994 and horticulture diploma teaching from 2001, with degree education developed later.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Valikandapuram, Tamil Nadu.
