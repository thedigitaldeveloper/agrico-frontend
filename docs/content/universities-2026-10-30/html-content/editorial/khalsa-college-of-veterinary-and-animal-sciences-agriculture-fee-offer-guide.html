Start with the qualification you would actually join

Khalsa College of Veterinary and Animal Sciences was established by Khalsa College Charitable Society. The college dates its formation to 2009 and GADVASU affiliation to 2010. It focuses on veterinary health, animal production and management, making it a distinct option from an undergraduate crop-science college.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.V.Sc. & A.H. at Amritsar, Punjab.

ProgrammeDuration / modePublished entry requirements
B.V.Sc. & A.H.5½ yearsGADVASU’s NEET-UG-2026 rules require 10+2 or equivalent with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English and 50% aggregate excluding optional subjects (47.5% for SC/ST, BC and DP categories), plus the relevant NEET cutoff. Category, domicile and quota conditions also apply.

Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

The 2026–27 GADVASU prospectus states that KCVAS students eligible for the Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme and admitted in the SC/ST university-quota category are charged the same fee as eligible GADVASU students. This is a restricted category rule, not a scholarship promise for management, NRI or every veterinary student.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.

SC/ST Post-Matric Scholarship-linked fee treatment, University Quota

Prospectus section 2.2.5.2(iii) limits the rule to eligible SC/ST students under University Quota. Confirm current government eligibility, documents, reimbursement processing and the payable statement for your individual offer.

Connect the learning activities with your own interests

The Veterinary Clinical Complex describes clinical medicine, surgery and radiology, and gynaecology/obstetrics services. It is located at Mahal, about two kilometres from the main campus according to the college.

Consider which of the documented activities you would like to practise and how they relate to the degree you are applying for. Use this list in a department discussion about the actual timetable and supervision.
