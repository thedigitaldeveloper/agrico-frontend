What the 3 September notice announced

Tamil Nadu Agricultural University's official press note dated 3 September 2026 announced second-phase undergraduate counselling for 9 September 2026. It also scheduled counselling for supplementary-examination candidates on 10 September. The announcement concerned the combined undergraduate admission process for TNAU and Annamalai University's agriculture faculty.

This report records the announcement. It does not independently establish that the scheduled sessions took place, or imply that the September dates remain available to applicants now.

The vacancy figures belonged to that stage

The press note reported a combined total of 5,907 seats, with 4,827 filled and 1,080 vacant at the stage described. It linked the second phase to filling the remaining vacancies. These figures are a dated snapshot across the process, not a vacancy list for an individual college.

Later allotments, reporting and further notices can change the position. Use the relevant subsequent official seat information for a specific decision instead of carrying this September total forward.

How to use the notice when comparing colleges

For background on affiliated options, see Palar Agricultural College, Adhiparasakthi Agricultural College and J.K.K. Munirajah College of Agricultural Science. Linking these profiles does not assign any part of the historical vacancy total to them.

Applicants should check the exact college-course combination, applicable admission channel and official allotment instructions. The TNAU route guide explains the background, while the counselling preparation checklist helps organise the documents and follow-up actions.

Distinguish the announcement from the scheduled session

This article is dated to the announcement. A scheduled session date serves a different purpose and does not establish that the session occurred. Keep both fields in an admission record instead of replacing one with a later review date.

Agrico reviewed this historical evidence on 30 October 2026. Check the official admission portal for the latest position before submitting documents, making a payment or travelling.

Questions answered

Why does the article date differ from the planned counselling date?

Publication and planned-session dates serve different purposes. This article dates the announcement, while its text identifies when the session was scheduled.

Are the 1,080 vacancies still available?

This report makes no such claim. The figure is the historical total stated in the 3 September press note and must not be used as live availability.

Did the notice confirm a seat at a linked college?

No college-specific vacancy is assigned in this article. Check the applicable official matrix and individual allotment rather than inferring availability from a combined total.
