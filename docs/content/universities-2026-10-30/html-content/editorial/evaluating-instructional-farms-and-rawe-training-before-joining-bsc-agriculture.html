Look past brochure photographs to weekly plot allocation

Nearly every agriculture brochure shows green fields and greenhouse structures. What matters for your learning is whether first-, second- and third-year undergraduates are assigned individual or small-group crop plots for sowing, irrigation, pest scouting and harvesting.

Compare documented facilities at institutions such as Centurion University of Technology and Management, Odisha, AKS University, Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences and SR University alongside RAWE and experiential learning in B.Sc. Agriculture.

Five practical-learning questions to ask the department

Take these five questions to your campus visit or faculty interaction and combine them with compare two agriculture campuses using a useful checklist:

  • How far is the instructional farm from the main lecture halls, and how do students travel there?
  • Does each student receive an individual crop-production plot during agronomy and horticulture practicals?
  • Which Experiential Learning Programme (ELP) enterprise units—such as mushroom, seed production, bio-inputs, apiculture or food processing—operated for the latest fourth-year batch?
  • How are host villages and Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs) assigned during the RAWE semester, and are travel or living allowances involved?
  • Are soil-testing, pathology and entomology laboratories equipped for hands-on undergraduate experiments?

Questions answered

What is the difference between RAWE and ELP in the fourth year of B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture?

RAWE (Rural Agricultural Work Experience) places students in villages, KVKs and agro-input centres to study real farming conditions, while ELP (Experiential Learning Programme) trains students in campus-based commercial enterprise modules.

Do all colleges pay a stipend during RAWE?

Stipend policies differ between State Agricultural Universities and private institutions. Never assume a RAWE stipend without checking the institution’s written academic rules.
