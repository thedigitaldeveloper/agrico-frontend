Start with the qualification you would actually join

TNU’s agriculture course connects crop science with work in farming environments. Its published curriculum includes soil science, horticulture, plant protection, genetics and agricultural economics. The degree page describes four years of study, with rural agricultural experience and an agro-industrial attachment in the final stage.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Sarisa, West Bengal.

ProgrammeDuration / modePublished entry requirements
B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture4 yearsCourse page: 10+2 with at least 55% in Physics, Chemistry and Biology/Mathematics, or the stated agriculture/horticulture vocational combination; reconfirm for the applying year.

Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

The approved 2026–27 fee schedule expressly excludes B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture from the university’s general 25–100% tuition-scholarship offer. Agriculture applicants should obtain written confirmation of any separate government or programme-specific support before using a scholarship in their budget.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.
