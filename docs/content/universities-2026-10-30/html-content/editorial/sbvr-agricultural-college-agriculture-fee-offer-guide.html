Start with the qualification you would actually join

SBVR Agricultural College is part of the institutions promoted by Badvel & Sidhout Higher Education Committee. Established for the 2016–17 period, it is located in Badvel, YSR Kadapa district, and affiliated to Acharya N. G. Ranga Agricultural University.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Badvel, Andhra Pradesh.

Prepare for the published admission route

SBVR’s academic page explains two entry routes. Applicants should check the current ANGRAU/state notification for exact eligibility, diploma equivalence, seat categories and counselling dates rather than use the page’s undated seat headline. For SBVR Agricultural College, the applicable ANGRAU counselling route now has a revised 30 October 2026 notice: registered candidates follow the stated November rank/category session and check the university’s live seat position. Being called does not guarantee admission.

Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

A current agriculture-specific scholarship schedule for SBVR was not verified. Request the scheme name, funding authority, category rules, renewal conditions and net payable amount in writing before relying on a concession.

Boys’ and girls’ hostels: The campus page reports accommodation for 200 boys and 200 girls, with single, double, triple and six-occupant room options. It describes reading and television rooms, drinking-water purification, dining and laundry facilities. Published capacity is not a live vacancy count.

Campus health centre and banking: SBVR describes a basic health centre and an arrangement with Sri Divya Hospital in Badvel for advanced emergencies. The page also identifies an on-campus Bank of Baroda banking/ATM facility.

SC/ST student support and anti-ragging: The college describes an SC/ST Cell that discusses scholarship and hostel concerns, alongside an institutional anti-ragging committee. Scholarship assistance should be distinguished from an approved award or a published fee-reimbursement entitlement.

Library: The campus page describes library and digital learning resources.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.

Connect the learning activities with your own interests
