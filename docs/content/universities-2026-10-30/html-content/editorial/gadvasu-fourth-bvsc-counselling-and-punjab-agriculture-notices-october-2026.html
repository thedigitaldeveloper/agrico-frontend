GADVASU fourth B.V.Sc. & A.H. counselling notice

Guru Angad Dev Veterinary and Animal Sciences University (GADVASU), Ludhiana issued a dated notice on 30 October 2026 regarding the fourth round of B.V.Sc. & A.H. counselling for the 2026–27 academic session. This round governs remaining state and private-college seats under the university’s admission framework.

Applicants targeting Khalsa College of Veterinary and Animal Sciences in Amritsar must track GADVASU’s official counselling schedule, category cutoffs, and VCI-recognised seat categories.

Separating veterinary counselling from Punjab B.Sc. Agriculture routes

In Punjab, veterinary admissions (B.V.Sc. & A.H.) operate through GADVASU and NEET-UG / university merit lists, whereas B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture admissions at Khalsa College, Amritsar, Chandigarh University, Lovely Professional University and Guru Kashi University follow each institution’s or affiliating university’s separate admission schedule.

Compare programme-level eligibility in B.Sc. Agriculture eligibility: subjects and admission routes and check ICAR unit accreditation in how to check ICAR accreditation.

Questions answered

Does GADVASU counselling cover B.Sc. Agriculture at private universities in Punjab?

No. GADVASU counselling covers veterinary, dairy and fisheries programmes under its jurisdiction, including affiliated colleges like Khalsa College of Veterinary and Animal Sciences. Private universities conduct separate agriculture admissions.

Where should I verify vacant seats before attending a late counselling round?

Always check the latest category-wise vacancy notification on the official GADVASU admissions portal before paying counselling or reporting fees.
