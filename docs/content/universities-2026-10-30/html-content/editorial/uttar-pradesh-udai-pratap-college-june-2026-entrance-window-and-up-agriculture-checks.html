Udai Pratap College’s 16–25 June 2026 entrance brochure record

Udai Pratap College in Varanasi (an autonomous college affiliated to Mahatma Gandhi Kashi Vidyapith) published its 2026–27 entrance examination brochure recording online application opening on 16 June 2026 and the initial application/fee payment deadline on 25 June 2026.

Autonomous and affiliated colleges in Uttar Pradesh often issue subsequent corrigenda extending deadlines or announcing counselling cutoffs on their notice boards; applicants should check the college portal for post-brochure amendments.

Comparing autonomous colleges and private universities in Uttar Pradesh

Uttar Pradesh’s five researched institutions span two distinct governance models: public-university affiliated colleges—Udai Pratap College in Varanasi and Tilak Dhari P.G. College, Jaunpur (affiliated to Veer Bahadur Singh Purvanchal University)—and private universities with their own entrance processes: Sanskriti University in Mathura, Integral University, Lucknow (IUET) and GLA University in Mathura (GLAET).

Questions answered

Do Udai Pratap College and Tilak Dhari P.G. College have lower fees than private universities?

Generally, aided/affiliated colleges under state universities publish lower annual tuition charges than private universities, but you must verify the current session’s exact fee schedule and hostel availability.

How do Integral University and GLA University select B.Sc. Agriculture students?

Integral University uses IUET and GLA University uses GLAET alongside qualifying Class 12 marks as stated in their admission notices.
