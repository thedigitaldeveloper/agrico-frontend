Start with the qualification you would actually join

Chandigarh University’s Punjab campus forms part of a multidisciplinary university with institutes spanning engineering, business, sciences and related fields. The agriculture qualification relevant to this page is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, named in ICAR’s accreditation decision. It should be assessed through its own programme information rather than through the university’s broader marketing claims.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Mohali, Punjab.

Read programme scope and career evidence together

ICAR’s 31st NAEAB meeting proceedings approved B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture from 19 July 2022 to 18 July 2027 and explicitly did not approve University Institute of Agriculture Sciences itself. This is programme-specific accreditation. The locally audited workbook identity and printed labels are retained below without expanding the approved scope.

CU pages display university-wide placement marketing, but the evidence read does not identify agriculture cohorts, recruiters or outcomes. Those wider figures are excluded from the agriculture profile.
