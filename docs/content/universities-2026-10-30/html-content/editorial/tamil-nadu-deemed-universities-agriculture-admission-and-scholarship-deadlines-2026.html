Sourced 2026 dates across Tamil Nadu deemed universities

In Tamil Nadu, deemed-to-be universities conduct their own admissions outside the TNAU single-window system. Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham scheduled its tentative AEEL 2026 entrance examination on 9 and 10 May 2026 and later recorded a Phase 2 Ph.D. Agronomy application deadline on 30 September 2026. Meanwhile, Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences published a 29 May 2026 deadline for its Founder’s Scholarship applications.

Vellore Institute of Technology operated its dedicated 2026 B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture merit-admission portal without an entrance exam, while SRM Institute of Science and Technology and Kalasalingam Academy of Research and Education published their own institution-specific entry and fee schedules.

How to compare deemed-university offers with state colleges

When comparing these five deemed universities, check the exact ICAR-accredited academic unit in how to check ICAR accreditation and verify whether a scholarship deadline (such as Karunya’s 29 May date) closes earlier than general admission counselling. Questions answered

Do VIT, SRM, Amrita, Karunya and Kalasalingam admit students through TNAU counselling?

No. They are deemed-to-be universities with independent admission portals, eligibility rules and fee structures.

Does missing a scholarship deadline mean general admissions are closed?

Not necessarily. Karunya’s 29 May 2026 date was specific to its Founder’s Scholarship scope; check the main admission office for seat availability.
