What the ANGRAU November 2026 counselling notice records

Acharya N. G. Ranga Agricultural University (ANGRAU), Guntur recorded a phased manual counselling schedule on its 2026–27 undergraduate admission portal following Phase II college reporting on 23 October 2026. Special-category and AP AGRICET manual counselling was listed for 2 November 2026, followed by AP EAPCET rank-band manual counselling sessions on 3, 4, 6, 7, 9 and 10 November 2026, and spot counselling on 11 November 2026.

Each session applies only to registered candidates within the notified rank bands and subject to actual category-wise seat vacancies on the day of allotment.

How this affects ANGRAU-affiliated private agriculture colleges

Applicants considering SBVR Agricultural College, N.S. Agricultural College, Sri Krishnadevaraya College of Agricultural Sciences and Sri Kinjarapu Yerran Naidu College of Agricultural Sciences should distinguish state convenor seats filled through ANGRAU manual/spot rounds from separate management-quota rules.

Read our ANGRAU private agriculture college admission guide and counselling preparation checklist before travelling for manual verification.

Documents and fee demand checks for manual counselling

Manual and spot counselling rounds typically require immediate production of original Class 10 and Intermediate marksheets, AP EAPCET rank card, residence/caste certificates, and readiness to pay the prescribed semester charge upon seat allotment. Confirm the exact fee category (convenor vs management) using the agriculture fee budget guide.

Questions answered

Does a manual counselling notice guarantee seat availability in every affiliated college?

No. Manual and spot counselling depend on real-time vacancies remaining after earlier online phases. Check the live vacancy matrix published by ANGRAU.

Are convenor-quota and management-quota fees identical at ANGRAU-affiliated colleges?

No. Convenor and management quotas have distinct fee structures approved under state and university norms. Confirm the exact quota on your allotment letter.
