Comparing Warangal and Hyderabad private agriculture options

In Telangana, two private universities appear in the audited ICAR List II register for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture: SR University at Ananthasagar in Hanumakonda (Warangal) and Malla Reddy University at Maisammaguda in Hyderabad.

Both institutions publish dedicated School of Agriculture pages outlining four-year Honours curricula, instructional farm training, and semester/annual fee structures separate from PJTSAU state convenor counselling.

Fee period, hostel charges and subject eligibility checks

Applicants comparing SR University and Malla Reddy University should verify whether quoted tuition figures are per semester or per year, confirm compulsory admission and examination fees, and review practical field-plot access using reading an agriculture fee letter before paying and RAWE and experiential learning.

Questions answered

Are SR University and Malla Reddy University affiliated with PJTSAU?

No. Both are state-enacted private universities in Telangana with their own degree-awarding authority and ICAR-accredited School of Agriculture units.

Can MPC (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry) students apply for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at these universities?

Check each university’s current eligibility table on its admission-process tab to confirm whether both BiPC and MPC streams are accepted for the intended intake.
