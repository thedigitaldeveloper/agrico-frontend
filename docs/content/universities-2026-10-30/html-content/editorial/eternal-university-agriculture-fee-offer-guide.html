Start with the qualification you would actually join

Eternal University is based in the Baru Valley of Himachal Pradesh and is governed by The Kalgidhar Trust. Its registration portal explicitly restricts programmes to female applicants. This is a material admission condition for families considering agriculture at the university.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Baru Sahib, Himachal Pradesh.

Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

Eternal’s published tuition-concession page contains a 2024–25 policy for newly admitted undergraduate and postgraduate students. It uses qualifying marks to set a first-semester tuition concession and separately describes certain family/category waivers. The session is historical; applicants need the policy for their intended intake.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.

Historical 2024–25 first-semester merit tuition concession

The published 2024–25 matrix gives 75% tuition concession for 95%and above,50% for 90–below 95,30% for 80–below 90, and 10% for 70–below 80, in the first semester for new UG/PG admissions, subject to the stated fee-payment condition. This is not a current-session scholarship promise.
