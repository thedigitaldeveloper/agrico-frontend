Start with the brochure for the right institution type

The ANGRAU BiPC information brochure for 2026–27 covers constituent and affiliated colleges. Its affiliated-college Category A counselling uses AP EAPCET 2026 ranks. An independent private university is a separate institutional category, so first establish which document covers your intended seat.

Write the college name and category at the top of your comparison sheet. This prevents a low fee copied from a constituent-college table from becoming your assumed private-college budget. Check the heading immediately above any table you use; two tables in the same brochure can describe different costs.

Check the entry route before registering

The brochure's Intermediate entry specifies BiPC/MBiPC or an accepted equivalent with physical and biological/natural sciences. Diploma candidates should identify the applicable route separately: ANGRAU also publishes AGRICET notices, so a diploma qualification should not be mapped casually onto an Intermediate application.

Keep your examination record, counselling registration and college admission as separate entries in your checklist. For each, record the official portal, the submitted information and the confirmation received. Ask the admissions office to identify the exact route if a college page describes more than one qualification.

The SBVR academics page is a useful example of why this matters: it discusses school-level entry and an ANGRAU agriculture-diploma route. Use the current university notice to establish how the route applies to your own qualification.

Read the exclusions and the period

The brochure explicitly excludes hostel, transport and mess from the convenor fee. These annual figures should therefore be kept separate from living-cost estimates and any one-time or refundable amounts on a college's written statement.

Ask the college to label each payment by purpose and frequency. If an offer quotes a semester instalment, request the full annual amount as well. If it quotes a package, ask which services and months are included. A package with an unclear period cannot be compared fairly with a clearly stated annual academic fee.

For management admission, obtain the actual charge and its category in writing. A published maximum is a useful reference point for discussion, while your payment decision needs a specific offer and itemised statement.

Build the living budget around your actual plan

Compare the same living arrangement at each college. A day scholar needs realistic transport and meal costs; a resident needs accommodation, food, travel home and incidental expenses. Ask about the number of months covered and whether staying during practical work or examinations changes the bill.

Keep an amount due at joining separate from the total expected annual cost. This helps a family see both the immediate payment requirement and the resources needed to continue. List uncertain items as questions instead of treating them as zero.

Use the agriculture fee-budget guide to organise the comparison and the fee-letter guide to review the college's written response. Neither a lower fee nor a higher fee establishes better teaching by itself.

Check category claims against documents

Treat a farmer, local-area, social-category or special-category claim as a separate eligibility check. Describe your situation accurately and ask which current rule and certificate establish the claim. Growing up around farming does not by itself tell you which admission category applies.

Make a document sheet with three columns: the requirement, the record you hold and the issue still to resolve. Check names, certificate validity and the issuing authority before submission. Keep a copy of any official clarification alongside the application.

Where a form asks you to select a category, do not choose the most favourable label on an assumption. Resolve the uncertainty with the responsible authority while there is time to provide the correct evidence.

Follow the current round and reporting instructions

The ANGRAU admissions index and official registration portal carry round-specific notices. Use the current instructions for web options, allotment and reporting rather than treating a past schedule as an open window.

Before arranging travel or making a payment, verify the allotted college, degree, quota, reporting requirements and payment destination together. Request the cancellation and refund terms for that academic year. Save the offer, receipt and acknowledgement as one record.

Complete the academic comparison as well: ask how field practicals, laboratories and rural learning are organised for the intended cohort. The college comparison checklist helps turn those questions into a useful visit or admissions discussion.

Revised manual counselling: the notice checked on 31 October 2026

ANGRAU’s revised manual-counselling notice addresses candidates already registered for the named 2026 admission routes. Its calendar covers special-category and AGRICET sessions on 2 November, AP EAPCET rank-based sessions during 3–10 November, and a registered-candidate spot session on 11 November. The schedule marks 5 and 8 November as non-counselling days.

Use the original timetable for your precise reporting time and rank band. The venue is Krishna Auditorium, RARS, Lam Farm. An invitation does not establish a seat at a particular affiliated college; the notice requires applicants to check the updated vacancy position. Its affiliated-college fee table retains annual convenor amounts and excludes hostel, transport and mess charges. These are dated channel instructions, not a promise that a future application window remains open.

Why a private-college last-rank table is not your college’s cutoff

The official 2025–26 last-ranks PDF separates constituent colleges from affiliated private colleges. The private-college table is grouped by region, reservation category, farmer/non-farmer status and gender; it does not identify a separate closing rank for each named college.

The footnote limits those figures to participation in the first two online counselling sessions for 2025–26 and warns against treating them as final ranks for 2026–27. Use the table to understand the published scope. Do not relabel a pooled private-college figure as the cutoff for SBVR, N.S., Sri Krishnadevaraya or Sri Kinjarapu, or use it as a prediction of your own admission.

Questions answered

Are these figures tuition-only or a four-year total?

They are the brochure's annual affiliated-college fee figures for the stated quota. Request an itemised college statement and avoid multiplying them into a guaranteed whole-course price.

Will I necessarily pay the management maximum?

A maximum does not establish your actual charge. Obtain the written offer for your degree, year and admission category before making a comparison or payment.

Can I use a constituent-college fee to budget for an affiliated college?

Use the table for your institution type and quota. The same university brochure contains different schedules, so check the heading and exclusions before using a number.

Does this guide confirm a seat or an open application window?

No. It explains the documented route and fee scope. Available choices, eligibility and current actions must come from the official notice and portal for your round.

Can I use a pooled ANGRAU private-college rank as an individual college cutoff?

No. The cited 2025–26 table groups affiliated private colleges together. It is not a named-college closing list and its footnote does not support predicting 2026–27 admission.
