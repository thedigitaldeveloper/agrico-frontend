Start with the qualification you would actually join

The Indian Agriculture College is located on the Kavalkinaru–Radhapuram Road in Tirunelveli district. Established in 2016 under Joshua Educational and Charitable Trust, it focuses on agricultural education and the farming needs of southern Tamil Nadu.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Radhapuram, Tamil Nadu.

Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

TIAC publishes information about the 7.5% government-school reservation route and past supported batches. This is a specific access/support route, not a universal cash scholarship. Confirm current schooling, counselling and funding conditions before claiming support.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.

The college describes Class 6–12 study in Tamil Nadu government schools and publishes batch counts through 2024–25. Current eligibility and financial coverage require the applicable official admission rules.
