Start with the qualification you would actually join

SRU describes agriculture as a four-year professional undergraduate programme. Its course page identifies instructional farms and a horticulture garden, while the R24 curriculum provides a subject-level reference. Rural Agricultural Work Experience, experiential learning and industrial attachment are named components of its teaching approach.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Warangal, Telangana.

B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture4 yearsOfficial programme directory: at least 55% in 10+2 with PCB; first-class two-year Agriculture/Seed Technology diploma after Class 10 is also listed.
