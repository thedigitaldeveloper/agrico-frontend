Start with the qualification you would actually join

The School of Agriculture describes laboratory experiments as a regular part of its teaching. It also reports a Centre of Excellence on Ecological Farming under the guidance of the Process and Products Development Centre associated with MSME. The centre’s stated remit includes sustainable practices and farm mechanisation training for students and farmers.

Prepare for the published admission route

Sanskriti publishes an admission-procedure page and links its application portal. Use the 2026-27 brochure for the programme-specific academic threshold and request the selection and document requirements for the intended course.

  • Signed admission form and proof of date of birth.
  • Class X and XII certificates/mark sheets; graduation or postgraduate records where applicable.
  • Migration/transfer certificate and conduct/character certificate.
  • Medical-fitness certificate from an MBBS or equivalent practitioner, and Aadhaar.
  • Originals and three photocopy sets; ten applicant and four parent passport-size photographs.
  • Prescribed result-awaited undertaking when applicable; anti-ragging undertakings at course registration.

Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

Sanskriti’s undated scholarship policy describes general categories including sports, defence, girls and disability. Its published conditions limit awards to tuition, allow one scheme at a time and require academic continuation without back papers. Agriculture applicants should obtain a dated programme-specific award; engineering and MBA merit tables are not agriculture entitlements.

Separate residential hostels: The university describes separate hostels for boys and girls, with cleaning and maintenance provision. A room offer and its applicable charges require written confirmation.

Campus and hostel safety: Its infrastructure account describes a secured campus boundary and measures restricting unauthorised entry.

Centre of Excellence on Ecological Farming: The centre is described as a training resource for students and farmers.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.

General-category scholarship conditions (undated)

The page lists defence, girls, disability and sports categories, with supporting-proof requirements. Only one scheme may apply, tuition-only limits and continuation conditions apply, and the stated exclusions include BAMS, BUMS, B.Sc. Nursing and Ph.D. Agriculture-specific current approval is still required.
