Dated notices across TNAU-affiliated private colleges

In Agrico’s audited Tamil Nadu research set, PGP College of Agricultural Sciences recorded its 2026–27 B.Sc. Agriculture application notification on 6 May 2026 with a stated closing date of 17 June 2026, aligned with the state’s undergraduate admission season. Later in the academic cycle, Thanthai Roever Institute of Agriculture and Rural Development published an academic notice dated 21 October 2026 for odd-semester registration of its continuing 2024 and 2025 undergraduate batches.

Separating new-admission application dates from continuing-batch semester registration notices prevents applicants from misreading an October academic circular as a fresh admission opening.

Using TNAU counselling and college notices together

Whether you are researching Adhiparasakthi Agricultural College, Adhiyamaan College of Agriculture and Research, J.K.K. Munirajah College of Agricultural Science, JSA College of Agriculture and Technology, Pushkaram College of Agriculture Sciences, The Indian Agriculture College or Palar Agricultural College, always verify both the central TNAU counselling allotment and the individual college’s reporting instructions.

Read applying to TNAU-affiliated private agriculture colleges for a complete breakdown of government-counselling and management-quota seats.

Questions answered

Is the 21 October 2026 notice at Thanthai Roever a new B.Sc. Agriculture admission window?

No. That notice governs semester registration for already enrolled 2024 and 2025 undergraduate batches.

Do all nine TNAU-affiliated colleges in this collection participate in TNAU counselling?

Yes, they are TNAU-affiliated private colleges, though seat allocation is divided between the TNAU government-counselling quota and the regulated management channel.
