Four regional institutions across Northern Hills and Eastern India

Students seeking ICAR-listed private agricultural education in the Himalayan states, West Bengal or the North-East can examine four profiled universities: Eternal University at Baru Sahib in Himachal Pradesh (a residential women’s university at undergraduate level), Shri Guru Ram Rai University in Dehradun, Uttarakhand, The Neotia University at Sarisha (Diamond Harbour Road) in West Bengal, and Arunachal University of Studies in Namsai, Arunachal Pradesh.

Each campus operates within a distinct agro-ecological zone, shaping crop practicals, polyhouse cultivation, soil management and local village attachments.

Checking gender rules, residential norms and domicile fee tiers

Before applying, note institution-specific policies—for example, Eternal University’s Akal College of Agriculture admits women candidates for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture in a fully residential hill setting, while Arunachal University of Studies and The Neotia University serve eastern and north-eastern cohorts with specific entrance or CUET/merit criteria.
