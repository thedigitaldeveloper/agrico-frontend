Six Madhya Pradesh private universities in the ICAR register

Madhya Pradesh accounts for six private universities in Agrico’s 58-institution ICAR-audited collection: G H Raisoni University, Chhindwara, AKS University in Satna, Rabindranath Tagore University in Raisen (Bhopal region), Sri Satya Sai University of Technology and Medical Sciences in Sehore, ITM University, Gwalior and RKDF University, Bhopal.

Each entry in the ICAR National Agricultural Education Accreditation Board (NAEAB) List II specifies the exact faculty or school name, the accredited degree (B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture) and the start and end dates of the accreditation cycle.

Why MP applicants must verify the exact school and campus

Several university groups operate multiple campuses across Madhya Pradesh and neighbouring states, while ICAR accreditation applies only to the specific unit and location named in the official register. Use how to check ICAR accreditation for an agriculture programme and RAWE and experiential learning in B.Sc. Agriculture when comparing these six institutions.

Questions answered

Does ICAR accreditation for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at an MP private university cover all other degrees at that university?

No. ICAR List II accreditation is strictly limited to the named agricultural unit and the specific degree programme(s) printed in the official register.

Do Madhya Pradesh private universities accept CUET or conduct their own admission processes?

Routes vary by institution: some accept CUET/ICAR or state scores alongside university-level entrance tests (such as ITM NEST) or Class 12 merit. Check each university’s admission tab.
