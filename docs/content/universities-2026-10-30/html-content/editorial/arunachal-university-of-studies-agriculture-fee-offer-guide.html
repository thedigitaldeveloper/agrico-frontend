Start with the qualification you would actually join

Arunachal University of Studies is a private university at Namsai. Its 2026–27 prospectus lists B.Sc. (Honours–Agriculture) as a four-year, eight-semester degree and publishes an annual programme fee of ₹90,000. The table distinguishes school-leaver entry from second-year diploma entry, helping applicants check their appropriate route.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Namsai, Arunachal Pradesh.

Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

The 2026–27 agriculture table describes government fee-reimbursement assistance for eligible APST students, subject to scheme terms and approval. The university also limits concurrent scholarship applications to one award. Its separate scholarship form deadline of 30 October 2026 is already past.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.

Conditional APST fee-reimbursement assistance

The 2026–27 agriculture table states reimbursement up to ₹50,000 for APST candidates with annual family income below ₹2,50,000, under Central/State government schemes and prescribed terms. This is a maximum conditional benefit, not an automatic deduction or a guaranteed award; the brochure does not give renewal rules.

Sources: [8]
