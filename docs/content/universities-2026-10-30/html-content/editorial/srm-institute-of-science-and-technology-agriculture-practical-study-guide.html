Start with the qualification you would actually join

SRMIST’s agricultural college provides separate four-year degrees in horticulture and agriculture. Its official 2026 workshop brochure places the campus at Vendhar Nagar, Baburayanpettai, near Acharapakkam. This location needs to be distinguished from the Kattankulathur wording used on an older application form and in the regulator’s recorded address.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Horticulture; B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Chengalpattu district, Tamil Nadu.

ProgrammeDuration / modePublished entry requirements
B.Sc. (Hons.) Horticulture4 years2026 agriculture application page: recognised 10+2 with at least 50% aggregate. Accepted academic groups are Physics-Chemistry with Biology and/or Mathematics, Botany-Zoology, Forestry, Biology-Agriculture or Agriculture; the vocational route is Biology with Agricultural Practices, including theory and practicals.
B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture4 years2026 page: 10+2 with one of seven published science/agriculture combinations, or the specified vocational stream, and 50% aggregate.
