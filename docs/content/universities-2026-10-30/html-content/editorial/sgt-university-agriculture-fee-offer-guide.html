Start with the qualification you would actually join

SGT’s undergraduate programme description combines crop, soil and plant-health subjects with practical components. It identifies Rural Agricultural Work Experience, Agro-Industrial Attachment and experiential learning units. These named components make the practical structure clearer than a general promise of hands-on study.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. (Agriculture) Agronomy at Gurugram, Haryana.

Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

The agriculture masters landing explicitly excludes M.Sc. Ag. Agronomy from its displayed postgraduate merit-scholarship scheme. Other listed agriculture/horticulture programmes have UG-mark-based tuition concessions on that page. B.Sc. applicants and Agronomy applicants should obtain the policy for their own programme rather than assume those awards apply.

Optional campus accommodation: SGT describes separate hostels for boys and girls and says residence is optional. Its undated tariff lacks an explicit period, so no annual room price is inferred.

Day-scholar transport: Students apply for pickup point and route through ERP; the transport team approves the request and issues a bus pass.

Health and student support: The FAQ identifies access to SGT Hospital, counselling and mental-health support for hostel residents.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.

Agriculture masters merit scheme: Agronomy exclusion

M.Sc. Ag. Agronomy is expressly excluded from the landing page’s postgraduate merit scheme. The page is undated and does not establish an award for a B.Sc. Agriculture applicant.

Connect the learning activities with your own interests
