Start with the qualification you would actually join

Udai Pratap College is an autonomous institution in Varanasi affiliated to Mahatma Gandhi Kashi Vidyapith. Its 2026–27 prospectus lists B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture alongside the wider college’s science, commerce and humanities courses. Agriculture is therefore part of a broader college academic setting.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh.

ProgrammeDuration / modePublished entry requirements
B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture4 years; semester system2026–27 official prospectus: candidates who passed or are appearing in Intermediate/10+2 or equivalent with Agriculture or Science subjects may take the entrance examination. It states no entrance age limit and a maximum 5% other-state admission allocation on merit.

Prepare for the published admission route

UP College’s 2026–27 prospectus allows agriculture or science Intermediate candidates, including those appearing in the qualifying examination, to enter its agriculture entrance process. Other-state applicants should check the stated allocation and counselling rules.

  • Qualifying-examination academic certificates
  • Caste/community certificate where applicable
  • Income and domicile certificates where applicable
  • Aadhaar identification
  • Supporting documents for any claimed weightage
  • Originals of uploaded certificates at counselling
  • Character certificate, transfer certificate and anti-ragging affidavit for completing admission
