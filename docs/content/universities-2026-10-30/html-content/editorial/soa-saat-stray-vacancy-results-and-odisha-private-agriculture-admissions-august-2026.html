SAAT 11–12 August 2026 stray-vacancy announcements

Siksha ‘O’ Anusandhan in Bhubaneswar recorded two dated late-cycle milestones on its 2026 admission notice board: the announcement of SAAT 2026 B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture stray-vacancy results on 11 August 2026, followed by M.Sc. Agriculture stray-vacancy results on 12 August 2026.

Stray-vacancy rounds occur after regular entrance counselling rounds conclude and require immediate document verification and fee payment from waitlisted or newly registered eligible candidates.

Comparing Odisha’s four ICAR-listed private and deemed universities

Odisha is a major hub for private agricultural education, hosting Siksha ‘O’ Anusandhan, Centurion University of Technology and Management, Odisha, Sri Sri University and GIET University. Each institution conducts its own university entrance test (such as SAAT, CUEE, SSU CET or GIETEE) in addition to accepting national or state scores where notified.

Questions answered

What is a stray-vacancy round in university agriculture admissions?

It is a late admission round conducted to fill seats that remain vacant or are surrendered after regular counselling phases close.

Do SOA, Centurion, Sri Sri University and GIET University share a single entrance exam?

No. Each university publishes its own entrance test and admission schedule, though some also accept CUET/ICAR scores for designated seats.
