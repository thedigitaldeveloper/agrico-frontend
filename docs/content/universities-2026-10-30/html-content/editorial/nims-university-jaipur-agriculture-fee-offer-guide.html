Start with the qualification you would actually join

NIMS University Rajasthan, Jaipur operates its dedicated NIMS Institute of Agricultural Sciences at Jaipur, Rajasthan, offering B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy), MBA Agribusiness Management. The curriculum follows the 5th Deans’ Committee / ICAR credit structure with core courses in agronomy, genetics and plant breeding, soil science, entomology, plant pathology, horticulture, agricultural economics and extension education.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy); MBA Agribusiness Management at Jaipur, Rajasthan.

Prepare for the published admission route

Admission to agriculture programmes at NIMS University Rajasthan, Jaipur is conducted through Rajasthan JET / NIMSEE / CUET (UG), alongside direct university merit counselling for eligible 10+2 Agriculture/Science candidates.

Both Rajasthan JET state counselling seats and university-level seats require verification of Class XII subject combinations (PCB, PCM, PCA or Agriculture) and original academic certificates at reporting.

  • Class X mark sheet and certificate (date of birth proof).
  • Class XII mark sheet and passing certificate with Agriculture / PCB / PCM.
  • Rajasthan JET / Pre-PG / CUET / University entrance rank card.
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) and Migration Certificate.
  • Domicile and Caste/Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/MBC/EWS where applicable).
  • Aadhaar card, ABC/APAAR ID and 6 passport-size photographs.

Check Rajasthan JET or University Application Window

Register through the official NIMS University Rajasthan, Jaipur admissions portal (https://www.nimsuniversity.org/nims-institute-of-agricultural-sciences/) or select the institution during Rajasthan JET / Pre-PG choice filling.

Submit Academic & Entrance Score Details

Provide Class X and XII mark sheets (or B.Sc. Agriculture transcript for PG), JET/CUET/University entrance scorecard and category certificate.

Document Verification & Tuition Fee Confirmation

Verify original documents on campus at Jaipur and obtain the itemised semester/annual fee receipt including hostel and examination heads.

Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

NIMS University Rajasthan, Jaipur offers merit-based tuition scholarships based on Class XII / qualifying examination marks or JET rank, alongside Rajasthan government post-matric scholarships (SJMS / Samaj Kalyan Vibhag) for eligible SC, ST, OBC, MBC and EBC students.

Student Hostels, Library & Transport: Separate boys and girls residential hostels at Jaipur, agricultural library journals, mess and city bus connectivity.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.

University Entrance & Qualifying Marks Merit Scholarship (2026-27)

Tuition fee waiver slabs (10% to 50% in first year) for applicants scoring 75%+ to 90%+ in 10+2 or high rank in Rajasthan JET / CUET; continuation requires maintaining minimum SGPA and 75% attendance.

Rajasthan State Post-Matric & Girl Child Agriculture Incentive

Eligible Rajasthan domicile students can apply on the Rajasthan SSO Scholarship Portal; girl students in agriculture may also check state Rajshree / Agriculture Department incentive schemes.

Connect the learning activities with your own interests

Consider which of the documented activities you would like to practise and how they relate to the degree you are applying for. Use this list in a department discussion about the actual timetable and supervision.

  • Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE) in adopted villages during the final year.
  • Experiential Learning Programme (ELP) modules in organic inputs, nursery management, Mushroom cultivation and seed production.
  • Field visits to Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs), state agricultural research stations and agri-input processing plants in Rajasthan.

Agronomy & Instructional Crop Farm

Field demonstration plots at Jaipur for kharif and rabi crop trials, seed bed preparation, weed management and micro-irrigation exercises.

Soil Science & Agricultural Chemistry Lab

Equipped for soil pH, electrical conductivity, organic carbon, macronutrient (N-P-K) and micronutrient testing.

Horticulture, Polyhouse & Orchard Unit

Protected cultivation polyhouse/shade-net house, fruit orchard nursery and post-harvest handling practical space.

Plant Pathology, Entomology & Genetics Labs

Compound and stereo microscopy, insect rearing cages, disease specimen collection and seed purity testing equipment.

Read programme scope and career evidence together

NIMS University Rajasthan, Jaipur is established by an Act of the Rajasthan State Legislature and recognized under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, 1956. Its NIMS Institute of Agricultural Sciences runs B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture and M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) and MBA Agribusiness Management following the ICAR 5th Deans’ Committee syllabus. Applicants should verify whether their specific cohort requires state Rajasthan JET counselling vs direct ICAR List-II programme accreditation.

The placement and training cell at NIMS University Rajasthan, Jaipur coordinates final-year agribusiness agro-industrial attachments, KVK internships and preparation for Rajasthan Agriculture Supervisor, AAO, Pre-PG, ICAR JRF and IBPS AFO competitive examinations.
