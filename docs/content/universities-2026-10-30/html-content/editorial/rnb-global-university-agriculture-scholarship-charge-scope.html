Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

The 2026 policy includes agriculture within its undergraduate scholarship grouping. Awards are limited to tuition; attendance, grades, backlogs and conduct affect continuation.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.

Undergraduate merit scholarships, 2026

The official 2026 policy sets achievement-based categories for its agriculture grouping. Confirm the award and exam-validity conditions; examination and enrolment charges are excluded from scholarship coverage.
