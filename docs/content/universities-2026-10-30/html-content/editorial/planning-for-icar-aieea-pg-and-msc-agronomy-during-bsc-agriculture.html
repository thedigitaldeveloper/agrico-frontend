Start by confirming your UG degree’s postgraduate eligibility

Many public State Agricultural Universities and the central ICAR AIEEA-PG counselling process stipulate that applicants must graduate from an ICAR-accredited programme or institution. Before or during your undergraduate studies, verify your institution’s status in how to check ICAR accreditation for an agriculture programme.

Students can also explore postgraduate programmes directly at researched universities offering M.Sc. Agronomy and allied specialisations, including Lovely Professional University, Siksha ‘O’ Anusandhan, Centurion University of Technology and Management, Odisha, ITM University, Gwalior and Tilak Dhari P.G. College, Jaunpur.

Aligning major subject groups with your thesis interest

Postgraduate entrance tests group closely related disciplines (such as Plant Sciences, Physical Science/Soil Science, Agronomy, Entomology & Nematology, and Social Sciences). Read choosing an M.Sc. Agriculture specialisation to compare qualifying degrees and research focus before your seventh-semester RAWE attachment.

Questions answered

Is M.Sc. Agronomy a two-year thesis-based programme?

Yes. Across ICAR-aligned universities, M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) is a four-semester (two-year) postgraduate degree combining advanced coursework, field trials and a research dissertation.

Can a 3-year general B.Sc. graduate apply for M.Sc. Agriculture at all universities?

Many SAUs and ICAR-aligned programmes specifically require a 4-year B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture or allied degree. Always check the target university’s postgraduate eligibility table.
