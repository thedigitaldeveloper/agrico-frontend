Start with the qualification you would actually join

Palar Agricultural College was established in 2017 under RS Educational and Charitable Trust. It is located at Kothamarikuppam Village, Mailpatti Post, in Vellore district and offers a four-year B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture under TNAU affiliation.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Kothamarikuppam, Tamil Nadu.

Connect the learning activities with your own interests

PAC’s official site lists agricultural teaching units, while its extension department describes rural engagement through RAWE and village appraisal. Confirm the current cohort’s fieldwork schedule and teaching-unit access.

Prepare for the published admission route

PAC instructs candidates to register with TNAU and obtain ranking for counselling as well as management admission. Its fee section refers to the Government of Tamil Nadu’s prescribed structure without publishing a verified numeric schedule.

Obtain TNAU ranking

The official page asks applicants for both admission channels to register through the TNAU admission website.
