Start with the qualification you would actually join

GKU’s agriculture programme page describes study in crop production, soil science, plant breeding, biotechnology and agribusiness. It lists a four-year bachelor’s degree and provides a defined school-level entry requirement. The wider agriculture faculty also offers postgraduate routes, which applicants should compare as separate programme choices.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Talwandi Sabo, Punjab.

ProgrammeDuration / modePublished entry requirements
B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture4 yearsOfficial page states 50% in 10+2 PCB/PCBM/PCM/Agriculture, or 50% in a two- or three-year agriculture/allied diploma after Class 10.
