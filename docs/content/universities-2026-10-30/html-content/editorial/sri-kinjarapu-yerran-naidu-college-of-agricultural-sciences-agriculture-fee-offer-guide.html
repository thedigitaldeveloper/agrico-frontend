Start with the qualification you would actually join

ANGRAU distinguishes SKYCAS from its constituent government colleges by listing it among affiliated agriculture colleges. The directory places it at Etcherla and gives 2016 as its establishment year. The college therefore needs to be assessed as an affiliated institution with its own teaching setting, while the university provides the academic and admission framework described in official notices.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Etcherla, Andhra Pradesh.
