Start with the qualification you would actually join

Tantia’s agriculture faculty introduced its four-year B.Sc. Agriculture programme in the 2015-16 academic session. The official programme catalogue also lists two-year agricultural master’s study across crop, soil, horticultural, economic and extension subjects. These subject choices provide a useful distinction between a broad undergraduate course and later agricultural specialisation.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture — Agronomy; M.Sc. Agriculture — Genetics and Plant Breeding at Sri Ganganagar, Rajasthan.
