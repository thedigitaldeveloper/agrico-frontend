Start with the qualification you would actually join

Khalsa College was established in 1892 and is affiliated to Guru Nanak Dev University. Its agriculture department has a long academic history: the official department account records degree affiliation to Panjab University in 1931 and then GNDU in 1971, with postgraduate teaching developed in 1967.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Amritsar, Punjab.

Prepare for the published admission route

Khalsa College publishes a dedicated agriculture entrance-registration link alongside its 2026–27 admissions material. Its linked procedure and fee PDFs were image-only in the reading tool. Applicants should request accessible current copies before relying on a cutoff or payable amount.

Register in the stated Punjab admission portal

The 2026–27 college document directs applicants to admission.punjab.gov.in and a separate new registration for UG orPG study.

Complete academic and personal registration

Enter the requested details, board/university roll number and passing year; follow the document’s instructions if academic data is not retrieved automatically.

Log in and submit the registration fee/form

Use the credentials sent to the registered phone/email, then complete the portal form and payment.

Follow the agriculture-specific selection notice

The college document labels B.Sc. Agriculture admission as entrance-test/merit based and leaves actual dates to be notified later. Portal registration alone does not guarantee a seat.

Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

The official homepage links stipends and scholarship information and posts dated support notices. Current agriculture-specific award terms were not readable from the linked PDF, so no concession amount or automatic eligibility is claimed.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.

Connect the learning activities with your own interests

The official agriculture department describes laboratories across its major subject areas. Its archive documents practical and rural engagement, with dates ranging across earlier cohorts rather than a guaranteed current-year schedule.

Consider which of the documented activities you would like to practise and how they relate to the degree you are applying for. Use this list in a department discussion about the actual timetable and supervision.

  • The activity archive lists RAWE training and experiential-learning modules.
  • Dated activity examples include commercial beekeeping and paddy-residue-management awareness.

Agricultural teaching laboratories

The department describes laboratories for agronomy, soils, crop protection, horticultural sciences and economics.
