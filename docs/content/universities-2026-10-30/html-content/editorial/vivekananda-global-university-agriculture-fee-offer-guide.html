Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

VGU’s scholarship page labels its detailed policy as 2025-26. It describes qualifying-mark, alumni/employee and co-curricular categories. Awards are restricted to academic fees for Indian students, credited in even semesters, and continue only with the stated performance and attendance conditions. A current agriculture-specific award should be confirmed separately.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.

Historical general scholarship policy, 2025-26

The policy requires at least 6 SGPA each semester, no detention for attendance shortage and only one applicable category. A later agriculture intake must not assume the older policy continues unchanged.
