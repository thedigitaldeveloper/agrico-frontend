Start with the qualification you would actually join

Sri Krishnadevaraya College of Agricultural Sciences identifies itself as a self-financed, unaided private coeducational institution. Founded in 2016 under Sri Balaji Educational Society, it is affiliated to Acharya N. G. Ranga Agricultural University. Its public contact address is on Alamuru Road in Anantapuramu.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Anantapuramu, Andhra Pradesh.

Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

A current agriculture-specific scholarship schedule for SKCAS was not verified. Request the scheme name, funding authority, category rules, renewal conditions and net payable amount in writing before relying on a concession.

Separate hostel buildings: SKCAS describes separate boys’ and girls’ buildings, each with 40 rooms. The published layout includes dining/kitchen areas, warden/caretaker offices and visitors’ space; rooms are described as shared by three or four students with basic study furniture.

SC/ST scholarship application assistance: The college’s SC/ST Cell describes help with government scholarships and fee-reimbursement matters for eligible students, including hostel residents. The page is a support-channel description and does not publish a current award matrix.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.
