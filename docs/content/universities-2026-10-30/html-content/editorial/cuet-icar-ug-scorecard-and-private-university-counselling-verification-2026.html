Central ICAR allotment versus university-level CUET admission

Applicants holding a 2026 CUET (ICAR-UG) scorecard encounter two distinct admission pathways: central ICAR online counselling for All-India Quota seats at participating universities, and direct institutional applications where universities such as Lovely Professional University, Chandigarh University, Suresh Gyan Vihar University, Tantia University, RKDF University, Bhopal and Arunachal University of Studies consider CUET scores alongside their own entrance or merit criteria.

Submitting a CUET scorecard directly to a private university’s admission office is an institutional admission route, not an ICAR All-India Quota allotment.

Verify domain-subject combinations and CUET scholarship slabs

Before relying on a CUET score for admission or a tuition waiver, read CUET and ICAR UG counselling: understand the admission route and confirm the required domain subjects (such as Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Mathematics/Agriculture) on the university’s official admission page.

Questions answered

Does applying for CUET (ICAR-UG) on the NTA website automatically enter me into a private university’s merit list?

No. Whether applying through central ICAR counselling or directly to a private university that accepts CUET scores, you must complete the respective counselling or institutional registration separately.

Do some private universities offer fee waivers based on CUET percentiles?

Yes, several private universities publish scholarship slabs linked to CUET scores, subject to programme applicability and renewal rules.
