Start with the qualification you would actually join

LPU’s agriculture school brings crop, livestock and post-harvest learning into one academic setting. Its facilities page identifies an animal husbandry and dairy unit, agricultural farms and subject laboratories. The breadth matters when comparing a general agriculture degree with a focused postgraduate specialisation.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. (Agriculture) Agronomy; M.Sc. (Agriculture) Genetics and Plant Breeding; M.Sc. (Hort.) Fruit Science; M.Sc. (Agriculture) Plant Pathology; M.Sc. (Agriculture) Entomology; M.Sc. (Agriculture) Vegetable Science; M.Sc. (Agriculture) Soil Science; M.Sc. (Agriculture) Floriculture and Landscaping at Phagwara, Punjab.

Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

LPU publishes university-wide scholarship categories based on academic performance, LPUNEST and other achievements. Agriculture applicants must establish which category and renewal conditions apply to their exact programme.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.
