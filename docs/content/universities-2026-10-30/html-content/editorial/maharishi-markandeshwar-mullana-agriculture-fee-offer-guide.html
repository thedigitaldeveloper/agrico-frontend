Start with the qualification you would actually join

The university’s agriculture pathway includes a four-year B.Sc. and two-year master’s degrees in agronomy and entomology. These courses serve different academic stages: a broad undergraduate foundation, crop-management specialisation and the study of agricultural insects. The 2026-27 information booklet specifies qualifying subjects and marks for each route.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture — Agronomy; M.Sc. Agriculture — Entomology at Mullana, Ambala, Haryana.

ProgrammeDuration / modePublished entry requirements
B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture4 years2026-27 booklet: 10+2 with five subjects including English and Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Mathematics or Agriculture stream; 50% aggregate, 45% SC/ST.
M.Sc. Agriculture — Agronomy2 years2026-27 booklet: B.Sc. Agriculture/equivalent with 60%; 50% SC/ST.
M.Sc. Agriculture — Entomology2 years2026-27 booklet: Agriculture/Horticulture/Forestry bachelor’s degree with 60% or equivalent; 50% SC/ST.
