Start with the qualification you would actually join

Jagan Nath University, Jaipur was established in 2008 under Rajasthan legislation. Its Faculty of Agriculture dates the start of B.Sc. Agriculture teaching to 2014–15. The agriculture setting includes agronomic fields, teaching laboratories and farm machinery, linking the degree to crop-production practice.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Chaksu, Rajasthan.

Connect the learning activities with your own interests

The faculty page describes 13 laboratories, agronomic fields, a tractor and farm machinery. This is an undated institutional inventory, so applicants should check current access and teaching schedules.
