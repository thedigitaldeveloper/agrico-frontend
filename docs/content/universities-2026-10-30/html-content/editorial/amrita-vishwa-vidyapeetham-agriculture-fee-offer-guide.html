Start with the qualification you would actually join

Amrita offers the four-year agriculture degree through its School of Agricultural Sciences in Coimbatore. The school emphasises field-oriented learning and connects agricultural training with technology. Its course list also names postgraduate agronomy and doctoral agronomy, creating a subject pathway beyond the undergraduate degree without implying that every course shares the same accreditation scope.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture — Agronomy; Ph.D. Agronomy at Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.

Prepare for the published admission route

The dedicated AEEL 2026 page includes undergraduate agriculture among its entrance programmes. The programme page states that admission depends on a valid AEEL rank. Reconfirm the final counselling notice and subject eligibility for the intended intake. The dated AEEL 2026 handbook specifies the agriculture PCB requirement and online application through applyug.amrita.edu. Its tentative 9–10 May 2026 examination dates are historical, not a current application opening.

  • Original academic certificates are required at counselling under the programme FAQ; photocopies alone are not sufficient.
  • The programme FAQ asks the candidate and a parent/guardian to attend the counselling desk.

Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

The labelled 2025-26 tuition table distinguishes scholarship and non-scholarship annual fees. The checked material does not establish the current award conditions or guarantee continuation of a fee concession.

Coimbatore hostel and mess tariff, 2026–27: The campus schedule lists old non-AC rooms at INR 52,000–60,000 per year by occupancy, and Kashyapa/Adithi rooms at INR 60,000–68,000. Annual mess is INR 62,500; caution deposit is INR 10,000. These separate campus costs do not confirm a particular room allotment.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.
