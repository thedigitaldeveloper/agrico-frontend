Start with the qualification you would actually join

The School of Agricultural Sciences is based at the university’s Dabok campus, rather than simply sharing the central administrative address in Udaipur. Its published teaching objectives address crop and pest management, preventing postharvest losses and taking agricultural knowledge to farming communities. These priorities provide useful context for applicants interested in extension as well as production science.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. (Agriculture) Agronomy; M.Sc. (Horticulture) Fruit Science; MBA Agribusiness Management at Udaipur, Rajasthan.
