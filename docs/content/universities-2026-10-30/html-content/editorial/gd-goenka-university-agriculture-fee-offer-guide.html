Start with the qualification you would actually join

The four-year B.Sc. Agriculture page describes study across crop production and protection, including plant disease, insects, weeds and nutrient problems. The school also lists master’s study and a separate agribusiness MBA offered by the School of Management. Applicants can therefore distinguish agricultural science specialisation from a management pathway.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture — Agronomy; M.Sc. Horticulture — Vegetable Science at Gurugram, Haryana.

Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

The scholarship policy expressly includes B.Sc. Agriculture in the 70–79.99% school-mark band and lists higher merit bands. Awards cover the stated academic fee, require evidence and remain subject to limited seats and the approved offer. The published scholarship-document deadline is 31 October 2026.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.

UG academic merit scholarship including B.Sc. Agriculture

The agriculture-specific 70–79.99% band offers 10%; 80–87.99% offers 20%, 88–91.99% 30%, 92–94.99% 50%, and 95%+ 100% academic-fee scholarship. Only one category applies. Continuation depends on performance and the applicable policy.
