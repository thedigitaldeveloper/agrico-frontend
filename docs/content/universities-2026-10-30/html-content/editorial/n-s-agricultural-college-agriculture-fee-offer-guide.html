Start with the qualification you would actually join

N.S. Agricultural College was established by Sri NS Educational Society in 2016. Located at Gobburu, eight kilometres from Markapur according to the college, it is affiliated to Acharya N. G. Ranga Agricultural University.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Gobburu, Andhra Pradesh.

ProgrammeDuration / modePublished entry requirements
B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture4 years (8 semesters)2026–27 BiPC route: Intermediate/equivalent with Physical and Biological/Natural Sciences; eligible ANGRAU diploma categories are separately listed.

Prepare for the published admission route

NSAC says university notifications lead the admission process and describes entrance-based counselling. Its site gives detailed but undated age, category and seat rules; these require the current university prospectus before use. For N.S. Agricultural College, the applicable ANGRAU counselling route now has a revised 30 October 2026 notice: registered candidates follow the stated November rank/category session and check the university’s live seat position. Being called does not guarantee admission.

  • All relevant original certificates required by the ANGRAU brochure
  • For already-joined students, the Associate Dean’s custodian certificate and college fee receipt where applicable

Identify the appropriate entrance route

The college distinguishes Intermediate applicants using EAPCET from agriculture-diploma applicants using AGRICET.

Follow the current university notification

Verify eligibility and counselling instructions with ANGRAU, then request the current category-wise sanctioned seat matrix.

Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

The admission FAQ states that eligible NSAC students may access the Andhra Pradesh fee-reimbursement scheme subject to prescribed criteria. No current scheme-year conditions, award amount or individual eligibility were verified.

Residential learning and mess facilities: The dedicated hostel page identifies separate boys’ and girls’ hostels with dining halls, study and sick rooms, recreation rooms and indoor gymnasiums. It also lists solar water heating, camera surveillance, backup generators and RO water.

Student Affairs Section: The college assigns hostel/mess coordination, health care, sports and cultural activities, NSS, scholarships and career guidance to its Student Affairs Section. This identifies the office’s remit; it does not establish a particular award or placement outcome.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.

Connect the learning activities with your own interests

The official farm page describes 74.8 acres: 43.9 at the main campus and 30.9 at Thokapalli. It lists 16 instructional units, including orchards, crop cafeterias, protected cultivation and a pest-and-disease-management module.

Consider which of the documented activities you would like to practise and how they relate to the degree you are applying for. Use this list in a department discussion about the actual timetable and supervision.
