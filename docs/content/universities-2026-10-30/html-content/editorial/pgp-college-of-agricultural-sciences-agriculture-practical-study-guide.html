Start with the qualification you would actually join

PGP College of Agricultural Sciences was established in 2013 and is affiliated to Tamil Nadu Agricultural University. Its dedicated college website describes a campus near the Kolli Hills and additional field training at the group’s main campus in Paramathi.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Namakkal, Tamil Nadu.

B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture4 years (8 semesters)The official college admission page requires the applicable TNAU eligibility norms. Current subject combinations, category thresholds and equivalence rules were not independently verified.

Connect the learning activities with your own interests

The programme outline gives concrete examples of practical teaching: seed and sowing exercises, horticultural propagation, surveying and irrigation work. These are published curriculum components, not confirmation that every listed visit occurs for every cohort.

Consider which of the documented activities you would like to practise and how they relate to the degree you are applying for. Use this list in a department discussion about the actual timetable and supervision.

Agricultural field setting

The college describes farmland around its Namakkal campus and additional training land at Paramathi.

Separate student hostels

PGPCAS describes separate accommodation for male and female students, with ventilated rooms and regular maintenance. A current room-allocation and residential fee statement is still needed.

Prepare for the published admission route

PGPCAS publishes a 65% TNAU single-window and 35% management split. Its 2026–27 page says applicants seeking management seats must first register with TNAU, then contact the college. These instructions need confirmation for a later cycle.

Complete TNAU registration

The college states that online submission to TNAU is mandatory for counselling and management channels.

Follow the relevant channel

For counselling, allocation follows rank and preferences. For management admission, the page asks registered applicants to contact the college directly.
