What the 31 October 2026 GD Goenka deadline covers

GD Goenka University in Sohna (Gurugram) recorded 31 October 2026 on its public scholarship schedule as the deadline for submitting completed scholarship forms and supporting documentary evidence.

This date applies specifically to scholarship consideration rather than establishing an open general-admission window. Applicants should always obtain written confirmation of whether a merit or sports concession applies to B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture.

Comparing SGT University, GD Goenka University and MM(DU) Mullana

Haryana’s researched collection includes two NCR campuses in Gurugram—SGT University and GD Goenka University—alongside Maharishi Markandeshwar (Deemed to be University), Mullana in Ambala district.

Questions answered

Does the 31 October 2026 date at GD Goenka University confirm open B.Sc. Agriculture seats?

No. That date was published for scholarship-form and supporting-evidence submission. Check the admissions office directly for intake status.

Are first-year merit scholarships automatically renewed for all four years of B.Sc. Agriculture?

No. Continuation usually requires maintaining a specified CGPA, attendance and discipline record each academic year.
