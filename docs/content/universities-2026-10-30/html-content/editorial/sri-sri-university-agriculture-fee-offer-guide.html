Start with the qualification you would actually join

The Faculty of Agriculture offers more than one route into agricultural study. University sustainability reporting describes undergraduate agriculture, horticulture, agribusiness and food, nutrition and dietetics, together with postgraduate agricultural disciplines. Applicants should compare the science and business orientation of these qualifications before selecting a course.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; B.Sc. (Hons.) Horticulture; B.Sc. (Hons.) Agribusiness at Cuttack, Odisha.

B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture4 years (8 semesters) · Full timeUndated official course fact sheet: 10+2 science with PCB or PCM and at least 50%; confirm the intended intake rule.
B.Sc. (Hons.) Horticulture4 years (8 semesters) · Full timeUndated official course fact sheet: 10+2 science with PCB or PCM and at least 50%; confirm the intended intake rule.

Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

SSU’s Scholarship Support Division helps its enrolled students find scholarship information and complete applications. Its published scope covers bona fide students across faculties. The checked page does not establish a dated agriculture-specific award amount or renewal entitlement.

Hostel and mess support: The finance help page directs hostel and mess questions to officeofdirectoratestudentaffairs@srisriuniversity.edu.in. It describes optional booking subject to capacity; applicants need confirmation of an available room.

Student grievance channel: The published student support channel is studentgrievance@srisriuniversity.edu.in. Individual dues and receipts are accessed through the university payment system.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.

Scholarship application support

The division disseminates available scheme information and helps students complete scholarship processes. A support service does not itself guarantee an award.

Connect the learning activities with your own interests

University sustainability evidence describes 40 acres of agricultural land, apiculture and mushroom production. Its report associates farm output with campus kitchens and local food procurement.

Consider which of the documented activities you would like to practise and how they relate to the degree you are applying for. Use this list in a department discussion about the actual timetable and supervision.
