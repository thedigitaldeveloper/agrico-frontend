Same university curriculum, different admission channels and fees

At private colleges affiliated with State Agricultural Universities—such as Palar Agricultural College under TNAU or SBVR Agricultural College and Sri Kinjarapu Yerran Naidu College of Agricultural Sciences under ANGRAU—students in the same classroom may have entered through two distinct channels: the state convenor counselling quota or the regulated management quota.

While the syllabus, university examinations and degree certificate come from the affiliating SAU in both channels, the annual tuition fee and admission timeline differ substantially.

Check the quota label on your fee schedule and allotment letter

Before paying any booking or first-semester charge, compare the published convenor and management rates in applying to TNAU-affiliated private agriculture colleges and ANGRAU-affiliated private agriculture admission and fee quotas. Also review reading an agriculture fee letter before paying.

Questions answered

Do management-quota students in SAU-affiliated colleges receive the same university degree?

Yes, provided the admission is within the university-sanctioned intake and verified by the affiliating SAU under its management-quota regulations.

Are state government fee-reimbursement schemes applicable to management-quota seats?

In many states, government tuition reimbursement applies only to convenor-quota seats allotted through state counselling. Confirm eligibility in writing before budgeting.
