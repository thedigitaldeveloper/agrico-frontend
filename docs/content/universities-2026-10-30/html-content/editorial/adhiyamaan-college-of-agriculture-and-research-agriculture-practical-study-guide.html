Start with the qualification you would actually join

Adhiyamaan College of Agriculture and Research was established by AERI Trust in 2016. The college describes a 122.5-acre setting at Athimugam, around 22 kilometres from Hosur. Its TNAU affiliation provides the academic framework for the undergraduate agriculture programme.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Athimugam, Tamil Nadu.

Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

ACAR publishes a scholarship-support sheet covering its enrolled cohorts. It names the 7.5% quota, First Graduate, Pudhumai Penn, Tamizh Pudhalvan, SC/ST/SCC and BC/MBC/DNC categories. These are reported support routes, not a promise that every new agriculture applicant qualifies.

Residential study and everyday services: The hostel description lists Wi-Fi, RO drinking water, reading space, newspapers and indoor games. These are published amenities; room allocation and charges are not supplied by that page.

Hostel health support: ACAR describes first-aid supplies and a vehicle for taking students requiring medical attention to a hospital in Hosur. This is a published support arrangement, not a medical-service availability guarantee.

Library: A dedicated library section is provided on the official college website.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.

Reported state-support and scholarship categories

The institutional sheet lists the named quota/community and state schemes and marks UMIS updates/verification. Request the current scheme notice, award amount and your category-specific documents from the scholarship office; its published nodal contact is office@acar.ac.in.
