Start with the qualification you would actually join

RNTU’s agriculture faculty describes its original purpose as expanding agricultural education in underserved parts of Raisen district. The degree began in 2015-16. The current course account covers agronomy, horticulture, soil science, plant health, breeding, economics and extension, bringing production science and the social setting of farming into the same undergraduate route.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agronomy; M.Sc. Horticulture — Fruit Science at Raisen district, near Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh.

Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

The admissions page names Shiksha Mitra Scholarships but its displayed 2022 scheme explicitly excludes B.Sc. Agriculture. Applicants should not assume that the advertised percentage waivers apply to this degree.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.
