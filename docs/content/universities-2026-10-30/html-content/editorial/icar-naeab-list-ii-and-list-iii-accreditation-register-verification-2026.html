What ICAR List II and List III actually record

The Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) publishes separate accreditation registers through its National Agricultural Education Accreditation Board (NAEAB). List II covers private agricultural colleges affiliated to State Agricultural Universities (such as Adhiparasakthi Agricultural College and SBVR Agricultural College) as well as private and deemed universities (such as Lovely Professional University, Centurion University of Technology and Management, Odisha and Vellore Institute of Technology).

List III records accredited constituent and affiliated colleges of public general universities, including Udai Pratap College, Khalsa College, Amritsar and Tilak Dhari P.G. College, Jaunpur.

Checking programme name and validity period before paying fees

An institution’s presence in the ICAR workbook does not mean every course or every campus is accredited in perpetuity. Each row specifies the exact academic unit, the listed degree(s) and the five-year validity term. Read how to check ICAR accreditation for an agriculture programme for a step-by-step verification guide.

Questions answered

Why does ICAR accreditation matter for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture students?

Many State Agricultural Universities and ICAR AIEEA-PG counselling brochures require graduates to hold a four-year degree from an ICAR-accredited institution/programme for postgraduate admission.

What is the difference between ICAR List II and List III?

List II covers private colleges and private/deemed universities, while List III covers constituent and affiliated colleges of public general universities.
