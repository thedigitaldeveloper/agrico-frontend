Start with the qualification you would actually join

SGVU was established through Rajasthan legislation in 2008. Its agriculture landing page lists B.Sc. Agriculture, agricultural engineering and master’s and doctoral subjects in agriculture and horticulture. The undergraduate agriculture degree is the programme explicitly printed in the checked ICAR row.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture — Agronomy; M.Sc. Horticulture — Vegetable Science at Jaipur, Rajasthan.

Connect the learning activities with your own interests

SGVU’s school page identifies instructional farms and protected-cultivation units. A separate school account describes farmer-student and farmer-faculty interactions on campus and in an adopted village.

Consider which of the documented activities you would like to practise and how they relate to the degree you are applying for. Use this list in a department discussion about the actual timetable and supervision.

  • The school’s farmer-interaction account describes work with farm families and extension tools.
  • The official school brochure describes summer/winter internships, field training and educational visits to ICAR research institutes, agricultural universities and KVKs. These are described learning activities, not guaranteed placement offers.
