Start with the qualification you would actually join

MRU’s agricultural sciences school lists the agriculture bachelor’s degree alongside horticulture and food science. The university locates the school at Maisammaguda, Dulapally, in Hyderabad. Its dedicated school pages identify academic practices and provide access points for curriculum, staff and facilities information.
