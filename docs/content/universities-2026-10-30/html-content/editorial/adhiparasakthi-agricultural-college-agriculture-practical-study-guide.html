Start with the qualification you would actually join

Adhiparasakthi Agricultural College is based at G. B. Nagar in Kalavai, Ranipet district. It is affiliated to Tamil Nadu Agricultural University and focuses on agriculture education, research and rural development. Its learning environment combines academic study with laboratory and farm facilities.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Kalavai, Tamil Nadu.

ProgrammeDuration / modePublished entry requirements
B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture4 yearsThe undated official course page lists 10+2 or equivalent with Physics, Chemistry and Biology/Botany/Zoology, and also refers to Agricultural Science/Vocational Agriculture streams. Minimum aggregate is subject to applicable admission regulations.

Prepare for the published admission route

Adhiparasakthi Agricultural College describes an application review followed by counselling or document verification. Its B.Sc. Agriculture page requires the relevant higher-secondary science or vocational agriculture background, while the applicable TNAU rules determine the final qualifying threshold and seat route.

These steps describe the TNAU counselling channel. Any separate college-management route must be checked independently.

  • Higher-secondary/12th marksheet for UG applicants
  • Transfer certificate
  • Community certificate where applicable
  • Conduct/character certificate
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Aadhaar card or another valid identity document
  • Originals for verification and application photocopies
