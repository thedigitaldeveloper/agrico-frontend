Start with the qualification you would actually join

The M.S. Swaminathan School of Agriculture describes learning through fields, laboratories and enterprise activity. Its school page identifies agronomy, crop improvement, horticulture, soil science and extension as teaching and research areas. Student READY activities connect experiential work, rural exposure and agro-industrial attachment.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; B.Tech. Agricultural Engineering; M.Sc. (Agri.) Agronomy; M.Sc. (Agri.) Agricultural Extension Education; M.Sc. (Agri.) Genetics and Plant Breeding; M.Sc. (Hort.) Vegetable Science; MBA Agribusiness Management; Bachelor of Fisheries Science (B.F.Sc.) at Paralakhemundi, Odisha.

Keep scholarship and residential arrangements separate

The published Amrit Kaal table includes Agricultural Engineering within its non-CSE B.Tech group at Paralakhemundi/Bhubaneswar. It sets first-year fee concessions by qualifying results. This is not evidence of the same award for B.Sc. Agriculture, M.Sc. Agriculture or Fisheries, and the page does not give a governing academic year.

Use the award terms to identify which fee heads a scholarship can cover. Residential costs and the amount initially payable should remain visible in your budget until your own offer confirms them.

Amrit Kaal scholarship: non-CSE B.Tech including Agri

The official table lists 10% for 60–70% qualifying marks and 15% for 70–80%; a separate top-score/JEE/OJEE band lists 20%. These are first-year fee concessions. Boundary wording, programme/campus eligibility and the current policy must be confirmed in the award letter.
