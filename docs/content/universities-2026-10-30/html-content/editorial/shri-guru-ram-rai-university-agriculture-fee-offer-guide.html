Start with the qualification you would actually join

SGRRU places agriculture in a dedicated constituent school. Its departmental list includes agronomy, horticulture, seed science, soil science, entomology, plant pathology, genetics and plant breeding, and food technology. For an undergraduate applicant, this shows the range of scientific subjects surrounding the agriculture degree rather than a single crop specialism.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture — Agronomy; M.Sc. Agriculture — Horticulture at Dehradun, Uttarakhand.
