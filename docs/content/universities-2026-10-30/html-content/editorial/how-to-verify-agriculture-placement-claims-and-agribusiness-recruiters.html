Why cohort tables tell you more than a recruiter logo wall

Multi-faculty private and deemed universities often display a single campus-wide recruiter banner covering B.Tech., MBA, Pharmacy and Agriculture together. A meaningful placement check asks how many students graduated in minimum time from the agriculture programme specifically, how many accepted job offers, what the median salary was, and how many progressed to higher studies.

On Agrico, when an institution such as Vellore Institute of Technology, Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Lovely Professional University or Siksha ‘O’ Anusandhan has sourced cohort or recruiter records, the Training & Placements tab keeps the exact reporting year and programme scope visible.

Balancing campus placements, higher studies and competitive exams

In agricultural sciences, a large share of graduates choose M.Sc. Agronomy, MBA in Agribusiness Management, or public-sector examinations (such as IBPS AFO, NABARD, FCI and State Agriculture Officer posts) rather than immediate campus placement. Read plan an agriculture study pathway after Class 12 to align your college choice with your post-graduation goal.

Questions answered

Does a highest-package headline on a university website apply to B.Sc. Agriculture?

Rarely. University-wide highest packages usually belong to engineering or management programmes. Always ask for the median salary and recruiter list specific to the B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture batch.

Why do NIRF tables show many agriculture graduates under "Higher Studies"?

Many B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture graduates immediately pursue M.Sc. Agriculture, MBA Agribusiness or research fellowships, which are recorded separately from campus job placements.
