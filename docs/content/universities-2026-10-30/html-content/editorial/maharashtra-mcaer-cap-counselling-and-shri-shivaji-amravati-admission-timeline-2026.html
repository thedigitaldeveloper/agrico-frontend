The 2026–27 Maharashtra CAP timeline on record

For Maharashtra’s Centralized Admission Process (CAP) governing affiliated agriculture colleges such as Shri Shivaji Agriculture College, Amravati (affiliated to Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh Krishi Vidyapeeth, Akola), the 2026–27 state calendar opened online registration on 10 July 2026 with an application deadline of 2 August 2026.

The first CAP allotment was published on 16 August 2026, followed by subsequent rounds and the college-level admission round from 13 September to 17 September 2026, with 17 September 2026 recorded as the final admission cutoff date for the cycle.

Why a final admission cutoff date is not a closing score

Applicants reading archived CAP schedules should note that "17 September 2026 final admission cutoff" refers to the administrative deadline for completing admissions, not a MHT-CET percentile or rank threshold. Questions answered

Which university affiliates Shri Shivaji Agriculture College, Amravati?

It is affiliated with Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh Krishi Vidyapeeth (Dr. PDKV), Akola in Maharashtra.

Were college-level admissions open after 17 September 2026 in the recorded schedule?

No. The 2026–27 schedule recorded 13–17 September 2026 for the college-level round and 17 September 2026 as the final admission cutoff date.
