Start with the qualification you would actually join

The undergraduate agriculture course at AKS sits within a faculty that also publishes curricula for agricultural engineering, food technology and several postgraduate agricultural subjects. This allows applicants to distinguish a broad first degree from later specialist study rather than treating every agriculture-related course as the same qualification.

The qualification determines the subjects, practical work and entry route you need to compare. This guide focuses on B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. (Agriculture) Agronomy at Satna, Madhya Pradesh.

ProgrammeDuration / modePublished entry requirements
B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture4 years2026-27: 12th PCM/PCB/Agriculture stream with at least 60%.
M.Sc. (Agriculture) Agronomy2 years (4 semesters)2026-27: B.Sc. Agriculture with at least 60%, including RAWE and experiential work.

Connect the learning activities with your own interests

The agronomy department reports practical training alongside classroom teaching. Its extension activities include workshops, field visits and demonstrations intended to connect university knowledge with farming practice.

Consider which of the documented activities you would like to practise and how they relate to the degree you are applying for. Use this list in a department discussion about the actual timetable and supervision.

  • Department-led farmer training and demonstrations are described on the agronomy page.
  • The B.Sc. curriculum assigns practical credit to subjects including soil science, crop physiology and entomology.

Faculty teaching laboratories
