Agriculture departments within a historic Jaunpur college

T.D. College traces its origins to a school founded by Sri Tilak Dhari Singh in 1914; its own history dates degree-college status to July 1947. Located in Husainabad, Jaunpur, it is a privately managed grant-in-aid college affiliated to Veer Bahadur Singh Purvanchal University. The founding year therefore describes the institution’s origins rather than the start of degree-level agriculture.

The agriculture faculty spans agronomy, genetics and plant breeding, agricultural economics, engineering, entomology, extension, horticulture, plant pathology, animal husbandry and soil science. The undergraduate agriculture degree is complemented by six postgraduate specialisms in the ICAR register. Applicants interested in a particular specialism can compare the departmental faculty list and the current entrance bulletin before choosing a route.
