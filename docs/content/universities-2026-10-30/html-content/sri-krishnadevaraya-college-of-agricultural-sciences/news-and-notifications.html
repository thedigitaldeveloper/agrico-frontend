How does admission to SKCAS work?

The college homepage carries a 2026–27 management-seat banner, while a retrievable university web-options notice relates to 2022–23. Neither establishes today’s vacancy position or current entry thresholds. Use the current ANGRAU notification and confirm availability directly. For Sri Krishnadevaraya College of Agricultural Sciences, the applicable ANGRAU counselling route now has a revised 30 October 2026 notice: registered candidates follow the stated November rank/category session and check the university’s live seat position. Being called does not guarantee admission. Use the Intermediate route where applicable: The course page requires the relevant Intermediate science qualification and AP EAPCET; the applicable ANGRAU notice determines the actual counselling process. Use the diploma route only with the stated qualification: The college describes SSC plus an ANGRAU two-year Agriculture diploma and AGRICET. Match your exact diploma and category to the2026–27 ANGRAU rules. Request a written cost statement: Obtain tuition, compulsory charges, accommodation options and refund terms for the intended entry year.
