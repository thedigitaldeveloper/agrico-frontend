The documented academic setting

SKCAS’s departmental descriptions identify a horticultural garden with propagation structures and model orchards, and a soil-science teaching laboratory. Entomology material links final-year rural experience to crop-pest problems.

Sources: [6][7][11][12]

Facilities identified in the sources

Horticultural teaching garden

The department describes propagation structures, a crop cafeteria and model fruit orchards.

Sources: [7]

Soil-science laboratory

A dedicated laboratory is described for undergraduate practical experiments.

Sources: [7]

Separate hostel buildings

SKCAS describes separate boys’ and girls’ buildings, each with 40 rooms. The published layout includes dining/kitchen areas, warden/caretaker offices and visitors’ space; rooms are described as shared by three or four students with basic study furniture.

Sources: [11]

SC/ST scholarship application assistance

The college’s SC/ST Cell describes help with government scholarships and fee-reimbursement matters for eligible students, including hostel residents. The page is a support-channel description and does not publish a current award matrix.

Sources: [12]
