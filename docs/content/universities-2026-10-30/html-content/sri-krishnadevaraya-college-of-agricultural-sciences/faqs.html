FAQs: questions answered

Is SKCAS a university?

No. It is an unaided private college affiliated to ANGRAU, according to the college and university directory.

When did the college begin?

The official about page states 2016.

Is this Sri Krishnadevaraya University?

No. This profile is for Sri Krishnadevaraya College of Agricultural Sciences, the ANGRAU-affiliated agriculture college.

Does the homepage banner prove a seat is available?

No. Its 2026–27 management-seat message needs direct confirmation and a current university seat-allotment notice.

What ICAR term is recorded?

The audited B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture row covers 8 February 2024 to 7 February 2029.
