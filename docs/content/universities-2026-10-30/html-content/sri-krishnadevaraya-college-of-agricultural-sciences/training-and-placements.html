Which teaching facilities are documented at SKCAS?

Horticultural teaching garden: The department describes propagation structures, a crop cafeteria and model fruit orchards. Soil-science laboratory: A dedicated laboratory is described for undergraduate practical experiments. Separate hostel buildings: SKCAS describes separate boys’ and girls’ buildings, each with 40 rooms. The published layout includes dining/kitchen areas, warden/caretaker offices and visitors’ space; rooms are described as shared by three or four students with basic study furniture. SC/ST scholarship application assistance: The college’s SC/ST Cell describes help with government scholarships and fee-reimbursement matters for eligible students, including hostel residents. The page is a support-channel description and does not publish a current award matrix.
