FAQs: questions answered

Where is GKU’s agriculture faculty located?

Guru Kashi University is at Talwandi Sabo in Bathinda district, Punjab.

How long is B.Sc. Agriculture?

The programme page lists four years.

Can an agriculture diploma holder apply?

The official course page includes qualifying two- or three-year agriculture/allied diploma routes after Class 10, subject to its 50% requirement.

What specialised practical units are documented?

The faculty identifies protected cultivation, seed technology, vermicomposting and sericulture resources.

Which degree is covered by this ICAR record?

B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture under the Faculty of Agriculture, from 29 March 2024 to 28 March 2029.

4 years. A broad agriculture degree with published crop, resource and agribusiness subject areas.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at GKU?

Official page states 50% in 10+2 PCB/PCBM/PCM/Agriculture, or 50% in a two- or three-year agriculture/allied diploma after Class 10.

How does admission to GKU work?

GKU’s general process moves from application and scrutiny to provisional allocation, eligibility verification, fee payment and final allotment. Submit the application: The procedure permits online or offline registration. Complete verification: Original documents are checked for eligibility before the university’s final seat allotment.

Seed technology and processing facilities: The faculty identifies seed testing, treatment and production research resources.
