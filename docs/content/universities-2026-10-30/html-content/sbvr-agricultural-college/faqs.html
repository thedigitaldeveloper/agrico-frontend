FAQs: questions answered

Which university is SBVR affiliated to?

The audit and ANGRAU directory identify Acharya N. G. Ranga Agricultural University.

What entry examinations are described?

The college academic page describes EAPCET for Intermediate applicants and AGRICET for its specified diploma route.

What diploma does the college mention?

It refers specifically to the two-year Diploma in Agriculture from ANGRAU, together with SSC and AGRICET. Current equivalence should be checked with the university.

Why is no current intake stated here?

The college site, ANGRAU directory and ICAR audit show different figures. A current sanctioned seat matrix is required to resolve the difference.

How does admission to SBVR work?

SBVR’s academic page explains two entry routes. Applicants should check the current ANGRAU/state notification for exact eligibility, diploma equivalence, seat categories and counselling dates rather than use the page’s undated seat headline. For SBVR Agricultural College, the applicable ANGRAU counselling route now has a revised 30 October 2026 notice: registered candidates follow the stated November rank/category session and check the university’s live seat position. Being called does not guarantee admission. Confirm the current prospectus: Ask the admissions office for the applicable intake, subject requirements and selection rules before submitting an application. Request a written cost statement: Obtain tuition, compulsory charges, accommodation options and refund terms for the intended entry year.
