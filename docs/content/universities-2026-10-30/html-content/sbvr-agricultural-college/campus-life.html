What is documented about the setting?

The college describes departmental laboratories, a library with digital resources and agricultural land for practical work. The homepage also mentions experiential units and RAWEP village placements; detailed current participation requirements should be requested from the college.

Student facility / serviceDocumented information
Boys’ and girls’ hostelsThe campus page reports accommodation for 200 boys and 200 girls, with single, double, triple and six-occupant room options. It describes reading and television rooms, drinking-water purification, dining and laundry facilities. Published capacity is not a live vacancy count.
Campus health centre and bankingSBVR describes a basic health centre and an arrangement with Sri Divya Hospital in Badvel for advanced emergencies. The page also identifies an on-campus Bank of Baroda banking/ATM facility.
SC/ST student support and anti-raggingThe college describes an SC/ST Cell that discusses scholarship and hostel concerns, alongside an institutional anti-ragging committee. Scholarship assistance should be distinguished from an approved award or a published fee-reimbursement entitlement.
LibraryThe campus page describes library and digital learning resources.
