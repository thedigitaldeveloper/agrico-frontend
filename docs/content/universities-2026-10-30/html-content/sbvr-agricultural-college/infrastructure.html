The documented academic setting

The college describes departmental laboratories, a library with digital resources and agricultural land for practical work. The homepage also mentions experiential units and RAWEP village placements; detailed current participation requirements should be requested from the college.

Sources: [4][5][10][11]

Facilities identified in the sources

Departmental laboratories

The campus and academic material describe laboratories serving agricultural disciplines.

Sources: [4]

Library

The campus page describes library and digital learning resources.

Sources: [4]

Boys’ and girls’ hostels

The campus page reports accommodation for 200 boys and 200 girls, with single, double, triple and six-occupant room options. It describes reading and television rooms, drinking-water purification, dining and laundry facilities. Published capacity is not a live vacancy count.

Sources: [10]

Campus health centre and banking

SBVR describes a basic health centre and an arrangement with Sri Divya Hospital in Badvel for advanced emergencies. The page also identifies an on-campus Bank of Baroda banking/ATM facility.

Sources: [10]

SC/ST student support and anti-ragging

The college describes an SC/ST Cell that discusses scholarship and hostel concerns, alongside an institutional anti-ragging committee. Scholarship assistance should be distinguished from an approved award or a published fee-reimbursement entitlement.

Sources: [11]
