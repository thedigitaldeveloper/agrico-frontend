Agriculture learning at Badvel

SBVR Agricultural College is part of the institutions promoted by Badvel & Sidhout Higher Education Committee. Established for the 2016–17 period, it is located in Badvel, YSR Kadapa district, and affiliated to Acharya N. G. Ranga Agricultural University.

The college describes departmental laboratories and a separate agricultural farm, with practical work connecting scientific subjects to cultivation. Its material also identifies experiential learning and village-based RAWEP. These give applicants concrete areas to inspect: the crop assignments students undertake, how fieldwork is assessed, and the current training calendar. Admission enquiries should also confirm the applicable Intermediate or diploma route and the sanctioned entry-year seat allocation.

Planning an application to SBVR

SBVR’s academic page explains two entry routes. Applicants should check the current ANGRAU/state notification for exact eligibility, diploma equivalence, seat categories and counselling dates rather than use the page’s undated seat headline. For SBVR Agricultural College, the applicable ANGRAU counselling route now has a revised 30 October 2026 notice: registered candidates follow the stated November rank/category session and check the university’s live seat position.
