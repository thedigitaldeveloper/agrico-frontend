FAQs: questions answered

What agriculture qualification is confirmed by ICAR?

The 31 st NAEAB proceedings approve B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture for 19 July 2022 to 18 July 2027.

Was the agriculture institute itself approved in that decision?

No. The same proceedings explicitly say the University Institute of Agriculture Sciences was not approved for accreditation. The degree decision must not be expanded into blanket institute accreditation.

Is a current agriculture intake confirmed?

No. The official admissions and institute pages read did not establish a current domestic agriculture application route. Ask CU admissions directly.

Can fees from the integrated international page be used?

No. That page concerns an integrated agriculture–MBA award and retains Admissions 2016 navigation. It is not a current domestic agriculture fee schedule.

Are CU’s wider placement figures agriculture outcomes?

The pages read do not provide an agriculture breakdown, so university-wide placement claims are not used as agriculture outcomes.

What establishment year is documented for CU?

Chandigarh University is recorded as established in 2012.

How does admission to CU work?

Chandigarh University’s general admissions page now refers to the 2027–28 intake and CUCET 2027. Its current general programme catalogue did not establish a domestic B.Sc.Agriculture offering in this research pass. Applicants must first obtain written confirmation of the programme, campus and intake before treating the historic accredited entry as an available seat. Confirm the current prospectus: Ask the admissions office for the applicable intake, subject requirements and selection rules before submitting an application. Request a written cost statement: Obtain tuition, compulsory charges, accommodation options and refund terms for the intended entry year.
