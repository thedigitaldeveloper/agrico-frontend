Agriculture enquiries at Chandigarh University

Chandigarh University’s Punjab campus forms part of a multidisciplinary university with institutes spanning engineering, business, sciences and related fields. The agriculture qualification relevant to this page is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, named in ICAR’s accreditation decision. It should be assessed through its own programme information rather than through the university’s broader marketing claims.

Applicants researching CU agriculture currently need a direct, programme-specific response from admissions. The official pages read include a current general admissions catalogue and an older international integrated agriculture–MBA page. Neither establishes the current domestic BSc agriculture intake, practical-learning schedule or payable charges. Obtain those details in writing before comparing CU with colleges whose current agriculture prospectus is available.

ICAR accreditation: programme, unit and validity

This is programme-specific accreditation. The locally audited workbook identity and printed labels are retained below without expanding the approved scope.
