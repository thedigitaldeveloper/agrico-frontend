A Sehore agriculture school with a published curriculum trail

The university prospectus dates the School of Agriculture’s start to July 2015. It describes undergraduate agriculture teaching, and the university’s scheme page makes the degree’s semester documents available. The first-semester listing explicitly refers to the Sixth Deans’ Committee from 2025–26, while earlier schemes remain alongside it.

Published curriculum documents give applicants a way to examine the agriculture course beyond its title. The scheme index carries both revised and earlier semester documents, so the version applicable to a student’s entry year matters when comparing subjects. The university also operates a training and placement cell whose stated activities include aptitude and interview preparation, and a scholarship section that describes assistance with government applications.
