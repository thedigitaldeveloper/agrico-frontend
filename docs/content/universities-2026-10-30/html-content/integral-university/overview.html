A network of teaching farms at IIAST

Integral’s agriculture institute was founded in 2014, according to its own farm information page. Its teaching context combines crop production with applied work across several farm units. These include plots for vegetables and flowers, organic farming activity and units that demonstrate links between farm resources.

The institute’s teaching units extend from crop plots to orchard, processing and integrated-farming activity. These settings allow different agricultural subjects to be considered together: soils influence crops, weather informs production decisions, and farm products enter processing and markets. The programme page describes a four-year B.Sc. Agriculture route with science or agriculture entry combinations, while the university’s admissions criteria identify IUET for the 2026 session.
