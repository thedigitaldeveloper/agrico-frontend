What is IIAST at Integral University?

It is the Integral Institute of Agricultural Science and Technology, which organises the university’s agriculture teaching.

Which degree is covered by this ICAR entry?

B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, for 26 September 2024 to 25 September 2029.

How long does Integral list the bachelor’s degree?

The institute programme page lists four years.

Separate units document activities including mushrooms, weather observation, organic production and integrated farming.
