An agriculture degree within the Gandhi Nagar university

RKDF’s agriculture faculty page identifies its bachelor’s degree and entry subjects. The 2026–27 university admission guidelines give a more detailed programme row, setting out eight semesters, category-specific marks and the available selection routes. This helps applicants separate agriculture requirements from the university’s many other disciplines.

The main university site also carries 2026 notices concerning precision-farming and smart-agriculture research positions. Those notices indicate activity in agricultural research, but they do not establish a guaranteed undergraduate research placement. Applicants interested in practical training should ask the faculty for the current farm, laboratory and attachment arrangements, as a detailed accessible inventory was not verified.
