FAQs: questions answered

Is TRIARD an independent university?

No. The audited entry identifies a private college affiliated to TNAU.

What does the 1994 establishment date refer to?

The college’s history dates the start of agriculture diploma courses to 1994. Degree education developed later.

What practical land is described?

The official about page describes 51.97 hectares, or 128.4 acres, and a crop cafeteria for observing varieties.

Are the website’s admission fees current?

The admission page includes older guidance and a 2025–26 form. No application charge is presented here as a verified 2026 fee.

Does TRIARD publish placement evidence?

It publishes named 2025–26 destinations and vacancy notices, but not a verified agriculture placement rate or salary distribution in the material read.

What entry qualification is published for TRIARD?

Historical official admission page: 10+2 with the listed science combinations, including Physics, Chemistry and Biology or Botany/Zoology. Current subject equivalence and minimum marks must be checked in the entry-year TNAU prospectus.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at TRIARD?

4 years.

How does admission to TRIARD work?

TRIARD’s published procedure directs both counselling and management applicants to TNAU registration. Because the page combines older instructions with a 2025–26 form, its stated application charges and links are not adopted as current. These steps describe the TNAU counselling channel. Any separate college-management route must be checked independently. Register with TNAU: Submit a single online application. Set course preferences: Order eligible college and course combinations. Follow counselling: General-quota counselling runs online; special-reservation sessions may be offline.
