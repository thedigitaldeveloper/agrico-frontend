The documented academic setting

TRIARD describes agricultural land used for practical teaching and a crop cafeteria. Its facilities page lists a college library, language laboratory, laboratories and residential arrangements, but includes older details that should be reconfirmed before making accommodation plans.

Facilities identified in the sources

Crop cafeteria

The about page describes a crop cafeteria for observing crop varieties.

Language laboratory

The facilities page describes English listening, speaking, reading and writing practice.

Library

A college library is described on the official facilities page.

Practical learning and student access

For B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, the usefulness of a facility depends on actual teaching access, supervision and the programme timetable. The documented learning activities are listed below; ask the department about your own cohort’s arrangements.

  • The college identifies practical training land and a crop cafeteria as part of agricultural learning.
