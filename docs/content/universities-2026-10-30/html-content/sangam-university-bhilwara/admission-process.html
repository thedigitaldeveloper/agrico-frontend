Which admission route applies?

Admission to agriculture programmes at Sangam University, Bhilwara is conducted through Rajasthan JET / CUET / Sangam University Merit, alongside direct university merit counselling for eligible 10+2 Agriculture/Science candidates.

Both Rajasthan JET state counselling seats and university-level seats require verification of Class XII subject combinations (PCB, PCM, PCA or Agriculture) and original academic certificates at reporting.
