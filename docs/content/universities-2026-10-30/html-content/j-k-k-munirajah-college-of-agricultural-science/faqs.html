FAQs: questions answered

Why do sources use both Science and Sciences?

The current official website uses College of Agricultural Science; the audited ICAR entry uses Agricultural Sciences. The profile preserves the official public-facing name and the exact register wording in recognition.

Where is JKKMCAS based?

The dated 2024 college invitation lists T.N. Palayam, Gobi, Erode 638506.

Which university is it affiliated to?

Both the official website excerpt and the ICAR audit identify Tamil Nadu Agricultural University.

What practical subjects can applicants inspect?

The official crop-management course table includes practical crop production, rainfed agriculture and soil-fertility management.

Does the ICAR entry confirm this year’s admission seats?

No. It records accreditation scope, including a printed intake, for 20 June 2024 to 19 June 2029. Ask for the current admission seat matrix.

What establishment year is documented for JKKMCAS?

J.K.K. Munirajah College of Agricultural Science is recorded as established in 2014.
