News & Notices: questions answered

Which dated admission notices are recorded for JKKMCAS?

No dated admission event is verified in this profile. The official channel is https://jkkmcas.org/admissions; check the notice for the programme and entry year you intend to join.

How does admission to JKKMCAS work?

The college’s TNAU affiliation is clear in its official identity material. A current official prospectus with application channels and subject thresholds was not retrievable, so applicants should request it directly rather than infer eligibility from a directory listing. These steps describe the TNAU counselling channel. Any separate college-management route must be checked independently. Register with TNAU: Submit a single online application. Set course preferences: Order eligible college and course combinations. Follow counselling: General-quota counselling runs online; special-reservation sessions may be offline.
