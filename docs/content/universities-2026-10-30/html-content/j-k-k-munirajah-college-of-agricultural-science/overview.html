Crop and soil learning in the Erode region

J.K.K. Munirajah College of Agricultural Science is a TNAU-affiliated agriculture college in the Erode region. Institutional historical material dates its start to 2014, and a dated college invitation identifies its location at T.N. Palayam, Gobi.

The crop-management curriculum connects cultivation decisions with scientific analysis. Published subject areas include agronomy, soil science, microbiology, crop physiology and agricultural engineering. The course table provides concrete examples for prospective students, from practical crop production to rainfed agriculture and soil-fertility management. Applicants can use those topics to discuss their interests and request the current laboratory and fieldwork timetable.
