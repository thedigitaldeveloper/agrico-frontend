Understand the academic context

The official department description connects crop-management teaching to laboratory analysis and farm management. Its course table includes practical crop production in kharif and rabi seasons, rainfed agriculture and soil-fertility subjects.

  • Published course titles include Practical Crop Production for kharif and rabi crops.
  • The curriculum material lists rainfed agriculture, watershed management and soil-and-water conservation.
