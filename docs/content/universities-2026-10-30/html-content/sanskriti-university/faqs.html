FAQs: questions answered

What annual agriculture fee does Sanskriti publish for 2026-27?

The dated brochure lists INR 90,000 annually for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture. This is not a full living-cost budget.

Which 10+2 subject combinations are accepted?

The 2026-27 brochure lists PCM, PCB or Agriculture, with a minimum 50% aggregate.

Does Sanskriti offer postgraduate agricultural study?

The brochure lists two-year M.Sc. options in Agronomy and Horticulture.

What is the ecological farming centre?

The school describes a centre working under PPDC/MSME guidance for training related to sustainable practices and mechanisation.

Does the ICAR entry automatically cover the M.Sc. programmes?

No. The audited School of Agriculture row explicitly prints B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture.

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at Sanskriti University?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agronomy; M.Sc. Horticulture. The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Sanskriti University?

4 years. An undergraduate agricultural science course with laboratory-based teaching described by the school.

How long is M.Sc. Agronomy at Sanskriti University?

2 years. Specialist postgraduate study listed in the agriculture section of the brochure.

How long is M.Sc. Horticulture at Sanskriti University?

2 years. A separate postgraduate horticulture route in the same brochure.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Sanskriti University?

2026-27 brochure: 10+2 with PCM, PCB or Agriculture and at least 50% aggregate.

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. Agronomy at Sanskriti University?

2026-27 brochure: graduation in the relevant subject with at least 50% aggregate.

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. Horticulture at Sanskriti University?

2026-27 brochure: graduation in the relevant subject with at least 50% aggregate.

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Sanskriti University?

₹90,000 / year · 2026-27. Annual course fee in the 2026-27 brochure; semester payment is separately shown as INR 47,000. Included charges: Published tuition/course fee; additional charges must be checked separately.

What fee is documented for M.Sc. Agronomy at Sanskriti University?

₹70,000 / year · 2026-27. Annual course fee; additional charges must be checked. Included charges: Published tuition/course fee; additional charges must be checked separately.

What fee is documented for M.Sc. Horticulture at Sanskriti University?

₹70,000 / year · 2026-27. Annual course fee; additional charges must be checked. Included charges: Published tuition/course fee; additional charges must be checked separately.

How does admission to Sanskriti University work?

Sanskriti publishes an admission-procedure page and links its application portal. Use the 2026-27 brochure for the programme-specific academic threshold and request the selection and document requirements for the intended course. Confirm the course: Use the official programme contact to confirm that the listed degree is taking applications for your intended intake. Request the intake notice: Ask for the applicable selection rules, fee breakdown and document checklist before submitting an application.

What is the recorded ICAR scope for Sanskriti University?

School of Agriculture: B. Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture (Intake- 120), recorded term 2022-10-11 to 2027-10-10. This entry applies to the named unit and programme.

What practical learning is described at Sanskriti University?

The school’s official account identifies subject laboratories and an ecological farming centre. Its description links the centre to training in sustainable farming and mechanisation; it does not document an identical practical timetable for every cohort.

Which teaching facilities are documented at Sanskriti University?

Agriculture subject laboratories: The school describes experiments supported by subject laboratories and technicians. Centre of Excellence on Ecological Farming: The centre is described as a training resource for students and farmers.

What student facilities are documented for Sanskriti University?

Separate residential hostels: The university describes separate hostels for boys and girls, with cleaning and maintenance provision. A room offer and its applicable charges require written confirmation. Campus and hostel safety: Its infrastructure account describes a secured campus boundary and measures restricting unauthorised entry. Centre of Excellence on Ecological Farming: The centre is described as a training resource for students and farmers.

What scholarship information is available for Sanskriti University?

Sanskriti’s undated scholarship policy describes general categories including sports, defence, girls and disability. Its published conditions limit awards to tuition, allow one scheme at a time and require academic continuation without back papers. Agriculture applicants should obtain a dated programme-specific award; engineering and MBA merit tables are not agriculture entitlements. General-category scholarship conditions (undated): The page lists defence, girls, disability and sports categories, with supporting-proof requirements. Only one scheme may apply, tuition-only limits and continuation conditions apply, and the stated exclusions include BAMS, BUMS, B.Sc. Nursing and Ph.D. Agriculture-specific current approval is still required.
