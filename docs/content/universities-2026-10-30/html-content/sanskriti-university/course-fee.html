What scholarship information is available for Sanskriti University?

Sanskriti’s undated scholarship policy describes general categories including sports, defence, girls and disability. Its published conditions limit awards to tuition, allow one scheme at a time and require academic continuation without back papers. Agriculture applicants should obtain a dated programme-specific award; engineering and MBA merit tables are not agriculture entitlements. General-category scholarship conditions (undated): The page lists defence, girls, disability and sports categories, with supporting-proof requirements. Only one scheme may apply, tuition-only limits and continuation conditions apply, and the stated exclusions include BAMS, BUMS, B.Sc. Nursing and Ph.D. Agriculture-specific current approval is still required.
