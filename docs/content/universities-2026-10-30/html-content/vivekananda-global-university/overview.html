An agriculture faculty with published intake information

VGU’s agriculture faculty combines undergraduate teaching with postgraduate subject options. The faculty page lists agronomy, horticulture, soil science and plant breeding and genetics at master’s level. These advertised offerings should be considered separately from the specific undergraduate programme supported by the audited ICAR record.

The most useful applicant evidence is the 2026-27 undergraduate table: it identifies a four-year B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture and a published annual fee. Entrance wording differs between two official pages, however. The course table says appearing in Rajasthan JET is mandatory, while the wider admission overview also mentions VGUCET. This unresolved detail should be settled with the university before applying.
