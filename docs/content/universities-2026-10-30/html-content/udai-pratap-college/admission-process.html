What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at UP College?

2026–27 official prospectus: candidates who passed or are appearing in Intermediate/10+2 or equivalent with Agriculture or Science subjects may take the entrance examination. It states no entrance age limit and a maximum 5% other-state admission allocation on merit.
