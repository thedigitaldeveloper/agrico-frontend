How does admission to UP College work?

UP College’s 2026–27 prospectus allows agriculture or science Intermediate candidates, including those appearing in the qualifying examination, to enter its agriculture entrance process. Other-state applicants should check the stated allocation and counselling rules. Confirm the current prospectus: Ask the admissions office for the applicable intake, subject requirements and selection rules before submitting an application. Request a written cost statement: Obtain tuition, compulsory charges, accommodation options and refund terms for the intended entry year.
