FAQs: questions answered

Is UP College a university?

It identifies as an autonomous college affiliated to Mahatma Gandhi Kashi Vidyapith; this profile retains the college classification.

Sources: [1][3]

Which agriculture degree is listed?

The official 2026–27 prospectus lists B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture and identifies it as a four-year semester-based programme.

Sources: [3]

What allied farm areas are published?

The college’s Agriculture Farm page lists dairy, poultry, fishery, beekeeping and practical crop production.

Sources: [4]

Does the ICAR row cover all postgraduate courses?

No. The audited row explicitly lists B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture. Postgraduate offerings in the prospectus are not automatically included in that accreditation statement.

Sources: [1][3]

Who may enter the agriculture entrance process?

The 2026–27 prospectus names passed or appearing Intermediate/equivalent candidates with Agriculture or Science subjects. Its other-state and counselling conditions also apply.

Sources: [6]

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at UP College?

4 years; semester system.

Sources: [1][3][6]

Which named academic staff are listed for UP College?

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at UP College?

2026–27 official prospectus: candidates who passed or are appearing in Intermediate/10+2 or equivalent with Agriculture or Science subjects may take the entrance examination. It states no entrance age limit and a maximum 5% other-state admission allocation on merit.

Sources: [1][3][6]

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at UP College?

A verified numerical fee is not available. A dated official fee schedule with tuition, compulsory charges and residential costs was not verified.

How does admission to UP College work?

UP College’s 2026–27 prospectus allows agriculture or science Intermediate candidates, including those appearing in the qualifying examination, to enter its agriculture entrance process. Other-state applicants should check the stated allocation and counselling rules. Confirm the current prospectus: Ask the admissions office for the applicable intake, subject requirements and selection rules before submitting an application. Request a written cost statement: Obtain tuition, compulsory charges, accommodation options and refund terms for the intended entry year.

Sources: [2][3][6]
