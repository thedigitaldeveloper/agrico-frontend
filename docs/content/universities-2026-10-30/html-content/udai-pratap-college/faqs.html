FAQs: questions answered

Is UP College a university?

It identifies as an autonomous college affiliated to Mahatma Gandhi Kashi Vidyapith; this profile retains the college classification.

Which agriculture degree is listed?

The official 2026–27 prospectus lists B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture and identifies it as a four-year semester-based programme.

What allied farm areas are published?

The college’s Agriculture Farm page lists dairy, poultry, fishery, beekeeping and practical crop production.

Does the ICAR row cover all postgraduate courses?

No. The audited row explicitly lists B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture. Postgraduate offerings in the prospectus are not automatically included in that accreditation statement.

Who may enter the agriculture entrance process?

The 2026–27 prospectus names passed or appearing Intermediate/equivalent candidates with Agriculture or Science subjects. Its other-state and counselling conditions also apply.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at UP College?

4 years; semester system.

Which named academic staff are listed for UP College?

The official faculty-and-departments page names Prof.Raghwendra Pratap Singh as Agriculture Dean and identifies ten agricultural subject departments. It does not establish a full departmental teaching headcount. Prof. Raghwendra Pratap Singh — Dean, Faculty of Agriculture.

Which application documents are listed for UP College?

Qualifying-examination academic certificates; Caste/community certificate where applicable; Income and domicile certificates where applicable; Aadhaar identification; Supporting documents for any claimed weightage; Originals of uploaded certificates at counselling; Character certificate, transfer certificate and anti-ragging affidavit for completing admission. Match these to the official requirements for your chosen programme and quota.

Which dated admission events are recorded for UP College?

Historical 2026–27 entrance application opening — 2026-06-16 — 2026–27; Historical 2026–27 entrance application/payment deadline in brochure; check later amendments — 2026-06-25 — 2026–27. These describe the cited events and do not establish a continuing application window.

What establishment year is documented for UP College?

Udai Pratap College is recorded as established in 1949.

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at UP College?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture. The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at UP College?

2026–27 official prospectus: candidates who passed or are appearing in Intermediate/10+2 or equivalent with Agriculture or Science subjects may take the entrance examination. It states no entrance age limit and a maximum 5% other-state admission allocation on merit.

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at UP College?

A verified numerical fee is not available. A dated official fee schedule with tuition, compulsory charges and residential costs was not verified.

How does admission to UP College work?

UP College’s 2026–27 prospectus allows agriculture or science Intermediate candidates, including those appearing in the qualifying examination, to enter its agriculture entrance process. Other-state applicants should check the stated allocation and counselling rules. Confirm the current prospectus: Ask the admissions office for the applicable intake, subject requirements and selection rules before submitting an application. Request a written cost statement: Obtain tuition, compulsory charges, accommodation options and refund terms for the intended entry year.

What is the recorded ICAR scope for UP College?

Udai Pratap College, Varanasi, U.P.: B. Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture (Intake- 160)*, recorded term 2022-10-11 to 2027-10-10. This entry applies to the named unit and programme.

What practical learning is described at UP College?

The official farm structure includes Practical Crop Production and allied activity sections.

Which teaching facilities are documented at UP College?

Agriculture farm: The college publishes a dedicated farm section linked to practical crop production. Allied activity sections: Dairy, poultry, fishery and beekeeping are individually listed on the farm page.

What student facilities are documented for UP College?

The official Agriculture Farm page provides separate sections for dairy, poultry, fishery, beekeeping, practical crop production and allied activities. These establish the farm’s published teaching areas, without proving current livestock numbers or student access hours. Accommodation availability, meal charges and transport arrangements for your entry year still need direct confirmation.
