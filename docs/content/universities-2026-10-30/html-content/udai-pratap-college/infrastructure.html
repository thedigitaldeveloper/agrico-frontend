The documented academic setting

The official Agriculture Farm page provides separate sections for dairy, poultry, fishery, beekeeping, practical crop production and allied activities. These establish the farm’s published teaching areas, without proving current livestock numbers or student access hours.

Facilities identified in the sources

Agriculture farm

The college publishes a dedicated farm section linked to practical crop production.

Allied activity sections

Dairy, poultry, fishery and beekeeping are individually listed on the farm page.

Practical learning and student access

For B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, the usefulness of a facility depends on actual teaching access, supervision and the programme timetable. The documented learning activities are listed below; ask the department about your own cohort’s arrangements.

People and supervision
