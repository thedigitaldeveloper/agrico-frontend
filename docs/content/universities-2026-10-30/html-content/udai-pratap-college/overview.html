Agriculture within an autonomous Varanasi college

Udai Pratap College is an autonomous institution in Varanasi affiliated to Mahatma Gandhi Kashi Vidyapith. Its 2026–27 prospectus lists B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture alongside the wider college’s science, commerce and humanities courses. Agriculture is therefore part of a broader college academic setting.

The agriculture-farm section covers crop production and allied activities, including dairy, poultry, fishery and beekeeping. The prospectus also lists postgraduate subjects in agricultural economics, animal husbandry and dairy, soil science and horticulture. Applicants can compare the undergraduate practical experience and later subject options while checking the college’s entrance-examination conditions for their intended entry year.
