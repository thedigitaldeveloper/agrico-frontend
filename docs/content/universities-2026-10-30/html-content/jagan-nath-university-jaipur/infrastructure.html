The documented academic setting

The faculty page describes 13 laboratories, agronomic fields, a tractor and farm machinery. It also identifies a polyhouse, vermicompost unit, drip and sprinkler irrigation, and medicinal/aromatic plants. This is an undated institutional inventory, so applicants should check current access and teaching schedules.
