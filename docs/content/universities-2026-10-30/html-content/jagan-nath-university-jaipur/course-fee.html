Courses & Fees: questions answered

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at JU Jaipur?

A verified numerical fee is not available. The verified Jaipur 2026–27 programme document did not supply a readable agriculture fee amount. Fees from the similarly named NCR/Haryana university are not transferable.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at JU Jaipur?

4 years. The four-year B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture is identified in the official 2026–27 programme list. Departmental teaching includes agricultural sciences with field and laboratory facilities.

What scholarship information is available for JU Jaipur?

The university lists merit support and government scholarship routes. Its scholarship page specifically links a Rajasthan agriculture incentive for female students. Eligibility, amount and current application dates must be checked with the administering department; no automatic award is promised. Rajasthan agriculture incentive for female students: The university scholarship page links the Department of Agriculture’s Incentive to Girls route. Current award amount and applicant conditions were not verified. SC/ST, OBC and PWD scholarship application processing: The university scholarship page asks eligible students in these categories to submit the academic-year scholarship form byFebruary for processing. The page is undated; verify the applicable scheme’s own session deadline, eligibility and sanction. It does not establish a guaranteed award amount.
