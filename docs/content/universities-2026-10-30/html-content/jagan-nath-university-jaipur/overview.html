Agriculture study at Chaksu

Jagan Nath University, Jaipur was established in 2008 under Rajasthan legislation. Its Faculty of Agriculture dates the start of B.Sc. Agriculture teaching to 2014–15. The agriculture setting includes agronomic fields, teaching laboratories and farm machinery, linking the degree to crop-production practice.

The faculty describes protected cultivation, composting, irrigation and medicinal-plant units. These offer useful questions for a campus visit: how frequently students use each unit, which crops are grown for teaching, and how practical assessment works. The university offers other agriculture-related qualifications as well, but applicants should compare each programme’s admission requirements and accreditation separately.

Scholarships and financial support
