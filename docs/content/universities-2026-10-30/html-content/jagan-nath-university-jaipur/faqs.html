How does admission to JU Jaipur work?

Use the Jaipur university’s 2026–27 programme list, which gives agriculture-specific academic requirements. The agriculture selection cell names JET/ICAR, while the official CUET page also maps the degree. Ask admissions to reconcile those routes before registering for an examination or paying. Confirm the current prospectus: Ask the admissions office for the applicable intake, subject requirements and selection rules before submitting an application. Request a written cost statement: Obtain tuition, compulsory charges, accommodation options and refund terms for the intended entry year.
