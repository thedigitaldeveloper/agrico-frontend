News & Notices: questions answered

Which dated admission notices are recorded for TNU?

No dated admission event is verified in this profile. The official channel is https://www.tnu.in/admissions/admission-process-best-private-colleges-in-kolkata/; check the notice for the programme and entry year you intend to join.

How does admission to TNU work?

TNU publishes a central application process. The course page confirms a university entrance test; its mixed-year dates require a fresh intake notice before planning a deadline. The live site now advertises 2027–28 admissions; the sourced 2026–27 fees and process documents below must not be relabelled as the next intake’s terms. Start the university application: Use the official admission process page and enter the Class 10 roll number correctly; the page identifies it as an application identifier. Confirm the intake notice: Request the applicable entrance schedule and a dated fee statement for B.Sc. Agriculture.
