Courses & Fees: questions answered

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at TNU?

₹71,000 / semester · 2026-2027. One-time fee ₹29,000; published course total ₹597,000. Confirm accommodation and any charges outside the listed course fee. Included charges: Published semester course fee for Indian students.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at TNU?

4 years. A broad agriculture degree with a published final-semester rural and industrial attachment.

What scholarship information is available for TNU?

The approved 2026–27 fee schedule expressly excludes B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture from the university’s general 25–100% tuition-scholarship offer. Agriculture applicants should obtain written confirmation of any separate government or programme-specific support before using a scholarship in their budget.

How can I contact TNU about an application?

Published phone: +91 7044446999. Published email: admission@tnu.in. Official admissions: https://www.tnu.in/admissions/admission-process-best-private-colleges-in-kolkata/

What are TNU’s published 2026–27 B.Sc. Agriculture fees?

For Indian students, the official table lists ₹71,000 per semester plus ₹29,000 one time, with a published course total of ₹597,000. These figures describe the listed course charges; confirm accommodation separately.
