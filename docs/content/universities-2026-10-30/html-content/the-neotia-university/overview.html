Agriculture study near Diamond Harbour

TNU’s agriculture course connects crop science with work in farming environments. Its published curriculum includes soil science, horticulture, plant protection, genetics and agricultural economics. The degree page describes four years of study, with rural agricultural experience and an agro-industrial attachment in the final stage.

The school’s older official brochure describes teaching farms, laboratories and small production units, including mushroom and apiculture activity. Such settings connect scientific observation with day-to-day production decisions. The 2026–27 fee schedule identifies the agriculture bachelor’s degree alongside agribusiness, diploma and postgraduate routes, giving applicants distinct study options to compare.
