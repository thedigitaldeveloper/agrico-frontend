FAQs: questions answered

Where is TNU’s teaching campus?

The campus address published on the agriculture page is Sarisa on Diamond Harbour Road in South 24 Parganas.

Which TNU course is in this ICAR record?

B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, with a register term of 20 June 2024 to 19 June 2029.

How long is the agriculture degree?

The official course page describes a four-year programme.

Does the course include village work?

Yes. The published final-stage curriculum includes a village attachment within rural agricultural work experience.

What are TNU’s published 2026–27 B.Sc. Agriculture fees?

For Indian students, the official table lists ₹71,000 per semester plus ₹29,000 one time, with a published course total of ₹597,000. These figures describe the listed course charges; confirm accommodation separately.

Does ICAR listing cover TNU’s fisheries courses here?

This audited TNU record names agriculture only. Fisheries accreditation cannot be inferred from this entry.

Are verified agriculture placement figures available?

The NIRF 2025 agriculture/allied submission reports UG four-year programmes (2023-2024): 59 placed from 211 graduates in minimum time, with ₹6,50,000 median annual salary among those placed; PG two-year programmes (2023-2024): 9 placed from 18 graduates in minimum time, with ₹7,00,000 median annual salary among those placed. These are historical group-level outcomes, not specialisation-level placement promises.

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at TNU?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture. The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at TNU?

4 years. A broad agriculture degree with a published final-semester rural and industrial attachment.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at TNU?

Course page: 10+2 with at least 55% in Physics, Chemistry and Biology/Mathematics, or the stated agriculture/horticulture vocational combination; reconfirm for the applying year.

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at TNU?

₹71,000 / semester · 2026-2027. One-time fee ₹29,000; published course total ₹597,000. Confirm accommodation and any charges outside the listed course fee. Included charges: Published semester course fee for Indian students.

How does admission to TNU work?

TNU publishes a central application process. The course page confirms a university entrance test; its mixed-year dates require a fresh intake notice before planning a deadline. The live site now advertises 2027–28 admissions; the sourced 2026–27 fees and process documents below must not be relabelled as the next intake’s terms. Start the university application: Use the official admission process page and enter the Class 10 roll number correctly; the page identifies it as an application identifier. Confirm the intake notice: Request the applicable entrance schedule and a dated fee statement for B.Sc. Agriculture.

What is the recorded ICAR scope for TNU?

The Neotia University: B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture (Intake- 120), recorded term 2024-06-20 to 2029-06-19. This entry applies to the named unit and programme.

What practical learning is described at TNU?

The published final-semester scheme includes village attachment and agro-industrial attachment. The older brochure describes compost, mushroom and beekeeping units.

Which teaching facilities are documented at TNU?

Agriculture fields and laboratories: Spaces for crop work and subject practicals. Polyhouse and net house: Protected cultivation facilities identified by the university. Farm workshop: Tractor and power tiller resources for machinery practicals.

What student facilities are documented for TNU?

The course page identifies experimental fields, crop laboratories and farm machinery for teaching. The older school brochure additionally describes production demonstrations. Accommodation availability, meal charges and transport arrangements for your entry year still need direct confirmation.

What scholarship information is available for TNU?

The approved 2026–27 fee schedule expressly excludes B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture from the university’s general 25–100% tuition-scholarship offer. Agriculture applicants should obtain written confirmation of any separate government or programme-specific support before using a scholarship in their budget.

What training and outcome evidence is available for TNU?

The university's NIRF 2025 Agriculture and Allied Sectors submission reports graduates, placements, higher-study progression and the median annual salary of placed graduates for the cohorts below. The figures apply to the reported programme-duration group, rather than an individual specialisation, and do not predict an applicant's future outcome.
