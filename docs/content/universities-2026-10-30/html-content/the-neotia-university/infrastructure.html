Practical learning and student access

For B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, the usefulness of a facility depends on actual teaching access, supervision and the programme timetable. The documented learning activities are listed below; ask the department about your own cohort’s arrangements.

  • The published final-semester scheme includes village attachment and agro-industrial attachment.
  • The older brochure describes compost, mushroom and beekeeping units.

Which teaching facilities are documented at TNU?

Agriculture fields and laboratories: Spaces for crop work and subject practicals. Polyhouse and net house: Protected cultivation facilities identified by the university. Farm workshop: Tractor and power tiller resources for machinery practicals.

What practical learning is described at TNU?

The published final-semester scheme includes village attachment and agro-industrial attachment. The older brochure describes compost, mushroom and beekeeping units.
