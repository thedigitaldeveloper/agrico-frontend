Which part of the cost could an award cover?

Start with the actual programme fee and charge notes. Ask whether an offered concession covers tuition alone or any other charge; do not subtract it from the whole cost without written confirmation.

Question for the admissions / scholarship officeWhy it matters
Does this scheme name my exact degree and intake?B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture
What is the award basis and written approval process?An eligibility category does not by itself confirm an award.
What are the renewal conditions?Budget for subsequent years using the actual continuation rules.

Prepare a programme-specific request

Identify your proposed qualification at The Neotia University, intended intake and fee category. Ask for the scheme document, application procedure, assessment date, documentary requirements and award letter. Only submit the documents the applicable policy actually asks for.
