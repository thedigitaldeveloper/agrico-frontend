What is documented about the setting?

The official farm page describes 74.8 acres: 43.9 at the main campus and 30.9 at Thokapalli. It lists 16 instructional units, including orchards, crop cafeterias, protected cultivation and a pest-and-disease-management module.

Student facility / serviceDocumented information
Residential learning and mess facilitiesThe dedicated hostel page identifies separate boys’ and girls’ hostels with dining halls, study and sick rooms, recreation rooms and indoor gymnasiums. It also lists solar water heating, camera surveillance, backup generators and RO water.
Student Affairs SectionThe college assigns hostel/mess coordination, health care, sports and cultural activities, NSS, scholarships and career guidance to its Student Affairs Section. This identifies the office’s remit; it does not establish a particular award or placement outcome.

How could the programme shape your week?

Read the actual practical components for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture alongside the academic timetable. Field work, laboratory sessions or clinical teaching may affect daily travel and preparation; confirm the arrangements rather than inferring them from a campus photograph.

  • Department-maintained instructional units are described as supporting hands-on exposure.
  • The farm inventory includes agrometeorology and integrated pest-and-disease management.
  • The degree page describes fourth-year RAWEP, experiential learning and an internship with an agriculture-related enterprise.
