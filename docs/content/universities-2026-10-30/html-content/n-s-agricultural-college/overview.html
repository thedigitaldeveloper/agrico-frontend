Agriculture study near Markapur

N.S. Agricultural College was established by Sri NS Educational Society in 2016. Located at Gobburu, eight kilometres from Markapur according to the college, it is affiliated to Acharya N. G. Ranga Agricultural University.

Its teaching farm is spread across the main campus and Thokapalli, with instructional units spanning crops, horticulture and crop protection. The four-year degree describes final-year rural experience, experiential learning and an agriculture-related internship. That practical structure is useful for students comparing field exposure: request the current unit allocations, village-work schedule and internship supervision arrangements alongside the entry-year admission notice.

Planning an application to NSAC

NSAC says university notifications lead the admission process and describes entrance-based counselling. Its site gives detailed but undated age, category and seat rules; these require the current university prospectus before use. For N.S. Agricultural College, the applicable ANGRAU counselling route now has a revised 30 October 2026 notice: registered candidates follow the stated November rank/category session and check the university’s live seat position. Being called does not guarantee admission.

Sixteen instructional units across two farm locations

The official farm page describes 74.8 acres: 43.9 at the main campus and 30.9 at Thokapalli. It lists 16 instructional units, including orchards, crop cafeterias, protected cultivation and a pest-and-disease-management module.
