FAQs: questions answered

Where is NSAC?

The college lists Gobburu Village, Peddaraveedu Mandal, Prakasam District, and places it eight kilometres from Markapur.

Who established the college?

Its official about page identifies Sri NS Educational Society and the year 2016.

What farm locations are described?

The farm page names the main campus and Thokapalli, totalling 74.8 acres in its published inventory.

What career support does NSAC describe?

It describes vacancy sharing, campus interview invitations, alumni communication and career-guidance activities.

Does the ICAR intake establish current seats?

No. The audit preserves a printed intake of 72, while other official records differ. Confirm the current sanctioned seat matrix with ANGRAU.

What entry qualification is published for NSAC?

2026–27 BiPC route: Intermediate/equivalent with Physical and Biological/Natural Sciences; eligible ANGRAU diploma categories are separately listed.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at NSAC?

How does admission to NSAC work?

NSAC says university notifications lead the admission process and describes entrance-based counselling. Its site gives detailed but undated age, category and seat rules; these require the current university prospectus before use. For N.S. Agricultural College, the applicable ANGRAU counselling route now has a revised 30 October 2026 notice: registered candidates follow the stated November rank/category session and check the university’s live seat position. Being called does not guarantee admission. Identify the appropriate entrance route: The college distinguishes Intermediate applicants using EAPCET from agriculture-diploma applicants using AGRICET. Follow the current university notification: Verify eligibility and counselling instructions with ANGRAU, then request the current category-wise sanctioned seat matrix.
