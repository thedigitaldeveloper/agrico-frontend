The documented academic setting

The official farm page describes 74.8 acres: 43.9 at the main campus and 30.9 at Thokapalli. It lists 16 instructional units, including orchards, crop cafeterias, protected cultivation and a pest-and-disease-management module.

Facilities identified in the sources

Teaching farm

The college identifies farm land at the main campus and Thokapalli.

Protected cultivation

A protected-cultivation unit is listed within the farm inventory.

Crop cafeterias

Crop, millet and fodder cafeterias appear in the instructional-unit table.

Residential learning and mess facilities

The dedicated hostel page identifies separate boys’ and girls’ hostels with dining halls, study and sick rooms, recreation rooms and indoor gymnasiums. It also lists solar water heating, camera surveillance, backup generators and RO water.

Student Affairs Section

The college assigns hostel/mess coordination, health care, sports and cultural activities, NSS, scholarships and career guidance to its Student Affairs Section. This identifies the office’s remit; it does not establish a particular award or placement outcome.

Practical learning and student access

For B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, the usefulness of a facility depends on actual teaching access, supervision and the programme timetable. The documented learning activities are listed below; ask the department about your own cohort’s arrangements.

  • Department-maintained instructional units are described as supporting hands-on exposure.
  • The farm inventory includes agrometeorology and integrated pest-and-disease management.
  • The degree page describes fourth-year RAWEP, experiential learning and an internship with an agriculture-related enterprise.
