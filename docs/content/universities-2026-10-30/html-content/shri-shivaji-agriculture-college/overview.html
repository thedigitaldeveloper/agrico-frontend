Agricultural education rooted in Amravati

The college traces its founding to Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh and is managed by Shri Shivaji Education Society. Its official history records earlier affiliation to Nagpur University, followed by agricultural-university affiliation. It identifies Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh Krishi Vidyapeeth, Akola, as the present affiliating university.

Its departmental structure includes agronomy and meteorology, agricultural botany, chemistry, engineering, horticulture and plant pathology. That spread places crop production alongside weather, soils, equipment and crop health in the college’s teaching setting. The official facilities page describes library and internet resources, and the homepage also mentions postgraduate work in agricultural economics and extension. The college’s affiliation gives applicants a clear university framework for comparing its undergraduate agriculture route.
