FAQs: questions answered

What is the B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture fee at Bhagwant University, Ajmer for 2026-27?

The published annual tuition fee for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Bhagwant University, Ajmer is INR 65,000 per year. Confirm hostel, examination and registration charges separately.

How can I get admission to B.Sc. Agriculture at Bhagwant University, Ajmer?

Admission is offered through Rajasthan JET / UJET (University Joint Entrance Test) for candidates who have passed 10+2 with Agriculture, PCB, PCM or PCA.

Where is the agriculture campus of Bhagwant University, Ajmer located?

The Faculty of Agriculture is located at Sikar Road, Ajmer, Rajasthan 305004, India.

What practical farm facilities are available at Bhagwant University, Ajmer?

Semi-arid central Rajasthan crop research farm on Sikar Road (Ajmer), farm machinery workshop, irrigation engineering lab and soil testing unit.

Does Bhagwant University, Ajmer offer M.Sc. Agriculture or postgraduate courses?

Covered programmes include: B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture (4 years); B.Tech. Agricultural Engineering (4 years); M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) (2 years).

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at BU Ajmer?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; B.Tech. Agricultural Engineering; M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy). The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at BU Ajmer?

4 years; study format: Full-time on campus. B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Bhagwant University, Ajmer is delivered on campus through the Faculty of Agriculture, combining classroom agronomy, laboratory practicals and field crop rotations.

How long is B.Tech. Agricultural Engineering at BU Ajmer?

4 years; study format: Full-time on campus. B.Tech. Agricultural Engineering at Bhagwant University, Ajmer is delivered on campus through the Faculty of Agriculture, combining classroom agronomy, laboratory practicals and field crop rotations.

How long is M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at BU Ajmer?

2 years; study format: Full-time on campus. M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at Bhagwant University, Ajmer is delivered on campus through the Faculty of Agriculture, combining classroom agronomy, laboratory practicals and field crop rotations.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at BU Ajmer?

10+2 with Agriculture or PCB / PCM with minimum 45% marks.

What are the entry requirements for B.Tech. Agricultural Engineering at BU Ajmer?

10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (PCM) or Agriculture with minimum 45% marks.

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at BU Ajmer?

4-year B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture from a recognized university.

How does admission to BU Ajmer work?

Admission to agriculture programmes at Bhagwant University, Ajmer is conducted through Rajasthan JET / UJET (University Joint Entrance Test), alongside direct university merit counselling for eligible 10+2 Agriculture/Science candidates. Both Rajasthan JET state counselling seats and university-level seats require verification of Class XII subject combinations (PCB, PCM, PCA or Agriculture) and original academic certificates at reporting. Check Rajasthan JET or University Application Window: Register through the official Bhagwant University, Ajmer admissions portal (https://bhagwantuniversity.ac.in/agriculture/) or select the institution during Rajasthan JET / Pre-PG choice filling. Submit Academic & Entrance Score Details: Provide Class X and XII mark sheets (or B.Sc. Agriculture transcript for PG), JET/CUET/University entrance scorecard and category certificate. Document Verification & Tuition Fee Confirmation: Verify original documents on campus at Ajmer and obtain the itemised semester/annual fee receipt including hostel and examination heads.

What practical learning is described at BU Ajmer?

Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE) in adopted villages during the final year. Experiential Learning Programme (ELP) modules in organic inputs, nursery management, Mushroom cultivation and seed production. Field visits to Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs), state agricultural research stations and agri-input processing plants in Rajasthan.

Which teaching facilities are documented at BU Ajmer?

Agronomy & Instructional Crop Farm: Field demonstration plots at Ajmer for kharif and rabi crop trials, seed bed preparation, weed management and micro-irrigation exercises. Soil Science & Agricultural Chemistry Lab: Equipped for soil pH, electrical conductivity, organic carbon, macronutrient (N-P-K) and micronutrient testing. Horticulture, Polyhouse & Orchard Unit: Protected cultivation polyhouse/shade-net house, fruit orchard nursery and post-harvest handling practical space. Plant Pathology, Entomology & Genetics Labs: Compound and stereo microscopy, insect rearing cages, disease specimen collection and seed purity testing equipment. Student Hostels, Library & Transport: Separate boys and girls residential hostels at Ajmer, agricultural library journals, mess and city bus connectivity.

What student facilities are documented for BU Ajmer?

Student Hostels, Library & Transport: Separate boys and girls residential hostels at Ajmer, agricultural library journals, mess and city bus connectivity.

What scholarship information is available for BU Ajmer?

Bhagwant University, Ajmer offers merit-based tuition scholarships based on Class XII / qualifying examination marks or JET rank, alongside Rajasthan government post-matric scholarships (SJMS / Samaj Kalyan Vibhag) for eligible SC, ST, OBC, MBC and EBC students. University Entrance & Qualifying Marks Merit Scholarship (2026-27): Tuition fee waiver slabs (10% to 50% in first year) for applicants scoring 75%+ to 90%+ in 10+2 or high rank in Rajasthan JET / CUET; continuation requires maintaining minimum SGPA and 75% attendance. Rajasthan State Post-Matric & Girl Child Agriculture Incentive: Eligible Rajasthan domicile students can apply on the Rajasthan SSO Scholarship Portal; girl students in agriculture may also check state Rajshree / Agriculture Department incentive schemes.

What training and outcome evidence is available for BU Ajmer?

The placement and training cell at Bhagwant University, Ajmer coordinates final-year agribusiness agro-industrial attachments, KVK internships and preparation for Rajasthan Agriculture Supervisor, AAO, Pre-PG, ICAR JRF and IBPS AFO competitive examinations. Graduates pursue roles in seed, fertilizer, crop-protection, micro-irrigation, agri-fintech, warehousing and rural banking sectors, as well as M.Sc. Agriculture through Rajasthan Pre-PG and CUET-PG.
