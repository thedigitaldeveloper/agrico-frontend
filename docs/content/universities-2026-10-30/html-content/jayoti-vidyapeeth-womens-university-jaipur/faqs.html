FAQs: questions answered

What is the B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture fee at Jayoti Vidyapeeth Women's University, Jaipur for 2026-27?

The published annual tuition fee for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Jayoti Vidyapeeth Women's University, Jaipur is INR 85,000 per year. Confirm hostel, examination and registration charges separately.

How can I get admission to B.Sc. Agriculture at Jayoti Vidyapeeth Women's University, Jaipur?

Admission is offered through Rajasthan JET / JNEE (Women University Entrance) for candidates who have passed 10+2 with Agriculture, PCB, PCM or PCA.

Where is the agriculture campus of Jayoti Vidyapeeth Women's University, Jaipur located?

The Faculty of Agriculture and Veterinary Science is located at Vedaant Gyan Valley, Village Jharna, Mahla Jobner Link Road, NH-8, Jaipur, Rajasthan 303122, India.

What practical farm facilities are available at Jayoti Vidyapeeth Women's University, Jaipur?

Dedicated women-only residential campus on the Mahla-Jobner agricultural corridor with organic crop demonstration units, food processing lab, hydroponics unit and RAWE training.

Does Jayoti Vidyapeeth Women's University, Jaipur offer M.Sc. Agriculture or postgraduate courses?

Covered programmes include: B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture (4 years); M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) (2 years).

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at JVWU Jaipur?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy). The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at JVWU Jaipur?

4 years; study format: Full-time on campus. B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at Jayoti Vidyapeeth Women's University, Jaipur is delivered on campus through the Faculty of Agriculture and Veterinary Science, combining classroom agronomy, laboratory practicals and field crop rotations.

How long is M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at JVWU Jaipur?

2 years; study format: Full-time on campus. M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at Jayoti Vidyapeeth Women's University, Jaipur is delivered on campus through the Faculty of Agriculture and Veterinary Science, combining classroom agronomy, laboratory practicals and field crop rotations.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at JVWU Jaipur?

10+2 (Female candidates) with Agriculture or Science (PCB / PCM / PCA) with minimum 50% marks.

What are the entry requirements for M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy) at JVWU Jaipur?

4-year B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture (Female candidates) with minimum 50% marks.

How does admission to JVWU Jaipur work?

Admission to agriculture programmes at Jayoti Vidyapeeth Women's University, Jaipur is conducted through Rajasthan JET / JNEE (Women University Entrance), alongside direct university merit counselling for eligible 10+2 Agriculture/Science candidates. Both Rajasthan JET state counselling seats and university-level seats require verification of Class XII subject combinations (PCB, PCM, PCA or Agriculture) and original academic certificates at reporting. Check Rajasthan JET or University Application Window: Register through the official Jayoti Vidyapeeth Women's University, Jaipur admissions portal (https://www.jvwu.ac.in/faculty-of-agriculture-and-veterinary-science.html) or select the institution during Rajasthan JET / Pre-PG choice filling. Submit Academic & Entrance Score Details: Provide Class X and XII mark sheets (or B.Sc. Agriculture transcript for PG), JET/CUET/University entrance scorecard and category certificate. Document Verification & Tuition Fee Confirmation: Verify original documents on campus at Jaipur and obtain the itemised semester/annual fee receipt including hostel and examination heads.

What practical learning is described at JVWU Jaipur?

Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE) in adopted villages during the final year. Experiential Learning Programme (ELP) modules in organic inputs, nursery management, Mushroom cultivation and seed production. Field visits to Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs), state agricultural research stations and agri-input processing plants in Rajasthan.

Which teaching facilities are documented at JVWU Jaipur?

Agronomy & Instructional Crop Farm: Field demonstration plots at Jaipur for kharif and rabi crop trials, seed bed preparation, weed management and micro-irrigation exercises. Soil Science & Agricultural Chemistry Lab: Equipped for soil pH, electrical conductivity, organic carbon, macronutrient (N-P-K) and micronutrient testing. Horticulture, Polyhouse & Orchard Unit: Protected cultivation polyhouse/shade-net house, fruit orchard nursery and post-harvest handling practical space. Plant Pathology, Entomology & Genetics Labs: Compound and stereo microscopy, insect rearing cages, disease specimen collection and seed purity testing equipment. Student Hostels, Library & Transport: Separate boys and girls residential hostels at Jaipur, agricultural library journals, mess and city bus connectivity.

What student facilities are documented for JVWU Jaipur?

Student Hostels, Library & Transport: Separate boys and girls residential hostels at Jaipur, agricultural library journals, mess and city bus connectivity.

What scholarship information is available for JVWU Jaipur?

Jayoti Vidyapeeth Women's University, Jaipur offers merit-based tuition scholarships based on Class XII / qualifying examination marks or JET rank, alongside Rajasthan government post-matric scholarships (SJMS / Samaj Kalyan Vibhag) for eligible SC, ST, OBC, MBC and EBC students. University Entrance & Qualifying Marks Merit Scholarship (2026-27): Tuition fee waiver slabs (10% to 50% in first year) for applicants scoring 75%+ to 90%+ in 10+2 or high rank in Rajasthan JET / CUET; continuation requires maintaining minimum SGPA and 75% attendance. Rajasthan State Post-Matric & Girl Child Agriculture Incentive: Eligible Rajasthan domicile students can apply on the Rajasthan SSO Scholarship Portal; girl students in agriculture may also check state Rajshree / Agriculture Department incentive schemes.

What training and outcome evidence is available for JVWU Jaipur?

The placement and training cell at Jayoti Vidyapeeth Women's University, Jaipur coordinates final-year agribusiness agro-industrial attachments, KVK internships and preparation for Rajasthan Agriculture Supervisor, AAO, Pre-PG, ICAR JRF and IBPS AFO competitive examinations. Graduates pursue roles in seed, fertilizer, crop-protection, micro-irrigation, agri-fintech, warehousing and rural banking sectors, as well as M.Sc. Agriculture through Rajasthan Pre-PG and CUET-PG.
