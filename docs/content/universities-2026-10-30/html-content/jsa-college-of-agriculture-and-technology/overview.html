Agriculture education on the Chennai–Trichy corridor

JSA College of Agriculture and Technology is based around Ma. Podaiyur and Avatti, near Tholudhur in Cuddalore district. Sri Venkateswara Educational Trust, Salem, established the college in 2015. It is affiliated to TNAU, with agricultural communities surrounding its highway-corridor location.

The college describes learning through projects, fieldwork and rural agricultural experience. Its published support includes computer and language laboratories, while the training and placement cell describes interview and competitive-examination preparation. Applicants considering undergraduate agriculture can ask how classroom learning, field assignments and rural activities are balanced, and confirm which practical modules are available for their cohort.
