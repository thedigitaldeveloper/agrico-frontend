The documented academic setting

JSACAT describes experiential learning and RAWE, while the parent group reports village-based agricultural activities. A separate official laboratory page describes computer access and a language laboratory for listening, speaking, reading and writing practice.

Facilities identified in the sources

Computer laboratory

The official facility page describes networked computer access.

Language laboratory

The laboratory description includes listening and speaking practice with recording and review.

Practical learning and student access

For B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, the usefulness of a facility depends on actual teaching access, supervision and the programme timetable. The documented learning activities are listed below; ask the department about your own cohort’s arrangements.

  • The official site identifies RAWE and experiential learning among its teaching methods.
  • The parent group describes agricultural specialist seminars and village engagement.
  • The published course structure uses an English-medium semester course-credit system.
