FAQs: questions answered

Where is JSACAT located?

The official site places it at Avatti near Tholudhur, in Cuddalore district on the Chennai–Trichy highway corridor.

Who established the college?

The parent-group page says Sri Venkateswara Educational Trust, Salem, established it in 2015.

Does the website list courses beyond agriculture?

Yes, it advertises horticulture and selected agriculture postgraduate programmes. This draft’s confirmed ICAR scope remains the B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture row in the audit.

What rural learning does JSACAT describe?

The official material lists RAWE, fieldwork, experiential learning and village interaction.

Which school subject groups are published?

The official page lists PCMB, PCB with a listed elective, and Physics/Chemistry/Botany/Zoology. Confirm current TNAU eligibility and category thresholds before applying.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at JSACAT?

4 years / 8 semesters.

Which named academic staff are listed for JSACAT?

JSA’s crop-management faculty page names teachers in agronomy, soils, microbiology, environmental science and agricultural engineering. The selected entries retain the role/qualification combinations shown on that page. Ms. S. Ragunath — Assistant Professor, Soil Science and Agricultural Chemistry — M.Sc.; Ph.D..

Which learning facilities are documented at JSACAT?

Computer laboratory: The official facility page describes networked computer access. Language laboratory: The laboratory description includes listening and speaking practice with recording and review.

Which university is named as the affiliating institution for JSACAT?

Tamil Nadu Agricultural University, Coimbatore is the affiliating institution named in this profile.

Where is the official application information for JSACAT?

https://www.jsaagri.in/eligibility. Read the programme, campus and applicable intake instructions on this official channel.

Which agriculture or allied programmes are covered at JSACAT?

This profile covers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture. The courses and fees table separates the qualifications and published fee periods.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at JSACAT?

College page lists Physics/Chemistry with Biology and Mathematics; or Biology with Biochemistry/Biotechnology/Microbiology/Home Science/Computer Science; or Botany and Zoology. Its displayed four-subject thresholds are OC 55%, BC/BCC/BCM 50%, MBC/DNC 45%, and a qualifying-subject pass for SC/ST. Confirm these undated college-page criteria against the intended TNAU intake-year prospectus.

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at JSACAT?

A verified numerical fee is not available. A dated official fee schedule with tuition, compulsory charges and residential costs was not verified.

How does admission to JSACAT work?

JSACAT publishes an academic-stream subject table and category thresholds on its eligibility page. Its banner asks students to register with TNAU and the college. Confirm the current university rules, because the banner year does not prove the eligibility table was revised. These steps describe the TNAU counselling channel. Any separate college-management route must be checked independently. Register with TNAU: Submit a single online application. Set course preferences: Order eligible college and course combinations. Follow counselling: General-quota counselling runs online; special-reservation sessions may be offline.

What is the recorded ICAR scope for JSACAT?

JSA College of Agriculture and Technology: • B.Sc. (Hons) Agriculture (Intake- 175), recorded term 2024-06-20 to 2029-06-19. This entry applies to the named unit and programme.

What practical learning is described at JSACAT?

The official site identifies RAWE and experiential learning among its teaching methods. The parent group describes agricultural specialist seminars and village engagement. The published course structure uses an English-medium semester course-credit system.

Which teaching facilities are documented at JSACAT?

Computer laboratory: The official facility page describes networked computer access. Language laboratory: The laboratory description includes listening and speaking practice with recording and review.

What student facilities are documented for JSACAT?

JSACAT describes experiential learning and RAWE, while the parent group reports village-based agricultural activities. A separate official laboratory page describes computer access and a language laboratory for listening, speaking, reading and writing practice. Accommodation availability, meal charges and transport arrangements for your entry year still need direct confirmation.

What scholarship information is available for JSACAT?

A current agriculture-specific scholarship schedule for JSACAT was not verified. Request the scheme name, funding authority, category rules, renewal conditions and net payable amount in writing before relying on a concession.

What training and outcome evidence is available for JSACAT?

JSA’s outcomes page gives named B.Sc. Agriculture alumni examples across employment, enterprise and higher study.
