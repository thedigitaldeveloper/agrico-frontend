FAQs: questions answered

Where is JSACAT located?

The official site places it at Avatti near Tholudhur, in Cuddalore district on the Chennai–Trichy highway corridor.

Sources: [2]

Who established the college?

The parent-group page says Sri Venkateswara Educational Trust, Salem, established it in 2015.

Yes, it advertises horticulture and selected agriculture postgraduate programmes. This draft’s confirmed ICAR scope remains the B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture row in the audit.

The official material lists RAWE, fieldwork, experiential learning and village interaction.

What are the entry requirements for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at JSACAT?

College page lists Physics/Chemistry with Biology and Mathematics; or Biology with Biochemistry/Biotechnology/Microbiology/Home Science/Computer Science; or Botany and Zoology. Its displayed four-subject thresholds are OC 55%, BC/BCC/BCM 50%, MBC/DNC 45%, and a qualifying-subject pass for SC/ST. Confirm these undated college-page criteria against the intended TNAU intake-year prospectus.

Sources: [1][2][5][8]

How does admission to JSACAT work?

JSACAT publishes an academic-stream subject table and category thresholds on its eligibility page. Its banner asks students to register with TNAU and the college. Confirm the current university rules, because the banner year does not prove the eligibility table was revised. These steps describe the TNAU counselling channel. Any separate college-management route must be checked independently. Register with TNAU: Submit a single online application. Set course preferences: Order eligible college and course combinations. Follow counselling: General-quota counselling runs online; special-reservation sessions may be offline.

Sources: [2][5][6]
