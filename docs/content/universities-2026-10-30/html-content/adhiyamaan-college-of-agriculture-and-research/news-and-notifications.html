News & Notices: questions answered

Which dated admission notices are recorded for ACAR?

No dated admission event is verified in this profile. The official channel is https://acar.ac.in/index/ug-programme/; check the notice for the programme and entry year you intend to join.

How does admission to ACAR work?

ACAR’s UG page asks all applicants to obtain a TNAU rank and describes university and management channels. Treat the published 65:35 split as undated guidance until checked against the intended admission year. Register for the TNAU ranking process: ACAR explicitly asks all candidates to register through the TNAU admission website and obtain a rank. Confirm the relevant admission channel: Ask ACAR which current seats are offered through university counselling or management and request the applicable eligibility rules.

How can I contact ACAR about an application?

Published phone: +91 6380277180. Published email: office@acar.ac.in. Official admissions: https://acar.ac.in/index/ug-programme/
