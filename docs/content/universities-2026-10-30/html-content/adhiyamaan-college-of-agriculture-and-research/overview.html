Agricultural study near Hosur

Adhiyamaan College of Agriculture and Research was established by AERI Trust in 2016. The college describes a 122.5-acre setting at Athimugam, around 22 kilometres from Hosur. Its TNAU affiliation provides the academic framework for the undergraduate agriculture programme.

The degree brings together crop improvement, plant protection, agricultural engineering, economics and extension. ACAR also publishes departmental material that helps applicants look beyond the course title: its plant-protection section describes insect specimens, microscopy and applied insect-related learning resources. A campus visit can examine how those resources support practical classes and how laboratory study connects with crop problems.
