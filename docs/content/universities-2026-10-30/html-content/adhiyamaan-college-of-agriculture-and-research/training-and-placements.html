How to assess a placement claim

For B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, ask for a report that matches the course, campus and graduating batch. Compare outcomes on the same basis rather than using an isolated maximum package.

Evidence to requestWhat to read
Cohort and reporting yearWhich programme, campus and graduating batch the report covers
Eligible students and offersHow the institution defines participation and reported placement
Roles and employersWhether the work and qualification requirements match the degree
Salary descriptionWhether a quoted figure is median, average, highest or another measure
Internship versus employmentWhether an experience is curricular training, a paid internship or a final job offer

Which teaching facilities are documented at ACAR?

Library: A dedicated library section is provided on the official college website. Laboratories: Department-based laboratories are listed by the secondary facilities directory. Plant-protection laboratory resources: The official department page lists insect specimens, microscopes and insect-related teaching equipment. Residential study and everyday services: The hostel description lists Wi-Fi, RO drinking water, reading space, newspapers and indoor games. These are published amenities; room allocation and charges are not supplied by that page. Hostel health support: ACAR describes first-aid supplies and a vehicle for taking students requiring medical attention to a hospital in Hosur. This is a published support arrangement, not a medical-service availability guarantee.
