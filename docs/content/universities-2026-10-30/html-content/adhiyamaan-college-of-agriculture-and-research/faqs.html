FAQs: questions answered

Who runs ACAR?

The official website identifies AERI Trust, Adhiyamaan Educational and Research Institutions, as the organisation running the college.

Sources: [2]

Where is the campus?

It is at Athimugam on the Shoolagiri–Berigai Road in Krishnagiri district; the college places it about 22 km from Hosur.

Sources: [2]

Must applicants register with TNAU?

The published UG page says all candidates should register through the TNAU admission website and obtain ranking. Confirm current instructions before applying.

Sources: [3]

Is ACAR a university?

No. The audited record identifies a private college affiliated to Tamil Nadu Agricultural University.

Sources: [1]

Until when does the audited ICAR term run?

The agriculture entry is dated 8 February 2024 to 7 February 2029. Its printed intake is not a live seat-availability statement.

Sources: [1]

What entry qualification is published for ACAR?

The official college admission page requires the applicable TNAU eligibility norms. Current subject combinations, category thresholds and equivalence rules were not independently verified.

Sources: [1][3]

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at ACAR?

4 years.

Sources: [1][3]

What residential or welfare facilities does ACAR describe?

The hostel description lists Wi-Fi, RO drinking water, reading space, newspapers and indoor games. These are published amenities; room allocation and charges are not supplied by that page. ACAR describes first-aid supplies and a vehicle for taking students requiring medical attention to a hospital in Hosur. This is a published support arrangement, not a medical-service availability guarantee.

Which scholarship or support information is published for ACAR?

ACAR publishes a scholarship-support sheet covering its enrolled cohorts. It names the 7.5% quota, First Graduate, Pudhumai Penn, Tamizh Pudhalvan, SC/ST/SCC and BC/MBC/DNC categories. These are reported support routes, not a promise that every new agriculture applicant qualifies. The institutional sheet lists the named quota/community and state schemes and marks UMIS updates/verification. Request the current scheme notice, award amount and your category-specific documents from the scholarship office; its published nodal contact is office@acar.ac.in.

Sources: [7]

How does admission to ACAR work?

ACAR’s UG page asks all applicants to obtain a TNAU rank and describes university and management channels. Treat the published 65:35 split as undated guidance until checked against the intended admission year. Register for the TNAU ranking process: ACAR explicitly asks all candidates to register through the TNAU admission website and obtain a rank. Confirm the relevant admission channel: Ask ACAR which current seats are offered through university counselling or management and request the applicable eligibility rules.
