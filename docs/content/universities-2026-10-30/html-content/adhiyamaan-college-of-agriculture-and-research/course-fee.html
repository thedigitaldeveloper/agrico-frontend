Courses & Fees: questions answered

What fee is documented for B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at ACAR?

A verified numerical fee is not available. A dated official fee schedule with tuition, compulsory charges and residential costs was not verified.

How long is B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture at ACAR?

4 years. The published course structure spans genetics, agronomy, soil science, plant protection, agricultural economics and related subjects within a four-year semester-based agriculture degree.

Sources: [1][3]

What scholarship information is available for ACAR?

ACAR publishes a scholarship-support sheet covering its enrolled cohorts. It names the 7.5% quota, First Graduate, Pudhumai Penn, Tamizh Pudhalvan, SC/ST/SCC and BC/MBC/DNC categories. These are reported support routes, not a promise that every new agriculture applicant qualifies. Reported state-support and scholarship categories: The institutional sheet lists the named quota/community and state schemes and marks UMIS updates/verification. Request the current scheme notice, award amount and your category-specific documents from the scholarship office; its published nodal contact is office@acar.ac.in.

Sources: [7]

How can I contact ACAR about an application?

Published phone: +91 6380277180. Published email: office@acar.ac.in. Official admissions: https://acar.ac.in/index/ug-programme/
