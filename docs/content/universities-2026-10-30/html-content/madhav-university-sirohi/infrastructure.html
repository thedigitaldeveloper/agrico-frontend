The documented academic setting

Abu Road / Pindwara campus agricultural farm featuring arid and semi-arid crop trials (castor, fennel, cumin, wheat), greenhouse unit and tribal farming outreach.

Facilities identified in the sources

Agronomy & Instructional Crop Farm

Field demonstration plots at Sirohi for kharif and rabi crop trials, seed bed preparation, weed management and micro-irrigation exercises.

Soil Science & Agricultural Chemistry Lab

Equipped for soil pH, electrical conductivity, organic carbon, macronutrient (N-P-K) and micronutrient testing.

Horticulture, Polyhouse & Orchard Unit

Protected cultivation polyhouse/shade-net house, fruit orchard nursery and post-harvest handling practical space.

Plant Pathology, Entomology & Genetics Labs

Compound and stereo microscopy, insect rearing cages, disease specimen collection and seed purity testing equipment.

Student Hostels, Library & Transport

Separate boys and girls residential hostels at Sirohi, agricultural library journals, mess and city bus connectivity.

Practical learning and student access

For B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture; M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy), the usefulness of a facility depends on actual teaching access, supervision and the programme timetable. The documented learning activities are listed below; ask the department about your own cohort’s arrangements.

  • Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE) in adopted villages during the final year.
  • Experiential Learning Programme (ELP) modules in organic inputs, nursery management, Mushroom cultivation and seed production.
  • Field visits to Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs), state agricultural research stations and agri-input processing plants in Rajasthan.

People and supervision

The Faculty of Agriculture at Madhav University, Sirohi comprises teaching staff across Agronomy, Genetics & Plant Breeding, Soil Science, Horticulture, Plant Pathology, Entomology and Agricultural Extension.
