Agricultural education at Madhav University, Sirohi (Faculty of Agriculture)

Madhav University, Sirohi operates its dedicated Faculty of Agriculture at Sirohi, Rajasthan, offering B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, M.Sc. Agriculture (Agronomy). The curriculum follows the 5th Deans’ Committee / ICAR credit structure with core courses in agronomy, genetics and plant breeding, soil science, entomology, plant pathology, horticulture, agricultural economics and extension education.

Applicants can enter the four-year B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture programme through Rajasthan JET / Madhav University Entrance & Merit. Abu Road / Pindwara campus agricultural farm featuring arid and semi-arid crop trials (castor, fennel, cumin, wheat), greenhouse unit and tribal farming outreach. Candidates comparing private agriculture universities in Rajasthan should verify both state JET counselling seat matrices and cohort-specific statutory accreditation terms before locking their choice.

Curriculum and practical learning

  • Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE) in adopted villages during the final year.
  • Experiential Learning Programme (ELP) modules in organic inputs, nursery management, Mushroom cultivation and seed production.
  • Field visits to Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs), state agricultural research stations and agri-input processing plants in Rajasthan.

Scholarships and fee concessions at Madhav University, Sirohi

Madhav University, Sirohi offers merit-based tuition scholarships based on Class XII / qualifying examination marks or JET rank, alongside Rajasthan government post-matric scholarships (SJMS / Samaj Kalyan Vibhag) for eligible SC, ST, OBC, MBC and EBC students.
