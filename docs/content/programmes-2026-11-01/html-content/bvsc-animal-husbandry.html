Professional veterinary degree

What the B.V.Sc. & A.H. degree covers

Bachelor of Veterinary Science and Animal Husbandry (B.V.Sc. & A.H.) is the statutory professional qualification required to practice veterinary medicine and surgery in India. The programme is strictly governed by the Veterinary Council of India (VCI) under the Minimum Standards of Veterinary Education (MSVE) Regulations.

The degree integrates veterinary medical sciences with animal husbandry and production management. Students undergo intensive training in veterinary anatomy, physiology, pharmacology, pathology, parasitology, microbiology, surgery, radiology, gynecology, obstetrics, and clinical medicine alongside livestock production management, animal genetics, and feeds and nutrition.

Practical clinical training takes place in Veterinary Clinical Complex (VCC) teaching hospitals and Instructional Livestock Farm Complexes (ILFC). In the final year, students complete a compulsory 12-month rotating internship spanning clinical practice, mobile veterinary clinics, slaughterhouse meat inspection, zoo / wildlife exposure, and farm placements.

Duration 5.5 years (4.5 years + 1-year rotating internship)
Statutory council Veterinary Council of India (VCI)
National entrance NEET-UG (Mandatory for all admissions in India)
Professional registration Registration with State Veterinary Council & VCI

Who should apply for this programme

This programme is well-suited for students and aspirants who align with the following academic profile and career ambitions:

  • Class 12 biology students passionate about animal health, clinical surgery, companion animal medicine, and wildlife conservation
  • Candidates qualified in NEET-UG who wish to build a professional medical career treating livestock, pets, poultry, and equine animals
  • Those keen on public health, zoonotic disease epidemiology, food safety inspection, and vaccine research
  • Students aiming for gazetted government service as Veterinary Assistant Surgeons or commission in the Indian Army Remount Veterinary Corps (RVC)

Professional curriculum framework under VCI MSVE Regulations

The B.V.Sc. & A.H. curriculum is structured as a professional year-based system under VCI regulations, combining preclinical, paraclinical, and clinical subjects.

Stage 1

First Professional Year: Pre-Clinical & Husbandry Foundations

Gross and microscopic anatomy, animal physiology, biochemistry, and livestock production systems.

Veterinary Anatomy (osteology, arthrology, myology, neurology, histology) Veterinary Physiology and Biochemistry Livestock Production Management (cattle, buffalo, sheep, goat, pig, and horse management) Instructional livestock farm management practicals
Stage 2

Second Professional Year: Paraclinical Sciences & Breeding

Pathology, microbiology, parasitology, animal genetics, and animal nutrition.

Veterinary Pathology (general, systemic, and avian pathology, necropsy) Veterinary Microbiology (bacteriology, virology, immunology, mycology) Veterinary Parasitology (helminthology, entomology, protozoology) Animal Genetics and Breeding (principles, population genetics, molecular breeding) Animal Nutrition (ruminant and non-ruminant nutrition, feed technology)
Stage 3

Third Professional Year: Pharmacology & Public Health

Drug actions, clinical pathology, public health, and livestock product technology.

Veterinary Pharmacology and Toxicology (chemotherapy, neuropharmacology) Veterinary Public Health and Epidemiology (zoonoses, food hygiene) Veterinary and Animal Husbandry Extension Education Livestock Products Technology (meat, milk, and wool processing) Veterinary clinical complex orientation and diagnostic practicals
Stage 4

Fourth Professional Year (1.5 Years): Clinical Disciplines

Comprehensive medicine, anesthesia, surgery, radiology, and animal reproduction.

Veterinary Clinical Medicine (infectious and non-infectious animal diseases) Veterinary Preventive Medicine and jurisprudence Veterinary Surgery and Radiology (anesthesia, diagnostic imaging, orthopedic and soft-tissue surgery) Veterinary Gynaecology and Obstetrics (infertility, artificial insemination, obstetrics) Veterinary Clinical Complex (VCC) hands-on hospital rotations
Stage 5

Fifth Year: Compulsory Rotating Internship (12 Months)

Full-time practical hospital rotations, field postings, and diagnostic laboratory exposure.

Clinical hospital rotations (medicine, surgery, gynecology) Ambulatory and emergency veterinary care Livestock and poultry farm management posting Meat inspection and slaughterhouse hygiene training Zoo, wild animal, and equine healthcare exposure

Hands-on practical learning & experiential attachments

Agricultural degrees place heavy emphasis on experiential, field-based and industrial training to ensure students graduate with job-ready operational competencies:

Veterinary Clinical Complex (VCC) Postings

Daily rotations handling indoor and outdoor animal patients, performing physical examinations, diagnostic ultrasounds, radiography, surgical procedures, and intensive medical care.

Instructional Livestock Farm Complex (ILFC)

Direct student management of dairy cattle, buffaloes, small ruminants, and poultry farms on university campuses, mastering milking, feed formulation, and artificial insemination.

Compulsory Rotating Internship

One full calendar year of clinical postings across state veterinary hospitals, disease diagnostic laboratories, dairy processing plants, and zoo reserves.

Eligibility criteria & subject prerequisites

To apply for admission to B.V.Sc. & A.H. (Bachelor of Veterinary Science and Animal Husbandry), candidates must fulfill the prescribed academic qualifications and subject requirements:

  • Must have passed the 10+2 qualifying examination with Physics, Chemistry, Biology (or Biotechnology), and English from a recognised education board.
  • Minimum 50% aggregate marks in Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology, and English (47.5% for SC, ST, and designated reserved categories as per VCI norms).
  • Candidate must have qualified in the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET-UG) of the respective admission year.
  • Must have attained the minimum age of 17 years on or before 31 December of the year of admission.
  • Specific physical fitness standards for handling large animals apply.

Admission routes & entrance examinations

Admissions are conducted through national, state-level and university-specific competitive selection channels:

Veterinary Council of India (VCI) 15% All-India Quota

Centralized online counselling conducted by VCI based on NEET-UG All-India Ranks for 15% seats across all recognised government veterinary colleges in India.

State Veterinary Counselling (85% State Quota)

Conducted by respective state veterinary universities or state CET boards based on NEET-UG merit scores for state domicile candidates.

Exam Name Conducting Body Key Details & Test Pattern
NEET-UG NTA Mandatory national entrance exam for 100% of B.V.Sc. & A.H. seats across all veterinary colleges in India.

Step-by-step admission journey

  1. Appear for NEET-UG: Register, appear, and qualify in NEET-UG with qualifying percentile.
  2. Register for VCI / State Counselling: Register for 15% all-India counselling on the official VCI portal and for 85% state quota on your home-state veterinary university portal.
  3. Choice Filling & Locking: Fill preferences of veterinary colleges based on rank and seat matrix.
  4. Physical Reporting & Verification: Complete medical fitness examination, document scrutiny, and fee payment at the allotted veterinary college.

Fee structure & scholarships guide

Tuition fees in government state veterinary universities (such as TANUVAS, GADVASU, LUVAS, MAFSU) are heavily subsidised, generally ranging from ₹20,000 to ₹55,000 per academic year. In private veterinary colleges approved by VCI, annual fees range between ₹3,50,000 to ₹6,50,000 under state quota and higher under management quotas. Interns receive a monthly stipend during their 12-month internship as per university/state norms.

Professional veterinary careers in healthcare, defense, and industry

B.V.Sc. & A.H. graduates become licensed veterinary doctors. The veterinary profession offers diverse opportunities in government animal husbandry departments, the armed forces, companion animal clinics, pharmaceutical research, and commercial dairy and poultry industries.

Veterinary Assistant Surgeon (VAS) / Veterinary Officer

Class-I / Gazetted medical officer in state animal husbandry departments running veterinary dispensaries and artificial insemination centres.

Remount Veterinary Corps (RVC) Officer

Commissioned officer (Captain) in the Indian Army responsible for the breeding, healthcare, and training of army horses, mules, and military working dogs.

Small Animal / Companion Veterinary Practitioner

Independent veterinary clinical practitioner treating domestic pets, performing surgeries, and providing preventative animal healthcare.

Dairy & Poultry Technical Consultant

Veterinary doctor supervising flock health, bio-security, vaccination, and nutrition in industrial poultry and dairy enterprises.

Wildlife & Zoo Veterinarian

Specialist providing healthcare, chemical immobilization, disease surveillance, and breeding management for captive and wild animals.

Quarantine / Food Safety Inspection Officer

Government officer checking livestock imports, animal disease outbreaks, and abattoir meat inspection at ports and borders.

Applicant questions answered

Frequently asked questions regarding admission, course syllabus, accreditation and career prospects:

Is NEET-UG compulsory for admission to B.V.Sc. & A.H.?

Yes. As per the Veterinary Council of India regulations and Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying guidelines, admission to 100% of B.V.Sc. & A.H. seats across all veterinary colleges in India is based strictly on NEET-UG scores.

Can B.V.Sc. & A.H. graduates use the title 'Dr.'?

Yes. Upon completing B.V.Sc. & A.H. and the compulsory 1-year internship, graduates register with the State Veterinary Council and the Veterinary Council of India, earning the statutory right to practice veterinary medicine and use the title 'Doctor' (Dr.).

Can a veterinary graduate join the Indian Army directly?

Yes. The Indian Army conducts direct SSB interviews for male and female veterinary graduates to join as commissioned officers with the rank of Captain in the Remount Veterinary Corps (RVC).

What is the duration of B.V.Sc. & A.H. under current regulations?

Under the VCI MSVE Regulations, the programme spans 5.5 years, comprising 4.5 professional academic years of coursework followed by a compulsory 12-month rotating internship.
